# Mahārthamañjarī — T2 (the alternative-reading), gāthās 8–70

*Pass 3 (= T2). Strategy: **S2 — the argument-priority** (render for the argument's flow, the connective tissue explicit) crossed with the register-shift (T1 formal-literal → T2 flowing-constructive). Composed from the itx + the R1 only; the T1's sentences were not re-read. The Parimala's Krama-self-understanding frames the terms.*

## The heart-pulse (8–12)

**g.8** — paryAlochanavimukhe vastusvabhAvasyAtmano hR^idaye | sha~NkAviShavegeneva saMsArabhayena muhyati lokaH ||
**T2:** "The world — its own-nature's object not yet examined — in the self's heart is dazed by the dread of saṃsāra, as by the venom of doubt."
*fork: "venom of doubt" (the śaṅkā-viṣa) — T2 presses the poison-image; the world's daze as the unexamined self's.*

**g.10** — Urdhvo jvalati prakAsho lokAlokasya ma~NgalapradIpaH | vimarshadashAmukhAnItadahyamAnamalAlitailavichChardaH ||
**T2:** "Upward the lamp-of-auspiciousness of the seen-and-unseen blazes — its flow of sense-oil, drawn into the ten-faces of reflexion, consumed [as] fuel."
*fork: the ten-faces of reflexion (the daśā-vimarśa-mukhā) as the burner — T2 makes the oil the sense-objects, the faces the reflexion's ten; the burning = the world's transfiguration into light.*

**g.11** — sannaiva hR^idayaprakAsho bhavanakriyAyA bhavati kartA | saiva kriyAvimarshaH svasthA kShubhitA cha vishvavistAraH ||
**T2:** "The heart's light, just by being, is the agent of the becoming-deed; the very action-reflexion — at ease, the world's widening once stirred."
*fork: "just by being" (sann-eva) — the light's mere presence is the agency; the still/stirred pair as the world's on/off.*

**g.12** — pR^ithvIparamashivayoH pratyAhAre prakAshaparamArthe | yo.anyonyavisheShaH sa eva hR^idayasya vimarshonmeShaH ||
**T2:** "In the withdrawal of earth and the supreme Śiva — the light's own essence — that which is the mutual differentia: that is the heart's reflexive upsurge."
*fork: "the mutual differentia" (the anyonya-viśeṣa) — T2 presses the distinction as the dynamism, not a static gap.*

## The powers (13–18)

**g.13** — tathA tathA dR^ishyamAnAnAM shaktisahasrANAmekasa~NghaTTaH | nijahR^idayodyamarUpo bhavati shivo nAma paramasvachChandaH ||
**T2:** "Thus, of the thousand powers as they show themselves, the single confluence — become the figure of the heart's own upsurge — is what's named Śiva, the supreme spontaneity."
*fork: "spontaneity" (parama-svacchanda) — T2 renders the freedom as spontaneity (the report's svātantrya-dossier: ontological efficacy).*

**g.14** — sa eva vishvamIkShituM sthAtuM kartuM chonmukho bhavan | shaktisvabhAvaH kathito hR^idayatrikoNamadhumAsalollAsaH ||
**T2:** "He, becoming poised to see, to stay, to act on the world — the power-natured — is spoken as the honey-flood of the heart's triangle."
*fork: the honey-flood (madhumāsa-lollāsa) — T2 keeps the sweetness; the triangle as the heart's own.*

**g.15** — j~nAnaM kriyeti dvayorapi prathamonmeShe sadAshivo devaH | dvitIyAyA ullekhe dvitIyaH sa bhavatIshvaro nAma ||
**T2:** "Knowledge and action — of both, at the first unsealing, the god is Sadāśiva; at the second's sketch, he turns the second, named the Lord (Īśvara)."
*fork: "unsealing" (unmeṣa) — T2 renders the upsurge as the unsealing (the opening of the eye); the sketch (ullekha) as the second's trace.*

**g.16** — j~nAtA sa AtmA j~neyasvabhAvashcha lokavyavahAraH | ekarasAM saMsR^iShTi yatra gatau sA khalu nistuShA vidyA ||
**T2:** "He, the self, is the knower; the world's commerce is of the known's grain; where the going is the one-flavored mingling — that indeed is the huskless lore."
*fork: "one-flavored mingling" (ekarasā-saṃsṛṣṭi) — T2 presses the single-taste; "huskless lore" for the nistuṣā vidyā.*

**g.17** — ekarase svabhAve udbhAvayantI vikalpashilpAni | mAyeti lokapateH paramasvatantrasya mohanI shaktiH ||
**T2:** "In the one-flavored nature, causing the crafts of division to well up — she is called māyā, the bewildering might of the world's lord, the utterly free."
*fork: "the crafts of division" (vikalpa-śilpāni) — T2 renders the alternatives as crafted structures; the free one's own bewilderment-power.*

**g.18** — sarvakaraH sarvaj~naH pUrNo nityo.asa~Nkuchitashcha | viparIta iva mahesho yAbhistA bhavanti pa~nchashaktayaH ||
**T2:** "The all-worker, all-knower, full, eternal, unsqueezed — the great Lord, as though inverted: by which [powers] those five powers come to be."
*fork: "unsqueezed" (asaṅkucita) — T2 renders the unshrinking as unsqueezed (the un-contracted); the inversion as the appearance under contraction.*

## The karaṇas (21–25)

**g.21** — kallolAyamAnAni sadA hR^idayAmbunidhau trINi karaNAni | AkarShantIdantAM tatrAhantAM (tathA ahantAM) chAtrArpayanti ||
**T2:** "Waving forever in the heart's sea, the three instruments pull the this-wise toward them and there hand it to the I-wise."
*fork: "this-wise / I-wise" (idantā / ahantā) — T2 renders the -ness as -wise to keep the direction; the offering of the this into the I.*

**g.22** — hR^idayasthitasya vibhorviShayAloko vishR^i~Nkhalo bhavati | j~nAnendriyadIpeShu nijanijagolAgranityalagneShu ||
**T2:** "For the lord at the heart, the world's light runs unchained, its lamps of sense ever-caught at the tips of their own spheres."
*fork: "their own spheres' tips" (nija-nija-golāgra) — T2 keeps the per-lamp focal tips; the unchained seeing.*

**g.23** — bhavanti karaNAni pa~ncha khalu karmapradhAnAni lokanAthasya | spandate svaira yairjano jaDAdvilakShaNo bhavan ||
**T2:** "The five instruments are the world-lord's deed-principals — by whose free ranging the man, growing unlike the inert, pulses."
*fork: "free ranging" (svaira) — T2 renders the roaming; the pulse (spanda) as the man's difference from the inert.*

**g.24** — vishvodyAnavirUDhAni gandhapramukhAni sugandhIni puShpANi | pa~nchApyAjighran krIDati trailokyadhUrto devaH ||
**T2:** "The five sweet flowers grown in the world's garden — scent and the rest — sniffing all of them, the three-worlds' rover god plays."
*fork: "rover" (dhūrta) — T2 renders the trickster as the rover (the play, less pejorative); the world as the garden of the senses.*

**g.25** — styAnasya kramavashAdikShurasasyeva shivaprakAshasya | guDapiNDA iva pa~nchApi bhUtAni madhuratAM na mu~nchanti ||
**T2:** "By the sequence of the settling, as with the cane-juice: the five elements, like lumps of raw sugar, never let go their sweetness — such is the Śiva-light."
*fork: "settling" (styāna) — T2 renders the thickening as the settling (the condensation); the sugar-lumps keep the sweetness (the prakāśa immanent).*

## The yāmala and the māyā (26–32)

**g.26** — sarvasya bhuvanavibhramayantrollAsasya tantuvallIva | vimarshasaMrambhamayI ujjR^imbhate shambhormahAshaktiH ||
**T2:** "As a thread-vine [strings] the whirling upsurge of all the worlds: the reflexion-fervor-bodied great might of Śambhu unfolds."
*fork: "thread-vine" (tantuvallī) — T2 keeps the threading-image; the unfolding (ujjṛmbhate) as the power's stretch.*

**g.27** — yadadhvanAM cha ShaTkaM tatra prakAshArthalakShaNamardham | vimarshashabdasvabhAvamardhamiti shivasya yAmalollAsaH ||
**T2:** "The sixfold of the courses: there the half bears the stamp of the light's sense; the half the grain of the reflexion-word — so the paired surge of Śiva."
*fork: "paired surge" (yāmala-ullāsa) — T2 renders the yāmala as the pair's surge; the six courses halved into light and reflexion.*

**g.28** — AlekhyavisheSha iva gajavR^iShabhayordvayaH pratibhAsaH | ekasminnevArthe shivashaktivibhAgakalpanAM kurmaH ||
**T2:** "As in a picture the double-show of elephant and bull: in the single object we frame the Shiva-Shakti split."
*fork: "double-show" (dvayaḥ pratibhāsa) — T2 keeps the image; the split as our framing (kalpanā) on one object.*

**g.29** — tilamAtre.api sharIre prekShadhvaM kITasyaitAvatI shaktiH | sA svachChandashriyo vishvasharIrasya kiyatI bhavatu ||
**T2:** "In a body no bigger than a sesame seed — look — such the might of a worm: how vast, then, the world-body's, the fortune of the free?"
*fork: the worm's sesame-might — T2 presses the scale; the free one's world-body as the unbounded.*

**g.30** — vishvonmeShadashAyAM deshikanAthasya yAvAnprasaraH | kalalAvasthayAM (sthito.api) vishvanimeShe.api tAvAnbhavati ||
**T2:** "In the state of the world's unsealing, whatever the teacher-lord's spread — even lodged in the embryo-state, at the world's closing, he is as much."
*fork: "spread" (prasara) — T2 renders the outflow as the spread; the teacher's constancy across the world's open and close.*

**g.31** — tripuTImayaM khalu samastaM tatra cha j~neye j~nAtari cha samam | dR^iDhagranthirj~nAnakalA kalayati trailokyamekakalam ||
**T2:** "All is woven of the triple — there, in the known and the knower, the same: the tight-knotted knowing-grain makes the three worlds one grain."
*fork: "grain" (kalā) — T2 renders the kalā as the grain/particle; the three-worlds made single-particle.*

**g.32** — kaH sadbhAvavisheShaH kusumAdbhavati gaganakusumasya | yatsphuraNAnuprANo lokaH sphuraNaM cha sarvasAmAnyam ||
**T2:** "What real difference springs of the sky-flower from the flower? — [they differ] in that whose flashing quickens the world: and the flashing is the common lot of all."
*fork: "quickens" (anuprāṇa) — T2 renders the breath-of-life; the flashing as the common (sarva-sāmānya) — the A-thesis's manifestness.*

## The pīṭha-arithmetic (33–41)

**g.34** — aNDamaye nijapiNDapIThe sphuranti karaNadevyaH | prasphurati cha paramashivo j~nAnanidhishcha tAsAM madhye ||
**T2:** "In the egg-made [world], on the body's own seat, the instrument-goddesses flash; and amid them the supreme Śiva flashes, the hoard of knowing."
*fork: "hoard of knowing" (jñāna-nidhi) — T2 renders the treasure; the midst-position of the supreme.*

**g.36** — shrIpIThapa~nchavAho netratrayavR^indachakrANi smarata | smarata cha gurUNAM pa~Nkti pa~nca cha shaktIH sR^iShTipramukhAH ||
**T2:** "Recall the blessed seat's five currents, the wheel-clusters of the triple eye; recall the gurus' file and the five powers, creation at their head."
*fork: "file" (paṅkti) — T2 renders the row as the file (the lineage); the seat's arithmetic.*

**g.39** — sR^iShTau dasha kalAH sthitau dvAvishatirbhavanti shaktayaH | ekAdasha saMhAre trayodasha tAsturIyaparvaNi ||
**T2:** "In creation, ten grains; in stay, two-and-twenty powers; eleven in the draw; thirteen at the fourth's turn."
*fork: "draw" (saṃhāra) — T2 renders the dissolution as the draw-in; the four turns' counts.*

**g.40** — bhAsAyAM na vikalpAH sphurati sphuradekaniShkalashriyAm | yadi pratibimbagatyA sphurati paraM ShoDashAdhikA devI ||
**T2:** "In the shimmering there are no splits — she flashes in the flashing glory of the single-partless; if she flashes by the mirror-way, the goddess is the sixteen-plus."
*fork: "shimmering" (bhāsā) — T2 renders the illumination as the shimmering; "sixteen-plus" (ṣoḍaśādhikā) kept.*

## The rites and the vāc (42–50)

**g.43** — vimraShTuM nijasattvaM vibhave kAryonmukhe stimite.api | bAhyavR^ittAntAnAM bha~NgaH prANasya saMyamo j~neyaH ||
**T2:** "To mull over one's own being in the majesty, even when the work-looms stand still: the breaking of the outer rounds — that is the breath's restraint."
*fork: "mull over" (vimraṣṭum) — T2 renders the reflection as the mulling; the breath-restraint as the outer-rounds' break.*

**g.45** — avikalpatayA vimarsho vikalpavargasyA~NgasannAhaH | arghyaM vedyavilAsaH puShpANi svabhAvapoShakAH bhAvAH ||
**T2:** "Reflexion [comes] by the non-split; the armor of the split-brigades; the oblation, the display of the known; the blooms, the states that feed the own-nature."
*fork: "split-brigades" (vikalpa-varga) — T2 renders the classes as brigades; the ritual-symbolism as the inner states.*

**g.46** — pUrNAhantAyA mukhe vishvavikalpA~NkurANAM vikShepam | mantrollekhavishuddhaM puNyaM kulabindutarpaNaM bhaNAmaH ||
**T2:** "At the mouth of the full-I, the scattering of the world's split-sprouts: we speak the mantra-sketch's pure merit, the libation at the Kula's point."
*fork: "the Kula's point" (kulabindu) — T2 renders the compound; the scattering + the libation as the two acts.*

**g.49** — mananamayI nijavibhave nijasa~Nkochabhaye trANamayI | kavalitavishvavikalpA anubhUtiH kApi mantrashabdArthaH ||
**T2:** "Woven of thought in one's own might, woven of refuge in the fear of one's own shrink; having gulped the world's splits — some such feel is the mantra-word's sense."
*fork: "woven of thought / woven of refuge" (manana-mayī / trāṇa-mayī) — T2 presses the woven-pair; the gulped-world experience.*

**g.50** — vaikhari(kA) nAma kriyA j~nAnamayI bhavati madhyamA vAk | ichChA punaH pashyantI sUkShmA sarvAsA samarasA vR^ittiH ||
**T2:** "The vaikharī is the deed; the mid-speech turns knowledge-bodied; the will again is the seer-speech, the subtle, the one-flavored working of them all."
*fork: "seer-speech" (paśyantī) — T2 renders the seeing-speech; the four-speech ladder mapped onto the powers.*

## The yogi's states and the close (51–71)

**g.51** — AnandollAsashrIH kShullakitAShTamahAsiddhisaubhAgyA | dR^ishyate yatra dashAyAM saiva devasya sarva(mahA)mudrAH ||
**T2:** "The bloom of bliss's fortune, tricked with the luck of the eight great attainments — in whatever state she shows, that is the god's all-seals."
*fork: "tricked" (kṣullakitā) — T2 renders the adorned as tricked-out; the all-seals as the mudrās.*

**g.53** — kramiko bhavati na devastasya kathaM kAlakalmaShasparshaH | nityanirAvaraNasyApi (ko jIvato mokShapratyUhaH) ko.api jIvanmuktipratyUhaH ||
**T2:** "The god has no serial order — how could the smear of time touch him, the ever-unveiled? What clog to the living one's release could there be?"
*fork: "serial order" (kramika) — T2 renders the sequential as the serial; the living-release (jīvanmukti) as the unclogged.*

**g.61** — yogI jAgarasvapnasuShuptyavasthAtrayasya paripATim | chitrAmiva maNimAlAM vimarshasUtraikagumphitAmudvahati ||
**T2:** "The yogi wears the series of the three states — waking, dream, deep sleep — as a painted string of pearls, threaded on the single cord of reflexion."
*fork: "the series... threaded on the single cord" — T2 keeps the garland; the three states as one thread.*

**g.65** — aho saMsArasukhakelI aho sulabhaM mokShamArge saubhAgyam | truTitAta~Nkakala~NkA aho shivayoginAM yAmalI siddhiH ||
**T2:** "Ah, the sport of the world's joy! Ah, the easy fortune on the release-road! Ah, the paired attainment of the Śiva-yogis, its fret-stain shattered!"
*fork: "paired attainment" (yāmala-siddhi) — T2 renders the yāmala as the paired; the three aho-exclamations kept.*

**g.68** — AstAmanyA vidyAshchatuHsrotasAmapi sAgarANAmiva | enamevAmR^itamayaM mathnAti manthAnabhairavo devaH ||
**T2:** "Let the other lores be — as the seas of the four streams: this nectar-bodied one alone the Churning-Bhairava god churns."
*fork: "Churning-Bhairava" (manthāna-bhairava) — T2 renders the name; the churning of the one nectar-lore.*

**g.70** — enameva mahArthaM yuddhArambhe pANDuputrasya | ShoDashasahasrashaktirdeva upadishati sma mAdhavaH ||
**T2:** "This very great-meaning, at the opening of the Pāṇḍu-son's war, the god of the sixteen-thousand powers, Mādhava, once taught."
*fork: the Gītā-frame kept; the "sixteen-thousand powers" as Kṛṣṇa's.*

## The T2 strategy-declaration and the forks
