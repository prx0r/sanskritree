# Mahārthamañjarī — T2 (the alternative-reading translation, the Krama-perspective)

*Pass 3 (= T2). Strategy: **S1 — the commentary-informed reading**, but the commentary is now the text's OWN (the Parimala, on disk) — the Krama's self-understanding. Composed from the ITRANS (`sources/gretil2/raw_maharthamanjari.itx`) + the R1's saṃvit-krama frame only; the T1's sentences were not re-read. The register-shift: T1 was formal-literal; T2 renders the Krama's cognition-sequence as the governing sense — every krama is the order of cognition, every vimarśa the reflexion-that-is-the-krama.*

## The maṅgala

**m.1** — namo nālayate śuṇḍāṃ viṣāṇena mṛṇāline | pratyakkamalakandāya karṇābhyāṃ parṇaśāline ||
**T2:** "Homage to the one who toys with his trunk, tusk against the lotus-stalk — the inner lotus-root, ear-leafed [Gaṇeśa]."
*fork: "viṣāṇena" (with the tusk) — T1 read "with his trunk fondles the stalk"; T2 presses the tusk-meets-stalk image (the elephant-god's double-play); the "ear-leafed" (karṇābhyāṃ parṇaśāline) as the leaf-ears.*

**m.2** — jayatyamūlamamlānamauttaraṃ tattvamadvayam | spandāspandaparispandamakarandamahotpalam ||
**T2:** "Triumphs the principle — rootless, unwithered, supreme, non-dual: the great lotus of the nectar that is the pulse, the pulse's rest, and the pulse's outpouring."
*fork: the spanda-triple as the cognition's own rhythm (the R1's Krama-perspective) — T2 renders spanda/aspanda/parispanda as "the pulse, the pulse's rest, the pulse's outpouring" (not the static "trembling/untrembling/all-trembling" of T1); the mahotpala (great lotus) as the cognition-flower.*

**m.3** — kāruṇyāmṛtasindhoruditamivāvartamīkṣaṇāpāṅgāt | mauktikamayaṃ dadhānā tāṭaṅkaṃ jayati gauravī mūrtiḥ ||
**T2:** "May the weighty form triumph, bearing the pearl-wrought earring, risen as it were from the whirlpool of the compassion-nectar sea, from the corner of her glance."
*fork: the glance-whirlpool — T2 keeps the image but reads the "weighty form" (gauravī) as the goddess's gravity (the Krama's Kālī-nature, weighty with the cognition's sequence).*

**m.4** — sphūrtaye viśvaśilpasya śrīśivānandamūrtaye | nityonmeṣanimeṣāyai nistuṣāyai namastviṣe ||
**T2:** "Homage to the radiance — the Śivānanda-form — for the flashing of the world's craft: the ever-opened-and-shut, the huskless [one]."
*fork: onmeṣanimeṣa (opening-closing) as the cognition's breath (the saṃvit's own blinking) — T2 presses the Krama-frame (the opening-closing IS the sequence's pulse), the huskless (nistuṣā) as the chaffless, the non-discursive.*

**m.5** — yasmādanuttaramahāhradamajjanaṃ me saubhāgyaśāmbhavasukhānubhavaśca yasmāt | tatsvātmacitkramavimarśamayaṃ gurūṇā-mavalliyugmamuditoditavīryamīḍe ||
**T2:** "From which my plunge into the unsurpassed great pool, and from which the auspicious Śāmbhava-joy — that pair of the gurus' feet, made of the self's consciousness-order-reflexion (svātma-cit-krama-vimarśa), its risen power risen — I praise."
*fork: the cit-krama — T2 takes the R1's sharpened reading: the guru-feet ARE the cognition-sequence's reflexion (not merely "order-of-reflection"); the guru-as-krama.*

**m.6** — namo nikhilamālinyavilāpanapaṭīyase | mahāprakāśapādābjaparāgaparamāṇave ||
**T2:** "Homage to the one skilled in dissolving the whole stain — to the supreme atom of the pollen of the great-prakāśa's lotus-feet."

**m.7** — gorakṣo lokadhiyā deśikadṛṣṭyā maheśvarānandaḥ | unmīlayāmi parimalamantargrāhyaṃ mahārthamañjaryām ||
**T2:** "I — Maheśvarānanda, a Gorakṣa to the world's eye, a teacher in the guru's view — open the Parimala, the inwardly-grasped [commentary] on the Mahārthamañjarī."

**m.8** — svakriyāyā api vyākhyāṃ svayameva prayuñjmahe | uparyapyātmasaṃrambhasambhogāmreḍanotsukāḥ ||
**T2:** "For my own work too I myself supply the gloss — eager, above it, for the reiteration of the enjoyment of my own fervor."

**m.9** — yadvā vineyajanacittacamatkriyārtha-matrodyamo'yamudito'stu tadetadāstām | saṃkṣepavistaravibhāgaviviktaśobhaḥ puṣpāñjalirbhavatu vāṅmaya eṣa śambhoḥ ||
**T2:** "Or else — for the wonder of the teachable minds, may this endeavor arise; let that be. May this verbal flower-offering of Śambhu, its beauty parted into brief-and-extended, stand [accepted]."

**m.10** — avagataśivadṛṣṭipratyabhijñārthatattva-kramasaraṇirahasyollāsasarvasvavedī | gurucaraṇasaparyācāturīcidghano'haṃ gahanamapi hṛdantarvyoma tad vyākaromi ||
**T2:** "I, dense with the skill of serving the guru's feet, who know the whole secret-springing of the Krama-path's truth — the meaning of the Śivadṛṣṭi and the Recognition understood — unfold even the dense inner-sky of the heart."
*fork: the Recognition-yoked Krama (the R1's sharpening) — T2 renders pratyabhijñā as "Recognition" (the report's term) and presses the kramasaraṇi as the path that IS the cognition's sequence.*

**m.11** — iha mahati rahasyonmīlane maṅgalāya prabhavati mama saṃvidyoginīnāṃ prasādaḥ | api tu kulasaparyābimbasaṃbandhyavandhyāḥ sakṛdapi matimanto nainamudghāṭayantu ||
**T2:** "Here, at the great unsealing of the secret, for the auspicious [rite] the grace of my awareness-yoginīs prevails; but the intelligent — barren, tied to the reflection (bimba) of the kula-worship — let them not open this, even once."
*fork: the bimba (reflection) — T2 keeps the R1's Krama-gloss: the reflection of the rite, not the cognition it mirrors; the ābhāsa-language of the report's vimarśa-dossier.*

**m.12** — svapnasamayopalabdhā sā sumukhī siddhayoginī devī | gāthābhiḥ saptatyā svāpitabhāṣābhirastu samprītā ||
**T2:** "May that fair-faced goddess — the accomplished yoginī, won at the time of dream — be pleased with the seventy gāthās in her own-awakened tongue."

**m.13** — vardhatāṃ deśikaḥ śrīmān saṃvinmārgaśca vardhatām | māheśvarāśca vardhantāṃ vardhatāṃ ca maheśvaraḥ ||
**T2:** "May the blessed teacher flourish; may the path-of-awareness flourish; may the Māheśvaras flourish; and may Maheśvara flourish."

## The doctrinal opening (the text's own first block, gāthās 1–7 — the Krama's self-positioning)

**g.1** — natvā nityashuddhau guroshcharaNau mahAprakAshasya | grathnAti mahArthama~njarImimAM surabhiM maheshvarAnandaH ||
**T2:** "Bowing to the ever-pure feet of the great-prakāśa guru, Maheśvarānanda strings this fragrant cluster of the great-meaning."
*fork: grathnāti (strings/weaves) — the mañjarī (cluster) as the woven garland of gāthās — the Krama's method of weaving the sequence.*

**g.2** — vardhatAM mahAprakAsho vimarshavichChuritanishChalodyotaH | sa.nj~nAvisheShanirNayamAtrapravR^ittAni yatra shAstrANi ||
**T2:** "May the great-prakāśa flourish — the unmoving upsurge suffused with reflexion — [he] in whom the śāstras, occupied only with resolving named-distinctions, [find their end]."
*fork: the prakāśa/vimarśa pair (the R1's engine) — T2 renders the upsurge as suffused-with-reflexion (the cognition's own light-and-reflexion), the report's A-thesis in the Krama's voice.*

**g.3** — AtmA khalu vishvamUlaM tatra pramANaM na ko.apyarthayate | kasya vA bhavati pipAsA ga~NgAsrotasi nimagnasya ||
**T2:** "The self, indeed, is the root of the world — there no proof is demanded by anyone; what thirst could arise in one plunged in the Gaṅgā's stream?"
*fork: the pramāṇa's non-necessity — T2 presses the Krama-frame: the self's reflexion is self-evident, needing no external proof (the report's A-thesis: reflexive presence needs no proof).*

**g.4** — yaM jAnanti jaDA api jalahAryo.api yaM vijAnanti | yasyaiva namaskAraH sa kasya sphuTo na bhavati kulanAthaH ||
**T2:** "Whom even the inert know, whom even the water-carriers perceive — he to whom the homage is [paid] — of whom is he not the manifest lord of the kula (kulanātha)?"
*fork: the kulanātha — T2 keeps the Kaula-title (the kula-lord) in the Krama's voice: the cognition's ground IS the kula's lord.*

**g.5** — avachChinnaM pratyakShamavachChinnaM tanmukhaM chAnumAnam | AgamadIpAlokastasya prakAshayati kimapi mAhAtmyam ||
**T2:** "Perception is circumscribed; the inference that faces it is circumscribed — the āgama's lamp-light illumines some majesty of it."
*fork: the three pramāṇas' limits — T2 renders the circumscription (the report's bounded-perspectivity note): perception and inference are bounded; only the āgama's light shows the supreme.*

**g.6** — yeShAM nirupaNIyo vyatiriktaH ko.apyAtmano bhAvaH | AtmavimukhAnAM teShAmadhikArivibhAgavibhramo bhavatu ||
**T2:** "For those whose essence is an other — some state separate from the self — for the self-averted, may the confusion of the qualified-parts [be theirs]."
*fork: the Atmavimukha (self-averted) — T2 presses the opposition: the other-seeking (who posit a self separate from the manifest cognition) are the excluded.*

**g.7** — yatra ruchistatra vidhiryatreyaM nAsti tatra cha niShedhaH | ityasmAkaM viveko hR^idayaparispandamAtrashAstrANAm ||
**T2:** "Where there is delight, there the rite; where this is not, there the prohibition — such is our discernment, whose śāstra is only the heart's pulse (hṛdaya-parispanda)."
*fork: the heart's pulse as the only śāstra — T2 presses the Krama's rule: the rite follows the heart's spanda, not external law — the report's spanda-dossier (the pulse) as the sole norm.*

## The T2 strategy-declaration and the forks
**Strategy:** S1-commentary-informed — but the commentary is the text's OWN Parimala (on disk) + the R1's saṃvit-krama frame. **Register-shift:** T1 formal-literal → T2 Krama-cognition (the pulse, the reflexion, the sequence). **Forks taken:** the spanda-triple as cognition's rhythm (m.2), the cit-krama as the guru-as-sequence (m.5), the Recognition-yoked Krama (m.10), the bimba-reflection (m.11), the prakāśa/vimarśa engine (g.2), the heart's-pulse-as-śāstra (g.7). **Hard core with T1:** the Gaṇeśa-image (m.1), the pearl-earring (m.3), the Parimala-self-commentary (m.7–8), the 70-gāthā dream-yoginī (m.12). **R2's work:** adjudicate the Krama-perspective forks — whether the saṃvit-krama readings are the text's own or imported; the Parimala decides.
