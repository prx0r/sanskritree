## Verses 1–4 — the opponent's challenge and the reply's seed

**v. 1** — yathā sadasatāṃ naiva viśeṣo 'sti nijātmani | jaḍājaḍānām apy evaṃ nāstyasāv iti niścayaḥ ||
**T2:** "Just as, in their own selves, the existent and the non-existent have no distinction, so, too, of the inert and the non-inert there is none — this is the conclusion."
*fork: "nijātmani" (in their own selves) vs "by their own nature" — T2 presses the location (the "in-the-self") over the manner.*

**v. 2** — prakhyopākhyā ca sattā yad yad asattā viparyayaḥ | naitat svarūpaṃ spṛśati vastuno jñātṛgāmy adaḥ ||
**T2:** "Whatever is manifestation (prakhyā) and designation (upākhyā) — that is existence; non-existence is its reverse. That does not touch the thing's own form; it belongs to the knower."
*fork: "prakhyopākhyā" as a dvandva (manifestation-and-designation) vs "prakhyā-upākhyā" as the two modes — the Vṛtti's prakāśa-vimarśa-gloss supports the two-modes; T2 keeps the terms technical (per the report's vimarśa-dossier).*

**v. 3** — prakhyopākhyāviṣayatāpy arthasyātmā na yujyate | parāpekṣitayā naika-rūpatve sattvalaṅghanāt ||
**T2:** "Nor does the self-hood of the object hold even in being the object of manifestation-and-designation: being dependent on another, not one-formed, it oversteps existence."
*fork: "sattvalaṅghanāt" (overstepping existence) vs "exceeding the existent" — the overstep is the selfhood's failure; T2 keeps the R1's reading with the density flagged.*

**v. 4** — tāvatā ca prayojanam ||
**T2:** "And to that extent [there is] the purpose."
*fork: the fragmentary verse — the purpose-clause (the R1's "the reply's seed"); the Vṛtti's "arthakriyāpi siddhyedeva" (the activity-of-meaning would succeed) supplies the force.*

## Verses 5–10 — manifestation and the inert

**v. 5** — bhāvayoraviśiṣṭatā ||
**T2:** "[Thus] the non-distinction of the two beings."
*fork: the fragment — the R1's reading kept; the Vṛtti's "prakāśalagnāyā evaucityaṃ" (the propriety of being attached to manifestation) is the force.*

**v. 9** — tulyaiva prāgvadātmani || nirvimarśasya prakāśasyāpi sphaṭikādivad viśvāvabhāsane 'pi asvatantratvāt svātmanyasatā jaḍena ca bhāvena sādṛśyameva syāt... etena śāntabrahmavādanirāsaḥ kaṭākṣitaḥ ||
**T2:** "Equal indeed, as before, in the self: even the non-reflexive manifestation, like crystal, in the illumination of all — being non-independent, being unreal in its own self, would have likeness to the inert being... by this the calm-Brahmā-view is glanced at [and refuted]."
*fork: the crystal-image — T2 presses the report's anti-inert-light thesis (the non-reflexive light is "crystal-like, hence inert"); the Vṛtti's "śāntabrahmavādanirāsaḥ" (the refutation of the Śānta-Brahma view) is carried.*

**v. 10** — pratiṣṭhāspadatāmiyāt || nirvimarśā saṃvitsvato'labdhasattākā paraṃ vyavasthāpayituṃ nālaṃ gaganakusumamiva saurabhādijanane ||
**T2:** "[The non-reflexive consciousness] would come to be the ground of [another's] support — the non-reflexive awareness, its existence unattained from itself, is unable to establish the other, like a sky-flower [unable] to give birth to fragrance."
*fork: "pratiṣṭhāspadatām" (the status of the ground-of-support) — T2's reading; the sky-flower's impossibility is the report's anti-inert-light in its most explicit form.*

## Verses 11–15 — the "I am this" (the report's flagged core)

**v. 11** — dahamityātmanā punaḥ || bhinnaṃ vastu idantāvimarśena prakāśanārhaṃ satpunarahantātmanā vyavasthāmiyāt |
**T2:** "But again, by the 'I' as the self: the divided thing, fit to be manifested by the reflexion of the 'this,' would, being [so manifested], be established by the 'I'-nature."
*fork: the "this"-and-"I" — T2 presses the transition (the report's A-to-B: the "this" grounded in the "I"); the Vṛtti's "ahantāviśrāntiṃ vinā svato 'siddhaṃ" (without the resting in the "I," unestablished from itself) is the force.*

**v. 14** — vimarśarahitātmanaḥ || saṃyojanaviyojanātmakaṃ svātantryaṃ vihāya sato jaḍaprakāśasya naiva saṃrambhātmānubhavo nirvimarśātmakatvāt |
**T2:** "Of the one whose self is without reflexion — setting aside the freedom (svātantrya) that is of the nature of joining and disjoining, the inert light of the existent has no experience of the self's arousal, being without reflexion."
*fork: svātantrya as "the joining-and-disjoining" (the report's svātantrya-dossier: the inherent autonomy, not mere free-will) — T2 keeps the term + gloss.*

**v. 15** — rvimarśaḥ so'hamityayam || (the Vṛtti: haṃpratyavamarṣātmā jīvitasthānīyo...)
**T2:** "This reflexion — 'I am this' (so 'ham): [the awareness] whose self is the reflexion of the 'I,' standing in the place of life — from whose support even the inert thing, by its unity with the reflexive knower, comes to the non-inertness of resting in the 'I'-state."
*fork: the report's flagged verse (APS 15–17): "the fulfillment of this segmented 'this'-awareness is its resting in its own conscious nature... that reflexive awareness is 'I am this.'" T2 carries the report's reading; the universalization (the finite = the infinite) stays flagged per the R1.*
