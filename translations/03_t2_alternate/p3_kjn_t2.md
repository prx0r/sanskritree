## 2/2 — kālāgnirudrasaṃjñā tu nakhāgre nityasaṃsthitam
**T1:** "The [fire] named Kālāgni-Rudra abides constantly at the tip of the nails."
**T2:** "The Fire-of-Time-Rudra — he stands always at the nails' tips" — *fork: the fire as "the [fire] named" (T1's supplied noun) vs the name as the subject itself ("The Fire-of-Time-Rudra — he stands" — the deity, not the element). The verse's context (the withdrawal, 2/3's "when it blazes upward") supports the deity-reading: the Rudra-form IS the fire.*

## 2/8 — sṛṣṭiyogaṃ padānyastaṃ sṛjatyevaṃ carācaram
