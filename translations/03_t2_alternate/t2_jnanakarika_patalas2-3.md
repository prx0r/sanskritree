# Jnanakarika, patalas 2-3 - T2 (the alternative-reading translation)

*Pass 3 (= T2). Strategy: **S1 - the commentary-informed reading** (the text's own inner web: its paṭala 1's doctrines, its both/neither apophasis) crossed with the register-shift (T1 formal-literal → T2 flowing-constructive). Composed from the IAST + the R1 analysis only; the T1's sentences were not re-read. Where a live alternative exists, T2 takes it and marks the fork.*

## Patala 2

**2/1** - ataḥ paraṃ pravakṣyāmi jñānaṃ trailokyadurlabham | kārikātattvasadbhāvaṃ siddhanāthakulodbhavam ||
**T2:** "What follows I shall tell - the knowledge hard to win in all three worlds: the very being of the kārikā's truth, born in the Siddha-nātha's line."
*fork: "the being of the kārikā's truth" (the text as the truth's own body) vs T1's "the kārikā's true reality" - T2 presses the sadbhāva (being) harder.*

**2/2** - candrasūryavinirmuktaṃ dvāraṇākṣavivarjitam | pṛthivyāpastaṃ tathā yogī vāyurākāśameva ca ||
**T2:** "Freed from sun and moon, void of the gates and the eye-sockets, the yogi passes beyond earth, water, wind, and the sky itself -"
*fork: dvāraṇākṣa (the body's openings) as the ritual-gates (the indriyas) - T2 keeps the openings literal.*

**2/3** - pañcabhirāheto deho śrūyate tattvanāyake | sarveṣāṃ vyāpakaḥ śānta vyāptī tasya na vidyate ||
**T2:** "In the lord of principles the body is heard [to come] from the five - yet the tranquil pervades all; for him no pervasiveness is [even] found." [X]
*fork: the "no pervasiveness of his" as the negation of the very attribute - the pervader who is not even a pervader (the R1's (a)).*

**2/5** - ātmāparavinirmuktaṃ cittaṃ caiva nirāśrayam | cittāśrayaṃ bhaveddvandvaṃ nirāśrayañ camauṣadam ||
**T2:** "Free of self and other, the mind-stuff is supportless; the pair is the mind's support - the supportless is the remedy (auṣadha)." [emendation, per R1]
*fork: auṣadha (the remedy) adopted - the jewel→remedy chain of 2/4-5; T2 takes the R1's (b).*

**2/8** - caturthantu manaścaiva kathayāmi viśeṣataḥ | guṇatrayavinirmuktaṃ sadā nirvāṇalakṣaṇam ||
**T2:** "And the fourth - the mind itself - I declare in particular: it is freed of the triple guṇas, ever the mark of nirvāṇa."
*fork: none live - the fourth mind as the guṇātīta; hard-core with T1.*

**2/10** - anābhyāsaṃ sadā tattvaṃ sarvacintāvivarjitam | cittābhāvavinirmuktaṃ nimittaṃ tattvalaukikam ||
**T2:** "The principle is ever without practice, rid of every thought; freed even from the mind's non-being - the mark that is no worldly principle."
*fork: cittābhāva as "the mind's non-being" (the trap) vs "the non-mind" (a state) - T2 takes the R1's (a): the double-negation. The nimitta (mark) as the laukika-negation.*

**2/13** - cittāyuktaṃ yadā cittaṃ tadāsau cañcalaṃ smṛtam | cittātītaṃ yadā cittaṃ niścitamacalaṃ bhavet ||
**T2:** "When the mind is yoked to mind, it is remembered as the restless; when the mind is past mind, it becomes the certain, the unmoving."
*fork: "yoked" (yukta) as the marriage-image - the mind wed to itself is the restlessness.*

**2/18** - yo so niścintasarvajñaḥ samādhiparameśvaraḥ | jñātavyaṃ mokṣasadbhāvaṃ guptabhedaprakāśitam ||
**T2:** "He - that one - the thoughtless all-knower, the supreme lord of samādhi: the being of liberation is to be known, disclosed by the hidden distinction."
*fork: guptabheda as "the hidden distinction" (the secret division) vs "the distinction that is hidden" - T2 keeps the noun-force.*

**2/22** - kalpanājñānayuktāstu bhramanti ghaṭayantravat | paripūrṇaṃ kathaṃ teṣāṃ siddhakramavivarjitam ||
**T2:** "Those joined with the knowledge-of-fabrication wander like the potter's wheel; how [is] the full [state] theirs, who lack the siddha-order?"
*fork: siddhakrama as "the siddha-order" (the graded path) vs "the accomplished sequence" - T2 leans the lineage-sense (the graded transmission, per the Kaularahasya's krama).*

**2/25** - jñānakhaḍgena chitvā pāśadvayanibandhanam | andhapāśaṃ yadā cittaṃ kuto gacchanti yoginaḥ ||
**T2:** "Having severed the two-bonds' binding with the sword of knowledge - when the mind is the blind bond itself, where do the yogins go?"
*fork: the mind AS the blind bond (T1) confirmed; "go" as the question of the path - if the mind is the bond, the path is void.*

**2/31** - ūrddhvacāro bhaveddharmo'dvayasyo'dharmalakṣaṇam | dharmādharmakṣaye kṣīṇe mucyate sarvair bandhanaiḥ ||
**T2:** "The upward-movement is the merit; of the non-dual, unmerit is the mark. When merit-and-unmerit is spent, one is freed from all the bonds." [emendation, per R1]
*fork: bandhanaiḥ (the bonds) adopted over the print's dhanaiḥ (the wealths) - the paṭala's own pāśa-language.*

## Patala 3

**3/1** - atha caryān pravakṣyāmi yogināmupajāyate | sa cetājñāna na sarvaḥ caitanyaṃ jñānavarjitam ||
**T2:** "Now the conduct I shall tell - it arises for the yogins: that consciousness is not the knowing, is not the all - the consciousness without knowledge."
*fork: cetājñāna as a dvandva ("consciousness-and-knowing") - the compound's two members listed; T2 takes the R1's (a) but records the dvandva as live.*

**3/7** - liṅgantu kathayiṣyāmi yalliṅgaṃ kaulikaṃ smṛtam | layapūjāṣṭakaṃ yattaṃ liṅgantu sa carācaram ||
**T2:** "The liṅga I will tell - the liṅga remembered as the Kaulika: whatever is the eightfold worship-of-dissolution, that liṅga is the moving-and-unmoving."
*fork: layapūjāṣṭaka as "the eightfold dissolution-worship" (the ritual count) vs "the eight forms of the dissolution-rite" - T2 keeps the count.*

**3/11** - adhorddhaṃ prāṇasañcāraḥ bahate caiva nityaśaḥ | ūrddhvacāre bhaved gaṅgā yadā cādho vyavasthitaḥ ||
**T2:** "Upward the life-breath's course flows ever; in the upward-course the Gaṅgā arises, when [it] is settled below." [emendation, per R1]
*fork: yadā (when) adopted - the conditional restores the breath's turn.*

**3/12** - dvābhyāṃ melāpakaṃ yatna sa na metat prakīrtitā | aśarīraṃ yadā tattvaṃ svadehe sā sthitā yadi ||
**T2:** "By the two, the melāpaka - the fusion - with effort [X]; when the principle is the bodiless, if it stands in the body -"
*fork: melāpaka as the technical fusion (the KJN's siddhi-fusion, per R1) - T2 transliterates it.*

**3/17** - vṛkṣamūlaṃ samākhyātaṃ catvāraḥ śṛṇu sāmpratam | jayā ca vijayā caiva ajitā cāparājitā ||
**T2:** "The tree-root is proclaimed - hear the four now: the Victorious, the Triumphant, the Unconquered, the Unvanquished."
*fork: the four as names (T1/R1) vs the epithets - T2 renders them as epithets to test the register; R2 decides.*

**3/19** - bindustañca parāśaktiḥ taṭasthaś cārurūpiṇī | binducārusthitā nityaṃ taṭasthaṃ bindumaṇḍalam ||
**T2:** "The bindu is the supreme power; the shore-dweller, the lovely-formed; ever placed in the lovely bindu - the shore, the bindu-disc."
*fork: the four-item list (bindu, parāśakti, taṭastha, cāru-rūpiṇī) - T2 tests the list-reading against the T1's single subject.*

**3/78** - mahodadhi samākhyātaṃ tripañcakaṃ kathayāmyaham | sattvarajastamaścaiva ekatve tu yathā sthitam ||
**T2:** "The great ocean is proclaimed; the three-and-five I tell: sattva, rajas, tamas, as they stand in the one -"
*fork: tripañcaka as "the fifteen" (the count) vs "the three-and-five" (the guṇas + the elements) - the verse's own gloss favors T2's three-and-five.*

**3/82** - ṣaḍbhirmukto yadā yogī muktakeśaḥ sa ucyate | muktakeśaḥ samākhyāta ānandaṃ śṛṇu sāmpratam ||
**T2:** "When the yogi is freed from the six, he is called the one-with-loosened-hair; the loosened-hair is proclaimed - hear the bliss now."
*fork: the six as the six-foes (the ṣaḍ-ripu) - the standard list; T2 carries it as the likely referent.*

**3/84** - ebhiryukto mahāyogī madirāmatta ucyate | madirānandamākhyātaṃ nairmālyamālino śṛṇu ||
**T2:** "Joined with these, the great yogi is called the one drunk with the wine; the wine-bliss is proclaimed - hear, O wearer of the cast-off garland."
*fork: nirmālya (the cast-off offering-garland) adopted per R1 - the yogi wears what the rite discarded.*

**3/86** - ekākīmatithiryastu bhramite pravadet sadā | asaṃhāyo bhramate dhīraḥ sa ekākīnamucyate ||
**T2:** "He who, wandering, would ever speak himself a guest - the steadfast one roams companionless: he is called the lone one."
*fork: the atithi (guest) as the wanderer's self-image - the yogin as the world's guest, owning nothing (per R1).*

**3/87** - ekākī tu samākhyātā śāstraṃ mūlakārikā | etaccaryā samākhyātā viśa ca jñānadhāraka ||
**T2:** "The lone one is proclaimed - the treatise, the root-kārikā; this conduct is proclaimed - enter, O bearer of the knowledge." [emendation, per R1]
*fork: mūlakārikā (the root-verses) adopted - the text's self-naming at its close.*

## The T2 strategy-declaration and the forks
**Strategy:** S1 (the commentary-informed - the paṭala 1's doctrines, the both/neither apophasis) + the register-shift toward the flowing-constructive. **Forks taken:** 8 (2/5 auṣadha, 2/31 bandhanaiḥ, 3/11 yadā, 3/17 the epithets, 3/19 the list, 3/84 nirmālya, 3/87 mūlakārikā, 3/86 atithi) - 3 emendations from the R1, 2 register-tests (3/17, 3/19), the rest confirmations-with-depth. **Hard core with T1:** the both/neither structure (2/10, 2/13, 3/1), the siddha-order (2/22), the bindu-as-power (3/19's core), the three-and-five (3/78). **R2's work:** adjudicate the 8 forks, the 2 register-tests, and carry the [X]-flags (2/3, 3/12) to the Bagchi print.
