# Kulānanda + Unmattabhairavahṛdaya — P3/T2 (the alternative-reading translations)

*Pass 3 (= T2). T1 = the Kulānanda/Unmattabhairava pass-1 files; R1 = `p2_kulananda_unmattabhairava.md`.*

# I. Kulānanda

## v. 4 — the siddhi-list
**T1:** "the noose-thrust (pāśastobha), the piercing (vedhana), the fumigation (dhūnana), the trembling (kampana), the sky-going (khecara), the equal-taste (samarasa), and the destruction of gray-hair-and-age."
**T2:** "the noose-stab, the piercing, the fumigating, the quaking, the sky-walking, the one-taste, and the end of age and grey" — *fork: the six as technical-names (T1 — the bundle's terms, each a doctrine) vs the plain-verbs (T2 — each a phenomenon). The verse is the checklist of the bundle's practices (per R1); T1's technical register is the glossary's; T2's plainness shows what the practices ARE.*

## v. 6 — brahmasthāne yat kamalaṃ catuḥṣaṣṭidalānvitam
**T1:** "The lotus in the brahma-station, endowed with sixty-four petals."
**T2:** "The lotus that stands at the brahma-seat — sixty-four-petaled" — *fork: sthāna = "station" (the cakra-locus) vs "seat" (the plain); the verse's cakra-map (the 64-petal lotus) is the same either way. Register only.*

## v. 10 — etad vijñānamātreṇa āveśaṃ kurute dhruvam
**T1:** "By the mere understanding of this he surely makes the possession (āveśa)."
**T2:** "By knowing this alone he surely enters the possession" — *fork: āveśaṃ kurute = "makes the possession" (T1 — the active: he CAUSES the possession) vs "enters the possession" (T2 — the middle: he falls into it). The KJN's āveśa (14/38 — "tāvat samādhimāyāti stobhāveśādilakṣaṇam" — he goes to samādhi, the stobha-possession marks) supports the entered-state: the possession is the siddhi-state, not something made. T2's is the better register.*

## v. 27 — palitāstambhayeddevi māsamekena suvrate
**T1:** "he stops the gray-hair (palitāstambhana), O Devī, within a single month, O good-vowed one."
**T2:** "within one month, O well-vowed one, he stays the greying" — *fork: palitāstambhana = the technical (the KJN 7's practice-name) vs the plain ("stays the greying"). The KJN-parallel governs: the term is the practice's name; T1 stands.*

## v. 34 — evaṃ jñātvā vīrārohe vicaret yathāsukham
**T1:** "Knowing this, O heroine (vīrārohe), one should move at ease."
**T2:** "Knowing this, O you who ride the hero, walk as you will" — *fork (resolved toward the vocative in R1): vīrārohe = "O heroine / O you who ride the hero" (the vocative — the Devī) vs "mounted on the hero" (the state). T2's "walk as you will" for vicaret yathāsukham is the freer register; the R1-vocative governs.*

## vv. 44–47 — the syllable-arithmetic
**T1:** the vargātīta-manipulations with the vowel-joins [X].
**T2 (the reading-strategy):** the arithmetic is a construction-procedure (the kula-vidyā built from the vargas and the vowels — per R1's note); render it as a procedure ("take the beyond-the-groups syllable; join it to the second vowel; mount it on the fire..."), the exact operations [X] until the print.

## v. 55 — saṃgamānmokṣahetukaṃ
**T1:** "...[the knowledge] that is the cause of liberation from union [X]."
**T2:** "...[the knowledge] that is liberation through the union" — *fork (R1 resolved toward the union-doctrine): saṃgamāt = "from the union" (the ablative — liberation THROUGH the union, the śakti-union) vs "from union" (liberation FROM — the ascetic reading). The verse's context (the samarasa, the lord-servant pair) supports the through-reading: T2's.*

## vv. 58–60 — the close
**T1:** "He should contemplate the equal-taste... by the mere understanding of this, it is sung there, in the middle... the knowledge of distant hearing; the thrust-and-stab in the middle; the possession, the vision from afar, the tremor-thrust likewise. By the entering of another's body the yogī proceeds..."
**T2:** "Contemplate the one-taste, O Devī — the master-and-servant [pair], the discerning one: by this knowing alone it is sung within. Far-hearing; the mid-thrust; the possession; the far-seeing; the quake-thrust too. And by entering another's frame the yogī moves..." — *fork: the register (T2's plain vs T1's technical); the "prabhubhṛtyam" (the lord-servant pair) as the master-servant (T2) vs the lord-and-servant (T1). The pair's doctrine (the Lord and the devotee as one taste) is the same.*

# II. Unmattabhairavahṛdaya

## v. 15 — chāgaivali prayuktārai bhūtvā dhāra vāruṇim
**T1:** "with the goat-offering (chāga-bali) having been made, he should bear the Vāruṇī [mudrā? wine?]."
**T2:** "having performed the goat-offering, let him hold the Water-gesture" — *fork (R1's (b/c)-resolution): vāruṇim = "the Vāruṇī" (the water-mudrā — the ritual gesture) vs "the Vāruṇī [liquor]" (the wine). The yantra-ritual context favors the gesture; T2 takes it.*

## K4 — amāvāsyāṃ caturdaśyāṃ daśamī dvādaśī tathā | bhāvayetpūjaka śreṣṭai anurādhā virodhitām
**T1:** "On the new moon, the fourteenth, the tenth, and the twelfth: the best of worshippers should contemplate the obstructed one (virodhitā) [X: 'anurādhā virodhitām' — the nakṣatra Anurādhā?]."
**T2:** "On the dark-moon, the fourteenth, the tenth, and the twelfth — the finest worshipper should hold in mind the One-who-stands-against (the nailing's foe) [X: the pāda remains corrupt — the Anurādhā-nakṣatra reading noted as the alternative]" — *fork: virodhitā as "the obstructed one" vs "the obstructing one" (the enemy — the nailing's target) vs the nakṣatra (Anurādhā). The kīlaka-context (the nailing of the enemy) favors the obstructing-one; the nakṣatra-reading remains [X].*

## S2 — nirlajjo paramānanda ghorarūpo bhayaṃkaraḥ
**T1:** "the Shameless (nirlajja), the supreme bliss (paramānanda), the terrible-formed, the fearsome."
**T2:** "the Shame-free, the Bliss-Supreme, the Terror-formed, the Dread" — *fork: the names as names (T1 — the 12-names' series) vs the epithets (T2). The stotra's structure (the names as the nyāsa's objects) governs: T1's names stand.*

## H50 — sāṅgaṃ hṛdayamunmattaṃ yo naraḥ paṭhyate sadā
**T1:** "Whoever always recites the Unmattā heart[-mantra] with its limbs."
**T2:** "Whoever recites the Mad-Lord's heart with all its members, day by day" — *fork: sāṅgam = "with its limbs" (the aṅga-nyāsas — T1's technical) vs "with all its members" (T2 — the plain); unmattaṃ hṛdayam = "the Unmattā heart" (the name) vs "the Mad-Lord's heart" (T2). The aṅga-nyāsa-context (the preceding section's nyāsa-liturgy) governs: T1's "limbs" is the technical.*
