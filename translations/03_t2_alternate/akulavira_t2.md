# Akulavīratantra, vv. 1–18 — T2 (the alternative-reading translation)

*Phase 3, REVISED per the flow's core requirement: T2 explores **alternative interpretations** — genuinely different readings of the Sanskrit, not re-compositions that agree. Where a real interpretive fork exists, T2 takes the alternative and marks the fork. Where the text is fixed, T2 composes fresh and the agreement is the honest hard core.*

**Strategy:** S2 (argument-priority, plain register) **+ the alternative-exploration**: each verse was re-derived from the IAST with the question "what else can this mean?" — checked against the glossary, the cross-text network (the KJN, the ŚS-stack, the archive anchor), and the grammar.

---

**Maṅgala** — śrīmacchandapādakebhyo namaḥ | śrīmīnasahajānandaṃ svakīyāṅgasamudbhavam | sarvamādhāragambhīram acalaṃ vyāpakaṃ param | māyāmalavinirmuktaṃ mīnanāthaṃ namāmyaham
> Homage to the blessed Candra-pādas. I bow to Mīnanātha — the innate bliss born **of his own members** [fork: aṅga = limb/body-part vs **member of the lineage-circle** — "born within his own retinue"], profound **in the depths of every support** [fork: the ādhāra-read — the Nātha tradition's cakras], unmoving, pervasive, supreme — **released from māyā and from impurity** [fork: māyā-mala as a **dvandva** — two things, not "māyā's impurity"].

**1** — athātaḥ sampravakṣyāmi akulavīraṃ mahadbhūtam | guhyād guhyataraṃ guhyaṃ siddhasadbhāvasantatiḥ
> Now, then, I declare the Akulavīra, the great being — more secret than the secret, the secret of secrets: **the very reality of the lineage of the siddhas** [fork: santati = the lineage vs the continuum — this text IS the lineage's being, not merely its teaching].

**2** — anugrahāya lokānāṃ siddhanāthena bhāṣitam | gopanīyaṃ prayatnena yadīcchan śāśvataṃ padam
> For the world's grace the Lord of the Siddhas spoke it — **you who desire the eternal state must guard it with effort** [fork: yadīcchan as the **direct address** to the reader, not the generic "one who"].

**3–4** — saṃsārārṇavamagnānāṃ bhūtānāṃ mahadāśrayam | yathā nadīnadāḥ sarve sāgare samupāgatāḥ || tathā akulavīreṣu sarvadharmā layaṅgatāḥ | sarvādhāramaśeṣasya jagataḥ sarvadā prabhuḥ
> The great refuge of beings sunk in the ocean of wandering: as all rivers come into the sea, so **in the Akulavīra-ones** all dharmas sink [fork: akulavīreṣu — the plural as **the class of the realized** — the adepts themselves, not merely the state]; the support of all — of the whole world — **he is ever the Lord** [fork: prabhuḥ as the predicate, the Akulavīra as a person, not an abstraction].

**5** — sahajānandaṃ na vindanti sarvadharmasamāsṛtāḥ | ajānantamalairgrastā mahāmāyāndhacchāditāḥ
> Those who rush to every dharma never find the innate bliss — the unknowing, **swallowed by the impurities, blindfolded by the great illusion** [fork: andha- — "blind" (the blindfold) vs "darkness" — "covered by the great illusion's dark"].

**6** — śāstrajālena santuṣṭā mohitāstyajayantitāḥ (?) | na vindanti padaṃ śāntaṃ kaulānāṃ niṣkalaṃ gurum
> Fed on the net of the śāstras, deluded, they do not find the tranquil state — **the partless, the weighty, of the Kaulas** [fork: gurum as the **adjective** "weighty/supreme" (modifying padam) vs the noun "the guru" — the state itself is the weighty, partless one; the guru-person reading is the tradition's, the adjective reading is the grammar's].

**7–8** — saṃvādayanti ye kecin nyāyavaiśeṣikāstathā | baudhāstu arihantā ye somasiddhantavādinaḥ || mīmāṃsa pañcasrotāśca vāmasiddhāntadakṣiṇāḥ | itihāsapurāṇañca bhūtatatvantu gāruḍam
> Whatever schools wrangle — Nyāya and Vaiśeṣika, the Buddhists and the Arhats, the Somasiddhānta-speakers; Mīmāṃsā and the Five Streams, the left and right Siddhāntas, the histories and the Purāṇas, the element-lore and the Gāruḍa —

**9** — ebhiḥ śaivāgamaiḥ sarvaiḥ parokṣañca kriyānvitaiḥ | savikalpasiddhisañcāraṃ tat sarvaṃ pāpabandhavit
> — with all these Śaiva āgamas, indirect and ritual-bound: **their construct-laden attainment and their roaming** [fork: sañcāra with the saṃsāra-tone — "their constructed wandering"] — all of it **is itself pierced by the bonds of sin** [fork: pāpabandhavit — the **passive** "pierced/bound by sin" vs the active "destroys sin's bonds": the schools' knowledge does not destroy the bonds — it IS bound; the passive is the sharper polemic].

**10** — vikalpabahulāḥ sarvairmithyāvādā nirarthakāḥ | na te muñcanti saṃsāre akulavīravivarjitāḥ
> Construct-heavy, all are false doctrines, worthless — **none of them releases from saṃsāra, being devoid of the Akulavīra** [fork: akulavīravivarjitāḥ as the cause ("being devoid") vs the condition ("those devoid of")].

**11** — sarvajñaṃ sarvamāsṛtya sarvato hitalakṣaṇam | sarveṣāṃ siddhistatra stāḥ sarvasiddhiñca tatra vai
> Resorting to the all-knowing — **the All itself** [fork: sarvajñaṃ sarvam — the identity-reading: the All is the all-knowing] — whose mark is good on every side: **there stands the siddhi of all, and there the All-Siddhi** [fork: sarvasiddhi as the **compound-name** — the Siddhi-of-All, a quasi-name of the state].

**12** — yatrāsau akulavīro dṛśyate sarvatomukham | taṃ viditvā paraṃ rūpaṃ mano niścalatāṃ vrajet
> Where that Akulavīra is seen, facing all ways — knowing that supreme form, the mind comes to stillness. [fixed]

**13** — śabdarūparasasparśagandhañcaivātra pañcamam | sarvabhāvāśca tatraiva pralīṇāḥ pralayaṃ gatāḥ
> Sound, form, taste, touch — and the fifth, smell — all beings are dissolved there, gone to dissolution. [fixed]

**14** — bhāvābhāvavinirmukta udayāstamanavarjitaḥ | svabhāvamatimataṃ śāntaṃ mano yasya manomayam
> Free of being and non-being, without rising and setting — **the tranquil [state] held as the own-nature-thought** [fork: mataṃ as "held/known as" — the construal that repairs the [X] without emendation: svabhāva-mati-mataṃ = "held by the own-nature-mind"], **he whose mind is [nothing but] mind-stuff** [fork: manomayam — the pure-subjectivity reading: the mind reduced to its own substance].

**15** — akulavīramiti khyātaṃ sarvādhāraparāparam | nādhāralakṣabhedantu na nādagocare paṭhet
> It is known as the Akulavīra — the support of all, beyond the beyond. **It is not to be recited** [fork: paṭhet = "recite" — the **ritual-prohibition** register: the text forbids its recitation in the contexts of the supports, the marks, and the nāda — vs the epistemic "it is not to be read/found there"] **at the divisions of the supports and marks, nor in the domain of nāda.**

**16** — hṛdi sthāne na vaktre ca ghaṇṭikā tālarandhrake | na iḍā piṅgalā śāntā na cāstīti gamāgame
> Not in the heart, not in the mouth's place, not in the uvula or the palatal aperture; not iḍā, not piṅgalā — **the tranquil is not [there], in their coming and going** [fork: gamāgame — the **cross-text reading** (KJN 14/136: "na iḍā piṅgalā caiva suṣumṇā ca gamāgame" — "in coming and going" = the breath's movement through the channels): the tranquil is not in the channels' traffic — vs T1's "so say āgama and its negation." The KJN-parallel decides: gamāgame = "in coming and going"].

**17** — na nābhicakrakaṇṭhe ca na śire naiva mastake | tathā cakṣurunmīlane ca na nāsāgranirīkṣaṇe
> Not in the navel-cakra or the throat, not in the head, not at all in the skull; nor in the opening of the eyes, nor in the gazing at the nose-tip. [fixed]

**18** — na pūrakakumbhake tatra recake (ca) tathā punaḥ | na bindubhedake granthau lalāṭe na tu vahnike
> Not in the in-breath, the retention, nor again the out-breath; not in the piercing of the bindu, not in the knots, not in the forehead, not in the fire. [fixed]

The fixed verses (12, 13, 17, 18) are the honest hard core. The gamāgame-fork (v. 16) is resolved by the cross-text parallel — the flow working as intended. Ready for R2.*
