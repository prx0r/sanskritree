# 10-Text Batch + Spanda Vivṛti — P3/T2 (the alternative-reading translations)

*Pass 3 (= T2). T1 = `ten_text_batch1_pass1.md` + `ten_text_batch2_pass1.md` + `spanda_vivrtti_ramakantha_opening_pass1.md`; R1 = `p2_ten_text_batch.md` + `p2_spanda_vivrtti.md`.*

# 1. Jñānasaṅkalinītantra

## v. 7 — yā punaḥ śāmbhavī vidyā guptā kulavadhūriva
**T1:** "but the Śāmbhavī-vidyā — hidden like a kula-bride."
**T2:** "but the Vidyā of the Auspicious One — concealed as the lineage's bride" — *fork: śāmbhavī = "Śāmbhavī" (the technical — the vidyā of Śambhu) vs "of the Auspicious One" (T2 — the rendered); kulavadhū = "a kula-bride" vs "the lineage's bride." The vidyā-name is the technical (the paṭala's subject); T1 stands, T2's rendering is the surface.*

## v. 8 — dehasthāḥ sarvavidyāśca dehasthāḥ sarvadevatāḥ
**T1:** "All the vidyās are in the body; all the deities are in the body."
**T2:** "Every knowing is body-dwelling; every god is body-dwelling" — *fork: vidyā = "the vidyās" (the technical — the goddess-teachings) vs "every knowing" (T2 — the epistemic); devatā = "the deities" vs "the gods." The verse's body-ontology (the KJN's kulapiṇḍa — everything in the body) is the content; the register-choice only.*

# 2. Kulasāra

## vv. 10–11 — surāvihīnāstri daśā balahinā... kathaṃ śuddhi bhavet tasyā vāsā medhyā bhavennavā
**T1:** "The three [worlds?] without the surā, weakened in power... how would there be purity? Would the woman's use (vāsā) become pure or not?" [X].
**T2:** "The three worlds, robbed of the surā, robbed of strength... how is purity possible? And her dwelling (vāsā) — would it become clean or not?" — *fork: vāsā = "the woman's use" (T1 — the kula's śakti-rite) vs "her dwelling" (T2 — the literal: the house/habitation). The verse's ritual-context (the soma-myth, the kula-mata's restoration) favors the ritual-reading; the [X] stands.*

# 4. Kākacaṇḍeśvarīmata

## The goddess-line — kaṃkālī kālarātrī ca kākacaṃḍā vacāṃvikā
**T1:** "Kaṅkālī and Kālarātrī, and Kākacaṇḍā, the mistress of speech."
**T2:** "The Skeleton-Queen, the Night-of-Time, and the Crow-Fierce — the speech-mistress" — *fork: the goddesses as names (T1 — the Krama's technical) vs the rendered (T2 — "Skeleton-Queen, Night-of-Time, Crow-Fierce" — showing what the names mean). The Krama's goddesses (cf. Wenta's twelve Kālīs) are names in the tradition; T2's rendering is the commentary's gloss-layer, noted, not adopted.*

# 5. Kulacūḍāmaṇi

## v. 1 — asaṃkhyā tripurādevī asaṃkhyātā ca kālikā
**T1:** "Countless is the goddess Tripurā; countless [also] is Kālikā."
**T2:** "The Tripurā-goddess is beyond counting; Kālikā too beyond counting" — *fork (R1 resolved toward the predicate): asaṃkhyā as the predicate ("countless is...") vs the quasi-name ("the Countless-Tripurā"). R1's predicate-reading governs; T2's phrasing is the register-choice.*

# 6. Nareśvaraparīkṣā

## v. 1 — meyābdhiratnasya śaṅkarasyāmitadyuteḥ
**T1:** "of Śaṅkara — the jewel of the sea of all that is to be known (meya), of unmeasured brilliance."
**T2:** "of Śaṅkara — the gem of the ocean of the knowable, of boundless glory" — *fork: meya = "all that is to be known" (T1 — the epistemic) vs "the knowable" (T2); amitadyuti = "unmeasured brilliance" vs "boundless glory." The parīkṣā's frame (the examination of the Lord) supports the epistemic register; register only.*

# 7. Kaulāvalīnirṇaya

## v. 1 — the maṅgala
**T1:** "I will declare the Kaulāvalīnirṇaya, which gives the Śāktas' [state of] pondering and liberation... the abode of the sole grace of the lotus-feet."
**T2:** "I declare the Kaula-line's Determination — the Śāktas' pondering-and-release, the kula's increase, ever served by the Kaulas with captivating devotions — the home of the lotus-feet's single grace" — *fork: kaulāvalī = "the Kaulāvalī" (the name) vs "the Kaula-line" (T2 — avalī = a line/row: the lineage-row); paribhāva = "pondering" vs "the pondering" — the register. The name is the title; T2's rendering is the commentary's gloss.*

# 9. Spanda Vivṛti (Rājānaka Rāma)

## v. 1 — the maṅgala
**T1:** "That which is ever flashing with the same self-experiencing subjectivity, its body the unobstructed light of consciousness, unseizable by the ten directions, time, and the rest — the own nature of Śambhu, rich with incomparable wonder — that supreme Power-principle triumphs in the world: it is called Spanda."
**T2:** "What flashes forever as the self-experiencer of its own kind, whose frame is the light of awareness uncaught by the quarters, by time, by all — the very being of Śambhu, alive with matchless marvel — that highest Śakti-principle prevails in the world: this is called the Pulse" — *fork: spanda = "Spanda" (the technical) vs "the Pulse" (T2 — the rendered — the term's meaning: the vibration); tādṛksvātmānubhavitṛtayā = "with the same self-experiencing subjectivity" (T1) vs "as the self-experiencer of its own kind" (T2). The term spanda is the work's title-principle — the glossary's transliteration governs; T2's "the Pulse" is the commentary's gloss, recorded as the meaningful rendering for the site-display.*

## v. 2 — the guide-critique
**T1:** "Let those wander, entering by themselves into the rugged wilds of the deep [ocean] to which no direction is shown by the knowers — but those who, by that very [false] path, induce others to journey [with them]: this is a great delusion."
**T2:** "Let the guide-less wander, entering the wild deeps on their own — but those who send OTHERS down that same lost road: that is the great bewilderment" — *fork: tajjñair adarśitadiśaḥ = "to which no direction is shown by the knowers" (T1) vs "the guide-less" (T2 — the compressed); mahān bhramo = "a great delusion" vs "the great bewilderment." The verse's social critique (the false-teacher) is the content; register only.*

## v. 3 — the churned ocean
**T1:** "The ocean of scriptures is churned by those who, with the gusts of many doctrines, breed terrible explosions of mutual collision... and the seeker, his mind unsteady... by them he is carried across."
**T2:** "They churn the scripture-ocean — the gusting schools clashing in monstrous bursts, their speech-storms splitting the ear — and the seeker, tossed by the whirlpools, the terrors, the shipwrecked meanings, unsteady of mind — by them he is ferried over" — *fork: the register (T2's flowing narrative vs T1's syntax-bound); the resolution (the seeker ferried despite the chaos — the grace-through-chaos) is the same in both.*
