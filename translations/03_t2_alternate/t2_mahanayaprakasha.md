# Mahānayaprakāśa — T2 (the alternative-reading), the opening + the continuation

*Pass 3 (= T2). Strategy: **S1 — the commentary-informed** (Śitikaṇṭhācārya's own vivṛti is the frame) crossed with the register-shift (T1 formal-literal → T2 flowing-constructive). Composed from the e-text + the R1 only. The saṃvit-krama governs.*

## The opening (vv. 1–5)

**1 — the daśākhaṇḍa**
**T1:** "she who gives rise to the illumination of the ten divisions (daśākhaṇḍa-avabhāsa)"
**T2:** "she who dawns as the light-burst of the ten-part cognition"
*fork: "ten-part cognition" (daśākhaṇḍa-avabhāsa) — T2 presses the cognition-ten (the Krama's ten Kālīs) over the bare "divisions"; the light-burst as the illumination.*

**2 — the caturaṇḍā**
**T1:** "she who, having swallowed the six courses of the four eggs"
**T2:** "she who gulps the four-and-twenty-fold courses into her belly"
*fork: the count — T2 reads the caturaṇḍā + ṣaḍadhvan as the 4×6 = 24 (the courses' arithmetic); the swallowing (jāṭharīkṛtya) as the gulping.*

**3 — the raudrī-śakti**
**T1:** "the Raudrī-power... who binds the dense host of the virāṭ-beings... to yoke them in the heart of Maheśa"
**T2:** "the Fierce power, binding the whole rout of the cosmic persons, to hitch them by choice means into Maheśa's heart"
*fork: "Fierce" (raudrī) as the name vs the epithet — T2 renders it as the epithet with the name-note; "hitch" (niyojayitum) as the yoking.*

**4 — the Uḍḍiyāna + the akula/kula**
**T1:** "in the Uḍḍiyāna-seat... the Akula-Kula-great"
**T2:** "at the Uḍḍiyāna-throne, the supreme-secret lore, fierce in rite — and in the front-and-back thrones' pair, the mixed-Śākta of the named Akula-Kula-great"
*fork: "throne" (pīṭha) — T2 renders the seat as the throne; the akula/kula pair kept (the shared cosmology).*

**5 — the declaration**
**T1:** "The counted phoneme-word-mantra-path, necessarily... the whole of what is to be expressed by that will be declared here"
**T2:** "The reckoned syllable-word-mantra-track, of necessity — its whole expression I shall set forth here; ponder it"
*fork: "track" (adhvan) — T2 renders the path as the track; the declaration as the setting-forth.*

## The continuation (the goddess-hymns)

**The first hymn — the kāla-Bhairava**
**T1:** "the world-devouring Bhairava she devours... Kāla creates beings..."
**T2:** "She — the mother, the slender, the highest, the ruler; the world-chomping Bhairava she chomps; the endless [power], the pure-regnant, the stiller of thought's surge —"
*fork: "chomps" (bhakṣeta) — T2 renders the devouring as the chomping (the colloquial register of the deśabhāṣā); the "stiller of thought's surge" (āśaya-takṣetā) as the mind-taming.*

**The commentary's doctrine (the kāla = prāṇa)**
**T2:** "'Kāla spawns beings; Kāla gobbles creatures; Kāla keeps vigil in sleepers; Kāla is unpassable.' — Kāla is Bhairava; he is of the resonance's grain, of the breath's shape: 'the rooted and the ranging — all is the breath's fodder.'"
*fork: "spawns/gobbles" — T2 presses the deśabhāṣā-flavor; "fodder" (bhojana) for the prāṇa's food; the Devī devours that very breath-Bhairava.*

**The second hymn — the ahalā-śakti**
**T1:** "the ahalā [śakti]... the vowel-natured kuṇḍalinī, unwritten, utterable"
**T2:** "The Akula-loosed, the pervading, the wakening-root, into the kula gone; the h-less [ahalā] power, the... rending knots, wheels, bases — she, the multiform, the Mother, may she win."
*fork: "h-less" (ahalā) — T2 renders the technical as the h-less (the akāra-hakāra pair); the kuṇḍalinī's rending kept.*

**The third hymn — the paryanta**
**T1:** "the paryanta (the limit)... the khacarī-cakra... the homeless (aniketa) triumph"
**T2:** "Round the rounding end, the water-parting's cause and causeless — the sky-wheel, its flash, whose fame it smites — that Mother, the great-meaning, may the unmoored win."
*fork: "unmoored" (aniketa) — T2 renders the homeless as the unmoored (the not-established); the water-parting (vanna-vibhāga) as the division's cause.*

**The paryanta's commentary**
**T2:** "the one who, in the field of birth-and-dissolution, stands at the fifth-and-sixtieth station, the crest beyond the four-and-sixtieth's last level — the unmoored, pervading all the place, quarter, time, kind, and appearing-distinctions by his single form"
*fork: "fifth-and-sixtieth station" — T2 keeps the count (the 65th, beyond the 64th); the crest-beyond as the paryanta's place.*

## The T2 strategy-declaration and the forks
**Strategy:** S1-commentary-informed (the self-vivṛti as frame) + the register-shift toward the flowing-constructive and the deśabhāṣā-flavor (the chomping, the fodder, the h-less). **Forks taken:** the ten-part cognition (v.1), the 24-fold count (v.2), the Fierce-epithet (v.3), the throne (v.4), the track (v.5), the chomping (hymn-1), the h-less (hymn-2), the unmoored (hymn-3). **Hard core with T1:** the daśākhaṇḍa's ten, the kāla-as-Bhairava, the prāṇa-as-food, the kuṇḍalinī's rending, the paryanta's count, the Pratyabhijñā-yoke. **R2's work:** adjudicate the register-forks; the commentary itself decides the deśabhāṣā-decode.
