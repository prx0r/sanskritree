## 1.1 — nandīśa
**T1:** "lord of Nandin" (with the bliss-reading [X]).
**T2:** "lord of bliss" (nandīśa = nandī + īśa — the bliss-lord) — *fork (resolved toward T2 in R1): the Kaula invocation's bliss-register (nityānanda, paramānanda — the text's own words) favors "bliss"; the purāṇic Nandin belongs to another register. T2 takes the bliss-reading as primary.*

## 1.9 — na prakāśyaṃ na prakāśyaṃ na prakāśyaṃ kadācana
**T1:** "Not to be revealed, not to be revealed, never at any time to be revealed."
**T2:** "Never show it, never show it — at no time show it" — *fork: the impersonal ("not to be revealed" — the gerundive's passive) vs the direct address ("never show it" — the second-person imperative-force). The verse's addressee (Pārvatī — the pupil) supports T2's address; the triple's formulaic force is the same.*

## 1.12 — mantrārthaṃ mantracaitanyaṃ yonimudrāṃ na vetti yaḥ
**T1:** "Whoever does not know the mantra's meaning, the mantra's consciousness, the yoni-mudrā —"
**T2:** "Whoever does not know the meaning of the mantra, the aliveness of the mantra, the yoni-gesture —" — *fork: caitanya = "consciousness" (the technical) vs "aliveness" (the animating — the mantra's life); mudrā = "mudrā" (the technical) vs "gesture" (the plain). The text's own paṭala 6 (the yonimudrā as the cakra-yoga) supports the technical register; T2's plainness is the surface.*

## 1.14 — kevalaṃ bhāvaśuddhyā ca mantrārthaṃ prāṇavallabhe
**T1:** "only by the purity of one's being is the mantra's meaning [attained], O beloved of my life."
**T2:** "but by the clarity of the state alone the mantra's goal is reached, O life's love" — *fork: bhāvaśuddhi = "the purity of being" (T1) vs "the clarity of the state" (T2 — bhāva as the meditative state, the text's own bhāva-triple of paṭala 7!); mantrārtha = "the mantra's meaning" vs "the mantra's goal" (artha = meaning AND aim). The paṭala-7 connection (the bhāva-triple) makes T2's "state" reading the more coherent: the sādhana's condition is the STATE's purity, not abstract being.*

## 2.1 — mantrārthaṃ sāramuttamam
**T1:** "the meaning of the mantra, the supreme essence."
**T2:** "the goal of the mantra — the highest essence" — *fork: mantrārtha as "the mantra's meaning" (the semantic) vs "the mantra's goal/aim" (artha's other face — the soteriological). Paṭala 2 IS the meaning-analysis; but the frame (the paṭala answers 1.12's mantrārtha) supports the semantic. Both live; the goal-reading is the deeper.*

## 3.63 — yatirvā bhupatirvāpi nānyaḥ śaktaḥ
**T1:** "Whether a renunciant (yati) or a lord of the earth (bhūpati) — no other is capable."
**T2:** "Monk or monarch — no one else has the power" — *fork: the pair yati/bhūpati as the renunciant-king pair (the two social poles — both unable without the vidyā) vs the plain "monk or monarch." T2's plainness keeps the pair's force; the verse's point (the vidyā's power exceeds both estates) is the same.*

## 6.67 — iti te kathitā devi yonimudrā maheśvari
**T1:** "Thus, O Devī, the yoni-mudrā has been told to you, O great Īśvarī."
**T2:** "So the Womb-Seal has been declared to you, O Devī, O great Lady" — *fork: yonimudrā as the technical (the transliteration) vs the rendered ("the Womb-Seal" — yoni = womb + mudrā = seal/gesture). The paṭala's content (the cakra-yoga + the kuṇḍalinī) makes the "Womb-Seal" the meaningful rendering — the seal of the womb-source. T2's is the commentary's rendering; T1's the glossary's.*

## 7.16 — strīṇāṃ padatalaṃ dṛṣṭvā guruvadbhāvayet sadā
**T1:** "Seeing the soles of women's feet, he should always contemplate them as the guru."
**T2:** "The feet of women — seeing them, he should hold them as the teacher" — *fork: guruvat = "as the guru" (the person) vs "as the teacher-principle" (the guru-force — cf. the KJN's guru-identity). The verse's radicalism (the women's feet as the guru) is the same in both; T2's "teacher" softens the capitalization.*

## 8.49 — kṣakāraṃ pūrvamuddhatya nādabindusamanvitam | dakṣiṇekālike devi kāmabījaṃ tataḥ param
**T1:** "having first uttered the kṣa with the nāda-bindu, then the kāma-bīja, the Dākṣiṇā-Kālikā [mantra]."
**T2:** "first raising the kṣa joined to the nāda-bindu, then the Kāma-seed, O Devī — the southern Kālikā" — *fork: dakṣiṇā-kālikā = "Dākṣiṇā-Kālikā" (the name — the southern Kālī, the fierce form) vs "the southern Kālikā" (T2 — the directional-name rendered). The name is the technical (the paṭala 6's goddess); T2's rendering is the surface.*

## 9.15 — nirākāraṃ samāsādya nirañjanapadaṃ labhet
**T1:** "attaining the formless, he obtains the station of the Stainless."
**T2:** "having reached the formless, he gains the Stainless-station" — *fork: nirañjanapada as "the station OF the Stainless" (T1 — the genitive) vs "the Stainless-station" (T2 — the appositional compound). The internal ring (1.2's nirañjanapadaṃ) governs: the station that is the Stainless — T2's apposition is the cleaner.*

## 10.1 — vandhyāyāścottamaṃ vidhim
**T1:** "the supreme rite for the barren woman."
**T2:** "the highest procedure for the childless woman" — *fork: vandhyā = "barren" (the blunt) vs "childless" (the gentle); vidhi = "rite" (the ritual) vs "procedure" (the medical — the paṭala IS medical). The paṭala's gynecological content supports T2's "procedure."*
