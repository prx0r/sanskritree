## 1.1 — nandīśa... nirañjana parātpara
**P1:** (v2 doc) "lord of Nandin" vs "lord of bliss" [X].
**Varieties:** a) "lord of Nandin" (Śiva's bull) · b) "lord of bliss" (nandī) · c) a Kaula form-name.
**Most likely:** (b) in this Kālīkula context — the Kaula invocation prefers the bliss-epithet (the text's own "ānanda" register: nityānanda, paramānanda), and the Nandin-reading belongs to the purāṇic register; the Kaula nandīśa (cf. the KJN's Nandīśa in the 22/2 list) functions as a bliss-lord. Recorded as a glossary-note; the v2 doc's open flag now resolved toward (b) with (a) noted.

## 1.5 — śapathaṃ me... vṛhadyoniṃ prakāśaya
**P1:** "[I give] my oath... reveal the Great Yoni."
**Varieties:** a) the pupil's oath ("I swear") · b) the oath-bound [teaching] ("reveal to me the oath-bound [secret]") · c) "by my oath, reveal."
**Most likely:** (a) — the parallel "śapathaṃ me" in the Sarvollāsatantra and Prāṇatoṣiṇī (our earlier greps) is the swearing-formula; and 1.8's double-oath structure (she swears, he swears) confirms the symmetric frame.
**Commentary:** the Great Yoni = the vidyā herself (the Ciñcinī-triangle note, Dyczkowski vol. 2): the request "reveal the Yoni" = "reveal the goddess." The verse's erotic-ontological register (yoni as the source AND the secret) is the Kālīkula's founding gesture.

## 1.21 — ke gotre na samudbhūtāḥ (corrupt)

## 1.31 — yā tu kṛṣṇā saiva kṛṣṇaḥ

## 2.1–2.2 — the krīṃ-analysis
**P1:** ka = the giver of desire; repha = the sin-destroyer.
**Most likely:** ours — the letter-theology of the Kālī-bīja (ka-repha-ī-nādabindu) is the text's own uddhāra; paṭala 8/49 confirms (kṣa-nādabindu + kāma-bīja).
**Commentary:** the bīja-theology is a compressed version of the whole paṭala-1 doctrine: the letters ARE the goddesses (the mātṛkā-body). The commentary should print the krīṃ-decomposition once, at 2.1.

## 7.6 — bhāvaśca trividho... divyavīrapaśukramāt
**P1:** "the bhāva is threefold, in the order divine-hero-bound."
**Most likely:** ours, confirmed by the paṭala's own conduct-sections (7/8's divya-conduct, 7/19's vīra-conduct, 7/26–31's paśu-disqualifiers).

## 9.15 — nirākāraṃ samāsādya nirañjanapadaṃ labhet
**P1:** "attaining the formless, he obtains the station of the Stainless."
**Most likely:** ours — and this is the text's own definitional verse (found in the very first searches): the opening's nirañjanapada (1.2) is here given its method (the sākāra→nirākāra ladder of 9/13–16).
**Commentary:** the internal ring: 1.2 asks "by what means (kenopāyena) does one attain the nirañjanapada?" — 9/15 answers: through the formed to the formless. The whole middle of the text is the upāya. This is the commentary's master-key.
