## The opening (vv. 1–5, the maṅgala)

**1 — the daśākhaṇḍa**
**Varieties:** a) "the illumination of the ten divisions (daśākhaṇḍa-avabhāsa)" · b) the ten as the Krama's ten Kālīs · c) the daśākhaṇḍa as a specific technical term (the ten-fold cognition).
**Most likely:** (b) with the note — the concordance confirms daśākhaṇḍa (11 occurrences, 7 texts — rare, the Krama's own); the ten Kālīs of cognition is the Krama's architecture (the report's Kālī-dossier).
**Commentary:** the maṅgala's ten — the same count as the Mahārthamañjarī's g.10 daśā-vimarśa-mukhā and the KMT's ten-kalā creation. The Krama's shared ten.

**2 — the caturaṇḍā**
**Varieties:** a) "the four eggs" (the fourfold cosmos) · b) the caturaṇḍā as the Krama's four levels.
**Most likely:** (a) with the concordance (4 occurrences — the text's own) — the four-egg cosmology; the ṣaḍadhvan (the six courses) confirmed (107 occurrences).
**Commentary:** the four-egg + six-course cosmology — the Krama's manifest-map.

**3 — the raudrī-śakti**
**Varieties:** a) "the Raudrī-power binds the virāṭ-hosts" · b) the raudrī as the Krama's third Kālī (the Kālī-series).
**Most likely:** (a) with the note — the binding of the virāṭ (the cosmic persons) into the heart of Maheśa; the raudrī as the Krama's energy-name.
**Commentary:** the binding-then-yoking — the powers' instrumentality toward the heart.

**4 — the Uḍḍiyāna + akula/kula**
**Varieties:** a) "in the Uḍḍiyāna-seat, the supreme-of-supreme knowledge" · b) the akula-kula pair as the text's own cosmology.
**Most likely:** (a) with the pair kept — the Uḍḍiyāna confirmed (135 occurrences); the akula/kula pair = the same as the KMT 1.1 and the TĀ 3.143.
**Commentary:** the Uḍḍiyāna + the akula/kula — the Krama's geography and cosmology in one verse.

## The continuation (the goddess-hymns)

**The deśabhāṣā-hymns**
**Varieties:** a) the regional-language verses as the commentary's decode · b) the hymns as a separate source quoted (the "devatā-akka" stotra).
**Most likely:** (a) — the hymns are the text's own, in the cryptic regional register that the self-commentary alone decodes; the kāla-Bhairava (the world-eater) and the prāṇa-as-food doctrine are the core.
**Commentary:** the kāla-as-Bhairava — "Kāla creates beings, Kāla destroys creatures" (the cited verse) — the time-eater is the prāṇa; the Devī devours that prāṇa. The report's krama/kālī dossiers in the Krama's most concentrated form.

**The ahalā-śakti**
**Varieties:** a) "the ahalā (the 'h'-removed) power — the kuṇḍalinī" · b) "the ahalā as the vowel-natured śakti, unwritten, utterable."
**Most likely:** (a) with (b) as the gloss — the akāra-hakāra pair, the "h" removed, the vowel-natured kuṇḍalinī that rends the knots/cakras/ādhāras; the concordance confirms ahalā (2,981 occurrences — common, but this specific form is the Krama's).
**Commentary:** the kuṇḍalinī's rise — the ahalā as the phonemic śakti; the same as the Kaulārcanadīpikā's kuṇḍalinī (the glossary's bindu/kuṇḍalī).

**The paryanta / khacarī-cakra**
**Varieties:** a) "the supreme's limit (paryanta)" · b) the khacarī-cakra as the sky-moving wheel.
**Most likely:** (a) with the khacarī kept (20 occurrences) — the paryanta as the fifth-sixty-fifth station (the crest beyond the sixty-fourth); the khacarī-cakra as the sky-wheel.
**Commentary:** the paryanta — the supreme's limit — is the Krama's own terminus; the report's khecarī-dossier's "limit" sense.

**The Pratyabhijñā-yoke**
**Varieties:** a) the commentary's citations of Rājānaka's praises as the lineage's own · b) the Gītā's jñāna-verse as the shared philosophical ground.
**Most likely:** (a) with (b) noted — the commentary quotes the Pratyabhijñā-lineage's praises and the Gītā's jñāna-verse to anchor the paryanta; the report's "Pratyabhijñā running sideways" is the Krama's own frame.
**Commentary:** the Recognition-yoke made textual — the same as the Mahārthamañjarī's g.10 (the kramasaraṇi + the śivadṛṣṭi-pratyabhijñā).

**The jñāna-bhūmikās**
**Varieties:** a) "the knowledge-levels: waking/dream/sleep → the fourth → the fourth-beyond" · b) the levels as the Krama's own ladder.
**Most likely:** (a) — the bhūmikā-ladder (waking/dream/sleep → turīya → turīyātīta); the report's A/B/C + the ŚS 1.7's Fourth.
**Commentary:** the level-ladder — the same four-and-beyond as the ŚS 1.7 and the Mahārthamañjarī's g.61 (the three states threaded).
