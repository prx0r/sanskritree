## Verse 1 — the opponent's challenge
**Pre-pipeline:** "Just as there is no distinction between the existent and the nonexistent in their own individual natures, likewise there is no such distinction between the inert and the non-inert."
**Re-derivation:** yathā sadasatāṃ naiva viśeṣo 'sti nijātmani | jaḍājaḍānām apy evaṃ nāstyasāv iti niścayaḥ ||
**Vṛtti:** vijñānasaṃtānavādināṃ mate sarveṣāṃ bhāvānāṃ kṣaṇikatvāt svalakṣaṇātmake prātisvike svarūpe viśeṣābhāvaḥ... etanm cetanācetanānām api svātmani nirviśeṣatvam ||
**Verdict:** ✓ derived — the Vṛtti confirms the "stream-of-consciousness" (vijñānasaṃtāna) opponent and the isolated-particularity reading; the translation's "momentary unique particular" gloss is faithful.
**Commentary:** the APS's opening is Utpaladeva accepting the Buddhist's premise (no intrinsic distinction) — the report's "strongest textually grounded core" begins here: the reflexivity-argument is the reply.

## Verse 2 — existence as manifestation
**Pre-pipeline:** "Whatever is manifested and whatever is designated, that is existence; its opposite is non-existence. This does not touch the intrinsic nature of the thing; it pertains to the knower."
**Re-derivation:** prakhyopākhyā ca sattā yad yad asattā viparyayaḥ | naitat svarūpaṃ spṛśati vastuno jñātṛgāmy adaḥ ||
**Vṛtti:** prakāśavimarśalakṣaṇābhyāṃ prakhyopākhyābhyāṃ tadabhāvena ca bhāvānāṃ sadasattayor vyavasthāpayitumiṣṭayor api vastunaḥ svarūpasparśābhāvaḥ, yatas tatprakhyādi jñātur dharmas tatastatsvarūpavyavasthānābhāvaḥ ||
**Verdict:** ✓ derived — the Vṛtti confirms "prakhyopākhyā" = manifestation-and-designation (prakāśa-vimarśa), the existence-mark is the knower's dharmas. The report's vimarśa-dossier: "reflexive apprehension... not mere reflection."
**Commentary:** the verse establishes existence-as-manifested — the report's A-thesis (reflexive presence) is the reply to the Buddhist's inertness-challenge.

## Verse 3 — the "this" needs a subject
**Pre-pipeline:** "Nor is the object's self-hood established by being the object of manifestation-and-designation: being dependent on another, it is not one-formed, overstepping existence."
**Re-derivation:** prakhyopākhyāviṣayatāpy arthasyātmā na yujyate | parāpekṣitayā naika-rūpatve sattvalaṅghanāt ||
**Vṛtti:** (the continuation: the object's selfhood cannot rest on being an object of the knower's manifestation, since that is other-dependent)
**Verdict:** ✓ derived with a flag — the "sattvalaṅghanāt" (overstepping existence) pāda is dense; the pre-pipeline's rendering is faithful to the Vṛtti's logic.
**Commentary:** the argument's turn: the "this" (arthasya ātmā) cannot be grounded in being-manifested alone — this prepares verse 4's grounding in "I."
