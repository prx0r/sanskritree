The frame: the report's saṃvit-krama — the Krama's own claim that the order of cognition IS the order of worship. Every krama, every Kālī, every sequence-term re-read from within.*

## What the anchor changes (the corrections)

**maṅgala 1 — the Gaṇeśa-homage.** My T1 read the elephant-trunk/lotus-stalk image correctly; the anchor confirms the reading (nālayate śuṇḍāṃ viṣāṇena mṛṇāline — "fondles the trunk with the tusk, [by] the lotus-stalk"). ✓ no change.

**maṅgala 2 — spandāspandaparispanda.** My T1: "the trembling and the untrembling and the all-trembling." The anchor confirms. **The Krama-perspective addition:** this threefold is NOT the Sāṃkhya-style static triad — in the Krama's register (per the report's spanda-dossier), the spanda/aspanda/parispanda is the cognition's own pulse across the sequence (the samvit's manifest/resting/bursting). The maṅgala's tattva is the Krama's tattva — the cognition-sequence's ground.

**maṅgala 5 — svātmacitkramavimarśamaya.** My T1: "made of the self-conscious order-of-reflection." **The Krama-perspective correction:** the compound is svātma-cit-krama-vimarśa — "the self, consciousness, the Krama, reflexion." In the Krama's own voice this is the *saṃvit-krama* — the reflexion (vimarśa) that IS the sequence of cognition. My T1's "order-of-reflection" was right but under-powered; the anchor's text (tatsvātmacitkramavimarśamayaṃ gurūṇām avalliyugmam) makes the guru-feet themselves the embodiment of the cognition-sequence's reflexion. **The Krama-world reading: the guru IS the krama.**

**maṅgala 10 — kramasaraṇi.** My T1: "the Krama-path's track." The anchor: avagataśivadṛṣṭipratyabhijñārthatattva-kramasaraṇirahasyollāsasarvasvavedī — "who knows the whole essence of the secret-springing of the Krama-path's truth, [having] understood the meaning of the Śivadṛṣṭi and the Pratyabhijñā." **Confirmed + sharpened:** the text explicitly yokes the Krama-path to the Śivadṛṣṭi and the Pratyabhijñā — the report's "Pratyabhijñā running sideways" is the text's own self-positioning. The Maheśvarānanda's claim: he knows the Krama's secret through the Recognition-lineage's meaning.

**maṅgala 11 — kulasaparyā.** My T1: "barren through their connection with the reflection of the kula-worship." The anchor: ati tu kulasaparyābimbasaṃbandhyavandhyāḥ — "but the barren ones, connected with the reflection (bimba) of the kula-worship." **Confirmed** — and the Krama-perspective gloss: the "reflection" (bimba) is the same ābhāsa-language as the report's vimarśa-dossier; those who know only the ritual's reflection, not the cognition it mirrors, are barred from the secret.

**maṅgala 12 — svāpitabhāṣā.** My T1: "in her own-awakened speech." The anchor: svāpitabhāṣābhiḥ — confirmed; the dream-revealed yoginī's gāthās.

## The Krama-perspective frame (what the anchor makes explicit)

The text's own core-claim (gāthā 2, the anchor): "vardhatāṃ mahāprakāśo vimarshavicchuritaniścalodyotaḥ" — may the great-prakāśa flourish, whose unmoving upsurge is suffused with reflexion (vimarsha-vicchurita). **This is the report's prakāśa/vimarśa pair in the Krama's own voice** — and the text's gāthās 21–27 (the karaṇas, the krama-vasha, the yāmala) build the saṃvit-krama systematically.

**The single sharpest Krama-perspective insight from the anchor:** the Mahārthamañjarī is a *tantra* (the text's own frame: "kramasaraṇirahasyollāsa"), and its 70 gāthās + the colophon ("gāthānām anubhāṣaṇaṃ... granthārthagrathanakriyāsu gahano hṛdyas'ca kaścit kramaḥ") present the **krama as the very method of the text** — the sequence by which the commentary grinds the meaning. My T1 translated the maṅgala; the R1 now frames the whole as the Krama's self-conscious method.
