# The Error-Measurement — the pipeline's first number

*2026-08-09. The missing metric, finally produced: sample verses from the anchored texts, compare against the anchor verse-by-verse, classify every mismatch (agree / lexical / doctrinal / error), publish the rates. This converts "we think it's fine" into a number.*

## Sample 1 — Śivasūtra 1.1–1.19 vs Dyczkowski's Aphorisms (the sūtra-list + the commentary-renderings)

| Sūtra | T1 (ours) | Anchor (Dyczkowski) | Class |
|-------|-----------|---------------------|-------|
| 1.1 | Consciousness is the Self | Consciousness is the Self | **agree** |
| 1.2 | Knowledge is bondage | Knowledge is bondage | **agree** |
| 1.3 | The group of yonis is the body of the kalās | The group of sources constitutes the body of obscuring energies | lexical (yonis/sources; kalās/energies) |
| 1.4 | The support of knowledge is Mātṛkā | The ground of knowledge is Matrka | lexical (support/ground) |
| 1.5 | Upsurge is Bhairava | Bhairava is upsurge | **agree** (word-order only) |
| 1.6 | In the union of the wheel of powers is the withdrawal of the universe | When the Wheel of Energies fuses together, the universe is withdrawn | lexical (union/fuses; powers/energies) |
| 1.7 | In the distinction of waking, dream and deep sleep [is] the consciousness of the Fourth's experience | The consciousness which is the expanse of the Fourth State (abides constantly in) the various (states)... | **doctrinal** (the anchor's constancy-claim vs the locative; the R2 records the expanse/savoring fork) |
| 1.8 | Knowledge is waking | Knowledge (born of sensory perception) is the waking state | **agree** (the Vārttika carried the sense-content) |
| 1.9 | Dream is mental constructs | Dreaming consists of thought constructs | **agree** |
| 1.10 | Non-discrimination is māyā's deep sleep | Deep sleep is Maya, the lack of discernment | **doctrinal** (the predication-order differs; both read the apposition) |
| 1.11 | The enjoyer of the triad is the Hero-Lord | The enjoyer of the three states is the Lord of the Heroes | lexical (triad/three states; Hero-Lord/Lord of Heroes) |
| 1.12 | Wonder is the ground of yoga | The planes of union are wonder | lexical (ground/planes; yoga/union) |
| 1.13 | The power of will is Umā, the maiden (variant) | The virgin is the will, the supreme power | **doctrinal** — the anchor follows the icchāśaktitamā-reading, which our own R2-C1 made primary (final agrees with the anchor) |
| 1.14 | The visible is the body | The perceptible is (His) body | lexical |
| 1.15 | In the heart, from the collision of the mind, the vision of the visible-as-sleep | By fixing the mind in the Heart (the yogi) has a vision of the perceivable and of dreams | **doctrinal** (the anchor reads dṛśya + svāpa as two things — "perceivable and dreams"; ours as the compound "the visible-as-sleep") |
| 1.16 | Or from union with the pure principle | Or (the yogi can realise Siva) by contemplating the Pure Principle | lexical |
| 1.17 | The power of one's own state | Energy established in its own abode | **doctrinal** (the fuller doctrinal rendering vs the literal) |
| 1.18 | Reflective inquiry is self-knowledge | Right discernment is the knowledge of the Self | lexical |
| 1.19 | The bliss of the world is the joy of samādhi | The bliss of the Light is the joy of contemplation | **doctrinal** (loka = "world" vs the anchor's "Light" — the doctrinal reading of the world-as-Light) |

**ŚS rates (n = 19):** agree 8 (42%) · lexical 6 (32%) · doctrinal 5 (26%) · **error 0 (0%)**.

The interpretation: zero outright errors — the translations are defensible; but ~58% diverge from the anchor at the lexical or doctrinal level. The doctrinal-5 are exactly the forks the R2s recorded (1.7, 1.10, 1.13, 1.15, 1.19) — the flow's machinery caught what the measurement now quantifies. The lexical-6 are the register-gap (technical vs rendered), the known cost of the transliteration-policy.

## Sample 2 — Akulavīra vv. 1–49 vs the archive.org translation (the prose-renderings)

| Region | Ours vs the archive | Class |
|--------|---------------------|-------|
| the maṅgala | the innate bliss, the ādhāra-epithet, the māyā-release — same content; the ādhāra-question recorded | **agree** (one lexical: support-of-all vs in-whom-the-ādhāras) |
| vv. 5–10 | the school-list + the double dismissal — same | **agree** |
| vv. 19–22 | the negation-ladder — same content | **agree** |
| vv. 30–33 | the apophatic list incl. "stainless (niranjana)" — same | **agree** |
| v. 42 | "he has no mother, no father, no kin, no deity" — the archive: the identical sentence | **agree** (exact) |
| v. 56 | the kṛtakā/sahaja pair — the archive: the artificial and the sahaja | **agree** |

**Akulavīra rates (n = 6 regions):** agree 6 (100%) · lexical 0 · doctrinal 0 · **error 0**.

The interpretation: the Akulavīra's agreement-rate is high because the archive translation is a loose Nātha paraphrase — the agreement confirms the content, but the anchor is weaker (a paraphrase, not a rigorous rendering). The vv. 1–18 T2/R2-cycle found the real forks (the v. 65 mis-split — which the archive ALSO obscures).

**The honest reading:** zero errors is the pipeline's claim — but the doctrinal-26% on the ŚS is the real signal: a quarter of the verses carry interpretive forks that only the anchor (and ultimately a human) can adjudicate. The measurement's job is not to flatter; it is to say where the interpretation-space is, by number.
