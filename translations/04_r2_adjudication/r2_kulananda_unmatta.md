# I. Kulānanda

## v. 10 — āveśaṃ kurute (the P3-find)
**Form:** āveśaṃ kurute — the active. **Grammar:** "makes the possession" (the causative-active) vs "enters the possession" (the middle-force).
**The parallel (the KJN 14/38, quoted from the text on disk):** "tāvat samādhimāyāti stobhāveśādilakṣaṇam" — he GOES to samādhi, with the marks of stobha-possession — the possession is the ARRIVED-state, not something made. The Kulānanda's own v. 34 (the sādhaka's rarity) confirms: the siddhi happens TO him.
**Verdict:** "enters the possession" — the KJN-parallel decides.
**T3-reading:** "By knowing this alone he surely enters the possession."

## v. 27 — palitāstambhana
**Form:** the practice-name. **The parallel:** the KJN 7's paṭala-title (palitastambhavijñāna — the same practice, same term) — the term is the practice's NAME.
**Verdict:** the technical kept.
**T3-reading:** T1's.

## v. 34 — vīrārohe
**Form:** the vocative-ambiguity. **Grammar:** "O heroine / O you who ride the hero" (the vocative) vs "mounted on the hero" (the state).
**The chain:** the text's addressee is the Devī throughout (the frame-questioner); the vocative is the natural reading; "vicaret yathāsukham" (move at ease) addresses her.
**Verdict:** the vocative (per R1); "walk as you will" for the close.
**T3-reading:** "Knowing this, O you who ride the hero, walk as you will."

## v. 55 — saṃgamānmokṣahetukaṃ
**Form:** the ablative. **Grammar:** "liberation FROM the union" (the ascetic) vs "liberation THROUGH the union" (the śakti-union).
**The chain:** the verse's drift (the samarasa, the lord-servant pair) is the union-doctrine; the Kaula register (the KJN's melaka, the Kubjikā's kumārī) — the union IS the means. The ascetic-from-reading would contradict the text's own practice-register.
**Verdict:** "liberation through the union."
**T3-reading:** "...[the knowledge] that is liberation through the union."

## vv. 58–60 — the close

**Verdict:** the technical register; the plain as the gloss.
**T3-reading:** T1's.

# II. Unmattabhairavahṛdaya

## v. 15 — vāruṇim
**Form:** the accusative. **Grammar:** Vāruṇī = the water-mudrā (the Varuṇa-gesture) or the Vāruṇī-liquor.
**The chain:** the yantra-ritual context (the goat-offering, the dhāraṇā) favors the gesture; the liquor-reading belongs to the kula's drink-register (elsewhere in the bundle).
**Verdict:** "the Water-gesture" (per R1's (b/c)-resolution); the liquor noted.
**T3-reading:** "having performed the goat-offering, let him hold the Water-gesture."
