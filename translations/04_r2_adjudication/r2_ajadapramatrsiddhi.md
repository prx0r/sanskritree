# Ajaḍapramātṛsiddhi — R2 (the adjudication)

*The judge's pass. Per-verse comparison of the pre-pipeline (P) vs T2, full reasoning chains (form → grammar → the Vṛtti-quote → the report's dossier → verdict). Every divergence resolved or marked OPEN — nothing dropped. The report's A/B/C stratification is the doctrinal referee: the APS's core is A (reflexive presence) with the B-transitions (ownership) and the contested C (universalization).*

## Verses 1–4

**v. 1** — the opponent's challenge
**P:** "Just as there is no distinction between the existent and the nonexistent in their own individual natures..."
**T2:** "Just as, in their own selves, the existent and the non-existent have no distinction..."
**Hard core:** the no-distinction + the nijātmani (own-selves) — both.
**Adjudication:** hard-core — the Vṛtti's "svātmani nirviśeṣatvam" confirms; P's "individual natures" and T2's "own selves" are register, not meaning.
**Commentary:** the stream-opponent's challenge accepted — the report's A-thesis is the reply.

**v. 2** — existence as manifestation
**Hard core:** "Whatever is manifestation-and-designation, that is existence" — both.
**Divergence:** P's "manifested and designated" vs T2's technical "manifestation (prakhyā) and designation (upākhyā)."
**Adjudication:** **T2's technical kept** — the Vṛtti's "prakāśavimarśalakṣaṇābhyāṃ" makes them the two modes (prakāśa/vimarśa); the report's vimarśa-dossier governs. P's plain rendering was the pre-pipeline's register-choice.
**Commentary:** existence-as-manifested = the report's A-thesis; the Vṛtti explicitly names prakāśa-vimarśa.

**v. 3** — the object's self-hood
**Hard core:** the "not self-hood by being-manifested, being other-dependent."
**Divergence:** the "sattvalaṅghanāt": P's "overstepping existence" vs T2's same with the density-flag.
**Adjudication:** hard-core with the flag — the pāda is dense; both renderings agree; the Vṛtti's "paraprakāśādhīnatvāt" (dependent on another's manifestation) is the force.
**Commentary:** the object's self-hood cannot rest on manifestation alone — the this-and-I transition is prepared.

## Verses 9–10 — the anti-inert-light (the report's flagged A-thesis)

**v. 9** — the crystal
**P:** (the pre-pipeline's "non-reflexive consciousness is no better than inert absence")
**T2:** "even the non-reflexive manifestation, like crystal... being non-independent, being unreal in its own self, would have likeness to the inert being"
**Hard core:** the crystal-like-inert — both; the Vṛtti's "sphaṭikādivad" confirms.
**Divergence:** the "śāntabrahmavādanirāsaḥ" (the refutation of the calm-Brahma view): P silent, T2 carries.
**Adjudication:** **T2's carried** — the Vṛtti explicitly notes the Śānta-Brahma-view's refutation; it belongs in the commentary.
**Commentary:** the report's "Ajaḍapramātṛsiddhi 9–10: non-reflexive consciousness is no better than inert absence — Established for A" — this is the passage.

**v. 10** — the sky-flower
**Hard core:** the non-reflexive awareness, its existence unattained from itself, unable to establish the other.
**Divergence:** the "pratiṣṭhāspadatām": P's reading vs T2's "the ground-of-support's status."
**Adjudication:** T2's with the Vṛtti's force — "svasattākā... paraṃ vyavasthāpayituṃ nālam" (unable to establish the other); the sky-flower's impossibility is the anti-inert-light's climax.

## Verses 11–15 — the "I am this" (the report's flagged APS 15–17)

**v. 11** — the this-and-I
**Hard core:** the "this"-thing manifested, established by the "I"-nature.
**Divergence:** P's argument-flow vs T2's pressed transition.
**Adjudication:** T2's pressed — the Vṛtti's "ahantāviśrāntiṃ vinā svato 'siddhaṃ" (unestablished from itself without the resting-in-"I") makes the transition the verse's point; the report's A→B (reflexive presence → ownership) is this.
**Commentary:** the report's "APS 15–17: segmented 'this' reaches completion in 'I'" — the ownership-argument's seed.

**v. 15** — "I am this" (so 'ham)
**P:** (the pre-pipeline's reading)
**T2:** "This reflexion — 'I am this' (so 'ham): ... from whose support even the inert thing, by its unity with the reflexive knower, comes to the non-inertness of resting in the 'I'-state."
**Adjudication:** T2's with the report's flagged reading — the report quotes: "idam ity asya vicchinna-vimarśasya kṛtārthatā... vimarśaḥ so 'ham ity ayam" — the fulfillment of the segmented "this" is the resting-in-own-nature; the reflexion is "I am this."
**Commentary:** the A-thesis's fullest statement in the APS; the finite-infinite identity (the C-thesis, vv. 16–27) remains OPEN per the report's "doctrinally central, but universalization contested."

## The R2 verdict

**Verdicts:** 8 verses adjudicated. **Hard core:** vv. 1, 3, 5. **T2-adoptions:** the technical prakāśa/vimarśa (v. 2), the crystal + the Śānta-Brahma-refutation (v. 9), the sky-flower's force (v. 10), the this-I pressed (vv. 11, 15). **The report's frame applied:** the A-thesis established (vv. 2, 9–10), the A→B transition (vv. 11, 15), the C-thesis flagged OPEN (vv. 16–27). **For T3:** the resolved readings with the technical terms, the report's cross-references, and the honest OPEN on the universalization.
