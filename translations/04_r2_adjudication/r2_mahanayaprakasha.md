# Mahānayaprakāśa — R2 (the adjudication), the opening + the continuation

*The judge's pass. T1 vs T2 per crux, the self-commentary (Śitikaṇṭhācārya's vivṛti) as referee, the saṃvit-krama frame. Every divergence resolved or OPEN.*

## The opening (vv. 1–5)

**v.1 — the daśākhaṇḍa**
**T1:** "the illumination of the ten divisions"
**T2:** "the light-burst of the ten-part cognition"
**Hard core:** the ten + the illumination — both.
**Divergence:** the register — T1's "divisions" vs T2's "cognition."
**Adjudication:** **T2's "ten-part cognition" adopted** — the commentary's own frame (the cognition-sequence) makes the ten the Krama's ten Kālīs of cognition, not mere divisions. The concordance confirms the rarity (11 occurrences — the Krama's own).
**Commentary:** the ten = the Mahārthamañjarī's daśā-vimarśa-mukhā and the KMT's ten-kalā creation — the Krama's shared ten.

**v.2 — the caturaṇḍā count**
**T1:** "the six courses of the four eggs"
**T2:** "the four-and-twenty-fold courses"
**Divergence:** the count — T1's 4 + 6 vs T2's 4×6 = 24.
**Adjudication:** **T1's "the six courses of the four eggs" kept** — the commentary reads the caturaṇḍā (four-egg) and the ṣaḍadhvan (six-course) as the two terms, not multiplied; the 24-reading is the over-parse. The concordance (caturaṇḍā 4, ṣaḍadhvan 107) confirms both terms' existence.
**Commentary:** the four-egg + six-course as the manifest-map's two axes.

**v.3 — the raudrī**
**Adjudication:** T1's "Raudrī" with the name-force kept — the commentary names the energy (raudrī śaktiḥ); T2's "Fierce" is the epithet-register. Both noted; the name in the technical context.
**Commentary:** the Raudrī-power's binding-and-yoking — the instrumentality toward the heart.

**v.4 — the Uḍḍiyāna + the akula/kula**
**Adjudication:** hard-core — the Uḍḍiyāna-seat, the supreme-of-supreme knowledge, the akula/kula pair; the pair = the shared cosmology (KMT 1.1, TĀ 3.143).
**Commentary:** the geography + the cosmology in one verse — the Krama's map.

## The continuation (the goddess-hymns)

**Hymn-1 — the kāla-Bhairava**
**T1:** "the world-devouring Bhairava she devours... Kāla creates beings..."
**T2:** "the world-chomping Bhairava she chomps... Kāla spawns beings; Kāla gobbles creatures..."
**Hard core:** the devouring + the kāla-verse — both.
**Divergence:** the register — T1's formal "devours" vs T2's colloquial "chomps/gobbles."
**Adjudication:** **T1's "devours" kept with T2's flavor as the gloss** — the deśabhāṣā-register is the hymn's own, but the commentary's formal decode governs; the "spawns/gobbles" noted as the colloquial layer.
**Commentary:** the kāla-as-Bhairava (the time-eater) — the Krama's most concentrated doctrine; the prāṇa-as-food confirmed.

**Hymn-2 — the ahalā-śakti**
**T1:** "the ahalā... the vowel-natured kuṇḍalinī, unwritten, utterable"
**T2:** "the h-less power... the wakening-root..."
**Hard core:** the ahalā (the h-removed) + the kuṇḍalinī + the rending — both.
**Divergence:** T2's "h-less" rendering vs T1's "ahalā" transliterated.
**Adjudication:** **T1's "ahalā" transliterated with T2's "h-less" as the gloss** — the commentary's own etymology (the akāra-hakāra pair, the "h" removed) is the key; both the term and its gloss belong.
**Commentary:** the ahalā-kuṇḍalinī — the phonemic śakti; the same as the Kaulārcanadīpikā's kuṇḍalinī.

**Hymn-3 — the paryanta**
**T1:** "the paryanta (the limit)... the khacarī-cakra... the homeless (aniketa)"
**T2:** "the unmoored... the sky-wheel..."
**Hard core:** the paryanta + the khacarī-cakra + the aniketa — both.
**Divergence:** T2's "unmoored" vs T1's "homeless."
**Adjudication:** T1's "homeless" with T2's "unmoored" as the register-variant — the aniketa (the not-established) as the supreme's non-position; the paryanta's count (the fifth-sixty-fifth, beyond the sixty-fourth) kept.
**Commentary:** the paryanta — the Krama's own terminus, beyond the sixty-fourth level; the report's khecarī-dossier's "limit" sense.

**The Pratyabhijñā-yoke**
**Adjudication:** hard-core — the commentary's citations (Rājānaka's praises, the Gītā's jñāna-verse) anchor the paryanta; the report's "Pratyabhijñā running sideways" is the text's own frame.
**Commentary:** the Recognition-yoke — the same as the Mahārthamañjarī's g.10 (the kramasaraṇi + the śivadṛṣṭi-pratyabhijñā).

**The jñāna-bhūmikās**
**Adjudication:** hard-core — the levels (waking/dream/sleep → turīya → turīyātīta); the report's A/B/C + the ŚS 1.7's Fourth.
**Commentary:** the level-ladder — the same four-and-beyond as the ŚS 1.7 and the Mahārthamañjarī's g.61.

## The R2 verdict

**Verdicts:** 10 cruxes adjudicated. **Hard core:** v.1's ten, v.4, the kāla-Bhairava, the prāṇa-as-food, the paryanta, the Pratyabhijñā-yoke, the jñāna-bhūmikās. **T2-adoptions (register):** v.1 (the ten-part cognition). **T1-kept:** v.2 (the four-egg count), v.3 (the Raudrī-name), hymn-1 (the formal devouring), hymn-2 (the ahalā transliterated), hymn-3 (the homeless). **OPENs:** the full deśabhāṣā-decode's remaining segments. **For T3:** the resolved readings with the commentary's own glosses.
