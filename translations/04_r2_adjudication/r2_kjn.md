## 2/2 — kālāgnirudra... nakhāgre
**Form:** the name + the locative; no stated verb-subject. **Grammar:** the name as the subject (the deity) vs a supplied "fire."
**The chain:** 2/3's "yadā prajvalate" (when it blazes) — the blaze's subject is this name; the withdrawal is a person-act (the Lord's). The somatic cosmography (the paṭala's own frame: the body as the cosmos) locates the deity at the body's edge.
**Verdict:** the deity-subject.
**T3-reading:** "The Fire-of-Time-Rudra abides ever at the nails' tips."

## 2/8 — padānyastam
**Form:** pada-nyasta (the step-placed). **Grammar:** the literal ("his step set down") vs the ritual-technical (the pada-nyāsa).
**The chain:** the verse's pair (the withdrawal's step, the creation's step) is the cosmogonic double — the Lord's two steps as the cosmos. The Viṣṇu-trivikrama resonance is a commentary-idea [X: unverified — flagged for C1]; the pada-nyāsa-technical (the ritual step-placement) is a real parallel register.
**Verdict:** the literal-mythic primary; the technical noted.
**T3-reading:** "With his step set down in the yoga of withdrawal, enraged, he destroys the world; with his step set down in the yoga of creation he brings forth the moving and the unmoving."

## 3/9 + 20/2 — nirañjana, the Uncoloured
**Form:** the same term at the terminus (3/9's formless lotus; 20/2's kapāla-bheda). **Grammar:** nirañjana — the bahuvrīhi.
**The anchor-gloss (TĀ 3.172, Dyczkowski vol. 2, on disk):** "The Stainless (nirañjana) is the Supreme Abode... 'Añjana' means a 'stain' or 'colouring'... Thus 'nirañjana' is not only the stainless but also the unmanifest." — the unmanifest-face is the TĀ's own.
**Verdict:** "the Uncoloured" at both termini (the TĀ-gloss), "the Stainless" as the register-default.
**T3-reading:** "the free lotus, immovable, all-pervading — the Uncoloured" (3/9); "the skull cracks apart — and the jīva goes to the Uncoloured" (20/2).

## 5/15 — the moon-triple
**Form:** saṃpūrṇa-upūrṇa-āpūrṇa. **Grammar:** the deliberate three-fold.
**Verdict:** the triple (complete, completing, filled).
**T3-reading:** "The disc of the moon — complete, completing, filled — contemplated, O Devī, old age and disease perish."

## 6/4 — the name-capital
**Form:** nirāmayanirañjanam — the jīva's predicates. **Grammar:** the capital as the naming (the identity-claim).
**The chain:** the paṭala's thesis (the jīva IS the Stainless) + 20/2's eschatology (the jīva goes to it) — the identity is the doctrine.
**Verdict:** the capital.
**T3-reading:** "It is the supreme, the partless, the eternal — the Stainless, free of illness."
