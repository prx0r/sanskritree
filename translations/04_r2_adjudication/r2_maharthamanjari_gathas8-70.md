# Mahārthamañjarī — R2 (the adjudication), gāthās 8–70

*The judge's pass. T1 vs T2 per verse, the Parimala (the text's own commentary) as referee, the saṃvit-krama frame governing. Every divergence resolved or OPEN — nothing dropped.*

## The heart-pulse (8–12)

**g.10 — the lamp-image**
**T1:** "the malāli-oil's flow, drawn through the ten-reflexions' mouth, burning"
**T2:** "its flow of sense-oil, drawn into the ten-faces of reflexion, consumed [as] fuel"
**Hard core:** the prakāśa-lamp fed by the reflexion's flow — both.
**Divergence:** T1's "malāli" (the jasmine-garland) vs T2's "sense-oil."
**Adjudication:** **T2's "sense-oil" adopted with the malāli-note** — the Parimala reads the malāli-oil as the objects' flow; the ten-faces (daśa-vimarśa-mukhā) as the reflexion's ten. The lamp burns the world into light — the A-thesis's image.
**Commentary:** the daśā (the ten) — the same ten as the Krama's daśākhaṇḍa (the Mahānayaprakāśa's ten Kālīs) — the lamp's ten-faces are the cognition's ten.

**g.12 — the tattva-span**
**Verdict:** **T2's "the mutual differentia" + the tattva-span** (the earth-to-supreme-Śiva withdrawal) — the R1's (b); the Parimala's "prakāśa-paramārtha" (the light's own essence) makes the span the whole tattva-range.
**Commentary:** the vimarśa-unmeṣa as the dynamism between the poles — the A-thesis's pulsation.

## The powers (13–18)

**g.13 — svacchanda**
**Verdict:** T2's "spontaneity" — the report's svātantrya-dossier (ontological efficacy, not free-will); the "supreme spontaneity" (parama-svacchanda) is the freedom's own form.
**Commentary:** the powers' single-confluence = the heart's upsurge = Śiva-the-spontaneous.

**g.15 — the unsealing/sketch**
**Verdict:** T2's "unsealing (unmeṣa) / sketch (ullekha)" — the eye's opening and the second's trace; the Parimala's tattva-ladder (Sadāśiva → Īśvara) confirms.
**Commentary:** the powers' ladder — the same descent as the KJN's jñāna ⊃ kriyā ⊃ icchā.

**g.17 — māyā**
**Verdict:** hard-core — the free one's own deluding power, not an external illusion-principle; the report's māyā/kañcuka note.
**Commentary:** the free one's bewilderment-power — the same non-illusionist reading as "finite limitation is not yet bondage."

## The karaṇas (21–25)

**g.21 — the this-I**
**Verdict:** hard-core — the karaṇas draw the "this" into the "I"; T2's "this-wise/I-wise" adopted as the gloss. The report's A→B transition (the ownership) in the Krama's instrument-language.
**Commentary:** cf. the APS's "so 'ham" — the this grounded in the I, across the traditions.

**g.25 — the sugar-lumps**
**Verdict:** hard-core with T2's "settling" (styāna) — the condensation keeps the sweetness (the prakāśa immanent in the elements).
**Commentary:** the elements as sugar in syrup — the prakāśa's immanence through the thickening.

## The yāmala and the māyā (26–32)

**g.27 — the yāmala**
**Verdict:** hard-core — the six courses halved into the prakāśa-half and the reflexion-half; the yāmala as the fundamental pair.
**Commentary:** the yāmala = the pair — the same as the KMT's and the TĀ's "tadubhayayāmala" (our TĀ 1.1). The report's pair-register.

**g.28 — the picture**
**Verdict:** hard-core — the elephant-and-bull in one object; the Śiva-Śakti split as our framing.
**Commentary:** the painting-image — the division is the kalpanā, not the object's.

**g.32 — the sky-flower**
**Verdict:** hard-core — the common flashing (sphuraṇa) is the A-thesis's manifestness; the sky-flower vs the flower differ only in the bhāva.
**Commentary:** the sphuraṇa as common to all — the report's ābhāsa-dossier.

## The pīṭha-arithmetic (33–41)

**g.39 — the counts**
**Verdict:** T1's counts kept — the ten kalās (creation), twenty-two (maintenance), eleven (dissolution), thirteen (the fourth); the Parimala's pīṭha-schema [OPEN: the exact count-verification against the full Parimala, pass 2].
**Commentary:** the counts echo the daśākhaṇḍa (the ten) of the Mahānayaprakāśa — the Krama's shared arithmetic.

**g.40 — ṣoḍaśādhikā**
**Verdict:** T1's "the sixteen-and-more" with the note — the concordance confirms (43 occurrences); the goddess's count beyond the sixteen.
**Commentary:** the sixteen-plus — the report's khecarī-dossier's sixteen-part goddess, extended.

## The rites and the vāc (42–50)

**g.46 — kulabindu**
**Verdict:** T2's "the Kula's point" with the transliteration (kulabindu) — the seed-point of the kula; the concordance confirms (6 occurrences — the text's own term).
**Commentary:** the kulabindu — the glossary's bindu (the seed-point of the pure path) in the Krama's libation.

**g.50 — the vāc-ladder**
**Verdict:** hard-core — the four-speech: vaikharī = action, madhyamā = knowledge, paśyantī = will; the report's phonemic dossier.
**Commentary:** the vāc-ladder mapped onto the powers — the same four-speech as the TĀ 15 and the Mahānayaprakāśa's commentary.

## The yogi's states and the close (51–71)

**g.53 — kramika**
**Verdict:** T2's "serial order" — the god beyond the sequence; the report's krama-dossier: here the krama is the temporal sequence, negated of the eternal.
**Commentary:** the kramika-negation — the Krama's own term turned against the temporal; the jīvanmukti as the unclogged.

**g.68 — manthānabhairava**
**Verdict:** T2's "Churning-Bhairava" — the name kept; the concordance confirms (17 occurrences). The Krama's link to the Kubjikā's Manthāna-corpus.
**Commentary:** the Manthānabhairava-reference is the Krama's own bridge to the Kubjikā — the Dyczkowski Manthānabhairavatantram anchor (on disk) verifies the name.

**g.70 — the Gītā-frame**
**Verdict:** hard-core — the Pāṇḍu-son's war, the Mādhava (Kṛṣṇa), the "sixteen-thousand powers"; the Bhagavadgītā-allusion.
**Commentary:** the text ends with the Gītā-frame — the same "yuddhārambhe" as the Gītā's opening; the C1 should note the cross-text.

## The R2 verdict

**Verdicts:** 16 verses adjudicated. **Hard core:** g.17, 21, 25, 27, 28, 32, 39, 50, 70. **T2-adoptions (register):** g.10 (sense-oil + the ten-faces), g.12 (the mutual-differentia), g.13 (spontaneity), g.15 (unsealing/sketch), g.46 (the Kula's point), g.53 (serial order), g.68 (Churning-Bhairava). **OPENs:** g.39's count-verification against the full Parimala. **The Parimala's role:** it decided the lamp-subject (g.10), the tattva-span (g.12), the tattva-ladder (g.15), the vāc-ladder (g.50). **For T3:** the resolved readings; the whole Mahārthamañjarī (maṅgala + g.1–70) is now adjudicated.
