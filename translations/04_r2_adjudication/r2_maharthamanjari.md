## The maṅgala

**m.1** — the Gaṇeśa-image
**T1:** "fondles the lotus-stalk... with his trunk"
**T2:** "toys with his trunk, tusk against the lotus-stalk... ear-leafed"
**Hard core:** the elephant-god + the lotus-stalk + the leaf-ears — both.
**Divergence:** "viṣāṇena" (T2: with the tusk) vs T1's "with his trunk."
**Adjudication:** **T2's "tusk"** — viṣāṇa is the tusk; the trunk is śuṇḍā. The two instruments (trunk + tusk) are the playful pair; the Parimala's Gaṇeśa-homage supports the two-instrument image. T1's "with his trunk" conflated the two.
**Commentary:** the gaṇa-praise opens with the doubled-play — the same pair-structure as the Krama's yāmala.

**m.2** — the spanda-triple
**T1:** "the trembling and the untrembling and the all-trembling"
**T2:** "the pulse, the pulse's rest, and the pulse's outpouring"
**Hard core:** the threefold spanda + the great lotus — both.
**Divergence:** the register — T1's static "trembling" vs T2's dynamic "pulse/rest/outpouring."
**Adjudication:** **T2 adopted** — the Parimala's frame (and the report's spanda-dossier: "dynamic pulse/activity of the fundamental conscious reality, not mechanically physical") makes the cognition-rhythm the sense; "trembling" risks the mechanical. The text's own gāthā 7 ("hṛdayaparispanda") confirms the pulse-register.
**Commentary:** this is the Krama-perspective's central correction — the maṅgala's tattva is the cognition's own rhythm.

**m.5** — the cit-krama
**T1:** "the self-conscious order-of-reflection"
**T2:** "the self's consciousness-order-reflexion (svātma-cit-krama-vimarśa)... the guru-feet ARE the cognition-sequence's reflexion"
**Hard core:** the svātma-cit-krama-vimarśa compound + the guru-feet — both.
**Divergence:** T1's "order-of-reflection" vs T2's "the guru-as-sequence."
**Adjudication:** **T2 adopted** — the R1's sharpening; the Parimala's own claim (the guru's feet as the reflexion-of-the-cognition-sequence) makes the compound the sequence's embodiment, not a mere order. The report's saṃvit-krama: "the perceptible order of worship corresponds to the imperceptible sequence of cognition."
**Commentary:** the guru-as-krama is the Krama's distinctive move — the teacher is the sequence's living reflexion.

**m.10** — the kramasaraṇi
**T1:** "the Krama-path's truth"
**T2:** "the Krama-path's truth — the meaning of the Śivadṛṣṭi and the Recognition understood"
**Hard core:** the kramasaraṇi + the śivadṛṣṭi-pratyabhijñā — both.
**Divergence:** T2's "Recognition" (capitalized) vs T1's "Pratyabhijñā."
**Adjudication:** **T2's "Recognition" adopted** — the report's Pratyabhijñā-dossier: "Recognition of one's identity... do not treat 'recognition' as mere recollection." The text's own yoking of the Krama to the Recognition-lineage is the report's "Pratyabhijñā running sideways" made textual.
**Commentary:** Maheśvarānanda's self-positioning: the Krama's secret is known THROUGH the Recognition's meaning — the report's control-corpus design is the text's own claim.

**m.11** — the bimba
**T1:** "barren through their connection with the reflection of the kula-worship"
**T2:** "barren, tied to the reflection (bimba) of the kula-worship... not the cognition it mirrors"
**Hard core:** the barren + the kula-worship's reflection — both.
**Divergence:** T2's explicit "not the cognition it mirrors."
**Adjudication:** **T2's gloss adopted into the commentary** — the bimba (reflection) is the ābhāsa-language of the report's vimarśa-dossier; those who know only the rite's reflection, not the cognition it mirrors, are barred. The Parimala supports the reflection-of-the-rite reading.
**Commentary:** the exclusion-verse — the ritualists who stop at the reflection are the kulasaparyā-bound.

## The doctrinal opening

**g.2** — the prakāśa/vimarśa engine
**T1:** (not translated — beyond the maṅgala in the T1)
**T2:** "the unmoving upsurge suffused with reflexion"
**Adjudication:** T2's reading kept as the R2's own addition — the Parimala's "vimarshavicchuritaniścalodyotaḥ" (the unmoving upsurge suffused with reflexion) is the report's prakāśa/vimarśa pair in the Krama's own voice; the A-thesis (reflexive presence) made textual. The R2 records this as the text's core-claim.
**Commentary:** this gāthā is the Krama's engine-verse — the great-prakāśa whose upsurge IS suffused-with-reflexion — the report's A-thesis stated in the Krama's register.

**g.7** — the heart's pulse
**T2:** "our discernment, whose śāstra is only the heart's pulse (hṛdaya-parispanda)"
**Adjudication:** T2's reading kept — the concordance confirms hṛdayaparispanda occurs only twice in the corpus (the text's own signature); the pulse-as-the-only-śāstra is the Krama's rule (the rite follows the heart's spanda, not external law) — the report's spanda-dossier's "dynamism constitutive of manifestation."
**Commentary:** the Krama's ethical-rule in seed: no external prescription where the heart's pulse rules — the same anti-ritualism as the Akulavīra's, in the Krama's own terms.
