# Akulavīratantra, vv. 19–155 — R2 (the full-reasoning-chain adjudications)

*The R2 as commentary. Anchor: the archive.org Nātha translation (on disk). vv. 1–18 already T3-FINAL.*

## v. 19 — the paired rituals
**Form:** the naiva-series (praveśa/nirgama, āvāhana/visarjana, āsana/mudrā). **Grammar:** naiva... naiva as the pair-structure ("neither...nor") vs the list ("no...no").
**The parallel:** the KJN's ritual-vocabulary (the āvāhana/visarjana pair is the standard paddhati-frame) — the refusals are of the ritual PAIRS.
**Verdict:** the pair-structure (T2) — the anuprāsa's mirror.
**T3-reading:** "Neither entering nor leaving; neither summoning nor sending away; no seat with the instruments, no mudrās, no fixing of the pierced palate."

## v. 23 — viṃśātmaka
**Form:** viṃśātmakaḥ... pudgalaḥ — the pair negated. **Grammar:** the compound "twenty-formed" vs the ordinal "the twentieth."
**The context:** the verse negates BOTH a scheme and a person — if viṃśātmaka is the tattva-count, the negation is of the counting itself.
**Verdict:** T1's "the twenty-formed" as the surface (the [X] stands — the print needed); the count-reading as the note.
**T3-reading:** T1's, with the count-reading noted.

## v. 39 — malakṣaṇam vs nirlakṣaṇam
**Form:** the verse's anuprāsa (nirākula, nirvikalpa, nirguṇa, sunirmala, nirākāra...) — one member breaks it. **Grammar:** the emendation nir-lakṣaṇam ("the mark-less") restores the series.
**The archive-anchor:** (vv. 39's region — the translation renders the apophatic list without the broken member.)
**Verdict:** the emendation for T3, the print-flag carried.
**T3-reading:** "Non-dual, unmoving, tranquil, free of attachment's fault; unagitated, construct-free — and the bound, the mark-less."

## v. 55 — koulikeḥ krameḥ
**Form:** krama, instrumental-plural. **Grammar:** krama = "procedure/order" — the initiatory ladders (the KJN's krama-terminology: the graded transmission).
**The parallel:** the KJN 21's krama-list (the graded kaulas); the "orders" sense is the lineage's.
**Verdict:** "the Kaulika-orders" (the initiatory) primary; "rites" noted.
**T3-reading:** "When the Akulavīra is gained, what need of the Kaulika-orders?"

## v. 65 — akule vīre (the P3-find)
**Form:** "akule vīre ca" — the locative-pair. **Grammar:** the title-word akulavīra in sandhi-split (akule vīre = akulavīre, the locative of the name).
**The internal evidence:** the colophon ("akulavīraṃ samāptam" — the name, singular); v. 67's "raktākhyakulavīre" (the same compound-pattern); the verse's own simile (the ghee-in-curd — one subject).
**Verdict:** **T1's pair-reading is a mis-split — the name stands.**
**T3-reading:** "When the akula-hero is reached, everything dissolves: as ghee [latent] in curd, as fire standing in wood —"

## v. 78 — baddho na ca bādhakaḥ
**Form:** the nominal-pair. **Grammar:** baddha/bādhaka as the nouns ("the bound one, the binder") vs the verbal (the passive/active force: "no one is bound; no one binds").
**The doctrine:** the verse's "I am no one; nothing is mine" demands the fullest negation — the relation itself dissolved, not its participants.
**Verdict:** the verbal negation (T2) — the radically nondual reading.
**T3-reading:** "I am no one; nothing is mine; no one is bound; no one binds."

## v. 106 — na jñātaṃ
**Form:** the print's negative vs the emendation. **Grammar:** the negative makes the apodosis contradict the text's epistemology.
**The parallel:** vv. 105, 107 (the knowledge-as-release affirmations); the KJN's jñāna-doctrine.
**Verdict:** the emendation (yena jñātaṃ), the print-flag carried.
**T3-reading:** "...by whom, dwelling in the body, it is known — he is freed from all bonds."

## v. 152 — bhināvasthā
**Form:** bhinna-avasthā. **Grammar:** "the divided state" vs "the state of difference."
**The context:** the verse opens the close's positive teaching — the dual state overcome.
**Verdict:** "the state of difference."
**T3-reading:** "Now I will tell the other — the state of difference — by its own portion..."
