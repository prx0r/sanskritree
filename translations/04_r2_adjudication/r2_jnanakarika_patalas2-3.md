## Patala 2

**2/3 - pañcabhirāheto deho**
**T1:** "the body is heard [to be built] of the five, in the Lord of principles"
**T2:** "in the lord of principles the body is heard [to come] from the five"
**Hard core:** "the body [is heard]... of/from the five" — both agree the five-built body; the syntax differs (T1's locative "in the Lord" vs T2's same with reordering).
**Adjudication:** T1's order — "in the Lord of principles" as the locative frame; the pāda's corruption ([X]) keeps the reading provisional; no emendation this pass (the sense is clear enough; the print pending).
**Commentary:** the five-vs-pervader contrast is paṭala 2's first move; the report's kula-dossier note (the body as the later Kaula sense) is NOT yet active here — the body is the five-elements, the plain sense. R2 keeps T1, the [X] stands.

**2/5 - nirāśrayañ ca mauṣadam**
**T1:** "the supportless is the medicine (?)"
**T2:** "the supportless is the remedy (auṣadha)" [emendation]
**Hard core:** "the supportless [is] the..." — the predicate is shared.
**Divergence:** the object: T1's print-"mauṣadam" flagged [X] vs T2's auṣadha-emendation.
**Adjudication:** **T2's auṣadha adopted** — the R1's (b); the jewel→remedy chain of 2/4–5 (the knowledge as the healing) is the text's own logic; the print's "mauṣadam" is the wobble. Flag: the Bagchi print's actual reading to confirm.
**Commentary:** the medicinal image is the bundle's "free of all diseases" soteriology; the auṣadha-emendation aligns the verse with 2/4's jewel.

**2/10 - cittābhāvavinirmuktaṃ**
**T1:** "free of the mind's absence [too]"
**T2:** "freed even from the mind's non-being"
**Hard core:** the double-negation — both read cittābhāva as the mind's absence-as-trap, negated.
**Divergence:** the register: T1 "free of... absence" vs T2 "freed even from... non-being" — the same structure, T2's "even" pressing the both/neither.
**Adjudication:** hard-core — the R1's (a) confirmed by both; the T2's "even" carried into T3 (the emphasis is the doctrine).
**Commentary:** the ladder (anābhyāsa → sarvacintā → cittābhāva) strips the mind's presence AND absence — the both/neither apophasis that 2/17's "na cittācittakaḥ" completes.

**2/18 - yo so niścintasarvajñaḥ**
**T1:** "he is the thoughtless all-knower"
**T2:** "he - that one - the thoughtless all-knower"
**Hard core:** the subject (the thoughtless all-knower) — identical.
**Divergence:** the "so" (the demonstrative): T1 drops it, T2 renders "that one."
**Adjudication:** **T2's "that one" adopted** — the demonstrative points to the fourth-mind knower of 2/8; the text's own emphasis.
**Commentary:** the paṭala's doctrinal peak (the guṇātīta knower); the C1 should mark the crowning.

**2/22 - kalpanājñānayuktāstu... siddhakramavivarjitam**
**T1:** "devoid of the siddha-order"
**T2:** "who lack the siddha-order" — "the siddha-order (the graded path) vs the accomplished sequence"
**Hard core:** "devoid/lack of the siddha-order" — the same loss.
**Divergence:** the krama's sense: the lineage-graded-path (T2's note) vs the general sequence.
**Adjudication:** T1's "siddha-order" with the report's krama-dossier in view — the report: "sequence... 'Krama' only when sectarian identity is demonstrable." Here the siddha-krama is the graded transmission (the lineage-sense), NOT the Krama-school; "order" is right, capitalize nothing.
**Commentary:** the report's krama-warning applies directly — no Krama-school inference from the noun alone; this is the graded siddha-path.

**2/25 - andhapāśaṃ yadā cittaṃ**
**T1:** "when the mind is the blind bond"
**T2:** "when the mind is the blind bond itself"
**Hard core:** the mind-as-blind-bond — identical.
**Divergence:** T2's "itself" (the emphasis) vs T1's plain.
**Adjudication:** T1, with T2's "itself" as the gloss — the mind IS the bond (the paśu-state); the question "where do the yogins go?" is the text's challenge.
**Commentary:** the jñānakhaḍga (sword of knowledge) cuts the two bonds; if the mind is itself the bond, the path is void — the C1 should carry the martial + the paradox.

**2/31 - mucyate sarvairdhanaiḥ**

**Hard core:** "released/freed from all the..." — the structure shared.
**Divergence:** the object: the print's "wealths" vs the emended "bonds."
**Adjudication:** **T2's bandhanaiḥ adopted tentatively** — the paṭala's pāśa-language (2/26–28) demands the "bonds"; the print's "dhanaiḥ" is the wobble; the Bagchi print decides. Flag: [X]-pending-collation.

## Patala 3

**3/1 - sa cetājñāna na sarvaḥ caitanyaṃ jñānavarjitam**
**T1:** "this consciousness is not [mere] knowing; not all - the consciousness free of knowledge"
**T2:** "that consciousness is not the knowing, is not the all - the consciousness without knowledge"
**Hard core:** "the consciousness is not the knowing, not the all — free of knowledge" — identical both/neither.
**Divergence:** T1's "[mere]" insertion vs T2's cleaner "not the knowing"; the cetājñāna-dvandva flagged in T2.
**Adjudication:** T2's syntax — "not the knowing, not the all" — with the dvandva-note ("consciousness-and-knowing") carried as the live alternative; the both/neither is the doctrine.
**Commentary:** the caryā opens with the same apophasis as paṭala 2's close — consciousness ≠ knowing; the report's vimarśa-dossier (reflexive, not discursive) is NOT yet active — this text's "knowledge" is the plain sense, negated.

**3/11 - ūrddhvacāre bhaved gaṅgā yasnā cādho vyavasthitaḥ**
**T1:** "in the upward-movement the Gaṅgā arises; [when] it is settled below [X]"
**T2:** "...when (yadā) it is settled below" [emendation]
**Hard core:** "the Gaṅgā arises in the upward-movement; [it] is settled below" — the geography shared.
**Divergence:** the conjunction: T1's [X]-flagged "yasnā" vs T2's yadā.
**Adjudication:** **T2's yadā adopted** — the conditional restores the verse's turn-structure (the breath's rise and fall); the print pending. Flag: [X]-pending-collation.
**Commentary:** the Gaṅgā = the ascending stream, the caryā's interiorized tīrtha; the same Gaṅgā-language as the Kaulārcanadīpikā's "kula-Gaṅgā" (the report's visarga/Kaula-dossier: the body-sacred).

**3/12 - dvābhyāṃ melāpakaṃ**
**T1:** "the union of the two, with effort"
**T2:** "by the two, the melāpaka - the fusion"
**Hard core:** "by the two... the melāpaka" — the two-agents + the term.
**Divergence:** the term's rendering: T1's "union" vs T2's transliterated "melāpaka (the fusion)."
**Adjudication:** **T2 adopted** — the concordance verifies the KJN's melāpaka = the siddhi-fusion (a technical term in this lineage); transliterate + gloss. This is the cross-text evidence the whole audit-layer exists for.
**Commentary:** the melāpaka is the bundle's fusion-terminology (the yoginī-siddhi's attainment, KJN); the same fusion the Kuladīpikā's kula/akula alternation presupposes — the report's kula-dossier (the kula/akula polarity) in its ritual form.

**3/17 - jayā ca vijayā caiva ajitā cāparājitā**
**T1:** "Jayā and Vijayā, Ajitā and Aparājitā" (the names)
**T2:** "the Victorious, the Triumphant, the Unconquered, the Unvanquished" (the epithets)
**Hard core:** the four members — identical list.
**Divergence:** the names vs the epithets.
**Adjudication:** **T1's names** — the established śakti-quartet (the mātṛkā-powers; "catvāro mātrakodbhavam" = born of the four Mothers makes them the letter-powers); the epithets are the names' plain senses, not the referents. T2's register-test correctly forces the question.
**Commentary:** the four powers = the same śakti-catuṣṭaya as the ŚS 2.7's bracket (Ambā/Jyeṣṭhā/Vāmā/Raudrī) under the names Jayā/Vijayā/Ajitā/Aparājitā — the cross-tradition identity to note in C1.

**3/19 - bindustañca parāśaktiḥ taṭasthaś cārurūpiṇī**
**T1:** "the bindu is the supreme power, standing at the shore, of lovely form"
**T2:** "the bindu is the supreme power; the shore-dweller, the lovely-formed" (the four-item list)
**Hard core:** "the bindu [is] the supreme power" — the identification shared.
**Divergence:** the syntax: T1's single subject (the bindu described) vs T2's list (bindu, parāśakti, taṭastha, cāru-rūpiṇī).
**Adjudication:** **T1's single-subject** — the verse's own grammar (bindus... parāśaktiḥ as the apposition: the bindu IS the supreme power) favors the single identification; the list-reading is the over-parsing. The glossary's bindu-dossier (the seed-point of the pure path, the ŚS 2.7's prollāsa) confirms.
**Commentary:** the bindu-as-parāśakti is the pure-path's seed; the taṭastha (shore) is the body-geography's liminal point — the report's bindu/visarga dossier in the caryā's map.

**3/78 - tripañcakaṃ**
**T1:** "the three-and-five"
**T2:** "the three-and-five (the guṇas + the elements)"
**Hard core:** "the three-and-five" — identical.
**Divergence:** none material — T2 glosses what T1 left bare.
**Adjudication:** hard-core, with the gloss: the three guṇas (the verse's own "sattvarajastamaś caiva ekatve") + the five elements; the report's krama-dossier's "sequence-only-when-demonstrable" applies to tripañcaka as a count, not a school.
**Commentary:** the guṇas-in-unity is paṭala 2's "guṇatrayavinirmuktaṃ" affirmed in the body — the caryā's turn from the negation to the embodiment.

**3/82 - ṣaḍbhirmukto yadā yogī muktakeśaḥ sa ucyate**
**T1:** "when the yogi is freed from the six, he is called the loosened-hair"
**T2:** "when the yogi is freed from the six, he is called the one-with-loosened-hair"
**Hard core:** "freed from the six → the loosened-hair" — identical.
**Divergence:** the six's referent: T1 silent, T2 notes the six-foes.
**Adjudication:** T1, with T2's note carried: the six-foes (ṣaḍ-ripu) is the standard list; the muktakeśa as the Kaula yogin's mark (cf. the paṭala's own 3/6 "muktakeśāḥ").
**Commentary:** the loosened-hair is the Kaula's social sign of the freed; the same signs (naked, lone, loosened-hair) as the Kulapradīpa's bhoga-yoga — the report's Kaula-dossier (the body/power) in its social form.

**3/84 - madirāmatta ucyate**
**T1:** "the one drunk with wine"
**T2:** "the one drunk with the wine" (with the kula-nectar-note)
**Hard core:** "the one drunk with the wine" — identical.
**Divergence:** the wine's referent: the literal vs the kula-nectar.
**Adjudication:** T1's "wine" with the internal-sense note — the caryā's register is the body-geography; the madirā here is the ascending kula-nectar (cf. the Kaulārcanadīpikā's "madirā jñānadā"); the C1 carries the ambiguity honestly.
**Commentary:** the Kaula wine-words (madirā, āsava, surā) share the yoni's double-face (literal/internal) — the report's visarga-dossier's "erotic/ritual context" tag, applied.

**3/85 - nairmālyamālino yogī**
**T1:** "the yogi wearing the stripped-garland (nairmālya-mālin)"
**T2:** "O wearer of the cast-off garland (nirmālya)"
**Hard core:** the garland-image — the yogi of the cast-off.
**Divergence:** T1's "stripped" vs T2's "cast-off" + the vocative.
**Adjudication:** **T2's "cast-off (nirmālya)" adopted** — the nirmālya (the discarded offering-garland) is the standard term; the yogi wears what the rite cast off — the Kaula's consecration-through-the-discarded. T1's "stripped" was the mis-split.
**Commentary:** the nirmālya is the paṭala's impure-as-pure — the same inversion as the śmaśāna/body-geography; the report's Kaula-dossier (the body as the rite's locus).

**3/86 - ekākīmatithiryastu bhramite pravadet sadā**
**T1:** "he who would ever speak [as] the guest while wandering"
**T2:** "he who, wandering, would ever speak himself a guest"
**Hard core:** "wandering... speak [himself] a guest" — identical.
**Divergence:** the order + the reflexive (T2's "himself").
**Adjudication:** T1, with T2's "himself" as the gloss — the atithi is the wanderer's self-image; the verse's own gloss ("asaṃhāyo bhramate dhīraḥ") makes the ekākī the companionless.
**Commentary:** the guest-lone is the social form of paṭala 2's supportless (nirāśraya) — the āśraya-negation made social; collect the social-images (lone, guest, naked).

**3/87 - ekākī tu samākhyātā śāstraṃ milakārikā**
**T1:** "the lone one is proclaimed - the treatise, the union-kārikā (?)"

**Divergence:** the compound: T1's [X]-"milakārikā" (union) vs T2's mūlakārikā (root).

**Commentary:** the close names the text (the root-kārikā) and its teaching (the caryā) — the genre-consciousness to note in C1.
