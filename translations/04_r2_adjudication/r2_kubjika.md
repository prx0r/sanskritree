# Kubjikātantra — R2 (the full-reasoning-chain adjudications)

*The R2 as commentary. No English anchor — the chains lean on the internal network (the paṭala-connections, the glossary, the batch's cross-texts). R1: `p2_kubjika.md`.*

## 1.1 — nandīśa
**Form:** the vocative in the epithet-chain. **Grammar:** nandi(n)-īśa — "lord of Nandin" (the bull) or nandī-īśa — "lord of bliss."
**The chain:** the text's own bliss-register (nityānanda, paramānanda — 1.8, 1.10); the Kaula invocation's register (the bliss-epithets over the purāṇic); the KJN's Nandīśa (22/2 — a lineage-name). The Muktabodha-epigraph's Nandi (the gatekeeper) is the purāṇic layer — a different world.
**Verdict:** "lord of bliss" primary; the Nandin-reading noted.
**T3-reading:** "O lord of bliss, sovereign of all creatures, O Uncoloured One..."

## 1.14 — bhāvaśuddhi (the P3-find)
**Form:** kevalaṃ bhāvaśuddhyā — the instrumental. **Grammar:** bhāva = "being" (the generic) or "the state" (the technical — the text's own bhāva-triple).
**The chain:** paṭala 7's bhāva-triple (divya/vīra/paśu — the soteriological STATES) is the text's own definition of bhāva; 1.14's condition ("only by the purity of the state") is then the same doctrine in seed-form — the paṭala 7 IS the expansion of this verse. The cross-text: the KJN's bhāva-usage (the devotional states, 22/7's "kulabhāvaparāḥ").
**Verdict:** "the state" primary (the paṭala-7 connection); "being" noted.
**T3-reading:** "but by the clarity of the state alone is the mantra's goal reached, O beloved of my life."

## 2.1 — mantrārtha
**Form:** the paṭala's title-word. **Grammar:** artha — meaning AND aim.
**The chain:** paṭala 2 IS the meaning-analysis (the krīṃ-decomposition) — the semantic face is the content; but the frame (1.12's mantrārtha as the siddhi-condition) gives the goal-face. The double is the doctrine: the meaning IS the goal.
**Verdict:** "the meaning" primary; "the goal" as the note.
**T3-reading:** T1's, with the goal-face noted.

## 6.67 — yonimudrā, the climax
**Form:** the delivery-formula (iti te kathitā... yonimudrā). **Grammar:** the term as the technical vs the rendered.
**The chain:** the term's arc (promised at 1.12, delivered here as the cakra-yoga) is the text's ring; the "Womb-Seal" (yoni = the womb-source — the Ciñcinī-triangle note, Dyczkowski vol. 2) is the meaningful rendering at the climax; the glossary's transliteration is the consistency-rule.
**Verdict:** the transliteration (the glossary) with the Womb-Seal as the climax-note.
**T3-reading:** T1's, with the Womb-Seal note.

## 9.15 — nirañjanapada
**Form:** the compound. **Grammar:** the genitive ("the station of the Stainless") vs the apposition ("the Stainless-station").
**The chain:** the ring (1.2's question → 9.15's answer) makes the compound the goal-name; the Akulavīra's nirañjana (the state-identity) supports the apposition; the TĀ-gloss (the Uncoloured) is the sense.
**Verdict:** the apposition; "the Uncoloured-station" as the gloss.
**T3-reading:** "having reached the formless, he gains the Uncoloured-station."

## 10.1 — the medical register
**Form:** vandhyāyāś ca uttamaṃ vidhim. **Grammar:** vandhyā — "barren"; vidhi — "rite/procedure."
**The chain:** the paṭala is gynecological-medical (the bādhaka-classifications, the herbal recipes, the tola-measures) — "procedure" fits the genre; "barren" (the text's bluntness) kept.
**Verdict:** "the supreme procedure for the barren woman."
**T3-reading:** T2's procedure + T1's barren.

## 14 — mānasapūjā
**Form:** the paṭala's title. **The chain:** the paṭala's content (the fifteen virtue-flowers as the offerings) IS the inner offering; the technical mānasapūjā is the paddhati-literature's standard (the KJN 3/24-27's parallel flower-list).
**Verdict:** the technical (T1); the inner-offering as the gloss.
**T3-reading:** T1's.
