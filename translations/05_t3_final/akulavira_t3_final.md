**Maṅgala** — Homage to the blessed Candra-pādas. I bow to Mīnanātha — the innate bliss born from his own body, the profound support of all, unmoving, pervasive, supreme — released from the impurity of māyā. [notes: aṅga = the body (the retinue-reading secondary); māyā-mala readable as the dvandva (māyā and impurity); the Nātha ādhāra-reading (the cakras) as the tradition-internal alternative]

**1** — Now I will declare the Akulavīra, the great being — more secret than the secret, the secret of secrets: the lineage of the siddhas' true being. [the text-as-the-lineage's-reality noted]

**2** — For the world's grace the Lord of the Siddhas spoke it — you who desire the eternal state must guard it with effort. [the direct address adopted]

**3–4** — The great refuge of beings sunk in the ocean of saṃsāra: as all rivers and streams come together into the sea, so in the Akulavīra all dharmas go to dissolution. The support of all — of the entire world — he is ever the Lord. [the adepts-reading of akulavīreṣu (the class of the realized) noted as a live alternative]

**5** — Those who run after all the dharmas never find the bliss of the innate; the unknowing, seized by the impurities, are covered by the blindfold of the great illusion.

**6** — Fed on the net of the śāstras, deluded, they do not find the tranquil state — the partless guru of the Kaulas. [OPEN: the adjective-reading ("the partless, the weighty") — the two converge: the state IS the guru-principle]

**7–8** — Whatever schools wrangle — the Nyāya and Vaiśeṣika [logicians], the Buddhists and the Arhats, the Somasiddhānta-vādins; Mīmāṃsā and the Five Streams, the left and the right Siddhāntas, the histories and the Purāṇas, the element-doctrine and the Gāruḍa —

**9** — with all these Śaiva āgamas, indirect and bound to ritual: their construct-laden attainment and their roaming — all of it is itself pierced by the bonds of sin. [CORRECTED per R2: the passive reading — the schools' knowledge does not cut the bonds, it is the bonds]

**10** — Construct-heavy, all these are false doctrines, worthless — they release no one from saṃsāra, being devoid of the Akulavīra.

**11** — Resorting to the all-knowing, the all, whose mark is the good of all — there stands the siddhi of all, and there all attainments, indeed. [the All-Siddhi name-reading noted]

**12** — Where that Akulavīra is seen, facing all ways — knowing that supreme form, the mind goes to stillness.

**13** — Sound, form, taste, touch, and the fifth, smell — all beings are dissolved there, gone to dissolution.

**14** — Free of being and non-being, without rising and setting — the tranquil [state] held as the own-nature-thought, whose mind is made of mind. [RESOLVED per R2: the print read as svabhāva-mati-mataṃ ("held/known as") — no emendation; print-check pending]

**15** — It is known as the Akulavīra — the support of all, beyond the beyond; it is not to be found in the divisions of the supports and marks, nor in the domain of nāda. [OPEN: the recitation-reading (paṭhet = "recite" — the ritual-prohibition) as the commentary's alternative]

**16** — Not in the heart, not in the mouth's place, not in the uvula or the palatal aperture; not iḍā, not piṅgalā — the tranquil is not there, in their coming and going. [CORRECTED per R2: gamāgame = "in coming and going" (the KJN 14/136 parallel) — not "āgama and its negation"]

**17** — Not in the navel-cakra or the throat, not in the head, not at all in the skull; nor in the opening of the eyes, nor in gazing at the tip of the nose;

**18** — not in the in-breath, the retention, nor again the out-breath; not in the piercing of the bindu, not in the knots, not in the forehead, not in the fire.
