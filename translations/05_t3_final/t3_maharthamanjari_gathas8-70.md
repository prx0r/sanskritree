## The heart-pulse and the prakāśa-lamp (8–12)

**g.8** — paryAlochanavimukhe vastusvabhAvasyAtmano hR^idaye | sha~NkAviShavegeneva saMsArabhayena muhyati lokaH ||
> The world — its own-nature's object not yet examined — in the self's heart is dazed by the dread of saṃsāra, as by the venom of doubt.

**g.9** — mANikyapraveka iva nicholito nijamayUkhalekhayA | pratibhAti laukikAnAmatyantasphuTo.apyasphuTa AtmA ||
> Like a peerless ruby wrapped in its own filaments of light: the self appears to the worldly as utterly-manifest-yet-unmanifest.

**g.10** — Urdhvo jvalati prakAsho lokAlokasya ma~NgalapradIpaH | vimarshadashAmukhAnItadahyamAnamalAlitailavichChardaH ||
> Upward the lamp-of-auspiciousness of the seen-and-unseen blazes — its flow of sense-oil, drawn into the ten-faces of reflexion, consumed [as] fuel. [ADJUDICATED: the sense-oil + the ten-faces — the lamp burns the world into light; the ten = the Krama's cognition-ten]

**g.11** — sannaiva hR^idayaprakAsho bhavanakriyAyA bhavati kartA | saiva kriyAvimarshaH svasthA kShubhitA cha vishvavistAraH ||
> The heart's light, just by being, is the agent of the becoming-deed; the very action-reflexion — at ease, the world's widening once stirred.

**g.12** — pR^ithvIparamashivayoH pratyAhAre prakAshaparamArthe | yo.anyonyavisheShaH sa eva hR^idayasya vimarshonmeShaH ||
> In the withdrawal of earth and the supreme Śiva — the light's own essence — that which is the mutual differentia: that is the heart's reflexive upsurge. [ADJUDICATED: the tattva-span + the mutual-differentia]

## The powers (13–18)

**g.13** — tathA tathA dR^ishyamAnAnAM shaktisahasrANAmekasa~NghaTTaH | nijahR^idayodyamarUpo bhavati shivo nAma paramasvachChandaH ||
> Thus, of the thousand powers as they show themselves, the single confluence — become the figure of the heart's own upsurge — is what's named Śiva, the supreme spontaneity. [ADJUDICATED: spontaneity — the report's svātantrya-dossier]

**g.14** — sa eva vishvamIkShituM sthAtuM kartuM chonmukho bhavan | shaktisvabhAvaH kathito hR^idayatrikoNamadhumAsalollAsaH ||
> He, becoming poised to see, to stay, to act on the world — the power-natured — is spoken as the honey-flood of the heart's triangle.

**g.15** — j~nAnaM kriyeti dvayorapi prathamonmeShe sadAshivo devaH | dvitIyAyA ullekhe dvitIyaH sa bhavatIshvaro nAma ||
> Knowledge and action — of both, at the first unsealing, the god is Sadāśiva; at the second's sketch, he turns the second, named the Lord. [ADJUDICATED: the unsealing/sketch — the powers' tattva-ladder]

**g.16** — j~nAtA sa AtmA j~neyasvabhAvashcha lokavyavahAraH | ekarasAM saMsR^iShTi yatra gatau sA khalu nistuShA vidyA ||
> He, the self, is the knower; the world's commerce is of the known's grain; where the going is the one-flavored mingling — that indeed is the huskless lore.

**g.17** — ekarase svabhAve udbhAvayantI vikalpashilpAni | mAyeti lokapateH paramasvatantrasya mohanI shaktiH ||
> In the one-flavored nature, causing the crafts of division to well up — she is called māyā, the bewildering might of the world's lord, the utterly free. [hard-core: the free one's own power, not an external illusion]

**g.18** — sarvakaraH sarvaj~naH pUrNo nityo.asa~Nkuchitashcha | viparIta iva mahesho yAbhistA bhavanti pa~nchashaktayaH ||
> The all-worker, all-knower, full, eternal, unsqueezed — the great Lord, as though inverted: by which [powers] those five powers come to be.

## The karaṇas (21–25)

**g.21** — kallolAyamAnAni sadA hR^idayAmbunidhau trINi karaNAni | AkarShantIdantAM tatrAhantAM chAtrArpayanti ||
> Waving forever in the heart's sea, the three instruments pull the this-wise toward them and there hand it to the I-wise. [hard-core: the this→I — the report's A→B in the Krama's instrument-language]

**g.22** — hR^idayasthitasya vibhorviShayAloko vishR^i~Nkhalo bhavati | j~nAnendriyadIpeShu nijanijagolAgranityalagneShu ||
> For the lord at the heart, the world's light runs unchained, its lamps of sense ever-caught at the tips of their own spheres.

**g.23** — bhavanti karaNAni pa~ncha khalu karmapradhAnAni lokanAthasya | spandate svaira yairjano jaDAdvilakShaNo bhavan ||
> The five instruments are the world-lord's deed-principals — by whose free ranging the man, growing unlike the inert, pulses (spanda).

**g.24** — vishvodyAnavirUDhAni gandhapramukhAni sugandhIni puShpANi | pa~nchApyAjighran krIDati trailokyadhUrto devaH ||
> The five sweet flowers grown in the world's garden — scent and the rest — sniffing all of them, the three-worlds' rover god plays.

**g.25** — styAnasya kramavashAdikShurasasyeva shivaprakAshasya | guDapiNDA iva pa~nchApi bhUtAni madhuratAM na mu~nchanti ||
> By the sequence of the settling, as with the cane-juice: the five elements, like lumps of raw sugar, never let go their sweetness — such is the Śiva-light.

## The yāmala and the māyā (26–32)

**g.26** — sarvasya bhuvanavibhramayantrollAsasya tantuvallIva | vimarshasaMrambhamayI ujjR^imbhate shambhormahAshaktiH ||
> As a thread-vine [strings] the whirling upsurge of all the worlds: the reflexion-fervor-bodied great might of Śambhu unfolds.

**g.27** — yadadhvanAM cha ShaTkaM tatra prakAshArthalakShaNamardham | vimarshashabdasvabhAvamardhamiti shivasya yAmalollAsaH ||
> The sixfold of the courses: there the half bears the stamp of the light's sense; the half the grain of the reflexion-word — so the paired surge of Śiva. [hard-core: the yāmala — the same pair as the TĀ 1.1's]

**g.28** — AlekhyavisheSha iva gajavR^iShabhayordvayaH pratibhAsaH | ekasminnevArthe shivashaktivibhAgakalpanAM kurmaH ||
> As in a picture the double-show of elephant and bull: in the single object we frame the Śiva-Śakti split.

**g.29** — tilamAtre.api sharIre prekShadhvaM kITasyaitAvatI shaktiH | sA svachChandashriyo vishvasharIrasya kiyatI bhavatu ||
> In a body no bigger than a sesame seed — look — such the might of a worm: how vast, then, the world-body's, the fortune of the free?

**g.30** — vishvonmeShadashAyAM deshikanAthasya yAvAnprasaraH | kalalAvasthayAM sthito.api vishvanimeShe.api tAvAnbhavati ||
> In the state of the world's unsealing, whatever the teacher-lord's spread — even lodged in the embryo-state, at the world's closing, he is as much.

**g.31** — tripuTImayaM khalu samastaM tatra cha j~neye j~nAtari cha samam | dR^iDhagranthirj~nAnakalA kalayati trailokyamekakalam ||
> All is woven of the triple — there, in the known and the knower, the same: the tight-knotted knowing-grain makes the three worlds one grain.

**g.32** — kaH sadbhAvavisheShaH kusumAdbhavati gaganakusumasya | yatsphuraNAnuprANo lokaH sphuraNaM cha sarvasAmAnyam ||
> What real difference springs of the sky-flower from the flower? — [they differ] in that whose flashing quickens the world: and the flashing is the common lot of all. [hard-core: the common sphuraṇa — the report's A-thesis]

## The pīṭha-arithmetic (33–41)

**g.34** — aNDamaye nijapiNDapIThe sphuranti karaNadevyaH | prasphurati cha paramashivo j~nAnanidhishcha tAsAM madhye ||
> In the egg-made [world], on the body's own seat, the instrument-goddesses flash; and amid them the supreme Śiva flashes, the hoard of knowing.

**g.36** — shrIpIThapa~nchavAho netratrayavR^indachakrANi smarata | smarata cha gurUNAM pa~Nkti pa~nca cha shaktIH sR^iShTipramukhAH ||
> Recall the blessed seat's five currents, the wheel-clusters of the triple eye; recall the gurus' file and the five powers, creation at their head.

**g.39** — sR^iShTau dasha kalAH sthitau dvAvishatirbhavanti shaktayaH | ekAdasha saMhAre trayodasha tAsturIyaparvaNi ||
> In creation, ten grains; in stay, two-and-twenty powers; eleven in the draw; thirteen at the fourth's turn. [OPEN: the counts' verification against the full Parimala]

**g.40** — bhAsAyAM na vikalpAH sphurati sphuradekaniShkalashriyAm | yadi pratibimbagatyA sphurati paraM ShoDashAdhikA devI ||
> In the shimmering there are no splits — she flashes in the flashing glory of the single-partless; if she flashes by the mirror-way, the goddess is the sixteen-plus.

## The rites and the vāc (42–50)

**g.43** — vimraShTuM nijasattvaM vibhave kAryonmukhe stimite.api | bAhyavR^ittAntAnAM bha~NgaH prANasya saMyamo j~neyaH ||
> To mull over one's own being in the majesty, even when the work-looms stand still: the breaking of the outer rounds — that is the breath's restraint.

**g.45** — avikalpatayA vimarsho vikalpavargasyA~NgasannAhaH | arghyaM vedyavilAsaH puShpANi svabhAvapoShakAH bhAvAH ||
> Reflexion [comes] by the non-split; the armor of the split-brigades; the oblation, the display of the known; the blooms, the states that feed the own-nature.

**g.46** — pUrNAhantAyA mukhe vishvavikalpA~NkurANAM vikShepam | mantrollekhavishuddhaM puNyaM kulabindutarpaNaM bhaNAmaH ||
> At the mouth of the full-I, the scattering of the world's split-sprouts: we speak the mantra-sketch's pure merit, the libation at the Kula's point. [ADJUDICATED: the kulabindu — the glossary's bindu in the Krama's libation]

**g.49** — mananamayI nijavibhave nijasa~Nkochabhaye trANamayI | kavalitavishvavikalpA anubhUtiH kApi mantrashabdArthaH ||
> Woven of thought in one's own might, woven of refuge in the fear of one's own shrink; having gulped the world's splits — some such feel is the mantra-word's sense.

**g.50** — vaikhari(kA) nAma kriyA j~nAnamayI bhavati madhyamA vAk | ichChA punaH pashyantI sUkShmA sarvAsA samarasA vR^ittiH ||
> The vaikharī is the deed; the mid-speech turns knowledge-bodied; the will again is the seer-speech, the subtle, the one-flavored working of them all. [hard-core: the vāc-ladder — the four-speech mapped onto the powers]

## The yogi's states and the close (51–71)

**g.51** — AnandollAsashrIH kShullakitAShTamahAsiddhisaubhAgyA | dR^ishyate yatra dashAyAM saiva devasya sarva(mahA)mudrAH ||
> The bloom of bliss's fortune, tricked with the luck of the eight great attainments — in whatever state she shows, that is the god's all-seals.

**g.53** — kramiko bhavati na devastasya kathaM kAlakalmaShasparshaH | nityanirAvaraNasyApi ko.api jIvanmuktipratyUhaH ||
> The god has no serial order — how could the smear of time touch him, the ever-unveiled? What clog to the living one's release could there be? [ADJUDICATED: serial order — the Krama's own term turned against the temporal]

**g.61** — yogI jAgarasvapnasuShuptyavasthAtrayasya paripATim | chitrAmiva maNimAlAM vimarshasUtraikagumphitAmudvahati ||
> The yogi wears the series of the three states — waking, dream, deep sleep — as a painted string of pearls, threaded on the single cord of reflexion. [hard-core: the pearl-garland of the states]

**g.65** — aho saMsArasukhakelI aho sulabhaM mokShamArge saubhAgyam | truTitAta~Nkakala~NkA aho shivayoginAM yAmalI siddhiH ||
> Ah, the sport of the world's joy! Ah, the easy fortune on the release-road! Ah, the paired attainment of the Śiva-yogis, its fret-stain shattered!

**g.68** — AstAmanyA vidyAshchatuHsrotasAmapi sAgarANAmiva | enamevAmR^itamayaM mathnAti manthAnabhairavo devaH ||
> Let the other lores be — as the seas of the four streams: this nectar-bodied one alone the Churning-Bhairava god churns. [ADJUDICATED: the name kept — the Krama's Kubjikā-bridge, the Dyczkowski anchor verifies]

**g.70** — enameva mahArthaM yuddhArambhe pANDuputrasya | ShoDashasahasrashaktirdeva upadishati sma mAdhavaH ||
> This very great-meaning, at the opening of the Pāṇḍu-son's war, the god of the sixteen-thousand powers, Mādhava, once taught. [hard-core: the Gītā-frame]

**g.71 (the closing praise)** — itthaM prAkR^itasUtrasaptatisamullAsaikasandhAyinIM jAgrattatkShaNanirvisheShasvapnAvatIrNAM pratij~nottarAm | lokolla~NghanayogyasiddhipadavIprasthAnabaddhodyamAM kanthAshUlakapAlamAtravibhavAM vande tAM yoginIm ||
> Thus — the one who unites the seventy mundane-sūtra-upsurges into one, descended from waking, that instant, the non-distinct dream; the answer to the promise; bound to the enterprise of the siddhi-path's going, fit to overleap the world; whose wealth is only the throat-bone and the skull — I praise that yoginī.
