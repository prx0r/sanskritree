# Śivasūtra — T3-FINAL (the adjudicated verses)

*The cherry on top: the R2-resolved finals. The remaining 68 sūtras stand at T1 (their R1-flags carried); the full T3 (all 78) is the mechanical completion of this adjudication.*

**1.1** — Consciousness is the Self. *(note: one's own essential nature, per the Vārttika's gloss)*
**1.2** — Knowledge is bondage. *(the I-and-mine cognition, per the Vārttika)*
**1.7** — In the distinction of waking, dream and deep sleep [is] the consciousness of the Fourth's experience. *(the expanse/savoring fork: ābhoga vs bhoga)*
**1.10** — Lack of discernment: the deep sleep that is māyā.
**1.13** — The maiden is the supremely-willed power (icchāśaktitamā kumārī). *(the umā-variant: "the power of will is Umā, the maiden" — the Abhinava-tradition reading; the ku-mārayati gloss)*
**3.19** — In the ka-varga and the rest, Māheśvarī and the others — the mothers who bind the bound.
**3.40** — From longing, the going-out of the conveyed one. *(the vehicle-image in the commentary)*
**3.44** — By the restraint at the nose's inner midpoint — what need of the left and right channels or suṣumnā?
