## The maṅgala

**m.1** — namo nālayate śuṇḍāṃ viṣāṇena mṛṇāline | pratyakkamalakandāya karṇābhyāṃ parṇaśāline ||
> Homage to the one who toys with his trunk, tusk against the lotus-stalk — the inner lotus-root, ear-leafed [Gaṇeśa]. [ADJUDICATED: the tusk (viṣāṇa) + the trunk (śuṇḍā) — the doubled-play]

**m.2** — jayatyamūlamamlānamauttaraṃ tattvamadvayam | spandāspandaparispandamakarandamahotpalam ||
> Triumphs the principle — rootless, unwithered, supreme, non-dual: the great lotus of the nectar that is the pulse, the pulse's rest, and the pulse's outpouring. [ADJUDICATED: the cognition-rhythm — the Krama-perspective's central correction; the Parimala's frame]

**m.3** — kāruṇyāmṛtasindhoruditamivāvartamīkṣaṇāpāṅgāt | mauktikamayaṃ dadhānā tāṭaṅkaṃ jayati gauravī mūrtiḥ ||
> May the weighty form triumph, bearing the pearl-wrought earring, risen as it were from the whirlpool of the compassion-nectar sea, from the corner of her glance.

**m.4** — sphūrtaye viśvaśilpasya śrīśivānandamūrtaye | nityonmeṣanimeṣāyai nistuṣāyai namastviṣe ||
> Homage to the radiance — the Śivānanda-form — for the flashing of the world's craft: the ever-opened-and-shut, the huskless [one]. [the opening-closing as the cognition's own pulse]

**m.5** — yasmādanuttaramahāhradamajjanaṃ me saubhāgyaśāmbhavasukhānubhavaśca yasmāt | tatsvātmacitkramavimarśamayaṃ gurūṇā-mavalliyugmamuditoditavīryamīḍe ||
> From which my plunge into the unsurpassed great pool, and from which the auspicious Śāmbhava-joy — that pair of the gurus' feet, made of the self's consciousness-order-reflexion (svātma-cit-krama-vimarśa), its risen power risen — I praise. [ADJUDICATED: the guru-feet ARE the cognition-sequence's reflexion — the guru-as-krama]

**m.6** — namo nikhilamālinyavilāpanapaṭīyase | mahāprakāśapādābjaparāgaparamāṇave ||
> Homage to the one skilled in dissolving the whole stain — to the supreme atom of the pollen of the great-prakāśa's lotus-feet.

**m.7** — gorakṣo lokadhiyā deśikadṛṣṭyā maheśvarānandaḥ | unmīlayāmi parimalamantargrāhyaṃ mahārthamañjaryām ||
> I — Maheśvarānanda, a Gorakṣa to the world's eye, a teacher in the guru's view — open the Parimala, the inwardly-grasped [commentary] on the Mahārthamañjarī.

**m.8** — svakriyāyā api vyākhyāṃ svayameva prayuñjmahe | uparyapyātmasaṃrambhasambhogāmreḍanotsukāḥ ||
> For my own work too I myself supply the gloss — eager, above it, for the reiteration of the enjoyment of my own fervor.

**m.9** — yadvā vineyajanacittacamatkriyārtha-matrodyamo'yamudito'stu tadetadāstām | saṃkṣepavistaravibhāgaviviktaśobhaḥ puṣpāñjalirbhavatu vāṅmaya eṣa śambhoḥ ||
> Or else — for the wonder of the teachable minds, may this endeavor arise; let that be. May this verbal flower-offering of Śambhu, its beauty parted into brief-and-extended, stand [accepted].

**m.10** — avagataśivadṛṣṭipratyabhijñārthatattva-kramasaraṇirahasyollāsasarvasvavedī | gurucaraṇasaparyācāturīcidghano'haṃ gahanamapi hṛdantarvyoma tad vyākaromi ||
> I, dense with the skill of serving the guru's feet, who know the whole secret-springing of the Krama-path's truth — the meaning of the Śivadṛṣṭi and the Recognition understood — unfold even the dense inner-sky of the heart. [ADJUDICATED: the Recognition-yoked Krama — the report's control-corpus design as the text's own claim]

**m.11** — iha mahati rahasyonmīlane maṅgalāya prabhavati mama saṃvidyoginīnāṃ prasādaḥ | api tu kulasaparyābimbasaṃbandhyavandhyāḥ sakṛdapi matimanto nainamudghāṭayantu ||
> Here, at the great unsealing of the secret, for the auspicious [rite] the grace of my awareness-yoginīs prevails; but the intelligent — barren, tied to the reflection (bimba) of the kula-worship — let them not open this, even once. [the ritualists who stop at the reflection, not the cognition it mirrors]

**m.12** — svapnasamayopalabdhā sā sumukhī siddhayoginī devī | gāthābhiḥ saptatyā svāpitabhāṣābhirastu samprītā ||
> May that fair-faced goddess — the accomplished yoginī, won at the time of dream — be pleased with the seventy gāthās in her own-awakened tongue.

**m.13** — vardhatāṃ deśikaḥ śrīmān saṃvinmārgaśca vardhatām | māheśvarāśca vardhantāṃ vardhatāṃ ca maheśvaraḥ ||
> May the blessed teacher flourish; may the path-of-awareness flourish; may the Māheśvaras flourish; and may Maheśvara flourish.

## The doctrinal opening (the Krama's self-positioning)

**g.1** — natvā nityashuddhau guroshcharaNau mahAprakAshasya | grathnAti mahArthama~njarImimAM surabhiM maheshvarAnandaH ||
> Bowing to the ever-pure feet of the great-prakāśa guru, Maheśvarānanda strings this fragrant cluster of the great-meaning.

**g.2** — vardhatAM mahAprakAsho vimarshavichChuritanishChalodyotaH | sa.nj~nAvisheShanirNayamAtrapravR^ittAni yatra shAstrANi ||
> May the great-prakāśa flourish — the unmoving upsurge suffused with reflexion — [he] in whom the śāstras, occupied only with resolving named-distinctions, [find their end]. [ADJUDICATED: the Krama's engine-verse — the report's prakāśa/vimarśa pair, the A-thesis in the Krama's voice]

**g.3** — AtmA khalu vishvamUlaM tatra pramANaM na ko.apyarthayate | kasya vA bhavati pipAsA ga~NgAsrotasi nimagnasya ||
> The self, indeed, is the root of the world — there no proof is demanded by anyone; what thirst could arise in one plunged in the Gaṅgā's stream? [the reflexive presence needs no external proof]

**g.4** — yaM jAnanti jaDA api jalahAryo.api yaM vijAnanti | yasyaiva namaskAraH sa kasya sphuTo na bhavati kulanAthaH ||
> Whom even the inert know, whom even the water-carriers perceive — he to whom the homage is [paid] — of whom is he not the manifest lord of the kula (kulanātha)?

**g.5** — avachChinnaM pratyakShamavachChinnaM tanmukhaM chAnumAnam | AgamadIpAlokastasya prakAshayati kimapi mAhAtmyam ||
> Perception is circumscribed; the inference that faces it is circumscribed — the āgama's lamp-light illumines some majesty of it. [the bounded-perspectivity: only the āgama's light shows the supreme]

**g.6** — yeShAM nirupaNIyo vyatiriktaH ko.apyAtmano bhAvaH | AtmavimukhAnAM teShAmadhikArivibhAgavibhramo bhavatu ||
> For those whose essence is an other — some state separate from the self — for the self-averted, may the confusion of the qualified-parts [be theirs].

**g.7** — yatra ruchistatra vidhiryatreyaM nAsti tatra cha niShedhaH | ityasmAkaM viveko hR^idayaparispandamAtrashAstrANAm ||
> Where there is delight, there the rite; where this is not, there the prohibition — such is our discernment, whose śāstra is only the heart's pulse (hṛdaya-parispanda). [ADJUDICATED: the Krama's rule — the rite follows the heart's spanda, not external law; the concordance confirms the signature phrase]

## T3 apparatus

The Parimala (the text's own commentary) is the referee — these are the Krama's self-understanding, not an imported Trika frame.
- **The report's dossiers in play:** spanda (the pulse, not mechanical), prakāśa/vimarśa (the A-thesis), saṃvit-krama (the sequence-cognition), the Recognition-yoke (the control-corpus design), the kula-lord (the Kaula title in the Krama's voice).
