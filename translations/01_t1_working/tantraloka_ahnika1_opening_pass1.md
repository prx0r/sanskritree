## The maṅgala — the anuttara-cosmology

**1/1** — vimalakalāśrayābhinavasṛṣṭimahā jananī bharitatanuśca pañcamukhaguptarucirjanakaḥ | tadubhayayāmalasphuritabhāvavisargamayaṃ hṛdayamanuttarāmṛtakulaṃ mama sasphuratāt ||
> May the great mother, whose fresh creation rests in the pure kalā, and the father, the five-faced one of hidden radiance with the full body — [and] the heart, made of the emission of the being that flashes in the yāmala of the two — the unsurpassed nectar-kula — shine forth for me.
> *[The report's flagged verse: anuttara + kula + visarga + the yāmala (the pair) in one compressed cosmology. Dyczkowski-rendering noted; the Kula/akula polarity active.]*

**1/2** — naumi citpratibhāṃ devīṃ parāṃ bhairavayoginīm | mātṛmānaprameyāṃśaśūlāmbujakṛtāspadām ||
> I bow to the goddess, the supreme reflexive consciousness (cit-pratibhā), the Bhairava-yoginī, whose seat is made of the trident and the lotus of the Mother, the measure, and the measured —

**1/3** — naumi devīṃ śarīrasthāṃ nṛtyato bhairavākṛte | prāvṛṇmeghaghanavyomavidyullekhāvilāsinīm ||
> — I bow to the goddess dwelling in the body, the radiant one of the Bhairava's form as he dances, flashing like the lightning's streak in the dense sky of the monsoon-clouds.

**1/4** — dīptajyotiśchaṭāpluṣṭabhedabandhatrayaṃ sphurat | stājjñānaśūlaṃ satpakṣavipakṣotkartanakṣamam ||
> The blazing light's radiance sears the threefold of the divided and the bound; [that] knowledge-trident flashes, able to cut away the friends' side and the foes'.

**1/5** — svātantryaśaktiḥ kramasaṃsisṛkṣā kramātmatā ceti vibhorvibhūtiḥ | tadeva devītrayamantarāstāmanuttaraṃ me prathayatsvarūpam ||
> The power of freedom (svātantryaśakti), the will-to-create in order (krama-saṃsisṛkṣā), and the order-nature (kramātmatā) — this is the Lord's majesty. That very triad of goddesses, within [all of which] stands the Unsurpassed — let it manifest my own nature.
> *[The report's flagged krama + svātantrya + anuttara cluster — the three powers (icchā/jñāna/kriyā or the report's reading) and the Unsurpassed as the ground. The "krama" here is the ordered unfolding, per the report's krama-dossier.]*

**1/6** — taddevatāvibhavabhāvimahāmarīcicakreśvarāyitanijasthitir eka eva | devīsuto gaṇapatiḥ sphuradindukāntiḥ samyaksamucchalayatānmama saṃvidabdhim ||
> The one whose own standing is the lordship over the great circle of rays latent in the gods' majesty of that [Unsurpassed] — he alone, the son of the goddess, Gaṇapati, with the gleaming moon-lustre, may he rightly swell the ocean of my awareness.

**1/7** — rāgāruṇāṃ granthibilāvakīrṇa yo jālamātānavitānavṛtti | kalombhitaṃ bāhyapathe cakāra stānme sa macchandavibhuḥ prasannaḥ ||
> [The Lord,] free and sovereign, pleased, who made the red-hued one, scattered in the womb of the scripture, whose net-like mother extends its activity, fettered (kalom-bhita) on the external path — may he make [it] mine.

## The guru-lineage (the report's flagged chain)

**1/8** — traiyambakābhihitasantatitāmraparṇīsanmauktikaprakarakāntiviśeṣabhājaḥ | pūrve jayanti guravo guruśāstrasindhukallolakelikalanāmalakarṇadhārāḥ ||
> May the earlier gurus triumph, who bear the special lustre of the true pearls of the Tāmraparṇī, the lineage declared by the Traiyambaka[-school] — the helmsmen of the play of the waves of the guru-śāstra-ocean.

**1/9** — jayati gurureka eva śrīśrīkaṇṭho bhuvi prathitaḥ | tadaparamūrtirbhagavān maheśvaro bhūtirājaśca ||
> The one guru alone triumphs, the blessed Śrīkaṇṭha, famous on earth; and the other form of him, the blessed Maheśvara, the king of being (bhūtirāja).

**1/10** — śrīsomānandabodhaśrīmadutpalaviniḥsṛtāḥ | jayanti saṃvidāmodasandarbhā dikprasarpiṇaḥ ||
> May [the works] issuing from the blessed Somānanda's awakening and the blessed Utpala triumph — the compositions of the awareness's fragrance, spreading through the directions.
> *[The report's flagged verse: Somānanda + Utpala named — the Pratyabhijñā-lineage as the metaphysical ground.]*

**1/11** — tadāsvādabharāveśabṛṃhitāṃ matiṣaṭpadīm | gurorlakṣmaṇaguptasya nādasaṃmohinīṃ numaḥ ||
> We bow to the sixth-part mind (mati-ṣaḍpadī) of the guru Lakṣmaṇagupta — swollen with the absorption of the surge of that taste — the deluding-by-nāda.

**1/12** — yaḥ pūrṇānandaviśrāntasarvaśāstrārthapāragaḥ | sa śrīcukhulako diśyādiṣṭaṃ me gururuttamaḥ ||
> He who, resting in the fullness of bliss, has crossed the meaning of all the śāstras — may the blessed Cukhulaka, my supreme guru, grant what is commanded to me.

## The purpose

**1/13** — jayatājjagaduddhṛtikṣamo@sau bhagavatyā saha śaṃbhunātha ekaḥ | yadudīritaśāsanāṃśubhirme prakaṭo@yaṃ gahano@pi śāstramārgaḥ ||
> May that Śambhunātha, able to raise the world, alone with the goddess, triumph — by whose uttered teaching's rays this dense śāstra-path is made manifest to me.

**1/14** — santi paddhatayaścitrāḥ srotobhedeṣu bhūyasā | anuttaraṣaḍardhārthakrame tvekāpi nekṣyate ||
> There are many fine ritual-manuals (paddhatis) among the differing currents; yet in the order of the unsurpassed six-and-a-half meanings, not even one is seen [that does justice].

**1/15** — ityahaṃ bahuśaḥ sadbhiḥ śiṣyasabrahmacāribhiḥ | arthito racaye spaṣṭāṃ pūrṇārthā prakriyāmimām ||
> Thus I, asked many times by the good, by disciples and fellow-brahmacārins, compose this clear, complete-in-meaning ritual-procedure (prakriyā).

**1/16** — śrībhaṭṭanāthacaraṇābjayugāttathā śrībhaṭṭārikāṃghriyugalādgurusantatiryā | bodhānyapāśaviṣanuttadupāsanotthabodhojjvalo@bhinavagupta idaṃ karoti ||
> From the pair of the blessed Bhaṭṭanātha's lotus-feet and the pair of the blessed Bhaṭṭārikā's feet, the guru-succession [comes]; Abhinavagupta, blazing with the awakening arisen from the worship of that, free of the other bonds' poison, makes this.

## The source-scripture (the report's flagged Mālinīvijayottara)

**1/17** — na tadastīha yanna śrī-mālinīvijayottare | devadevena nirdiṣṭaṃ svaśabdenātha liṅgataḥ ||
> There is nothing here that is not declared in the blessed Mālinīvijayottara by the god of gods — whether in its own words or by indication (liṅgataḥ).

**1/18** — daśāṣṭādaśavasvaṣṭabhinnaṃ yacchāsanaṃ vibhoḥ | tatsāraṃ trikaśāstraṃ hi tatsāraṃ mālinīmatam ||
> Whatever teaching of the Lord is divided into the ten, the eighteen, the eight, [the sixty-four] — its essence is the Trika-śāstra; its essence is the Mālinī-doctrine.

**1/19** — ato@trāntargataṃ sarva saṃpradāyojjhitairbudhaiḥ | adṛṣṭa prakaṭīkurmo gurunāthājñayā vayam ||
> Therefore — [this teaching,] all-contained in that — we make manifest, by the command of the guru-lord, what the wise, abandoned by the traditions, have not seen.
