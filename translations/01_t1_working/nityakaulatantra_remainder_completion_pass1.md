# Nityākaulatantra — T1 (the working translation), the readable remainder — Completion

*Hop 1 completion — the fragment's readable coverage extended. The opening (the āsana-frame, the visualization, the Nityā-list, the cakra-rule) is in `ten_text_batch2_pass1.md` §10; the remainder here.*

### Provenance
- **Author:** anonymous; the fragment's frame is the Bhairava-Devī dialogue (the text's own genre)
- **Text:** Nityākaulatantra (alias Nityātantra) — the pre-Śrīvidyā Nityā-rites; only one recovered manuscript (badly damaged leaves)
- **Digital source:** M00316 (Muktabodha, rev. 2014, data under Dyczkowski's direction) — **heavy asterisk-lacunae throughout; folios 4–5 missing** (the e-text marks the gap); folio-sequence 2a/2b/3a/3b/6a/7a/7b/8a/8b/9a/9b
- **Collation:** [X] no second manuscript known (Dyczkowski's note: "the only manuscript that has been recovered")
- **Anchors:** none; Dyczkowski's own e-text header is the scholarly anchor: the fragment predates the Śrīvidyā consolidation — the Nityā-pool from which Tripurāsundarī/Tripurabhairavī emerged, with the sixteen Nityās configured in the śrīcakra

### Time-place-context
- **PERIOD:** certainly prior to the 10th c. (Dyczkowski's note) — the pre-Śrīvidyā layer of the Kālīkula/Nityā-cultus
- **PLACE/PATH:** NGMCP Manuscript No. 2-226 (Reel B26/21a) — Nepalese recovery of a pre-Śrīvidyā liturgy
- **GENRE/REGISTER:** yantra/bīja-mantra liturgy + rite-procedures (the Kāma-siddhi, the yoni-mudrā, the sūtra-rites, the dīkṣā-fragment) — the terse ritual register of the Nityā-rites
- **FRAME:** the fragment's own arc: the Nityā-deity's visualization (opening) → the bīja/mahāvidyā construction (the kāma-bīja) → the rites (the subjugations, the yoni-mudrā, the sūtra-karmas) → the dīkṣā's opening. The doctrine: the Nityā (here = the goddess of the rite) is the mahāvidyā whose parts are the mātṛkā-letters

## The remainder (the readable fragments after the opening)

### The bīja-construction (the mahāvidyā's letters)
**The seed-letters** — ṭādyamādyasamāyuktaṃ bhānte na ca samanvitaṃ | cchādyoparisthitaddevi * * * * * * * *? || dha sa madhyagataṃ bījaṃ ṣa ha madhyoparisthitaṃ | yāntamūdhnaṃ mahādeviṃ viṃduyuktaṃ dvitīyakaṃ ||
> Joined with the initial ṭā (ṭa-series), not accompanied at the end [X: lacuna]... standing above the covering, O Devī... The seed in the middle of dha and sa; ṣa and ha standing above the middle: [this is] the second, joined with the bindu, ending in yānta (the ya-family), O great Devī —

**The letters** — ṣa * * * * * * * *? dyena tu vibheditaṃ | tṛtīya kathitaṃ bījaṃ caturthaṃ kādya biṃdukaṃ || na kha madhya gataṃ yacca phāntena * * * * *? | *? nta bhiṃnaṃ pha ū madhya sthita taṃ pañcamākṣaraṃ | ṣa sa madhya sthitaṃ ṣaṣṭhaṃ yānte na ca vibheditaṃ ||
> ...pierced by [the ṣa?]... the third is declared the seed; the fourth is the ka-family with the bindu. That which is not in the middle of na and kha, by the pha-ending... pierced at the end; pha and ū standing in the middle — that is the fifth syllable. ṣa and sa standing in the middle — the sixth, not pierced at the yānta-end —

**The last letters** — * * * * * * * *? cādya ikāra vatsale | aṣṭamaṃ aighma? madhya madhyasthaṃ ṛ ḷ madhyaṃ navamaṃ smṛtaṃ || pa sa madhyagataṃ gṛhyaṃ chakārā * * * *? | * * *? kathitaṃ devi dha va madhya gataṃ paraṃ || ka la madhyena saṃbhinaṃ ekādaśa mamakṣaraṃ | * * * * * * * * *? ktraṃ tridaśaḍāmaraṃ ||
> ...and the initial i-kāra, O dear one. The eighth in the middle of...; ṛ and ḷ in the middle is remembered the ninth. The seed in the middle of pa and sa; chakāra... declared, O Devī, the supreme in the middle of dha and va. Pierced by the middle of ka and la — the eleventh syllable... the tridaśa-ḍāmara.

**The mahāvidyā** — eṣā devī mahāvidyā nityākhyā ca su madhyame | ādyaṃtara hi * * * * * * * *? sādhanī || na cāsya dīpanādeyaṃ ādyaṃ tena ca dīpitaṃ | dīpanāt parame śā * * * * * * * * *? ||
> This goddess is the mahāvidyā, called the Nityā, in the good middle. The inner initial... the sādhanī [X: lacuna]. Its kindling is not to be taken [from elsewhere]; the initial is kindled by it — from the kindling, in the supreme śā[stra]... [X: lacunae]

**The Kāma-deva seed** — uddhariṣyāmyahaṃ devi kāmadevaṃ sasāyakaṃ | phāntabiṃdu samāyuktaṃ śi * * * * * * *? || * * * *? sadṛśaṃ kāmasarvāṃga saṃsthitaṃ | ṛ ḷ madhya gataṃ gṛhya kakārādyuparisthitaṃ || viṃdunā * * * * * * * * * * * *? naṃ | smarametat samākhyātā śṛṇu pārvati sāyakāṃ || sa pa madhyagataṃ ūccha madhye * * * * *? | * * * *? smarasādhyaṃ raktantadamṛtasthitaṃ ||
> I will draw up, O Devī, Kāma-deva with his arrows: joined with the phānta-bindu, with the śi... [X: lacuna]... like [Kāma], standing in all the limbs of Kāma; the seed in the middle of ṛ and ḷ, standing above the ka-family beginning; with the bindu... This is declared the smara (the memory-of-love, Kāma); hear, O Pārvatī, the arrows: the seed in the middle of sa and pa, ū in the middle... the smara-sādhya, the red one, standing in the nectar.

**The mantra's use** — evaṃ maṃtrī sadādhyāyajjapanni * * * * *? | * * * * * * * * * * *? gṛhaṇīmapi || yadicchet sādhakavaraṃ trailokyavaśamānayet | guhye * * * * * * * * * * * * *? ||
> Thus the mantrin, ever meditating, reciting... even the householder. If the best of sādhakas wishes, he brings the three worlds under his power; in secret...

**The Kāma-rites** — lāpāccandra pathagā(?) pūrva bhuktā vaśī bhavet | tasya guhya * * * * * * * * * * * *? || tvā tu hṛdaye ānītvā karṣayed valāt | saptarātre sura * * * * * * * * * *##? ||
> From the calling, [one who] walks the moon-path, previously enjoyed, becomes subjugated. His secret... bringing [her] to the heart, he draws [her] by force; in seven nights the divine [woman]... [X: lacunae]

**The kulastrīs** — * * *? nāṃ kulastrīṇāṃ antapuranivāśināṃ | nāmāntadīyaśu * * * * * * * * *? || * * * * * * *? tā tu caṃpaka prakhyā || naryāntī pūrvavaccaran | tanmadhya gataṃ dākāraṃ sa * * * * * * *? |
> ...of the kula-women dwelling in the inner apartments, [on] the syllables of their names... like the campaka [flower]. Walking as before... the dā-syllable in their middle... [X: lacunae]

**The obstacles and the flame** — * * *? stambhanantasya vivāde vighnameva ca | kāryāraṃbhe tathā śainya stambhane garbhasaṃtatau | sarvasta * * * * * * * *? krameva vā || kṛṣṇabhṛṅga pratīkāsaṃ pūrvavacchāṅkarī kalāṃ | cintyeta madhyagaṃ śādhyaṃ tadādhāraṃ vicintayet ||
> ...paralyzing, and obstruction in dispute; at a work's beginning, in cooling, in paralysis, in the lineage of the womb — all... in order. Like the black bee, as before, the Śāṅkarī kalā: the sādhya in the middle should be thought, its support contemplated.

### The rites (folio 6a on)
**The cakra-karma** — *? ccakraṃ mahābhāge cchinde marmāntarāṇi tu | tat kṛtvā khaṇḍakhaṇḍāni mārayatyavisaṃkitaṃ || dhūmravarṇā ma * * *? cakramvātra tadā kṛti | bhramamānaṃ mahāvegāduccāṭayati parvatāḥ || evamanyāni karmāni * * * * * * *? rā | tantra tantrāntarānisphatāni sādhayate śivā ||
> The cakra, O great-fated one, cuts the inner vital-points (marmāṇi); having made it into pieces, it kills without hesitation. The smoke-colored [cakra]... [being] its form here; whirling with great speed, it expels [even] mountains. Thus the other rites... [she] accomplishes what is not accomplished in tantra after tantra, O Śivā.

### The sūtra-rites (the thread-practices)
**The finger-rite** — athānyaṃ saṃpravakṣyāmi prayogambhuvi durlabhaṃ | * * * * * * * * *? yāvadā gatai || aṃgulyāntaritaṃ nāma sādhya tu vicitra * *? | * * * * * * * * * * *? rbhitameva vā ||
> Now I will declare another rite, rare on earth... the finger-interval, named the sādhya, various... or pierced.

**The yoni-mudrā's power** — vadhvā tu yoni mudrā ca vaśī kuryānna śaṃsa *? | * * * * * * * * * * * * *? rohitā || mudrā prabhāva vasagā kṣubhyaṃte sarvayoṣitāṃ | prayacchantī * * * * * * * * * * * || kāmavāṇavalāvidvāmṛdhnikāmāṃkuśāhatā || saptāhādānayet maṃtrī yadi sākṣādarundhatī | nadyaughaṃ tuṃgaprākārā go purāṭālasaṃsthitāḥ || laṃghayitvā samāyāni gatairapi surakṣitā | vratamudrāsamāyuktaṃ sādhakaṃ siddhibhāgbhavet ||
> Having bound the yoni-mudrā, one makes [her] subjugated — do not speak [of it]... red [X: lacuna]. By the mudrā's power, subjugated, all the women are agitated; they give... struck by Kāma's arrow of desire... Within a week the mantrin brings [her] — even Arundhatī herself; the river-floods, the high walls, the cow-stronghold's ramparts — having leapt over them, even well-guarded [women] come. Joined with the vow-mudrā, the sādhaka attains siddhi.

**The seven women** — devakanyā tathā nāgī gāndharvī yakṣa kanyakā | vidyādharīśvarī caiva tathā mānuṣayoṣitaḥ | saptaṣaṭpaṃcacatvāri stri dvi ekagaṇaṃ priye || vratamantra prabhāvena māsata kiṃkarī bhavet ||
> The deva-maiden, the nāgī (serpent-woman), the gāndharvī, the yakṣa-maiden, the vidyādharīśvarī, and the human women — in groups of seven, six, five, four, three, two, one, O dear one — by the vow-mantra's power, within a month she becomes the servant.

### The yoni-mudrā (the gesture described)
**The fingers** — sanmukhau tu karau kṛtvā anyonyā laya kṛtā | madhyame dve samullasya saṃlagne tu parasparaṃ || tarjanībhyāṃ samākrānte samyagaṃ tuṅga?namike | aṃguṣṭau tu samau kṛtvā madhyamā tu kaniṣṭhikau || eṣā te yoni mudrā tu kṣobhaṇī sarvado *? tāṃ | nirmathya hastau vegena yāvanmauṣṭau sumadhyame || tadeva mudrā vadhnīyā pūrvavadvasamāna *? | * yaṃ mudrā na vadhnīyā gurudārāgrataḥ priye ||
> Making the two hands facing each other, mutually dissolved; the two middle [fingers] raised, clinging to each other; pressed by the two index-fingers, with the tips [X: corrupt]; making the two thumbs equal, the middle [and] the little fingers — this is the yoni-mudrā for you, the agitator of all... churning the hands with speed, until the fists, O fair-waisted one — then one should bind that same mudrā, as before, equal... One should not bind this mudrā before the guru's wife, O dear one.

**The great agitation** — mahākṣobhakarī devī tasmādekāntataḥ * *? | * *? dhyagataṃ nāmaṃ yasya yasyaṃ tu saṃsmaret || raktāraktākṣarairdevī smaraṇādvasakṛjjagat | sādhya * * * * * *? tvātaduparisthitaṃ || raktā devī smaren maṃtrī sa bhaved dāsavad vasaḥ ||
> The goddess is the great-agitator; therefore in a solitary place [X: lacuna]. Whatever name stands in the middle [of the rite], whom he remembers — the goddess with the red, red letters, by [her] mere remembrance [agitating] the world: the sādhya... standing above it. The mantrin should remember the red goddess — he becomes [the woman] subjugated like a slave.

### The Devī's question and the pratyaya-rites
**The question** — devyuvāca | kathitaṃ sa rahasyaṃ tu maṃtramudrā vratā tathā | idānīṃ pratyayā ye tu bruhi me tripurāntaka || vāho kathayasva yathārthataḥ |
> The Devī said: "The secret is told — the mantra, the mudrā, the vow. Now the pratyayas (the proofs/tests) — tell me, O ender of Tripurā, tell [them] truly, O lord."

**The red-thread rite** — bhairava uvāca | raktaśūtra gatāṃ devī padmarāga * * * *? | * * *? jrā? karaṃ megha hūṁkāraṃ sughanī bhavet | evaṃ sā raktaśūtra *? niryāṃtā sughānāṃ * *? | * * *? bhrānta susro *? vanmasthānādviniśṛtaṃ || gajapuskarasaṃkāsaṃ niryātaṃ praviśaṃ hṛ *? | * * * * *? sādhyaṃ lalāṭavadhya ānayet || svahṛtpadmagataṃ dhyātvā ākarṣaṃ paramo mataṃ | * * * * * * *? raktajālakasaṃnihaṃ || tenācchādya viyanmārga bhuvanāni diśodaśaḥ | ākarṣayati yanna * * * *? |
> Bhairava said: "The goddess gone along the red thread, like the ruby [X: lacuna]... the hand, the cloud, the hūṁ-sound — she becomes the dense [form]. Thus that [goddess] issuing along the red thread, the dense... whirling, flowing out from the body's station. Like the elephant's frontal-globe [puskara], issuing and entering the heart... the sādhya, brought to the forehead, one leads [it]. Meditating on [her] in one's own heart-lotus — the attraction is held the supreme: [she is] like a net of red [threads]; covering the sky-path with it, the worlds and the ten directions — that which she draws [into herself]...

**The blue-thread rite** — nīlaśūtraṃ dvidhā bhūtaṃ nīlakākā kṛtiṃ smaret | dvitīyaṃ kauṣikākāraṃ niryāntaṃ brahma * * *? || * * *? ṣṭitau dvau tu sādhyau prīti yutau yadi | ānītau hṛdaye tau tu apāna ghaṭayet tataḥ || atya * * * * * * *? dviṣṭau sumadhyame | atyarthaṃ bhavate bhadre yadi yoga na muñcati ||
> The blue thread, made twofold, one should remember in the form of the black crow; the second in the form of the owl (kauśika), issuing from the brahma[randhra]... the two sādhyas standing, if joined with love — having brought them to the heart, then one should unite the apāna [breath]... excessively... O fair-waisted one, if the union does not release —

**The smoke-form** — dhūmrākāraṃ smaret maṃtraṃ vā * * * * * * *##? | sādhya tu preritā tena nīyate vāyavī diśaṃ || uccāṭayati vegena meru tulya * * * *? | śūtra nibhā devīntayā saṃveṣṭya yannataḥ || sādhyantadupare samyak stambhitastu bha * * *? | * * *? mbhamananaiva prakāreṇa tu dāpayet ||
> One should remember the mantra in the smoke-form... the sādhya, impelled by it, is carried to the vāyavī direction (the north-west). It expels with speed [even one] equal to Meru... wrapping the goddess with the thread [so] that [she] bows down; the sādhya above it, properly paralyzed... in this manner one should make [her] give.

**The Nityā's beauty** — nityā devī sadā * *? lkā saṃpūrṇā vaktra * *? | * * *? nāktaśūtraiṇa pustakena tu sobhitā || kadambavanama * *? pavisatī svaka * *? | * * * * *? taṃ tatvaṃ śāntasyoparibhūṣitaṃ || tadvisargena sahitaṃ vidyāyāṃte sthitaṃ jayet | niryānta * * * * * *? vegena cāviset ||
> The goddess Nityā, ever... complete in face... adorned with the red threads and the book; entering the kadamba-grove... her own... that principle, adorned above the śānta; joined with its emission (visarga), standing at the vidyā's end, it conquers; issuing... she enters with speed.

**The speech-power** — hṛdguhāntargataṃ kṛtvā māsādvācāmpatirbhavet | sarvavāṅmaya vettāsau * * * * * *? vet || kāryakartā mahādevi lakṣajāpād bhavet naraḥ | tatsūtraṃ māraṇī kṛṣṇaṃ śitaṃ śuklā kṛtiṃ smara ||
> Having placed it within the heart's cavern, within a month one becomes the lord of speech; he knows all the speech-realms... a doer of works, O great Devī, one becomes a man by a lakh of japas. That thread, the killer, black and cool; remember the white form —

**The white-form** — * * * *? vidva smṛtvā mārayate cirāt | pratyā nayanamathavā karoti kṛpayā bhavet || śuklā taṃtu * * * *? haśraśaśi sannibhaṃ | vahavā mṛtaka * *? phena lolākulākulaṃ | mūrdhni daśa sthita * * * *? mapyamṛtaṃ bhavet | vāk stobhe vajra sadṛsācchindantī rasanāntaraṃ ||
> ...remembering, it kills after a while; or it brings [one] back, out of grace. The white thread... like the autumn moon; carrying the corpse..., foam, restless, turbulent; standing ten [fingers] at the head... it becomes the nectar. In the stobha of speech, like the thunderbolt, cutting the tongue's inner [ties] —

**The sun-form** — sūryākārā kṛtiṃ vāpi * * *? smṛtvā tu vedhayet | vidvān vācaspati samyak sopi muko bhaveccirāt || pralālasūtra *? trā bhāvi santī vyomamaṇḍalaṃ | aṃgamarmāṇi cchindantī pravisantīṃ svaketanai || apāna yāvataṃ sthāpya siddhi hānintu tasya vai | bhavate nātra sandeha śāntikarma mahocyate ||
> Or in the sun-form, remembering, one pierces; the learned man [becomes] a Vācaspati properly — even he is freed after a while. The pralāla-thread... the future, standing in the sky-disc, cutting the body's vital-points, entering with her own banners; having fixed [her] as far as the apāna, the loss of his siddhi — it occurs, no doubt; that is declared the great śānti-karma (the pacification-rite).

**The milk-thread** — kṣīradhārā kṛtiṃ tantu viṣantī brahmaraṃdhrakaṃ | tena sādhyaṃ mahāprocya sarvaśānti karaḥ paraḥ ||
> The thread in the form of a stream of milk, entering the brahmarandhra — by it the sādhya is declared great; it is the supreme doer of all-pacification.

### The sixteenth kalā (the death-conquering)
**The kalā** — kalā ṣoḍaśa yā hṛsthā himakundendu pāṇḍarā | ādyante vidyayā yogā ekaikākṣara yogataḥ || hṛdguhāntā viniṣkrāntā plāvayed vyomamaṇḍalaṃ | prati prati smared devī ṣaṭmāsā mṛtyujid bhavet || saṃpūrṇakāyo śubhagaḥ sarvataḥ priyadarśanaḥ | homavicchāstravanto yo dīkṣitaḥ śāstrasiddhibhāk ||
> The sixteenth kalā, established in the heart, white like snow, jasmine, and the moon: joined with the vidyā at beginning and end, by the union of each single syllable; issuing from the heart's cavern, it floods the sky-disc. Ever and again one should remember the goddess — within six months one conquers death. Complete in body, auspicious, everywhere lovely to see, versed in the homa and the śāstra — the dīkṣita attains the śāstra-siddhi.

### The dīkṣā-fragment (the last readable section)
**The question** — devyuvāca | devadevaṃ mahādeva dīkṣā bruhi samāsataḥ | agnikāryanta vidhivadupadeśaṃ sakautukaṃ || sā * * *? kaṃ devyā uccāroccāra lakṣaṇaṃ |
> The Devī said: "O god of gods, great God, tell the dīkṣā in brief: the teaching of the fire-work according to the rite, with curiosity; ...the Devī's uccāra, the mark of the uccāra."

**The answer's opening** — bhairava uvāca | * * *? kṣā samāsena tava snehādvadāmyahaṃ | guṇavān satvasaṃpaṃno prāk siṣyaṃ suparīkṣayet || * * * *? niṣpaṃnaṃ dīkṣākāryā tanūdari | pūrvantu maṇḍalaṃ likhya śālipiṣṭa mayena tu || rajaḥ sindūra yāte * * * * *? pivā | ṣaṭkoṇa pūrvamuddiṣṭaṃ pūjāpūrva krameṇa tu || raktavastrā vṛtaṃ kṛtvā vidyālabdhvā tu yojaye || * * * *? cchādya rakta naivātha vāsasā ||
> Bhairava said: "...in brief, out of love for you, I will tell. One should first examine well the disciple, endowed with qualities, complete in sattva. ...having been prepared, the dīkṣā is to be done, O slender-waisted one. First drawing the maṇḍala with rice-flour (śālipiṣṭa); with the dust of vermilion (sindūra)... the six-cornered [maṇḍala] declared before, by the order of the worship: making [the disciple] covered with red cloth, having obtained the vidyā, one should join [them]... covering with the red garment."
