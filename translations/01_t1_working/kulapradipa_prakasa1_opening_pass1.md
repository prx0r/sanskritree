## Prakāśa 1 — the praise of the kula-dharma

**The maṅgala (1-1)** — guruṃ praṇamya prathamaṃ tato'haṃ gaṇādhipaṃ śrīparadevatāṃ ca | mude mahopāsakakaulikānāṃ nibaṃdhamekaṃ racayāmi ramyam ||
> Having first bowed to the guru, then to the lord of the gaṇas (Gaṇeśa) and the blessed supreme goddess, I compose this one delightful treatise for the joy of the great Kaulika worshippers.

**The text's necessity (1-2)** — grantheṣu yeṣveṣu sadāśivena savistaraḥ śrīkulamārga uktaḥ | prāyaḥ kalau te sulabhā na saṃti jñānaṃ ca teṣāṃ paramaṃ durāpam ||
> Among the treatises in which the blessed kula-path is spoken at length by Sadāśiva — in the Kali-age those are for the most part not to be obtained, and their knowledge is supremely hard to reach.

**The guru's grace (1-3)** — guroḥ kṛpā yatra bhavet prabhūtā śrīdevatāyāśca mahān prasādaḥ | tasyaiva puṃsaḥ kulaśāstrabodhastasyātra bhaktirna khalāpanoghā ||
> Where the guru's grace abounds and the blessed goddess's great favor [falls], for that man alone is there the kula-śāstra's understanding; and his devotion here is not destroyed by the wicked.

**The devotee's reward (1-4)** — yeṣāṃ dṛḍhā bhaktirihāsti śāstre vidaṃti samyak sakalaṃ rahasyam | prāgjanmapuṇyād vimalā manīṣā tai niṃdakānāṃsya bhavanti lokāḥ ||
> Those who have firm devotion here in the śāstra know rightly the whole secret; by the merit of former births their intellect is pure; among them the people [become] the censurers' [opposite — the blessed]. [X: the final pāda's syntax wobbles]

**The denouncers (1-5)** — bhītāḥ paśubhye nijagopanārthaṃ nidaṃti kecit kulaśāstrametat | kecit tu niṃdāniratā abodhā dauṣṭyaina ye te niraye navastum ||
> Some, afraid for the beasts (paśus), for their own concealment, denounce this kula-śāstra; some, fools delighting in censure, through wickedness — those should not dwell in hell [for it].

**The right use (1-6)** — sādhasya bhaktiḥ sudṛḍhātra śāstre pravarttatāṃ svasvaśubhāpanūnam | yasyāsti sā naivasabhinnaśāstre pravartatāṃ nindakatā tu nārhāḥ ||
> Let the sādhaka's devotion be very firm here in the śāstra; let it proceed for his own good, without lack. For him who has that [devotion], let it not proceed in a divided śāstra; censure, however, is not the worthy [deed].

**The two paths (1-7)** — mārgā dakṣiṇavāmasaṃjñau pramāṇabhūtau vacanairanekaiḥ | śāstraiśca śaivairapi vaiṣṇavaiśca sarvairniṣavyau samayādibhedāt ||
> The two paths, named the dakṣiṇa and the vāma, are authoritative by many utterances and by the Śaiva and Vaiṣṇava scriptures alike — established (niṣavyau) by the difference of the samayas.

**The benediction (1-8)** — brahmaitā pādakahetuyuktaṃ śrāvyaṃ samastāgamasārabhūtam | kūlapradīpākhyamidaṃ nibaṃdhaṃ vilokya kaulāḥ sukhino bhavantu ||
> This treatise named the Kulapradīpa, joined with the Brahma-pāda's cause, worthy of hearing, the essence of all the āgamas — having looked on it, may the Kaulas be happy.

### The Kulārṇava-frame (the text's authority)

**The Devī's question (1-9)** — śrīdevyuvāca | kuleśa śrotumichāmi sarvabhūtadayānidhe | kuladharmastvayā deva sūcitā naprakāśitā ||
> The blessed Devī said: "O lord of the kula, ocean of compassion for all beings, the kula-dharma has been indicated by you, O god, but not made manifest —"

**The request (1-10)** — tasya dharmasya māhātmyaṃ sarvadharmottamasya ca | vada me parameśāna yaditestiṣṭaṃ yā mayi ||
> "— tell me the majesty of that dharma, the best of all dharmas, O supreme Lord, if it is established and pleasing to me."

**The answer (1-11)** — īśvara uvāca | śṛṇu devi pravakṣyāmi yanmāṃ tvaṃ pariprachasi | tasyā śravaṇamātreṇa yoginīnāṃ priyo bhavet ||
> The Lord said: "Hear, O Devī, I will declare what you ask me: by the mere hearing of it, one becomes dear to the yoginīs."

**The secrecy (1-12, 1-13)** — brahmāviṣṇuguhādīnāṃ na mayā kathitaṃ purā | kathayāmi tava snehā śṛṇuṣvekāgracetasā || pāraṃparyakramāyātaṃ vaktrād vaktreṣu saṃsthitam | akathyaṃ pāramārthena tathāpi kathayāmi te ||
> Not to Brahmā, Viṣṇu, Guha, and the rest was it told by me before; I tell it out of love for you — hear with one-pointed mind. It has come by the succession-order, standing from mouth to mouth; in the supreme truth it is unutterable — yet I tell it to you.

**The secrecy's condition (1-14)** — tvayāpi gopitavyaṃ hi na deyaṃ yasya kasyacit | deṣaṃbhaktāya śiṣyāya anyathā patanaṃ bhavet ||
> It must be concealed by you too; it is not to be given to just anyone — [only] to a devotee-disciple; otherwise there is a fall.

**The path-hierarchy (1-15, 1-16)** — sarvebhyaścottamā vedāvedebhyo vaiṣṇavaṃ param | vaiṣṇavāduttamaṃ śaivaṃ śaivād dakṣiṇamuttamam || dakṣiṇāduttamaṃ vāmaṃ vāmāt sidhāṃatamuttamam | siddhāṃtāduttamaṃ kaulaṃ kaulāt parataraṃ na hi ||
> Above all the Vedas is the Vaiṣṇava [path] supreme; above the Vaiṣṇava is the Śaiva; above the Śaiva the dakṣiṇa is excellent; above the dakṣiṇa is the vāma; above the vāma the siddhānta is excellent; above the siddhānta is the kaula — than the kaula there is nothing higher.

**The secret's degrees (1-17)** — guhyā guhyataraṃ devi sārāt sārataraṃ param | sākṣāt paraśivaṃ devi karṇāt karṇagataṃ kulam ||
> More secret than the secret, O Devī, more essence than the essence, supreme: the kula, gone from ear to ear, is directly the supreme Śiva.

**The extraction (1-18)** — mathitvā jñānadaṇḍena vedāgamamahārṇave | sārajñena mayā devi kuladharmaḥ samuddhṛtaḥ ||
> Having churned the great ocean of Veda and āgama with the churning-staff of knowledge, by me who know the essence, O Devī, the kula-dharma has been drawn up.

**The kula's weight (1-19)** — ekataḥ sakalā dharmāḥ yajñatīrthavratādayaḥ | ekataḥ kuladharmastu tatra kaulodhika priye ||
> On one side all the dharmas — the sacrifices, the sacred-fords, the vows and the rest; on one side the kula-dharma: there the Kaula is the greater, O dear one.

**The rivers-to-ocean (1-20)** — praviśanti yathā nadyaḥ samudraṃ tuchacakragāḥ | tathaiva samayāḥ sarve praviṣṭāḥ kulameva hi ||
> As the rivers enter the ocean, [even] those that run into the shallow pools, so all the samayas have entered the kula itself.

**The elephant-foot (1-21)** — yathā hastipade līnaṃ sarvaprāṇipadaṃ bhavet | darśanāni ca sarvāṇi samayānyaḥ samo bhavet ||
> As in the elephant's foot[-print] all the creatures' footprints are absorbed, so all the darśanas [and] the samayas become one.

**The mare's speed (1-22)** — yathā maraturaṃginyā na samāḥ sakalāyagāḥ | tathaiva samayāḥ sarve kuladharmeṇa nosamāḥ ||
> As no other steeds are equal to the mare, so all the samayas are not equal to the kula-dharma.

**The gap (1-23)** — merusarvapayoryadvat sūryakhadyotayoriva | tathā ca samayāsyāṃ ca kulasya mahadaṃtaram ||
> As [the gap] between Meru and all the hills, or between the sun and the glow-worm — so is the great difference between the samayas and the kula.

**The challenge (1-24)** — asticetvat samānārī matsamaḥ puruṣosti cet | kulena samadharmaṃ tu kathayet tadā priye ||
> If there is a woman equal [to me], if there is a man like me, then let one speak of a dharma equal to the kula — then, O dear one.

**The slander's sin (1-25, 1-26)** — kuladharmaṃ hi mohena yonya dharmeṇadurmatiḥ | sakṛt sādhāratāṃ brūyāt sotyajonāṃ tyajotyajaḥ || yo vai kulādhikaṃ dharmamajñānādvadati priye | brahmahatyādhikaṃ pāpaṃ samāpnoti na saṃśayaḥ ||
> He who in delusion, evil-minded, through another dharma, would once declare the kula-dharma's equality — abandoning the abandoner, he abandons [all]. He who in ignorance speaks of a dharma higher than the kula, O dear one, attains a sin greater than brahmahatyā — no doubt.

**The vessel (1-27)** — kuladharmapravahaṇaṃ samāruhyanarottamaḥ | svargadvīpāntaraṃ gatvā mokṣaratnaṃ samukta te ||
> Mounting the kula-dharma's ship, the best of men, having gone to the heaven-island, obtains the mokṣa-jewel — [so] it is told to you.

**The immediacy (1-28)** — darśaneṣu ca sarveṣu cirābhyāsena mānavāḥ | mokṣaṃ labhante kaule tu sadya eva na saṃśayaḥ ||
> In all the darśanas, men obtain mokṣa by long practice; in the Kaula, at once — no doubt.

**The vow (1-29)** — bahune hi kimukte na śṛṇumatprāṇavallabhe | na kaulasamadhrmosti tvāṃ śape kulanāyike ||
> What [need] of much speech — hear, O my life's darling: there is no dharma equal to the Kaula; I swear to you, O lady of the kula.

**The bhoga-yoga (1-30, 1-31)** — yogī cainnaiva bhogī syād bhogī cainnaiva yogavit | bhogayogātmakaṃ kaulaṃ tasmāt sarvādhikaṃ priye || bhogo yogāyate sākṣāt pātakaṃ sukṛtāyate | mokṣāyate ca saṃsāraḥ kuladharme kuleśvari ||
> The yogi is not an enjoyer; the enjoyer is not a knower of yoga. The Kaula is of the essence of enjoyment-and-yoga both — therefore it exceeds all, O dear one. In the kula-dharma, O lady of the kula, enjoyment becomes yoga directly, sin becomes merit, and saṃsāra becomes mokṣa.

**The pantheon (1-32)** — brahmendrācyutarudrādi devatāmunirākṣasāḥ | kuladharmaparā devi mānaveṣu ca kākathā ||
> Brahmā, Indra, Acyuta, Rudra and the rest — gods, sages, rākṣasas — [are] intent on the kula-dharma, O Devī; what talk of [ordinary] men?

**The single path (1-33)** — vihāya sarvadharmāṃśca nānāgurumatāni ca | kulameva hi jānīyād yadichet siddhimātmanaḥ ||
> Abandoning all the dharmas and the many gurus' doctrines, one should know the kula alone — if one wishes one's own siddhi.

**The former-birth merit (1-34, 1-35)** — pūrvajanmakṛtābhyāsāt kulajñānaprakāśite | suptotthitapraduddhirvā upadeśādikaṃ vinā || janmāntarasahastreṣu yā vuddhirvihitānṛṇām | tāmeva labhate jaṃtu rūpadeśonirarthakaḥ ||
> From the practice of a former birth, [when] the kula-knowledge shines — the awakening of one risen from sleep, without the teaching and the rest. Whatever intelligence was won by the unindebted [souls] over thousands of other births — that the creature obtains; without it the teaching is vain.

**The purification (1-36)** — śaivavaiṣṇavadaurgārka gaṇapatyendusaṃbhavaiḥ | etairviśuddhacittasya kaulajñānaprakāśate ||
> For one whose mind is purified by the Śaiva, Vaiṣṇava, Durgā, Arka, Gaṇapati, and Indu[-cults] — the kula-knowledge shines.

**The irreversibility (1-37)** — sarvadharmāśca deveśi pusarāvartakā smṛtāḥ | kuladharme sthitā ye ca te sarvepyanivartakāḥ ||
> All the dharmas, O Devī, are remembered as returning (punar-āvartakāḥ); those who stand in the kula-dharma are all non-returning.

**The karma's exhaustion (1-38)** — purā kṛta tapodānaṃ yajñatīrthajapavrataiḥ | kṣīṇādyānāṃ nṛṇāṃ devi kulajñānaṃ prakāśate ||
> When the meritorious deeds formerly done — by austerity, giving, sacrifice, sacred-ford, japa, vows — are exhausted for men, O Devī, then the kula-knowledge shines.

**The eligible (1-39, 1-40)** — śuddhacittasya śāntasya dharmiṇo gurusevina | api guhyasya bhaktasya kaulajñānaṃ prakāśate || sasadbhāvini harṣāḍhye sadācāre dṛḍhavratai | gurvājñā pālake dhanye kaulajñānaṃ prakāśite ||
> For the pure-minded, the peaceful, the righteous, the guru's servant, even the hidden devotee — the kula-knowledge shines. For the woman of true being, rich in joy, of good conduct, firm in vows, the guardian of the guru's command, the blessed — the kula-knowledge shines.

**The unworthy (1-41)** — anarhekulavijñānaṃ na tiṣṭhati kadācana | tasmāt parīkṣavaktavyaṃ kulajñānaṃ mayoditam ||
> In the unworthy the kula-knowledge never stands; therefore the kula-knowledge is to be told only after examination — so I have declared.

**The misgiving (1-42, 1-43)** — na brūyāchivadharmaṃ tu ayogye śivaśāsanam | ājñābhaṃgahiyaḥ kuryā devatāśāpamāpnuyāt || abodhya samayācāraṃ kuladharmaṃ vadedyadi | saguruścāpiśiṣyaśca yoginīnāṃ bhavet paśuḥ ||
> One should not speak the Śiva-dharma, the Śiva-teaching, to the unfit; he who would break the command attains the goddesses' curse. If one declares the kula-dharma to one who has not understood the samaya-conduct — both guru and disciple become a paśu of the yoginīs.

**The guru's gift (1-44)** — bodhayitvā guruḥ śiṣyaṃ kulajñānaṃ prakāśayet | labhate tā bubhausākṣāt yoginīvīramelanam ||
> Having awakened the disciple, the guru reveals the kula-knowledge; [then] he attains, in this very body, the union of the yoginī-heroes (yoginī-vīra-melana). [X: "bubhau" corrupt]

**The crossing (1-45, 1-46)** — anāyasena saṃsārasāgaraṃ yastitīryati | kuladharmamimaṃ jñātvā mucyate nātrasaṃśayaḥ || kuladharmamahāmārgagantā muktipurīṃ brajet | acirānnātrasaṃdeha tasmātkaulaṃ samācaret ||
> He who crosses the ocean of saṃsāra without effort — knowing this kula-dharma, he is released, no doubt here. The goer on the kula-dharma's great road reaches the city of liberation; without delay, no doubt — therefore one should practice the Kaula.

**The fool's turn (1-47)** — kulaśāstramanādṛtya paśuśāstrāṇiyobhyaset | svagṛhe pāya saṃktā bhikṣāaṭatidurmatiḥ ||
> He who, disregarding the kula-śāstra, practices the paśu-śāstras — in his own house [there is] plenty, yet he wanders for alms, the fool.

**The gems and glass (1-48, 1-49, 1-50)** — vihāyakuladharmaṃ yaḥ paśudharmaṃ paro bhavet | karasthaṃ ratnamutsṛjya kācakhaṇḍaṃ samṛgdhate || saṃtyajya kulamantrāṇi paśumantrāṇi yobhyaset | sadhānyarāśimutsṛjya pāṃśuśāśeṃjighṛkṣati || kulānyayantiraskṛtya yovānānvayamīkṣate | taḍāgaṃ tyajya nṛṣṇārtā mṛgatṛṣṇāṃ pradhāvati ||
> Abandoning the kula-dharma, he who is devoted to the paśu-dharma — dropping the jewel in his hand, he seeks a shard of glass. Abandoning the kula-mantras, he who practices the paśu-mantras — dropping the heap of grain, he longs for a fistful of dust. Disregarding the kula, he who looks to another lineage — leaving the pond, thirsty, he runs after the mirage.

**The mirage (1-51)** — pathendrajālajalabhāḥ kṣaṇameva sukhāvahā | śrīkaulādanya samayāstādṛśāḥ kulanāyike ||
> The water-falls of the path's illusion-net bring happiness but a moment — such are the samayas other than the blessed Kaula, O lady of the kula.

**The impossible wish (1-52)** — kuladharmamajananyo mokṣamichati durmatiḥ | pārāvāramapāraṃ sapāṇibhyāṃ tat tu michati ||
> He who, not knowing the kula-dharma, wishes mokṣa, the fool — it is the far shore without a far shore that he wishes to grasp with his two hands.

**The dream-wealth (1-53, 1-54)** — yovānya darśanebhyaśca bhuktimuktiṃ ca kāṃkṣati | svapnalabdhe dhanenaiva dhanavyāna sabhad yadi || suktau rajatavibhrāntiryathā jāyet pārvati | tathānya samayebhyaśca bhuktirmuktiḥ prasasyate ||
> He who from the other darśanas longs for bhukti and mukti — like [trying to gain] wealth with dream-gotten wealth, he would bear the wealth. As in a pearl-shell the illusion of silver arises, O Pārvatī, so from the other samayas is bhukti and mukti praised [falsely].

**The outcast's worth (1-55, 1-56)** — sarvakarmavahīnopi varṇāśramavivarjitaḥ | kulaniṣṭaḥ kuleśāni bhuktimuktyoḥ sabhājana || kulajñānavihīnopi kulabhaktyāḥ śrayobhavet | yopitadgatimāpnoti kimutāsya parāyaṇaḥ ||
> Even one bereft of all works, outside the varṇa-āśrama [order], if established in the kula — he is the vessel of bhukti and mukti, O lady of the kula. Even one without the kula-knowledge, resorting to the kula by devotion — whatever final-state that woman attains, what [of] his who is its devotee?

**The dharma's protection (1-57)** — kuladharmohitohantī rakṣito rakṣati priye | pūjitaḥ pūjayātyāśu tasmāt ta na parityajet ||
> The kula-dharma, honored, destroys; protected, it protects, O dear one; worshipped, it worships quickly — therefore one should not abandon it.

**The vow of endurance (1-58, 1-59)** — nindantu bāndhavā sarve tyajantu strīsutādayaḥ | janāsantu māṃ dṛṣṭvā rājino daṇḍayaṃ tu vāḥ || seve seve punaḥ sevet vāmeva paradevate | tva dharmaṃ nevamucāmi manovākkāya karmabhiḥ ||
> Let all the kinsmen revile, let the wife and sons abandon; let the people, seeing me, [be hostile], let the kings punish... I will serve, and serve, and serve again — the Vāma, the supreme goddess: this dharma I proclaim by the works of mind, speech, and body.

**The fixed mind (1-60, 1-61)** — evamāpadgatasyāpi yasya buddhiḥ suniścitāḥ | sa tu saṃpūjyate deviramutrāṃba śivo bhavet || rogādāridryaduḥkhādyaiḥ pāḍitopyaniśaṃ śive | yastvāmupāsataṃ bhaktyā śaśvatpadamāpnuyāt ||
> Even for one fallen into such calamity, whose intellect is well-fixed — he is honored [even] by the gods, and here-and-hereafter, O mother, he becomes Śiva. Even one tormented constantly by disease, poverty, sorrow and the rest — he who worships you with devotion, O Śive, attains the eternal state.

**The constancy (1-62)** — janāstuvantunindantu lakṣmīstiṣṭhatu gachatu | mṛtyuradyayugānte syāt kulaṃ naparibarjayet ||
> Let people praise or revile; let the fortune stand or go; let death come at the age's end — one should not abandon the kula.

**The unworthy birth (1-63)** — yo jāto nārcayet tvā tu kuladharmaṃ samāśritaḥ | kuṃsito jātamātreṇa bhūbhāreṇātmaśatruṇā ||
> He who, born [into the kula], would not worship you, having resorted to the kula-dharma — he is defiled from the very birth, with the earth's burden, his own enemy.

**The husks (1-64)** — pulākā iva dhānyeṣu pūjyaṃ dhā-ī ca jantuṣu | vudbudā iva toyeṣu ye kaulavimukhāhi te ||
> Like husks among the grains, like chaff among creatures, like bubbles among the waters — those who turn from the Kaula are such.

**The true life (1-65)** — taravopi hi jīvanti jīvantī mṛgapakṣiṇaḥ | sajīvatimano yasya kuladharme vyavasthitam ||
> Even the trees live; the beasts and birds live. His mind is truly alive whose [mind] stands in the kula-dharma.

**The breathless (1-66, 1-67)** — kuladharmāvihīnasya dinānyāyānti yānti ca | salohakārabhasneva śvasannapi najīvati || gachatatiṣṭhatocyāpi jāgrato svapatopi vā | kuleśvari kulājñasya tat paśoriva jīvitam ||
> For the one without the kula-dharma the days come and go; breathing, he does not live — like the bellows of the blacksmith. Whether walking, standing, speaking, waking, or sleeping, O lady of the kula, the life of one ignorant of the kula is like a paśu's.

**The learned fool (1-68)** — vidvānapi ca mūkhosaudhārmikovāpyadhārmikaḥ | vratasthopyavratasthaḥ syād yaḥ kaulavimukhojanaḥ ||
> Whether learned or a fool, whether righteous or unrighteous, whether vow-keeping or vowless — the man turned from the Kaula is [all the same].

**The living (1-69)** — jātāsta eva jagatijaṃtavaḥ sādhujīvinaḥ | kuladharmaparā devi śeṣā jaṭharagarddabhāḥ ||
> Those alone are born, the world's creatures living well, who are intent on the kula-dharma, O Devī; the rest are asses of the belly.

**The golden channel (1-70)** — suṣumānucyate sadbhiḥ kuladharmaparāyaṇāḥ | aparastu paraṃ pṛṣṭyā matsyakūṭatvacāvṛtaḥ ||
> The intent on the kula-dharma is called the suṣumṇā by the good; the other, [going] along the back, is covered with the fish's trick-skin.

**The learned rank (1-71)** — caturvedikalājñānīścapacādadhamaḥ priye | ścapacopikulajñānī brāhmaṇāditiricyate ||
> The knower of the four Vedas' arts is the lowest of the low, O dear one; the knower of the kula, even half-knowing, surpasses the brāhmaṇa.

**The purified (1-72)** — gurukāruṇyapūtastu dīkṣānirdhūtakalmaṣaḥ | kulapūjārato devi kāyakāyo nacetaraḥ ||
> Purified by the guru's compassion, with the stain blown off by the dīkṣā, delighting in the kula-worship, O Devī — he is the [true] body; not the other.
