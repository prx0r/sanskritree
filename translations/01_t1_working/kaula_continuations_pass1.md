# I. Kulānanda, vv. 18–34 — the piercing-essence (vedhasāra)

*Same Matsyendra-school context as the opening. The practices: the fire-piercing of the granthis, the head-lotus, the amṛta-descent, the gray-hair-stopping, the crossroads-vidyā.*

**18** — śṛṇu devi pravakṣyāmi vedhasāraṃ yathākramam | brahmasthāne.....padmaṃ catuḥṣaṣṭidalānvitam ||
> Hear, O Devī — I will declare the essence of the piercing (vedhasāra), in order: the lotus in the brahma-station, endowed with sixty-four petals.

**19** — tatraivotpādayet sṛṣṭiḥ daṇḍākārāgnitejasā | bhedayitvā mahāgranthiṃ dvicakrañca tathaiva ca ||
> There he should generate the creation with the rod-formed fire-brilliance (daṇḍākārāgnitejas); having pierced the great knot (granthi) and the two cakras likewise —

**20** — mūrdhni kamalasaṃsthānaṃ jvalanākāraṃ vicintayet | calantaṃ bhrāmayettena niyataṃ tu mahītale ||
> — in the head he should contemplate the lotus-station, of fire-form; moving, he whirls with it, surely, upon the earth.

**21** — bedhaye girivṛkṣāṇi kiṃ punaḥ kṣudrajantavaḥ | athānyaṃ paramaṃ devi vijñānaṃ bhadrakaṃ śṛṇu ||
> He pierces mountains and trees — how much more the small creatures! Now the other supreme knowledge, O Devī — hear the auspicious:

**22** — bhruvoścakṣurmadhyasthaṃ ca kālāvunā (?) ....vicintayet | yāvadviśramate nityaṃ bhramate cakravat śiraḥ ||
> The [point] in the middle of the brows and the eye... [X: "kālāvunā" corrupt] he should contemplate; until it rests — the head whirls like a wheel.

**23** — durāśravaṇavijñānaṃ bhūtadetasya (?) cintayet | vāmasrotaṃ tu yāvat tīvratejasamaprabham ||
> The knowledge of distant hearing... [X: "bhūtadetasya" corrupt] he should contemplate; the left current (vāmasrota), of fierce-brilliance form.

**24** — balīpalitakṣayaṃ deva tvayā khyātaṃ mamāgrataḥ | etanme saṃśayo deva kathayasva mama prabho ||
> The destruction of age and gray-hair (balīpalita), O god, was proclaimed by you before me — this is my doubt, O god; tell it, my Lord.

**25** — śṛṇu devi pravakṣyāmi yat surāṇāmapi durlabham | dvātriṃśatidalaṃ padmaṃ tiṣṭhati tanmūrdhni madhyataḥ ||
> Hear, O Devī — I will declare what is hard even for the gods to obtain: the thirty-two-petaled lotus stands in the middle of the head.

**26** — tatraiva bhāvayedamṛtaṃ kalāṣoḍaśasamanvitam | hṛccakraṃ pātayeddvāraṃ (?) yāvadānābhimaṇḍalam ||
> There he should contemplate the nectar, joined with the sixteen kalās; the heart-cakra... [X: "dvāraṃ" doubtful] he should cast down, up to the navel-disc.

**27** — cintayet kṛṣṇavarṇañca bhedaṃ tu sakalaṃ puraḥ | palitāstambhayeddevi māsamekena suvrate ||
> He should contemplate the black color, and the whole piercing before [him]: he stops the gray-hair (palitāstambhana), O Devī, within a single month, O good-vowed one.

**28** — athānyaṃ paramaṃ devi guhyasaṃśayasthitikārakam | yatra catuṣpathasthānaṃ tatra sarvāsurālayam ||
> Now the other supreme [knowledge], O Devī, establishing the hidden certainty: where the crossroads-station is, there is the abode of all the asuras.

**29** — tatraiva sthāpayeccittaṃ divyabālapravartata(?) | aśrutāṃ vadate bālāṃ...........ḍivya varānane ||
> There he should place the mind... [X: corrupt]; the girl speaks the unheard [words], the divine, O fair-faced one.

**30** — viṣaprahāraṃ kurute jvaravyādhiṃ vināśayet | tataḥ parataraṃ sthānaṃ tatra sarve pravartate ||
> He makes the poison-stroke; he destroys fever and disease. Then the higher station — there everything proceeds.

**31** — athānyaṃ paramaṃ devi durāvedhaṃ vadāmyaham | jvalajjvalanasaṃdhyasthaṃ tatraiva lakṣayeddevi ||
> Now the other supreme [knowledge], O Devī — I tell the hard piercing (durāvedha): standing in the blazing fire-twilight, there he should fix, O Devī —

**32** — taṃ tritakoṭisamaprabhā ūrddhvaṃ śakti nipātānyataḥ (?) | vedhayedvicakṣaṇaḥ vedhayitvā tu taṃ lakṣayet ||
> — that [fire], with the brilliance of three crores [of suns], the power rising upward... [X: corrupt]; the wise one pierces; having pierced, he fixes on it.

**33** — jvalanākāṣṭhaprabhāṃ ūrddhvaśaktiṃ cakrayitvā acalaṃ bhrāmayet punaḥ (?) | vedhayet sā manaḥsahasrāṇi samakāni tu kā kathā ||
> Making the upward-power, of the brilliance of the fire-stick, whirling the immovable again... he pierces — the thousands of minds [pierced] equally — what is there to say?

**34** — evaṃ jñātvā vīrārohe vicaret yathāsukham | devāsuramanujānāṃ durlabho bhavati sādhakaḥ ||
> Knowing this, O heroine (vīrārohe — "mounted on the hero"), one should move at ease: the sādhaka becomes hard to obtain for gods, asuras, and men.

Notes: the vedhasāra — the piercing-praxis (the fire-rod through the cakras, the head-lotus, the amṛta's sixteen kalās, the gray-hair-stopping, the crossroads-oracle, the thousand-mind piercing). [P: the piercing (vedha) — the KJN 4.10's śaktivijñānavedha; the amṛta's kalās — the kalā-terminology in its nectar-form; the siddhi-claims' escalation (mountains → the three-worlds rarity).]

# II. Unmattabhairava, the yantra-uddhāra and the kīlaka-section

## The yantra-uddhāra's close (vv. 13–16)
**13** — pūjāṃ cakāra bhaktirvā vāñchākāmo bhavetphalam | yaccakraṃ dhārayetsiddhiṃ maramānandasundarī ||
> The worship done — or the devotion — the fruit of the desired desire arises; whoever bears that cakra [gains] the siddhi, O beautiful one of delight [X: "maramāna" corrupt — possibly "paramānanda"].

**14** — bālānāṃ sukhabodhāya yatrarājo mahātmanaḥ | svacakrairparacakrairvā ḍaṃkinyādayatoṣikā ||
> For the easy understanding of the children [of the tradition], the king of yantras of the great soul: with one's own cakras or with the cakras of others, the ḍākinīs and the rest are pleased.

**15** — unmādabhaganāśārthaṃ pūjayedbhairavāya ca | chāgaivali prayuktārai bhūtvā dhāra vāruṇim ||
> For the destruction of the madness and the... [X: "bhaga" doubtful] he should worship Bhairava: with the goat-offering (chāga-bali) having been made, he should bear the Vāruṇī [wine?] [X: "vāruṇim" — the Varuṇa-mudrā or the liquor?].

**16** — anekārthaṃ yantrarājo samādhānaṃ bhavecchiva | pūjāṃ cakāra vidhinā dadyāt cetkāraṇaṃ balim ||
> The king of yantras, of manifold ends, is the concentration (samādhāna), O Śiva: the worship done by the rule — if one gives the bali with the cause [X: "kāraṇaṃ" doubtful].

## The kīlaka-section (the second uddhāra)
**K1** — kīlakaṃ nāthanāthasya trivedaṃ sayutai samā | ṛgyajuḥ sāmavedānāṃ mantrarājo maheśvaraḥ ||
> The nailing (kīlaka) of the lord of lords, joined with the three Vedas: of the Ṛg, Yajur, and Sāma Vedas, the king of mantras, the great Lord.

**K2** — paryeṣaṇānāṃ mantrāṇāṃ divyacakṣurbhavennṛṇām | mantrāṇāṃ vidyusāmarthā nānāmantreṣu kīlakam ||
> Of the sought mantras, the divine eye arises for men: the mantras' power like lightning — the nailing among the manifold mantras.

**K3** — yadvidāryai bhairavasya pātakaṃ pātakasya ca | nānāmokṣārtha bhinnārthai bhāvābhāveṣu bhairavaḥ ||
> That which pierces Bhairava's sin, and the sinner's sin: for the manifold ends of liberation, with the divided ends — in being and non-being, Bhairava.

**K4** — amāvāsyāṃ caturdaśyāṃ daśamī dvādaśī tathā | bhāvayetpūjaka śreṣṭai anurādhā virodhitām ||
> On the new moon, the fourteenth, the tenth, and the twelfth: the best of worshippers should contemplate the obstructed one (virodhitā) [X: "anurādhā virodhitām" — the nakṣatra Anurādhā?].

**K5-K6** — māse māse pakṣa pakṣe uttamottamamaṅgalaiḥ | vaiśākhe phālgune māghe kārtike mārgaśīṛṣayoḥ || anyamāsai vibhinnānāṃ pāṭhakai kṛṣṇapakṣayoḥ | dhārābhiḥ saptamadhyeṣu bhānusomavibhinnataḥ ||
> Month by month, fortnight by fortnight, with the best of auspicious [times]: in Vaiśākha, Phālguna, Māgha, Kārtika, Mārgaśīrṣa; in the other months, [in] the dark fortnights, with the reciters of the divided [mantras]; with the streams (dhārā) in the midst of the seven, distinguished by sun and moon.

**K7** — māraṇamohanaṃ caiva vaśīkaraṇatāḍanam | uccāṭanena saṃyuktai yatpṛthak pūjayedguruḥ ||
> Killing, deluding, subjugating, striking — joined with expulsion: whatever [act], separately, the guru should worship.

**K8** — samādhāne dhyānagamyai vaśiṣṭaimunikīlakaiḥ | viśvāmitraiḥ kīlakañca bhāradvājau samāvisat ||
> In the concentration, attainable in meditation: with the Vasiṣṭha-muni nailings; with the Viśvāmitra[-nailings], the nailing; with the Bhāradvāja, it enters [X: "samāvisat" doubtful].

**K9** — tvameva kīlakaṃ siddhamamanāni kīlakam | sarveṣāṃ kīlakaṃ kṛtya bhānucandreṇa kīlakam ||
> You alone are the perfected nailing; the nailing of the nameless [mantras]; making the nailing of all, the nailing by sun-and-moon.

**K10** — tasmātkāruṇi he rudra nityaṃ dhāraṇakīlakam | paṭhedvā dhārayet śreṣṭai yantrastotrai vidhānataḥ ||
> Therefore, O compassionate Rudra, the constant bearing-nailing: one should recite or bear, O best one, the yantra-stotras by the rule.

**K11** — etanmataṃ kīlakasya bhairavasya mahātmanaḥ ||
> This is the doctrine of the nailing of the great-souled Bhairava.

Notes: the kīlaka-doctrine (the nailing — the fixing of the mantra's power): the Vedic-mantra nailings, the muni-nailings (Vasiṣṭha/Viśvāmitra/Bhāradvāja), the sun-moon nailing, the tithi-rules. [P: the kīlaka — the technical term of the mantra-śāstra (the "nail" that pins the mantra's deity); cf. our Kubjikā paṭala 4's kīlaya-kīlaya (Vagalā) and paṭala 8's daśadik-kīlaka (the directional nailing) — the term spans the batch.]

# III. Kaulajñānanirṇaya, Paṭala 5 completed + Paṭala 6 opening (the jīva-determination)

## Paṭala 5, closing (5/15–5/35)
**5/15** — saṃpūrṇopūrṇamāpūrṇaśaśāṅkaparimaṇḍalam | dhyāyamānamidandevi jarāvyādhirvinaśyati ||
> This — the full-moon-disc, filled and filling — contemplated, O Devī: old age and disease perish.

**5/16-5/17** — athānyaṃ śṛṇu kalyāṇi yathātathyena bhāvini | dhyāyeccandramidaṃ devi nābhimūrdhni ca hṛdgatam || abhyaset samacittastu abdamekaṃ nirantaram | tataḥ svātantramāyāti jarāmaraṇavarjitam ||
> Now hear the other, O auspicious one, as it truly is, O Devī: one should meditate on this moon, O Devī, in the navel, the head, and the heart. The even-minded one should practice for a full year, unbroken: then he attains freedom (svātantra), free of old age and death.

**5/18-5/20** — punaranyaṃ pravakṣyāmi śṛṇu tvaṃ vīravandite | sahasradalaśobhāḍhyaṃ gokṣīradhavalopamam || devyā cakragatañcakraṃ tādṛśaṃ khecaraiḥ sthitam | adhaścakrasamārūḍhaṃ ūrddhacakreṇa plāvitam || satatamabhyased yogī siñcyamānaśca vi(śe)ṣaiḥ | valīpalitanirmuktaḥ sarvavyādhivivarjitam ||
> Again I will tell another — hear, O honored of the heroes: the lotus adorned with a thousand petals, white like cow's milk; the cakra within the cakra of the Devī, standing thus with the sky-goers (khecara); mounted on the lower cakra, flooded by the upper cakra. The yogī should practice constantly, sprinkled by the excellences: freed from wrinkles and gray-hair, free of all disease.

**5/21-5/24** — krīḍate saḥ samudrāntaṃ svātantraḥ sidhyate priye | anyadapyadbhūtaṃ devi śṛṇutaikāgramānasam || ṣoḍaśāramnahāpadmaṃ himakundendusamprabham | sthāne sthāne tu taṃ dhyātvā śivādyāvīcigocaram || bindudhārānipātaiśca viplavaiḥ pūritantanuḥ | niṣkrāntaṃ romakūpa	iśca gokṣīrahimasannibham || na jarāmaraṇantasya vyādhiroganna vidyate | svātantraḥ śivatulyastu svacchandagaticeṣṭitaḥ ||
> He plays to the ocean's end; the free one attains [it], O beloved. And another wonder, O Devī — hear with one-pointed mind: the great lotus of sixteen spokes, radiant like frost, jasmine, and moon — meditating on it in station after station, from Śiva down to the lowest (āvīci); his body filled by the downpours of the bindu-streams and the floods; issuing from the pores, white like cow's milk and snow — for him there is no old age, no death, no disease, no sickness: free, equal to Śiva, moving at will (svacchandagati).

**5/25-5/27** — pūjyate suravṛndaistu divyakanyāranekadhā | guhyanābhihṛdi kaṇṭhe vaktre mūrdhni śikhāntare || muṇḍasandhigatā devi pṛṣṭhamadhye tridaṇḍakam | ekādaśavidhā devi cakrāṇi ca sahasrikā || pañcāraṃ aṣṭapatrañca daśa dvādaśa patrakam | ṣoḍaśaṃ śatapatrañca koṭipatraṃ yathaiva ca ||
> He is worshipped by the hosts of gods, by the divine maidens, in many ways. In the secret [parts], the navel, the heart, the throat, the mouth, the head, within the crest — in the joints of the skull, O Devī, in the middle of the back, the three staffs (tridaṇḍaka): the eleven kinds, O Devī, of the cakras, and the thousand-petaled. The five-spoked, the eight-petaled, the ten and the twelve-petaled, the sixteen, the hundred-petaled, and the crore-petaled, likewise.

**5/28-5/31** — ebhiḥ sthāne samabhyastaṃ dadate vividhaṃ phalam | raktaṃ vaśyaṃ sadā devi mahābhogapradāyakam || pītaṃ stambhakarannāthe dhūmramuccāṭanaṃ sadā | śuklamāpyāyane proktaṃ viśeṣaṃ śāntikārakam || gokṣīradhārādhavalaṃ kathitaṃ mṛtyuñjaye hitam | taptacāmīkaraprabhaṃ purakṣobhādikāraṇam || bindunādatathāśaktirevaṃ dhyātvā pṛthak pṛthak | dharmārthau kāmamokṣau ca aṇimādiguṇāṣṭakam ||
> Practiced in these stations, they give the manifold fruits: the red gives subjugation, O Devī, ever granting great enjoyment; the yellow, immobilizing, O Lord; the smoke-colored, always expulsion; the white is proclaimed for nourishment, specially making peace. The white-like-cow's-milk is told as good for the death-conqueror; the brilliance of molten gold is the cause of the shaking of cities and the rest. The bindu, the nāda, the śakti — meditating on them separately, [one gains] dharma, artha, kāma, mokṣa, and the eight guṇas beginning with aṇimā.

**5/32-5/35** — atītānāgatandevi rūpasya parivartanam | bhavatyevaṃ na sandeho abhyāsāt tadgatasya tu || gurubhaktiryoginīnāṃ kulakaulāgameṣu ca | amaratvaṃ bhaveddevi jayenmṛtyuṃ na saṃśayaḥ || kathitaṃ mṛtyuñjayandevi sarvajñānasya nirṇayam | gopitavyaṃ prayatnena martyaloke narādhame || durlabhaṃ siddhisandohaṃ gopitavyaṃ prayatnataḥ | dātavyaṃ pūrvasiddhasya abdamekaparīkṣitam ||
> The past and the future, O Devī, the transformation of the form: it occurs — no doubt — from the practice of the one absorbed in it. The devotion to the guru, to the yoginīs, in the kula-and-Kaula-āgamas: immortality arises, O Devī; he conquers death — no doubt. The death-conqueror has been told, O Devī, the determination of all knowledge: it must be guarded with effort in the mortal world, from the lowest men. The hard-to-obtain mass of siddhis must be guarded with effort: it is to be given to the one already perfected, tested for a full year.

## Paṭala 6 — the determination of the jīva (6/1–6/7 + opening)

**6/1-6/3** — adya me saphalaṃ janma mama dehe kuleśvara | tvatprasādena kauleśa jñāto'haṃ kaulanirṇayaḥ || anyāni jñātumicchāmi vada me jīvalakṣaṇam | ko'sau jīvaḥ samuddiṣṭaḥ kiṃ vā jīvasvarūpakam || kiṃ varṇaṃ kimpramāṇañca kasmin sthāne vyavasthitam | kasmiṃścaiva layaṃ yāti tatsarvaṃ kathayasva me ||
> Today my birth bears fruit, in my body, O Lord of the kula; by your grace, O Lord of the Kaula, I have known the Kaula-determination. I wish to know the other [teachings] — tell me the mark of the jīva: what is this jīva that is taught? What is the jīva's own form? What color, what measure, in what station does it stand? And into what does it go to dissolution? — all that, tell me.

**6/4-6/5** — śṛṇu tvaṃ vīracāmuṇḍe varaṃ jīvasya nirṇayam | sa(ḥ)paraṃ niṣkalaṃ nityaṃ nirāmayanirañjanam || paramāṇumucyate nātho sa śivo vyāpakaḥ paraḥ | sa(ḥ) jīvaḥ parataro yastu sa(ḥ) haṃsaḥ śaktipudgala(ḥ) ||
> Hear, O Vīracāmuṇḍā — the excellent determination of the jīva: it is the supreme, the partless (niṣkala), the eternal, the stainless (nirañjana), free of illness. The Lord is called the atom (paramāṇu); he is Śiva, the pervasive, the supreme. He is the jīva — the higher one; he is the haṃsa, the power-individual (śaktipudgala).

**6/6-6/7** — sa(ḥ) mano matparaṃ prāṇaḥ sa(ḥ) buddhiścittameva ca | samīrapūrako vāyuḥ sarvajīveṣu saṃsthitam(ḥ) || dehasthastiṣṭhate yāvattāvajjīvo'pi gīyate | sa dehatyaktamātreṇa paraṃ śivo nigadyate ||
> It is the mind; the supreme is the prāṇa; it is the intellect and the mind-stuff (citta); the moving-filling wind, standing in all the jīvas. As long as it stands in the body, so long it is called the jīva; by the mere abandonment of the body it is proclaimed the supreme Śiva.

Notes: paṭala 6's thesis: **the jīva IS the stainless, partless, eternal** — "nirāmayanirañjanam" — our glossary's nirañjana in its most explicit soteriological claim; the jīva's identity with Śiva "by the mere abandonment of the body" (dehatyaktamātreṇa). The haṃsa (the swan) — [P: cf. the Kaulāvalīnirṇaya's "keciddhaṃsapadaṃ nirañjanapadaṃ" (from our earlier greps) — the same haṃsa/nirañjana pairing across the Matsyendra-school texts.]
