## The pīṭha-arithmetic (33–41)

**g.33** — mANikyamaratakayoriva bhAvAbhAvayorbhedapratibhAsam | ekaraso.anyonya dvayorapyunmArShTi (dvayoH pramArShTi) sphuraNasambhedaH ||
> Like the ruby and the emerald: the appearance of the distinction of being-and-non-being — the one-taste, mutually, of the two, the flashing's distinction sweeps away (unmārṣṭi).

**g.34** — aNDamaye nijapiNDapIThe sphuranti karaNadevyaH | prasphurati cha paramashivo j~nAnanidhishcha tAsAM madhye ||
> In the egg-made [universe], in one's own body-seat, the instrument-goddesses flash; and the supreme-Śiva flashes, the knowledge-treasure, in the midst of them.

**g.35** — sa tatrArchanIyo vimarshapuShpAdhivAsasurabhibhiH | chittachaShakArpitairvedyasudhAvIrapAnavastubhiH ||
> He is there to be worshipped with the fragrance of the reflexion-flowers' perfume, with the oblations of the experienced-nectar-drink offered in the cup of the mind.

**g.36** — shrIpIThapa~nchavAho netratrayavR^indachakrANi smarata | smarata cha gurUNAM pa~Nkti pa~ncha cha shaktIH sR^iShTipramukhAH ||
> Remember the blessed seat's five currents (pañcavāha), the wheels of the three-eye-hosts; remember the gurus' row and the five powers headed by creation (sṛṣṭi).

**g.37** — pIThe kalA nava pa~nchaiva pa~nchavAhapadavyAm | saptadasha phA(bhA)lanetre dvAdasha ShoDasha chAnyanetrayoH ||
> In the seat: the nine kalās and the five in the five-currents' path; the seventeen in the phāla-eye, the twelve and the sixteen in the other two eyes.

**g.38** — prakaTitapa~nchaskandhe chatuShShaShTirbhavanti vR^indachakre | na khalu maNDale gurUNAM niyamo niyamAtila~NghinA (niyamAnalaM vinA) yuktaH ||
> In the revealed five-branched [tree], in the host-wheel, the sixty-four come to be; in the gurus' maṇḍala there is no rule, for the one who overpasses the rule [is not bound]. [variant: "without the rule's lack"]

**g.39** — sR^iShTau dasha kalAH sthitau dvAvishatirbhavanti shaktayaH | ekAdasha saMhAre trayodasha tAsturIyaparvaNi ||
> In creation, the ten kalās; in maintenance, the twenty-two powers; the eleven in dissolution; the thirteen in the fourth-level.

**g.40** — bhAsAyAM na vikalpAH sphurati sphuradekaniShkalashriyAm | yadi pratibimbagatyA sphurati paraM ShoDashAdhikA devI ||
> In the bhāsā (the illumination) there are no alternatives — she flashes in the flashing majesty of the single-partless; if by the reflection's way she flashes, the goddess is the sixteen-and-more (ṣoḍaśādhikā).

**g.41** — sR^iShTeH pa~nchamakalA bhAseti jano gaNayati vyavadhAnam | sR^iShTermUlaskandhe bhAsAyAM pallavaH sR^iShTiH ||
> The fifth kalā of creation is called the bhāsā — people count [it] the interval. In the bhāsā, the root-branch of creation, the sprout [is] creation.

## The shuddhi and the rites (42–46)

**g.42** — nijabalanibhAlanameva varivasyA sA cha durlabhA loke | sulabhAni vishvapaterAsavatAmbUlagandhapuShpANi ||
> The service is the mere tending of one's own power — and that is rare in the world; easy [are] the world-lord's wine, betel, incense, and flowers.

**g.43** — vimraShTuM nijasattvaM vibhave kAryonmukhe stimite.api | bAhyavR^ittAntAnAM bha~NgaH prANasya saMyamo j~neyaH ||
> To reflect on one's own being, in the majesty, even [when] intent on the work and stilled: the breaking of the external activities — that is to be known as the prāṇa's restraint.

**g.44** — shoSho malas.nAsho dAha etasya vAsanochChedaH | AplAvanaM tanUnAM j~nAnasudhAsekanirmitA shuddhiH ||
> The drying of the stain, the destruction, the burning of this, the cutting of the impression: the bathing of the bodies, made by the sprinkling of the knowledge-nectar — the purification.

**g.45** — avikalpatayA vimarsho vikalpavargasyA~NgasannAhaH | arghyaM vedyavilAsaH puShpANi svabhAvapoShakAH bhAvAH ||
> The reflexion [is] by the non-alternative; the armor of the alternative-classes; the arghya [is] the known's display; the flowers [are] the states that nourish the own-nature.

**g.46** — pUrNAhantAyA mukhe vishvavikalpA~NkurANAM vikShepam | mantrollekhavishuddhaM puNyaM kulabindutarpaNaM bhaNAmaH ||
> In the mouth of the full-I-ness, the scattering of the world's alternative-seeds: we declare the mantra-tracing's pure merit, the kula-bindu's libation.

## The mantras and the vāc (47–50)

**g.47** — yo yasya bhAvayogastasya khalu sa eva devatA bhavati | tadbhAvabhAvitA abhilaShita tathA phalanti pratimAH ||
> Whoever's state-union is his — he indeed becomes his deity; the images, made of that state, thus bear the desired [fruits].

**g.48** — chitraM na likhati chitraM chitrakaraH pashyata likhati tachchitram | tad bhaNata kutra yogyA kartuM dvayorapi (riti) devatAbuddhiH ||
> "The painting does not paint the painting; the painter paints — see, he paints that painting." Tell [us] where the deity-intelligence is fit to make, of the two.

**g.49** — mananamayI nijavibhave nijasa~Nkochabhaye trANamayI | kavalitavishvavikalpA anubhUtiH kApi mantrashabdArthaH ||
> Made of thought in one's own majesty, made of protection in the fear of one's own shrinking; having devoured the world's alternatives — some such experience [is] the mantra-word's meaning.

**g.50** — vaikhari(kA) nāma kriyA j~nAnamayI bhavati madhyamA vAk | ichChA punaH pashyantI sUkShmA sarvAsA samarasA vR^ittiH ||
> The vaikharī is the action; the madhyamā-speech is made of knowledge; the will again [is] the paśyantī — the subtle, the equal-taste's activity of all [the levels].

## The yogi's states (51–66)

**g.51** — AnandollAsashrIH kShullakitAShTamahAsiddhisaubhAgyA | dR^ishyate yatra dashAyAM saiva devasya sarva(mahA)mudrAH ||
> The bliss-upsurge's fortune, adorned with the eight great-siddhis' good-luck — in whatever state she is seen, that [is] the god's all-(great-)mudrās.

**g.52** — hR^idayasthAnaprarUDho vimarshakalpadrumo mahAshAkhaH | puShpyati bhogashriyA phalati cha niShkalasukhotsavAlokam ||
> Grown in the heart's place, the reflexion-wish-tree, the great-branched: it flowers with the enjoyment-fortune and fruits with the sight of the partless-bliss's festival.

**g.53** — kramiko bhavati na devastasya kathaM kAlakalmaShasparshaH | nityanirAvaraNasyApi (ko jIvato mokShapratyUhaH) ko.api jIvanmuktipratyUhaH ||
> The god is not sequential (kramika) — how [could there be] for him the touch of time's stain, [who is] ever-uncovered? What obstacle to the living one's liberation [could there be]?

**g.54** — yat kimapi yena kenApi rUpeNa yatra kutrApi kiM nAsti | tasmAdAtmA nityasthiraH kShaNabha~Nga evAsthiro bhavati ||
> Whatever [is], in whatever form, wherever — nothing is not. Therefore the self is ever-stable; the momentary-breaking alone is unstable.

**g.55** — nanvAtmanaH priyArthaM sarvasya priyatvaM bhaNati shrutiH | tasmAdAnandasvabhAva AtmA mukto.amukto vA ||
> "For the self's sake all is dear" — the śruti says. Therefore the self is of the bliss-nature, freed or unfreed.

**g.56** — yadi nijahR^idayollAsaM nirNetuM nityaniShkalamichChA | madhyatuTistruTitavyA asta~NgatayoH somasUryayosttarhi ||
> If the desire [is] to draw out one's own heart's upsurge, the ever-partless — then the middle's crack is to be broken, at the setting of the moon and the sun. [variant: "the middle's crack is to be broken, the weapon, of the moon and sun"]

**g.57** — sthUlatareShvapi prekShadhvaM bhUteShu khasya niShkalAvasthAm | ShaTtriMshikAtila~NghI kIdR^isho bhavatu somanAthaH ||
> Even in the grosser elements, look at the sky's partless-state: crossing the thirty-sixth, what should Somanātha be?

**g.58** — ye kulakumbhasudhAsavapAnamahAmakhotsave pravartante | te khalu vikalpA~NkurasikA upadaShTuM pragalbhante ||
> Those who set out in the great sacrifice-festival of the kula-jar's nectar-wine-drinking — they, indeed, the tasters of the alternative-seeds, are bold to instruct.

**g.59** — hanta mukhaM pratibimbatu pratibimbayatu tathA tadapi darpaNaH | darpaNaH punaryasmin pratibimbati so.api j~nAtavyaH ||
> "Ah — let the face be reflected; let the mirror also reflect that." And the mirror in which it is reflected — that too is to be known.

**g.60** — avikArobhayapArshve chAShapi~njasadR^ikShe.arthe | antarmukho yogI bahirmukha iti kalpanA kutaH ||
> Unchanging on both sides, in the object like the ash-piñja's glance: "the yogi is inner-facing, outer-facing" — whence this imagining?

**g.61** — yogI jAgarasvapnasuShuptyavasthAtrayasya paripATim | chitrAmiva maNimAlAM vimarshasUtraikagumphitAmudvahati ||
> The yogi bears the sequence of the three states — waking, dream, deep-sleep — like a pictured pearl-garland, threaded on the single reflexion-thread.

**g.62** — ullokAnandasudhAshIdhurasodvejitena hR^idayena | abhilaShati lokayAtrAM tintiNicharvaNarasAntaraM yogI ||
> With the heart stirred by the surge of the upward-glance's bliss-nectar, the yogi longs for the world-course — [like one who] tastes the inner savor of the tintiṇī-chewing.

**g.63** — yAbhirgR^ihNAti yogI karaNapraNAlIbhirviShayasaukhyAni | nijahR^idayodvamanashIlAbhiH sphuraNamaya tAbhiH karoti trailokyam ||
> With the instrument-channels by which the yogi grasps the objects' joys — with those, whose nature is the heart's vomiting-out, he makes the three worlds flashing.

**g.64** — yathA tava sthitistathAssva nishchinta iti pratiShThito.arthaH | tatrApyasti viveka evamupadishati ta(ka)sya ko.anyaH ||
> "As is your state, so be — unconcerned" — the established meaning. And in that too there is the discernment — thus he teaches; who else [could]?

**g.65** — aho saMsArasukhakelI aho sulabhaM mokShamArge saubhAgyam | truTitAta~Nkakala~NkA aho shivayoginAM yAmalI siddhiH ||
> Ah, the sport of saṃsāra's joy! Ah, the easy good-luck on the mokṣa-path! Ah, the yāmala-siddhi of the Śiva-yogis, its anxiety's stain broken!

**g.66** — kShaNamAtraspR^iShTenApyamR^itasvabhAvena j~nAnabhAvena | sarvottirNaH sarvaH sarvachiraM labhate sarvasaubhAgyam ||
> Touched for but a moment by the nectar-natured knowledge-state, the one who has crossed all obtains all, for long, all good-luck.

## The close (67–71)

**g.67** — gUDhAdguDhataro yo bhavati sphuTAdapi sphuTatara eShaH | daishikakaTAkShapAte pakShaH prathamo na bhavati dhanyAnAm ||
> He who is more hidden than the hidden, this one more manifest than the manifest: in the teacher's glance's fall, the first wing [is] not [given] to the fortunate.

**g.68** — AstAmanyA vidyAshchatuHsrotasAmapi sAgarANAmiva | enamevAmR^itamayaM mathnAti manthAnabhairavo devaH ||
> Let the other vidyās be — like the seas of the four streams: this nectar-made one alone the Manthānabhairava-god churns.

**g.69** — hanta rahasyaM bhaNAmo muDhA mA bhramata garbhagoleShu | atyAsanna hR^idaya paryAlochayata tasyodyogam ||
> "Ah, we tell the secret — O fools, do not wander in the wombs' globes: the heart is very near — consider its effort."

**g.70** — enameva mahArthaM yuddhArambhe pANDuputrasya | ShoDashasahasrashaktirdeva upadishati sma mAdhavaH ||
> This very great-meaning, at the beginning of the Pāṇḍu-son's war, the god with the sixteen-thousand powers, Mādhava, taught.

**g.71 (the closing praise)** — itthaM prAkR^itasUtrasaptatisamullAsaikasandhAyinIM jAgrattatkShaNanirvisheShasvapnAvatIrNAM pratij~nottarAm | lokolla~NghanayogyasiddhipadavIprasthAnabaddhodyamAM kanthAshUlakapAlamAtravibhavAM vande tAM yoginIm ||
> Thus — the one who unites the seventy mundane-sūtra-upsurges into one, descended from waking, that instant, the non-distinct dream; the answer to the promise; bound to the enterprise of the siddhi-path's going, fit to overleap the world; whose wealth is only the throat-bone and the skull — I praise that yoginī.
