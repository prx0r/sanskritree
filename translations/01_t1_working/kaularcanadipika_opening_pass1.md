## The opening (the kulācāra's law)

**The frame** — oṃśrīgaṇeśāyanamaḥ oṃnamaḥparamadevatāyai oṃkuladīpikāyai ||
> Oṃ, homage to the blessed Gaṇeśa; oṃ, homage to the supreme goddess; oṃ, homage to the Lamp of the Kula.

**The fool's course (1)** — oṃkuladharmamanādṛtya paśuśāstrāṇi yo'bhyaset | svagṛhe pāyasaṃ tyaktvā bhikṣāṃ aṭati durmatiḥ ||
> He who, disregarding the kula-dharma, practices the paśu-śāstras — abandoning the milk-rice in his own house, the fool wanders for alms.

**The mirage (2)** — vihāya kuladharmaṃ yaḥ paśudharmaratobhavet | taḍāgādivat tṛṣṇārto mṛgatṛṣṇāṃ sa dhāvati ||
> Abandoning the kula-dharma, he who delights in the paśu-dharma — thirsty for the pond and the rest, he runs after the mirage.

**The constancy (3)** — nāpi lobhān na ca krodhān na hi dveṣān na matsarāt | na kāmān na bhayād vāpi kuladharmaṃ parityajet ||
> Neither from greed, nor from anger, nor from hatred, nor from envy, nor from desire, nor from fear should one abandon the kula-dharma.

**The rank (4)** — caturvedī kulājñānī śvapacād adhamaḥ priye | śvapaco 'pi kulajñānī vrāhmaṇād atiricyate ||
> The knower of the four Vedas who does not know the kula is lower than a śvapaca (a dog-cooker), O dear one; even a śvapaca who knows the kula surpasses the brāhmaṇa.

**The cakra's rule (5-6)** — pravṛtte bhairavīcakre sarve varṇā dvijātayaḥ | nivṛtte bhairavīcakre sarve varṇā pṛthak pṛthak || āmiṣāsu raurabhyahīnaṃ yasya sukhaṃ bhavet | prāyaścittī sa varjyaśca paśur eva na saṃśayaḥ ||
> When the Bhairavī-cakra is begun, all the varṇas are twice-born; when the Bhairavī-cakra is ended, all the varṇas are separate. He whose delight is [merely] in meat and wine, without [the deeper] essence — he is to be excluded from the prāyaścitta; he is a paśu, no doubt.

**The pūjā-degrees (7)** — uttamā nitya-pūjā syād medhyamaṃ pūrva-pūjanaṃ | māsa-pūjā dhamā devī māsād ūrddhaṃ paśur bhavet ||
> The supreme is the daily worship; the mediocre is the fortnightly worship; the monthly worship [is] the low; beyond a month, [the practitioner] is a paśu.

**The pañcatattva's power (8)** — kāmākhyātantre pañcatattvena deveśi mahāpātakarāśayaḥ | naśyanti tatkṣaṇenaiva tūlarāśim ivānalaḥ ||
> In the Kāmākhyā-tantra: by the pañcatattva, O Devī, the heaps of great sins perish in that very instant, like a heap of cotton [in] fire.

**The goddess's seat (9)** — yatraiva pañcatattvāni tatra devī vaset bhuvi | pañcatattvavihīne tu vimukhī jagadambikā ||
> Wherever the pañcatattvas are, there the Devī dwells on earth; in [a rite] without the pañcatattvas, the mother of the world turns away.

**The age-question (10-11)** — jambudvīpe kalau devī vrāhmaṇo hi viśeṣataḥ | paśur nāsyāt paśur na syāt paśur nāsyāc chivājñayā || nānukalpaḥ kalau durge nānukalpaḥ kalau yuge | na sandeho na sandeho na sandehaḥ kalau yuge ||
> In the Kali-age, in Jambudvīpa, O Devī, especially the brāhmaṇa: the paśu is not [possible], the paśu should not be, the paśu is not [possible] by Śiva's command. There is no anukalpa (substitute-rite) in the Kali-age, O Durgā — no substitute in the Kali-age. No doubt, no doubt, no doubt in the Kali-age.

**The seed-analogies (12-13)** — śilāyāṃ sasyavāpaś ca na bhaved aṅkuro yathā | anāvṛṣṭau kṣitau devi karṣaṇaṃ ca yathā nahi || ṛtuṃ vinā striyāṃ devi kuto 'patyaṃ prajāyate | gamanaṃ ca vinā devi sthānaprāptir yathā na ca ||
> As no sprout arises from sowing seed in stone; as plowing is futile in a rainless land, O Devī — so without the season, no offspring is born in a woman, O Devī; and without the union, there is no reaching of the [womb's] place.

**The rite's futility (14-15)** — ato devyāḥ sādhanaṃ ca pañcatattvaṃ vinā vṛthā | kālītārāmanuṃ prāpya vīrācāraṃ karoti na || śūdratvaṃ taccharīre tu prāptaṃ tena na cānyathā | kṣatriyo 'pi tathā devi vaiśyaś caṇḍālatāṃ vrajet ||
> Therefore the Devī's sādhana without the pañcatattva is vain: having obtained the Kālī-Tārā-mantra, [if one] does not do the vīrācāra — śūdra-ness has been attained in that body, by that, and not otherwise; even a kṣatriya, O Devī, and a vaiśya would go to caṇḍāla-ness.

**The śūdra's fate (16)** — śūdraś ca śūkaratvaṃ ca yāti yāti na saṃśayaḥ | na sāraḥ kadalīvṛkṣo vaṃśadaṇḍe yathā priya ||
> And the śūdra goes to boar-ness, goes — no doubt. The plantain-tree has no core, like the bamboo-cane, O dear one.

**The hidden vision (17)** — darśanena tathā muktis nāsti nāsti tathā priye | kuloḍḍīśe pūjākālaṃ vinā naiva paśyec chaktiṃ digambarāṃ ||
> And by seeing [her] there is no liberation — none at all, O dear one: in the kuloḍḍīśa, at the worship-time, one should not see the sky-clad Śakti [otherwise].

**The sautrāmaṇī (18)** — āyuṣā hīyate dṛṣṭā pītvā ca narakaṃ vrajet | sautrāmaṇyāṃ kulācāre vrāhmaṇaḥ prapibet surāṃ ||
> Seen [improperly], one's life is lessened; having drunk [improperly], one goes to hell. In the sautrāmaṇī, in the kulācāra, the brāhmaṇa should drink the surā.

**The yuga-sequence (19-21)** — anyatra kāmataḥ pītvā prāpyaścittīyate dhruvaṃ | taduktaṃ rudrajāmale satye kramāc caturvarṇaiḥ kṣīrājyamadhu piṣṭakaiḥ || tretāyāṃ pūjitā devī ghṛtena sarvajātibhiḥ | madhubhiḥ sarvavarṇaiś ca pūjayed vā pare yuge || pūjanīyā kalau devī kevalair āsavaiś cataiḥ | kulāmṛte ca sautrāmaṇyāṃ kulācāre vrāhmaṇaḥ prapibet surāṃ ||
> Otherwise, drinking at will, one surely undergoes the prāyaścitta. This is said in the Rudrajāmala: in the Satya[-age], in order, by the four varṇas [she is worshipped] with milk, ghee, honey, and cakes; in the Tretā, the Devī is worshipped with ghee by all the classes; in the Dvāpara one should worship with honey by all the varṇas; in the Kali, the Devī is to be worshipped with the spirituous liquors (āsavas) alone. And in the kula-nectar, in the sautrāmaṇī, in the kulācāra, the brāhmaṇa should drink the surā.

**The inner purity (22-24)** — anyatra kāmataḥ pītvā prāyaścittīyate dhruvaṃ | atrāpi saṃśayo nāsti uḍḍīśottarakhaṇḍe | kintu vatsa vinā hetuṃ cetaḥ kasmāt prasīdati || antaḥśuddhir manaḥśuddhir hetuśūnyā kathaṃ bhavet | surāhetuḥ kāraṇaṃ ca paramāmṛtam eva ca || jñānadā śubhadā tīrthaṃ madirā modinī satī | kuladā kula-gaṅgā ca pāvanī tāriṇīti ca ||
> Otherwise, drinking at will, one surely undergoes the prāyaścitta; here there is no doubt — in the Uḍḍīśottarakhaṇḍa: but, O child, without the cause, how would the mind be pleased? The inner purity, the mind's purity — how could it be, being causeless? The surā is the cause and the means, the supreme nectar itself. The wine (madirā) gives knowledge, gives good, is the sacred-ford, the rejoicing, the virtuous; gives the kula, is the kula-Gaṅgā, the purifying, the ferry (tāriṇī) indeed.

**The wine-goddess (25-26)** — mamānāmani gopyāni jānīhi kula-nandana | tatsvarūpāṃ madonmattāṃ madirāṃ śaṃkhabhūṣaṇāṃ || dhyāyet prītimukhīṃ smaret sudatīṃ śubhadāṃ śubhaṃ | locanenobhivīkṣethāḥ sadā śāntena cetasā ||
> Know these, my secrets, O child of the kula: the wine as her own form — the intoxicated, shell-adorned. One should meditate on her, the loving-faced, the fair-toothed, the good-giving; with a pleased eye one should gaze on her, always, with a peaceful mind.

**The cause's necessity (27-28)** — jñānadā cāmaheśāni samāśreyā yuge yuge | hetuśūnyāḥ kriyā naṣṭānām antaḥśuddhir na vā sukhaṃ || dehaṃ vṛthāiva jānīhi vatsa mat-sādhanaṃ vinā | sādhanaṃ ca vṛthā putra hetu-vādaṃ vinā śṛṇu ||
> She gives knowledge, O great Lady — to be resorted to in every age: for those whose works are ruined, being causeless, [there is] no inner purity nor ease. Know the body as vain, O child, without my sādhana; and the sādhana is vain, O son, without the cause-teaching — hear:

**The japa (29)** — putra kevala-jāpena siddhiḥ syād iti niścitaṃ | tasmāj japamahādevi modinīmuditaḥ sadā ||
> "By japa alone, O son, siddhi arises" — this is certain; therefore, O great Devī, [let him] rejoice in the japa of Modinī always.

**The tarinī (30-32)** — brahmahatyādipāpāni janmajanmakṛtānyapi | naśyanti bindumātreṇa tāriṇīrasanāgatā || aiśvaryādilatāpūjā ratipūjā ca muktidā | vīrāṇāṃ sādhanaṃ kāmyaṃ siddhaye bhāvanājapaḥ || na bhāvena vinā siddhir na bhāvena vinā japaḥ | na bhāvena vinā hetuḥ sarvaṃ bhāvapratiṣṭhitaṃ ||
> The sins of brahmahatyā and the rest, even those done birth after birth, perish by a single drop of the Tārinī taken on the tongue. The worship of the Aiśvaryādi-latā and the Rati-worship give liberation; the vīras' sādhana is the desire [fulfiller]; for siddhi, the bhāvanā-japa. Without the bhāva (the felt-intention), no siddhi; without the bhāva, no japa; without the bhāva, no cause — everything is established in the bhāva.

**The bhāva's truth (33-35)** — atastu tāriṇīṃ vatsa matamā tejaḥsvarūpiṇīṃ | jānīhi bhakti saṃyuktātmā āśraya vidhānataḥ || sudharmasevanādvatsa naite doṣā bhavanti hi | anye ca bahavo doṣā naśyanti yuktiyogataḥ || śāpāś cañcalacittānāṃ brahmādīnām ihoditāḥ | mantreṇa ca samuddhṛtya amapūjā paro bhava ||
> Therefore know the Tārinī, O child, as my form, of the nature of tejas; [let] the self, joined with devotion, resort [to her] by the rite. From serving the good-dharma, O child, these faults do not arise; and the many other faults perish by the fitting means. The curses of the fickle-minded, of Brahmā and the rest, are declared here: having drawn them up with the mantra, be devoted to the am[a]-pūjā [X: "ama" uncertain].

**The body's purity (36-37)** — mamasvarūpāṃ tāṃ devīṃ pavitrāṃ viddhi sarvadā | tayā cinnāmano nityaṃ mālinyaṃ na parityajet || parityakte ca mālinye mano dehe svarūpabhāk | ataḥ surāṃ samāśritya sādhanaṃ kuruyatnataḥ ||
> Know that goddess, my own form, as ever pure; with her, [even] the mind's stain one should not abandon [thinking] the name forever. When the stain is abandoned, the mind becomes the self-natured in the body; therefore, resorting to the surā, one should perform the sādhana with effort.

**The secrecy (38-40)** — aprakāśaṃ guhyatamaṃ sukhaṃ sarvaviśodhanaṃ | prakāśayen na devatvā prakaṭaṃ kriyate thavā || kulaṃ tasya nāśayāmi tadraktaṃ prīyate gaṇaiḥ | atuttarakhaṇḍīye viramatvaṃ maheśvara || kulaprakāśo nāśāya siddhaye parigopanaṃ | evameva vilokyaivaṃ bhava śāṃto 'ca bhairava ||
> [This is] the non-public, the most secret, the joy that purifies all; one should not reveal it — the devatā [does not reward] what is made public. His kula I destroy; the Gaṇas delight in its blood. In the Uttarakaṇḍīya, "the abandoning-ness, O Maheśvara"; the kula's revelation is for destruction; for siddhi, concealment. Having looked on this very [law], be at peace, O Bhairava.

**The great goddess's turn (41-42)** — śrīśivuvāca | praṇatosmi mahākāli kṛpayā vadasūcitaṃ | yenadhvāṃtā 'mayād devi vimucye mohito hyahaṃ || pāṣāṇḍādūṣakāyesyu stastrāhaṃ na prakāśavān | bhaviṣyāmi na pāṣaṇḍo mahāpāṣaṇḍakhaṇḍini ||
> The blessed Śiva said: "I bow, O Mahākālī; by [your] grace the indication is spoken — by which I am freed from my darkness and error, O Devī, I who am deluded. Among the defilers of the heretics, I am not the revealer; I will not be a heretic, O cutter of the great heretics.

**The surā's necessity (43-44)** — na vināsurayā siddhir na dināsurayā manuḥ | na vināsurayā pūjā surāhīno 'suro bhavet || surāṃ vinā bhramo naiva dūrī bhavati śaṅkara | bhramabuddher ahaṃ dūre tasmāt surāśrito bhava ||
> Without the surā, no siddhi; without the surā, no mantra; without the surā, no worship; one devoid of the surā becomes an asura. Without the surā, the error is not driven away, O Śaṅkara; I am far from the erroneous mind — therefore be one who resorts to the surā.

**The Mahācīna (45-46)** — mahācīnakrameṇaiva varttethāḥ sarvadā śiva | sthiracittena śāntena kartavyaṃ yaminā tvayā || ādau kulāmṛtaṃ pānaṃ tataḥ śuddhyati mānasaṃ | kulīnaḥ sarvadā śreṣṭaḥ sarvayogeśvareśvaraḥ ||
> By the Mahācīna-order alone you should ever proceed, O Śiva; with a steady mind, with a peaceful one, the work is to be done by you, the yamin. First the kula-nectar's drinking; then the mind is purified; the kulīna (the kula-born) is ever the best, the lord of the lords of all the yogas.

**The secrecy (47-48)** — śrīśyāmovāca | vatsa gopyaṃ punargopyaṃ punargopyaṃ punaḥ punaḥ | agopanāt siddhihānir ity evaṃ kathitaṃ tvayi || śṛṇu bhairava mūlaṃ hi jñānam aikyaṃ yathottaraṃ | striyaṃ vīkṣan spṛśan paśyan surāghūrṇitalocanaḥ ||
> The blessed Śyāma said: "O child, the secret, again the secret, the secret again and again: from non-concealment, the siddhi's loss — so it has been told to you. Hear, O Bhairava: the root is knowledge; the union, as the higher [teaching]: looking at the woman, touching, seeing, with the eyes rolling in the surā —

**The japa (49-50)** — anudvignamanā vatsa kevalaṃ japam ācara | kevalaṃ mātṛrūpeṇatāṃ paśyet parinanditaḥ || japo hi sarvajñānānām aham eva na saṃśayaḥ | yonitavre maithunena vinā muktir neti śāstrasya nirṇayaḥ ||
> — with an unagitated mind, O child, perform the japa alone; looking at her in the mother-form alone, rejoicing. The japa is I myself, of all the knowledges — no doubt. 'In the yoni-rite, without the union, there is no liberation' — this is the śāstra's determination.

**The scriptures' purpose (51-53)** — śrutismṛtipurāṇāni kṛtāni vividhāni ca | paśūnāṃ buddhināśāya śṛṇuṣvaprāṇavallabhe || paramānandarūpeṇa bhajet yoniṃ sakuntalāṃ | viśeṣataḥ kaliyuge yonirūpāṃ jaganmayīṃ || yo bhajet parayā śaktyā tasya muktiḥ karesthitā | dakṣiṇā tāriṇīsiddhir naiva cīnakramaṃ vinā ||
> The śrutis, smṛtis, and purāṇas, made manifold — [they are] for the destruction of the paśus' minds — hear, O my life's darling. In the form of the supreme bliss one should worship the yoni, the Sakuntalā; especially in the Kali-age, the yoni-formed, the world-made. He who worships [her] with the supreme power — liberation stands in his hand; the dakṣiṇā, the Tārinī-siddhi [comes] only with the Cīna-krama.

**The own-mantra (54-55)** — svasmin mantra yācāraḥ sevā paramo mataḥ | phalahānis tvaviśvāsāt tasmād bhāvaparo bhavet || yadatralikhitaṃ devi tantre ca yonisaṃjñike | tatsarvaṃ sādhakānāṃ ca kartavyaṃ bhāvamichatāṃ ||
> In one's own mantra, the practice, the service — this is held supreme; the fruit's loss [comes] from distrust — therefore become intent on the bhāva. Whatever is written here, O Devī, in this yoni-named tantra — all that is to be done by the sādhakas who wish the bhāva.

**The supreme secret (56-59)** — śṛṇudhvaṃ parayā bhaktyā sāvadhānāvadhāraya | yasyaikasmain na vārtavyaṃ prāṇānte ca na saṃśayaḥ | svayonir iva deveśi gopanīyā sadā priye || na gṛḍhaṃ te pravakṣyāmi satyaṃ satyaṃ suniścitaṃ | yasyānuṣṭānamātreṇa bhavābdhau na nimajjati || yonirūpā mahāmāyā liṃgarūpaḥ sadāśivaḥ | retasā tarpaṇaṃ tatra madhyair māṃsaiś ca sundari || yonyāṃ liṅgaṃ samākṣipya śaktyaisarvaṃ samarpayet ||
> Hear with supreme devotion; attend carefully: this is not to be spoken to anyone — even at life's end, no doubt. Like one's own yoni, O Devī, it is ever to be concealed, O dear one. I will declare to you, not in secret: truly, truly, well-certain — by the mere performance of which one does not sink in the ocean of existence. The yoni-formed is the great māyā; the liṅga-formed is Sadāśiva. With the seed, the libation there; with the middle, and with the meats, O fair one: having thrown the liṅga into the yoni, one should offer all to the Śakti.

**The yoni's grace (60-62)** — tatvenatoṣayed devīṃ bhagarūpāṃ jaganmayīṃ | pratyāghātena deveśa brahmahatyā vyapohati || nālpapuṇyavatāṃ durge viśvāso jāyate dhruvaṃ | sarvasādhanamadhyetu yonisādhanam uttamaṃ || bhuktāpītvā ca deveśi yadi yoniṃ prapūjayet | koṭijanmārjitaṃ pāpaṃ tatkṣaṇādeva naśyati ||
> By the truth [of the rite] one should please the Devī, the bhaga-formed, the world-made; by the counter-stroke (pratyāghāta), O Devī, it removes brahmahatyā. For those of small merit, O Durgā, trust [in this] is surely not born. Among all the sādhanas, the yoni-sādhana is the supreme. If, having enjoyed and drunk, O Devī, one then worships the yoni — the sin earned over crores of births perishes in that very moment.

**The bhogin's mokṣa (63-66)** — bhogena labhate mokṣaṃ bhogena labhate sukhaṃ | tasmāt sarvaprayatnena sādhako bhogavān bhavet || yoninindāṃ ghṛṇāṃ lajjāṃ matim ātvarjayet sadā | kulācāraprasaṃgena yadi yoniṃ na pūjayet || kiṃ tasya sādhanairlakṣaiḥ sarvantasya vṛthā bhavet | yonidarśanamātreṇa muktaḥ koṭikulaiḥ saha || candrasūryoparāge ca yadi yoniṃ prapūjayet | tarpaṇaṃ yonitatvena punarjanma na vidyate ||
> By the bhoga one obtains mokṣa; by the bhoga one obtains joy. Therefore, with every effort, the sādhaka should be a bhogin. The yoni's censure, disgust, shame — [that] mind one should always reject. If, in the kulācāra's context, one does not worship the yoni — what of his lakhs of sādhanas? All of his [works] would be vain. By the mere seeing of the yoni one is freed, with crores of kulas. And at the eclipse of sun and moon, if one worships the yoni, [making] the libation by the yoni-principle — there is no rebirth.

**The yoni-mūla (67-69)** — yonimūle vased devī yonyāṃ ca naga-nandinī | kālītārā yonicakre kuntale chinnamastakā || bhāvacūḍāmaṇau pīyate khādyate yadyat tatsarvaṃ kuṇḍalīmukhe | hutvā siddhim avāpnoti na ca bandhena badhyate ||
> The Devī dwells at the yoni's root; in the yoni, the mountain-Nandinī; Kālī and Tārā in the yoni-cakra; in the Kuṇṭala, the headless (Chinnamastā). In the Bhāvacūḍāmaṇi: whatever is drunk and eaten, all that [is] in the Kuṇḍalinī's mouth; having offered it, one attains siddhi and is not bound by the bond.
