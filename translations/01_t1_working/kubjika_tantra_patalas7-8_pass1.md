# Paṭala 7 — the bhāvas and conduct

## The question and the three bhāvas (7.1–7.7)
**7/1-7/2** — devadeva mahādeva parameśa purātana | bhāvañca śrotumicchāmi devatāyā viśeṣataḥ || kena bhāvena deveśa devatā ca prasīdati | tatsarvaṃ śrotumicchāmi kathyatāṃ prāṇavallabha ||
> God of gods, great god, ancient supreme Lord — I wish to hear the bhāva (state) of the deity specifically. By what state, O Lord of gods, does the deity become pleased? All that I wish to hear — tell it, O beloved of life.

**7/3** — bhadraṃ bhadraṃ pravakṣyāmi bhadraṃ bhadraṃ śuciśmite | tava snehāt pravakṣyāmi sāvadhānena sundari ||
> Good, good — I will tell; good, good, O pure-smiling one. Out of love for you I will tell, with attention, O lovely one:

**7/4-7/5** — yad yajjñātvā viriñciśca sṛṣṭikartā na saṃśayaḥ | pālanaṃ kurute viṣṇū rūdraśca nāśakaḥ punaḥ || jagadvaśyañca kurute jñātvā tu sādhakottamaḥ | sa eva vandyo lokesmin sa eva puruṣottamaḥ ||
> Knowing which, Viriñci [Brahmā] is the creator — no doubt; Viṣṇu performs preservation; Rudra the destroyer. Knowing which, the best of sādhakas makes the world subject [to him] — he alone is to be revered in this world; he alone is the supreme person (puruṣottama).

**7/6-7/7** — bhāvaśca trividhodevi divyavīrapaśukramāt | viśvañca devatārūpaṃ bhāvayet surasundari || strīrūpañca jagat sarvaṃ pūruṣaṃ śivarūpiṇam | hṛdaye pārvatīṃ devīṃ śirasā śivarūpiṇam ||
> And the bhāva is threefold, O Devī, in the order divine-hero-bound (divya-vīra-paśu). The world is of the deity's form — one should contemplate, O lovely goddess. All the world is of woman-form (strīrūpa); the man is of Śiva-form: in the heart the goddess Pārvatī; in the head the Śiva-formed one.

Notes: the **bhāva-triple** — the soteriological classes of the Kaula (divya/vīra/paśu) — [P: our glossary's paśu in its full class-form; the strīrūpa/śivarūpa pair — the world-as-woman doctrine (cf. the Azeṣakulavallarī's strī-varga and the Kulacūḍāmaṇi's goddess-list)].

## The conduct of the divine-state (7.8–7.18)
**7/8-7/10** — abhedecintayedyastu sa eva devatātmakaḥ | nityasnānaṃ nityadānaṃ trisandhyāñca japārcanam || vimalaṃ vasanaṃ devi paridhānaṃ samācaret | vede śāstre dṛḍhajñānaṃ gurau deve tathaiva ca || mantrecaiva dṛḍhajñānaṃ pitṛdevārcanantathā | vaiśvadevantathā śrāddhaṃ nityaṃ kāryāṃ śucismite ||
> Whoever contemplates non-difference (abheda) — he is of the deity's nature: the daily bath, the daily giving, the three-times japa and worship. Pure garments, O Devī, one should wear; firm knowledge in the Veda and śāstra, in the guru and the god; firm knowledge in the mantra; the ancestor-and-god worship; the vaiśvadeva and the śrāddha — daily they should be done, O pure-smiling one.

**7/11-7/13** — śatrau mitre samaṃ devi cintayettu maheśvari | annañcaiva maheśāni sarveṣāṃ parivarjayet || gurorannaṃ maheśāni bhoktavyaṃ sarvasiddhaye | kadaryañca mahādevi niṣṭhuraṃ parivarjayet || devatānindakaṃ dṛṣṭvā nālāpañca samācaret | satyañca kathayeddevi na mithyāñca kadācana ||
> In enemy and friend alike, O Devī, one should contemplate, O great Īśvarī. The food of all others, O great Īśānī, one should avoid; the guru's food, O great Īśānī, is to be eaten for the sake of all siddhi. The miserly, O great Devī, and the harsh, one should avoid. Seeing the deity-slanderer, one should not converse. And one should speak truth, O Devī, never falsehood.

**7/14-7/18** — kevalaṃ divyabhāvena pūjayet surasundarīm | gurorārādhanaṃ devi pratyahaṃ cintayet sudhīḥ || sarvatra devatāvandyaṃ parameṣṭisvarūpakam | ekagrāme sthitaṃ nityaṃ trisandhyaṃ praṇamedgurum || gurutulyaṃ maheśāni namakuryādvarānane | strīṇāṃ padatalaṃ dṛṣṭvā guruvadbhāvayet sadā || śrīkhaṇḍapaskaruṣitaṃ bhūṣitaṃ sumanoharam | śarīraṃ kārayeddevi gandharvadevamuttamam || ālāpaṃ svastriyāsārddhaṃ parayoṣāṃ vivarjayet | nityaṃ satvaguṇenaiva pūjayet sūrasundarīm ||
> With the divine-state alone one should worship the lovely goddess. The guru's adoration, O Devī, the wise one should contemplate daily. Everywhere [one sees] the deity-worthy, of the supreme-offering's form: dwelling in one village, daily, at the three times, he should bow to the guru. Equal-to-the-guru, O great Īśānī, he should salute, O fair-faced one. Seeing the soles of women's feet, he should always contemplate them as the guru. With sandal, adorned, lovely — the body one should make, O Devī, like the supreme god of the gandharvas. Conversation with one's own wife — and other women one should avoid; with the sattva-guṇa always he should worship the lovely goddess.

Notes: the divya-bhāva conduct: the abheda-contemplation as the core; the guru-deity-identity (strīṇāṃ padatalaṃ — the feet of women as the guru!); the sattva-worship. [P: the guru-elevation — cf. our KJN 4/20's guru-transmission and the ŚS 2.6's gurur-upāyaḥ.]

## The vīra-bhāva (7.19–7.31)
**7/19-7/21** — bīrabhāvaṃ maheśāni śṛṇuṣva haravallabhe | devatā ca mahādevi nānyosti mama dehake || raktavastraparidhānaṃ raktamālyānulepanam | tripuṇḍaṃ bhasmanā vāpi raktacandanakena vā || rudrākṣabhūṣitodevi sarvāṅge ca maheśvari | kevalaṃ bhairavobhūtvā yajeddevīṃ sanātanīm ||
> The hero-state (vīrabhāva), O great Īśānī — hear, O beloved of Hara: the deity, O great Devī, is none other than my own body. Red garments as the dress, red garlands and anointing; the tripuṇḍa with ash, or with red sandal; adorned with rudrākṣa on every limb, O great Īśvarī: becoming Bhairava alone, he should sacrifice to the eternal Devī.

**7/22-7/25** — pratyahañca valindadyāddevatābhāvasiddhaye | samayāñca gṛhītvā tu pūjādau ca maheśvari || niśīthe pūjanañcaiva kartavyañca maheśvari | kulavṛkṣantathā dṛṣṭvā kartavyañca maheśvari || praṇāmaṃ vandanañcaiva pratyahañca maheśvari | rātrau ca pūjayeddevīṃ na divāpi kadācana || rātrau tāmbulapūrṇāsyojapenmantraṃ maheśvari | sarvañcaiva maheśāni paradevesamarpayet ||
> Daily he should give the bali-offering for the attainment of the deity-state; having taken the samaya (the vow), at the start of the worship, O great Īśvarī. At midnight the worship is to be done, O great Īśvarī; seeing the kula-tree, [the worship] is to be done, O great Īśvarī. The bow and the homage, daily, O great Īśvarī. At night he should worship the Devī — never by day at any time. At night, with the mouth full of betel, he should chant the mantra, O great Īśvarī. And everything, O great Īśānī, he should offer to the supreme deity (paradeveśa).

**7/26-7/31** — paśubhāvaratā ye ca kevalaṃ paśurūpiṇaḥ | rātrau yantrañca mālāñca naspūśettu kadācana || saṃśayo validāne ca tantre ca saṃśayaḥ sadā | mantre cākṣaravuddhiñca aviśvāsogurau sadā || pratimāyāṃ śilāvuddhirbhedakodaivate sadā | nirāmiṣeṇa deveśi devatāyāḥ prapūjanam || ajñānena sadā snānaṃ pratyahaṃ dehabhāḍanam | sarveṣāñcaiva nindāñca mantra kūryānmaheśvari || sa eva paśubhāvena cyadhamaḥ parikīrtitaḥ | uttamodivyabhāvaśca vīrabhāvaśca madhyamaḥ || adhamaḥ paśubhāvaśca devi satyaṃ na saṃśayaḥ | paśubhāve sthitā mantrāḥ kevalā varṇarūpiṇaḥ ||
> Those who delight in the paśu-state — they are merely of the paśu-form: at night they should never touch the yantra and the mālā. Doubt in the offering, and in the tantra, is always doubt; the intellect that sees letters in the mantra, and distrust of the guru, always [is doubt]; the intellect that sees stone in the image, and division toward the deity, always [is division]. With the meatless [offering], O Lady of the gods, is the worship of the deity [in the paśu-state]; with ignorance, always the bath — the daily adorning of the body; and the blaming of all — one should not do, O great Īśvarī. He is proclaimed the lowest by the paśu-state. The divine-state is the highest; the hero-state is the middle; the paśu-state is the lowest, O Devī — truly, no doubt. In the paśu-state the mantras stand as mere letter-forms (varṇarūpiṇaḥ).

Notes: the three-state ethics: the vīra's night-worship, the bali, the betel-mouth japa; the paśu's disqualifiers (the meatless worship, the stone-seeing, the letter-seeing — the mantras dead in the paśu's hands). [P: the paśu/pati pair of our glossary in its class-form; the samaya (the vow) — cf. the ŚS 2.6's gurur-upāyaḥ and the samayācāra of the KJN.]

## The kula-conduct and the kumārī (7.32–7.46)
**7/32-7/34** — dṛṣṭvā kulaṃ maheśāni kulavṛkṣaṃ viśeṣataḥ | kulagehaṃ mahādevi namaskuryādvarānane || kūlastriyaṃ mahādevi tantramantraviśāradām | dūtīyāgaratāñcaiva veśyāmvā puṃścalīntathā | vṛddhāṃ vā yuvatīṃ vāpi namaskūryādvarānane ||
> Seeing the kula, O great Īśānī, and the kula-tree especially; the kula-house, O great Devī — one should salute, O fair-faced one. The kula-woman, O great Devī, skilled in tantra and mantra; the dūtī (messenger), the courtesan (veśyā), the wanton (puṃścalī); the old or the young — one should salute, O fair-faced one.

**7/35-7/39** — kumārīpūjanāccaiva sadā bhaktiyutobhavet | pañcavaryaṃ samārabhya yāvaddvādaśavārṣikī || kumārī sā bhaveddevi nijarūpaprakāśinī | ṣaḍvarṣañca samārabhya yāvacca navavārṣikī || tāvaccaiva maheśāni sādhakābhīṣṭasiddhaye | aṣṭavarṣaṃ samārabhya yāvattrayodaśavārṣikī || yuvatīṃ tāṃ vijānīyāttatra pūjāṃ samācaret | daśavarṣaṃ samārabhya yāvat ṣoḍaśavārṣikī || yuvatīṃ tāṃ vijānīyāddevītulyāṃ vicintayet ||
> And with the worship of the virgin (kumārī) one should ever be devotion-joined. From the fifth year up to the twelfth, she is the kumārī, O Devī, shining with her own form. From the sixth to the ninth, [she is] for the sādhaka's desired siddhi, O great Īśānī. From the eighth up to the thirteenth — one should know her as the maiden (yuvatī) and perform the worship there. From the tenth up to the sixteenth — one should know her as the yuvatī and contemplate her as equal to the Devī.

**7/40-7/46** — annaṃ vastraṃ tathā nīraṃ kumāryai yo dadāti hi | annaṃ merusamaṃ devi jalañca nāgaroḥ pamam | vastraiḥ koṭisahasrantu śivaloke mahīyate || pūjopakaraṇañcaiva kumāryai yodadāti hi | santuṣṭā devatā tasya putratvenānukampate || aśokaṃ vilvavṛkṣañca śivāñcaiva sureśvari | dṛṣṭvā prāṇāmaṃ kuryācca sarvakāmārthasiddhaye || bhallukaṃ śaṅyacillañca gṛdhrañcaiva sureśvari | namaskuryānmahākālīṃ yuddhe ca jayamālabhet || catuṣpathaṃ śmaśānañca namaskuryādviśeṣataḥ | tatra gatvāvadhūtañca namaskuryādvarānane || uśāsakaṃ sadā dṛṣṭvā madhuraṃ bhāṣaṇaṃ caret | dānādānañca puṇyaṃ syāt tasyāddānaṃ samācaret || samayāṃ vinā mahādevi na śaktiḥ syāt kadācana | tasmāccaiva pramatnena gṛhṇīyānnātra saṃśayaḥ ||
> Whoever gives food, garments, and water to the kumārī — the food is equal to Meru, O Devī, and the water [equal to] the... [X: "nāgaroḥ pamam" corrupt — possibly "nāgarūpa" — the serpent-form?]; by the garments, a thousand crore, he is magnified in Śiva's world. Whoever gives the worship-apparatus to the kumārī — the deity, pleased, regards him with sonhood. Seeing the aśoka and the bilva-tree, and the śivā [-bird?], O Sūreśvarī, he should make the bow for the attainment of all desires. The bear, the śaṅyacilla [?], and the vulture, O Sūreśvarī — saluting the great Kālī, he gains victory in battle. The crossroads and the burning ground he should salute especially; going there, the avadhūta he should salute, O fair-faced one. Always seeing the alms-man (uśāsaka), he should speak sweetly; the gift's merit — of that one the giving should be performed. Without the samaya, O great Devī, there would never be power at any time — therefore with great effort one should take it; no doubt here.

Notes: the kumārī-arithmetic (the age-classes 5–16 — the virgin-worship of the kula); the auspicious/inauspicious sightings (the aśoka, bilva, the bear/vulture); the samaya's necessity. [P: the kumārī-pūjā — cf. the Dyczkowski-MBT treatment of the Kaula cultus (the virgin-worship and the dūtī); the strīṇāṃ padatalam of 7/16 and the kūlastrī of 7/33 — the woman-as-guru complex.]

## The pīṭha-list (7.47–7.57)
**7/47-7/48** — bhagavana sarvadharmajña sarvāgamaviśārada | siddhapīṭhaṃ mahādeva kathayasva mayi prabho || śruyatāṃ sāvadhānena siddhapīṭhaṃ pativrate | yasmin sādhanamātreṇa sarvasiddhīśvarobhavet ||
> O Lord, knower of all dharmas, skilled in all the āgamas — tell me the siddha-seats (siddhapīṭha), O great god, O Lord. Hear with attention the siddha-seats, O devoted wife: by the mere practice at which one becomes the lord of all siddhis.

**7/49-7/56** — māyāvatī madhupurī kāśī gorakṣakāriṇī | hiṅgulā ca mahāpīṭhaṃ tathā jālandharaṃ punaḥ || jvālāmukhī mahāpīṭhaṃ pīṭhaṃ nagarasambhavam | kāmagirirmahāpīṭhaṃ tathā godāvarī priye || nepālaṃ kālasūtrañca mahāvarṇaṃ tathā priye | ayodhyāñca kurūkṣetraṃ siṃhanādaṃ manoharam || maṇipūraṃ hṛṣīkeśaṃ prayāgañca tapovanam | vadarīñca mahāpīṭhaṃ amvikāmurddhamānakam || triveṇīñca mahāpīṭhaṃ gaṅgāsāgarasaṅgamam | nārīkolañca virajāmuḍḍīyānaṃ maheśvari || kurugrāmaṃ vaidyanāthaṃ jānīyādvāmalocane | kāmarūpaṃ mahāpīṭhaṃ sarvakāmaphala pradam || kalau śīghraphalaṃ devi kāmarūpamidaṃ smṛtam | kāmākhyāvastramādāya japapūjāṃ samācaret || pūrṇaṃ kāmaṃ labheddevi satyaṃ satyaṃ na saṃśayaḥ ||
> Māyāvatī, Madhupurī, Kāśī, Gorakṣa-kāriṇī; Hiṅgulā, the great seat, and Jālandhara; Jvālāmukhī, the great seat, and the Nāgara-born seat; Kāmagiri, the great seat, and Godāvarī, O beloved; Nepāla, Kālasūtra, Mahāvarṇa, O beloved; Ayodhyā and Kurukṣetra, Siṃhanāda the lovely; Maṇipūra, Hṛṣīkeśa, Prayāga, Tapovana; Badarī, the great seat, and Ambikā-Ūrdhvamānaka; Triveṇī, the great seat, and the Gaṅgā-sāgara confluence; Nārīkola and Virajā, Uḍḍīyāna, O great Īśvarī; Kurugrāma, Vaidyanātha — one should know, O soft-eyed one. Kāmarūpa, the great seat, giving the fruit of all desires — in the Kali age this is remembered as the swift-fruit, O Devī: taking the Kāmākhyā-cloth, one should perform japa and worship; the full desire he gains, O Devī — truly, truly, no doubt.

Notes: the pīṭha-catalog (the classic 51-seat list in its Kālīkula recension) — culminating in **Kāmarūpa** (the Kāmākhyā-seat — the great Kālīkula center) as the Kali-age's swift-fruit seat. [P: the pīṭha-list — cf. the MBT's pīṭha-material (Dyczkowski's edition, on disk); the Kāmākhyā-cloth (the red cloth of the goddess) — the dūtī-material of the Kula.]

## The flower-rules (7.57–7.66)
**7/58-7/66** — puṣpasya niyamaṃ devi śṛṇuṣva haravallabhe | yasya dānena deveśi santuṣṭā cāmvikā dhruvam || aśokaṃ padmapuṣpañca campakaṃ nāgakeśaram | dhūstūraṃ mālatīpuṣpaṃ jātīñca yūthikāntathā || vakulaṃ tagarañcaiva madhupuṣpaṃ vasantikām | kahlāraṃ karavīrañca vandhūkañca lavaṅgakam || japāpuṣpañca deveśi raktapuṣpaṃ viśeṣataḥ || aparājitāyā māhātmyaṃ vaktuṃ naiva ca śakyate | vilvapatraṃ maheśāni brahmaviṣṇuśivātmakam || svarṇasya puṣpabhāvasya pradāne yat phalaṃ bhavet | ekena vilvapatreṇa tat phalaṃ labhate dhruvam || javāpuṣpapradānena paṭṭavastraphalaṃ labhet | padmapuṣpapradānena gaurīloke mahīyate || padmapuṣpairjavāpuṣpairhomaṃ kuryādvicakṣaṇaḥ | raktacandanasaṃmiśraṃ raktapuṣpaṃ sureśvari || dadyādbhaktyā sadā devyai vājimedhaphalaṃ labhet | jalajaṃ sthalajaṃ vāpi yacca puṣpaṃ manoharam | dadyāccaiva maheśāni sarvakāmaphalapradam ||
> The flower-rules, O Devī — hear, O beloved of Hara: by the giving of which the goddess Ambikā is surely pleased: the aśoka, the lotus-flower, the campaka, the nāgakeśara; the dhūstūra, the mālatī-flower, the jātī, the yūthikā; the vakula, the tagara, the honey-flower, the vasantikā; the kahlāra, the karavīra, the bandhūka, the clove; and the japā-flower, O Lady of the gods — the red flower especially. The greatness of the aparājitā cannot be told: the bilva-leaf, O great Īśānī, is of Brahmā-Viṣṇu-Śiva nature — whatever fruit the giving of gold's flower-form would have, by a single bilva-leaf one surely gains that fruit. By the giving of the javā-flower he gains the fruit of silk-garments; by the lotus-flower he is magnified in Gaurī's world. With lotus-flowers and javā-flowers the wise one should make the homa. The red flower mixed with red sandal, O Sūreśvarī — giving it with devotion always to the Devī, he gains the horse-sacrifice's fruit. Whatever lovely flower, water-born or land-born — giving it, O great Īśānī, [it] gives the fruit of all desires.

Notes: the flower-taxonomy of the worship; the bilva-leaf as the gold-equivalent (the same claim as 7/40's food=Meru hyperboles). [X: 7/42's "śivā" — the "auspicious" bird or the goddess? — pass 2.]

# Paṭala 8 — the sādhana-paṭala (the Kālī-praxis)

## The frame (8.1–8.2)
**8/1-8/2** — mahādeva jagannātha prāṇeśa parameśvara | sādhakānāṃ hitārthāya sādhanaṃ me prakāśaya || sādhanaṃ vahūvidhaṃ devi kalau purṇaphalapradam | kevalaṃ vīrabhāvena sādhanañca samācaret ||
> O great god, lord of the world, lord of life, supreme Lord — for the good of the sādhakas, reveal the sādhana to me. The sādhana is manifold, O Devī, giving full fruit in the Kali age — but with the hero-state (vīrabhāva) alone should the sādhana be performed.

## The enemy-destructions (8.3–8.33)
**8/3-8/6** — kujavāre mahādevi kṛṣṇāṣṭamyāṃ mahāniśi | catuṣpathe mahāghore ayutaṃ prajapet sudhīḥ || daśākṣaraṃ mahāmantraṃ(?) ugrāyāśca sureśvari | pūjanaṃ kṛṣṇapuṣpeṇa śmaśānakusumena vā || mahācīnakrameṇaiva sarvaṃ kuryāt kuleśvari | juhūyānnīlapuṣpeṇa cāṣṭottaraśataṃ punaḥ || tadtasmāni ca tanmantrairnikṣipet vairiveśmani | daśahābhyantare vairivināśo jāyate dhruvam ||
> On Tuesday, O great Devī, on the black eighth (kṛṣṇāṣṭamī), at the great midnight, at the terrible crossroads, the wise one should chant ten thousand [times]. The ten-syllable great mantra of Ugrā, O Sūreśvarī; the worship with the black flower or the burning-ground flower. By the great Cīna-procedure (mahācīnakrama) alone one should do all, O Lady of the kula; he should offer the homa with the blue flower, 108 times. That... [X: "tadtasmāni" corrupt] he should cast with those mantras into the enemy's house: within ten days the enemy's destruction surely arises.

## The corpse-seat rites (8.34–8.51)
**8/34-8.38** — kujāvāre maheśāni citāmadhyasamāgataḥ | yoginīṃ tatra sampūjya bhairavaṃ pūjayettataḥ || deveśīṃ tatra saṃspūjya tāriṇīṃ tatra pūjayet | kṣetrapālañca saspūjya tāriṇīṃ tatra pūjayet || daśadikṣu kīlakañca kartavyañca sureśvari | viṃśatyakṣaramucārya nāmnomadhye sanātani || pañcasahasraṃ japenmantraṃ yāvaccaiva mahāniśā | tāvajjapaṃ prakurvīta śatrūṇāṃ nāśanāya ca || juhūyādraktapuṣpeṇa śatrūn mardayate kṣaṇāt ||
> On Tuesday, O great Īśānī, coming to the middle of the funeral-pyre: worshipping the Yoginī there, then worshipping Bhairava; worshipping the Deveśī there, worshipping Tāriṇī; worshipping the Kṣetrapāla, then worshipping Tāriṇī. The nailing (kīlaka) in the ten directions is to be made, O Sūreśvarī; uttering the twenty-syllable [mantra] in the middle of the name, O eternal one: he chants the mantra five thousand [times], until the great midnight — so long he makes the japa for the destruction of the enemies; he offers the oblation with the red flower — he crushes the enemies in an instant.

**8/39-8.46** — kujavāre maheśāniśavamādāya yatnataḥ | kīlakaṃ tatra vaddhvā ca mūlamantrairmaheśvari || tatra sañcintayeddevīṃ daityadarpanisūdanīm | bhairavaṃ tatra sampujya śavasnānaṃ samācaret || catvāraḥ kīlakāstatra pūjanañca samācaret | vaṭukaṃ kṣetrapālañca yoginīṃ bhairavīntathā || striyaṃ vinā maheśāni brāhmaṇaṃ yavanantathā | yavanīñca vinā devi śavaṃ sañcintayet sudhīḥ || antyajaśca śavaḥ śreṣṭhaḥ praśastoyuddhakarmaṇi | śavarūpaṃ śivantatra cintayet parameśvari || kāryaṃ devi mahādeva siddhiṃ dehi sadā vibho | mantrasiddhintathā devi rakṣa māṃ śaraṇāgatam || śavarūpamahādeva sarvadeva namostute | paṭhitvaivaṃ mahādevi śavapṛṣṭhaṃ samāhitaḥ || japantatra prakurvīta sarvakāmārthasiddhaye | karālīntatra sampūjya homaṃ kuryādvicakṣaṇaḥ ||
> On Tuesday, O great Īśānī, taking the corpse carefully; having bound the nailing there with the root-mantras, O great Īśvarī — there he should contemplate the Devī, the crusher of the demons' pride. Worshipping Bhairava there, he performs the corpse-bath (śavasnāna). The four nailings are there, and the worship should be performed: the Vaṭuka, the Kṣetrapāla, the Yoginī, the Bhairavī. Without a woman, O great Īśānī, and without a brāhmaṇa and a Yavana; without a Yavanī, O Devī, the wise one should contemplate the corpse. And the corpse of the outcast (antyaja) is the best, praised in the works of war (yuddhakarma). The corpse-form as Śiva — there he should contemplate, O supreme Īśvarī: "The work, O Devī — O great god, give siddhi, always, O Lord; the mantra's success — O Devī, protect me, the refugee. O corpse-formed great god, O god of all gods, homage to you!" — having recited thus, O great Devī, seated on the corpse's back, collected, he makes the japa there for the attainment of all desires; worshipping Karālī there, the wise one makes the homa.

**8/47-8/51** — utakaṭe ca mahādevi kṛṣṇāṣṭamyāṃ kuleśvari | raktavastraṃ samādāya kuladravyairmaheśvari || nagnaḥ śaktisamāyukto bhutvā tu bhairavottamaḥ | kṣakāraṃ pūrvamuddhatya nādabindusamanvitam || dakṣiṇekālike devi kāmabījaṃ tataḥ param | iti mantreṇa deveśi pūjāṃ kuryādviśeṣataḥ || gajāntakasahasrantu japaṃ kuryādvarānane | raktapuṣpeṇa homantu kartavyaṃ varavarṇini || sarvakāmārthasiddhiḥ syāt sa eva bhairavottamaḥ ||
> At the Uṭakaṭa [seat?], O great Devī, on the black eighth, O Lady of the kula: taking the red cloth, with the kula-substances, O great Īśvarī — naked, joined with the power (śakti), becoming the supreme Bhairava — having first uttered the kṣa with the nāda-bindu, then the kāma-bīja, the Dākṣiṇā-Kālikā [mantra] — with this mantra, O Lady of the gods, the worship should be performed especially. The "gajāntaka"-thousand the japa should be made, O fair-faced one; with the red flower the oblation is to be made, O fair-colored one: the attainment of all desires arises — he is the supreme Bhairava.
