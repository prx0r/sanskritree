# Kaula Frame Texts — Kaulajñānanirṇaya (Pp. 2–3) + Akulavīratantra (vv. 1–18) — Pass 1

*Cluster T2 (Kaula core, Matsyendra school), batch pass 1. Same 10th-c. Kaula dialect as our Kubjikā and Śivasūtra work: kula, śakti, bindu, liṅga, nirañjana, samaya.*
*Sources: Kaulajñānanirṇaya — Matsyendranāth (10th c.), Calcutta Sanskrit Series 3, ed. P. C. Bagchi 1934, via Muktabodha M00027 · Akulavīratantra — same Matsyendra bundle, via M00003 · No English anchors exist — first translations.*

---

# I. Kaulajñānanirṇaya, Paṭala 2 — The withdrawal (saṃhāra)

*Frame: Pārvatī has heard the yoga of creation (paṭala 1); she asks for the withdrawal. Bhairava answers.*

**2/1** — devyuvāca: sṛṣṭiyogaṃ mayā jñātaṃ saṃhāraṃ kathayasva me
> The Goddess spoke: I have come to know the yoga of creation — tell me the withdrawal.

**2/2** — bhairava uvāca: sādhu tvāṃ kathayiṣyāmi saṃhārantu yathā bhavet |
kālāgnirudrasaṃjñā tu nakhāgre nityasaṃsthitam ||
> Bhairava spoke: Well asked — I will tell you the withdrawal as it takes place. The [fire] named Kālāgni-Rudra (the Fire of Time, Rudra) abides constantly at the tip of the nails (nakhāgra).

*Notes: nakhāgra — "the tip of the nails": the same bodily-locus language as our Śivasūtra Vārttika (the tattvas as bodily states — 2/5's dehamadhye). The dissolution-fire located in the body's periphery.*

**2/3** — yadā prajvalate ūrddhaṃ saṃhārantu tadā bhavet |
vaḍavāmukhamahattvañca pātāle sahasaṃsthitaḥ ||
> When it blazes upward, then the withdrawal takes place. The great Vadavā-mouth (the submarine mare-fire) abides in the nether world.

**2/4** — saptapātālamuddiṣṭaṃ tasyorddhe svargasaṃsthitam |
etāni kathitā bhadre bhuvanāni caturdaśa ||
> The seven nether worlds are declared; above it the heavens abide — these fourteen worlds, O blessed one, are told.

**2/5** — jñātavyā dehamadhye tu tattvarūpā sthitā priye |
sarvajñā [yatna] saṃlīnaṃ layañca śaktigocare ||
> They are to be known within the body, O beloved — the forms of the principles (tattva-rūpāḥ) abide [there]. Omniscience, dissolved, [is] the dissolution within the domain of the power (śaktigocara).

*[X: "sarvajñā (yatna ?) saṃlīnaṃ" — the edition prints a doubtful reading ("yatna" queried); sense: the all-knowing [Śiva], having dissolved, [abides] in the domain of śakti.]*

**2/6** — śivamadhye gatā śaktiḥ kriyāmadhyasthitaḥ śivaḥ |
jñānamadhye kriyā līnā kriyā līyati icchayā ||
> In Śiva the power has gone; in action (kriyā) stands Śiva; in knowledge (jñāna) action is dissolved; action dissolves by will (icchā).

**2/7** — icchāśaktirlayaṃ yāti yatra tejaḥ paraḥ śivaḥ |
saṃhārantu imaṃ bhedena kathitantava śobhane ||
> The power of will goes to dissolution — there the supreme Śiva is the radiance (tejas). This withdrawal has been told to you in its divisions, O beautiful one.

*Notes: the descent-chain of 2/6-2/7: jñāna ⊃ kriyā ⊃ icchā ⊃ tejas(Śiva) — the same icchā-first structure as the Śivasūtra's Vārttika (1-13, icchāśaktir umā) and the Trika's three powers. Our specialist vocabulary holds across all four texts translated today.*

**2/8** — saṃhārantu padānyastaṃ kruddhaḥ saṃharate jagat |
sṛṣṭiyogaṃ padānyastaṃ sṛjatyevaṃ carācaram ||
> With the foot-placement (pada-nyāsa) of withdrawal, enraged, he destroys the world; with the foot-placement of the yoga of creation, he creates the moving and the unmoving.

**2/9** — adhasthā saṃsthitā bhuktiḥ ūrddhaṃ muktirvarānane |
pūrvaṃ yat sūcitaṃ devi sṛṣṭiyogaṃ mayā tava ||
> Below is established enjoyment (bhukti); above, liberation (mukti), O fair-faced one. That which was indicated before, O Devī — the yoga of creation — [was told] to you by me.

**2/10** — sṛṣṭisaṃhāranyāyena kulādhāramupavarṇitam |
kimanyaṃ pṛcchase nāthe sugopyaṃ prakaṭīkṛtam ||
> By the principle of creation and withdrawal, the foundation of the lineage (kulādhāra) has been described. What else do you ask, O Lady — the well-hidden which has been made manifest?

*Notes: kulādhāra — "the support of the kula" — the description of creation+withdrawal IS the lineage's foundation doctrine. Paṭala 2 ends with the transmission formula (the "iti jñānanirṇiti-yoginīkaula..." colophon naming the Candradvīpa transmission).*

# II. Kaulajñānanirṇaya, Paṭala 3 — The mark of the kula (kula-lakṣaṇa)

**3/1** — devyuvāca: kulalakṣaṃ maheśāna ātmasamvittipūrvakam |
etanme saṃśayo nātha prasādībhava śaṅkaraḥ ||
> The Goddess spoke: The mark of the kula, O great Lord, [is] preceded by self-awareness (ātmasaṃvitti) — this is my doubt, O Lord; be gracious, O Śaṅkara.

**3/2** — bhairava uvāca: śṛṇu mekāgracittā kulalakṣannigadyate |
yatra dṛṣṭirmanastatra bhūtendriyamapudgalaḥ ||
> Bhairava spoke: Listen with one-pointed mind — the mark of the kula is being declared: where the gaze is, there the mind [goes]; the elements, the senses, the atoms [dissolve].

*[X: "apudgalaḥ" — probably "apūrvaḥ/anu-pudgala" — a doubtful reading; sense: the coarse elements and subtle senses dissolve into the gaze.]*

**3/3** — svaśaktijīvabhūtāni dṛṣṭirlakṣairlayaṃ gatā |
sthānaṃ dhyānañca varṇañca lakṣañcaiva catuṣṭayam ||
> The own-power, the living-beings (jīva), the beings (bhūta) — the gaze has gone to dissolution through the marks. The place (sthāna), the meditation (dhyāna), the syllable (varṇa), and the mark (lakṣa) — the fourfold.

**3/4** — piṇḍasaṃjñā bhavet sthānaṃ dhyānantatpadamucyate |
varṇarūpaṃ vinirdiṣṭaṃ lakṣaṃ vai rūpavarjitam ||
> The place is called the piṇḍa (form/body); meditation is declared to be that state (tat-pada); the syllable is stated as form; the mark is what is free of form (rūpavarjita).

**3/5** — piṇḍañcopapadarūpaṃ rūpātītaṃ varānane |
sthāne sthāne sthitandevi ekaikasya caturvidham ||
> The piṇḍa, and the subsidiary-form (upapada), beyond form, O fair-faced one — in each place, O Devī, [there is] the fourfold of each single one.

**3/6** — catuṣpatrañca aṣṭāraṃ dvādaśāraṃ varānane |
pañcāraṃ ṣoḍaśārañca catuḥṣaṣṭidalampriye ||
> Four-petaled, eight-spoked, twelve-spoked, O fair-faced one, five-spoked, sixteen-spoked, and sixty-four-petaled, O beloved —

**3/7** — śatapatraṃ suśobhāḍhyaṃ sahasraṃ dalaśobhitam |
koṭipatraṃ sutejāḍhyaṃ tasyorddhe anyathā śṛṇu ||
> — the hundred-petaled adorned with beauty, the thousand adorned with petals, the crore-petaled endowed with brilliance; above that, hear the other [state]:

**3/8** — arddhakoṭisamāyuktaṃ koṭitrayasamanvitam |
karṇikākeśarairyuktaṃ dīpyamānaṃ (tathaiva ca) ||
> Joined with half a crore, endowed with three crores, furnished with pericarp and filaments, blazing —

**3/9** — tasyorddhe vyāpakantatra nityoditamakhaṇḍitam |
svātantramabjamacalaṃ sarvavyāpī nirañjanam ||
> — above it is the pervasive [mark], ever-risen, unbroken: the free (svātantra) lotus, immovable, all-pervading, the Stainless (nirañjana).

*Notes: the ascending lotus-arithmetic (4 → 8 → 12 → 5 → 16 → 64 → 100 → 1000 → 10⁷ → 10⁷+3×10⁷ petals) culminates in the *formless* — nirañjana, our Kālīkula/Trika terminus from the Kubjikā and TĀ-3 work: "not only the stainless but also the unmanifest." The same word, same function, across three texts today.*

**3/10** — tasyecchayā bhavet sṛṣṭirlayantatraiva gacchati |
tena liṅgantu vikhyātaṃ yatra līnaṃ carācaram ||
> By its will (icchā) is creation; dissolution goes there alone — by that it is known as the liṅga, in which the moving and unmoving are dissolved.

**3/11** — akhaṇḍamaṇḍalaṃ rūpaṃ nirvikāraṃ saniṣkalam |
ajñātvā bandhamuddiṣṭaṃ jñātvā bandhaiḥ pramucyate ||
> The unbroken circle, the form free of modification, without parts (niṣkala) — not knowing it, bondage is declared; knowing it, one is released from bonds.

**3/12** — unmanammanarahitaṃ dhyānadhāraṇavarjitam |
pratyakṣaṃ sarvadā nityaṃ atasipuṣpasannibham ||
> The unmana (beyond-mind), devoid of mind, free of meditation and concentration, directly perceived, always, eternal — like the aṭasī flower (blue flax).

**3/13** — sarvavarṇamayandevaṃ sarvavarṇairvivarjitam |
jñānagamyaṃ sadā devi pāramparyakramāgatam ||
> The god made of all colors, yet devoid of all colors — attainable through knowledge, O Devī, always, coming down through the succession of the lineage (pāramparyakrama).

*Notes: the paradox-cluster of the supreme mark: colored/all-colored/colorless; formed/formless; known/unknowable; and — decisively — *pāramparyakramāgatam*: it comes through the lineage-succession, not through individual attainment. The kula is epistemologically *transmitted* (cf. our Kubjikā's sampradāya-emphasis, 1.30-31).*

**3/14** — kathitaṃ devi saṅkāśaṃ kulalakṣaṃ (tathā) sthitam |
na kāṣṭhaṃ mṛṇmayaṃ liṅgaṃ na śailaratnasambhavam ||
> The mark of the kula, O Devī, has been declared as it stands — not a wooden liṅga, not a clay one, not one born of stone or jewel —

**3/15** — na cittaṃ rītikādinī na hemaṃ lohasambhavam |
sphaṭikaṃ maulikaṃ vāpi trapuṃ sīsakatāmrajam ||
> — not of bronze (citta? rīti), not gold, not iron-born — not crystal, not pearl, not tin, not lead, not copper —

*[X: "na cittaṃ rītikādinī" — doubtful; the verse lists the rejected materials for liṅga-worship.]*

**3/16** — puṣparāgodbhavādīni ye cānye lokapūjitāḥ |
aṣṭādaśa lokaśāstrāṇi krurāścādhyātmikāstathā ||
> — ruby-born and the like, and the other [liṅgas] worshipped by the world; the eighteen worldly sciences, and the cruel ones, and the spiritual ones —

**3/17** — taistu yat pūjitaṃ devi manasāpi na pūjayet |
kiṃ kāraṇaṃ mahādevi paśavo jñānavarjitāḥ ||
> — what is worshipped by them, O Devī, one should not worship even in mind. Why, O great goddess? The bound ones (paśu) are devoid of knowledge.

**3/18** — ajñānāste durācārāḥ kulājñakavivarjitāḥ |
na taistu saṅgabhoktavyaṃ na kuryāddravyasaṃgraham ||
> They are ignorant, ill-behaved, devoid of the command of the kula (kulājñā) — with them one must not associate or eat, nor gather goods.

**3/19** — brahmādyāstu surāḥ sarve asurāśca tapodhanāḥ |
yakṣagāndharvasiddhāśca tṛṇagulmapipīlikāḥ ||
> Brahmā and all the gods, and the asuras rich in austerity, yakṣas, gandharvas, siddhas — and grass, bushes, ants —

**3/20** — grahanakṣatratārādijagatsthāvarajaṅgamā(ḥ) |
niṣkrāntā bindumadhye tu laulībhūtantu tatsamam ||
> — the planets, asterisms, stars, and the rest of the world, stationary and moving: they have issued from within the bindu and become rolled-up (laulībhūta) in it, like that.

*Notes: the bindu as the point of origin and dissolution of the entire cosmos — the same bindu/liṅga complex as 3/9-3/11; "laulībhūta" (rolled-up) — the world as the bindu's coiled state. The paṭala's teaching: the true liṅga is the formless Stainless, transmitted by the lineage, not any material emblem.*

# III. Akulavīratantra, vv. 1–18 — The Beyond-the-Kula Hero

*The "anti-kula" text of the Matsyendra school: the supreme state is *beyond* every technique and emblem. Frame: an introductory statement; opening verses (maṅgala to Mīnanātha, the teacher of the lineage).*

**Maṅgala** — śrīmacchandapādakebhyo namaḥ | śrīmīnasahajānandaṃ svakīyāṅgasamudbhavam | sarvamādhāragambhīramacalaṃ vyāpakaṃ param | māyāmalavinirmuktaṃ mīnanāthaṃ namāmyaham ||
> Homage to the blessed Candra-pādas. I bow to Mīnanātha (the Fish-Lord) — the bliss of the innate (sahajānanda) born from his own body, the profound support of all, immovable, pervasive, supreme, freed from the impurity of māyā.

*Notes: Mīnanātha = the legendary first teacher of the lineage (the "Fish"; Matsyendra's teacher). The maṅgala's epithets are the soteriological program of the whole text: māyāmalavinirmukta — freed from māyā's impurity (our Śivasūtra Vārttika vocabulary: māyīyamala).*

**1** — athātaḥ sampravakṣyāmi akulavīraṃ mahadbhūtam |
guhyād guhyataraṃ guhyaṃ siddhasadbhāvasantatiḥ ||
> Now I will declare the Akulavīra, the great being — more secret than secret, the secret of secrets, the lineage of the siddhas' true being.

**2** — anugrahāya lokānāṃ siddhanāthena bhāṣitam |
gopanīyaṃ prayatnena yadīcchan śāśvataṃ padam ||
> Spoken by the Lord of Siddhas for the grace of the worlds — to be guarded with effort by one who desires the eternal state.

**3** — saṃsārārṇavamagnānāṃ bhūtānāṃ mahadāśrayam |
yathā nadīnadāḥ sarve sāgare samupāgatāḥ ||
> The great refuge of beings sunk in the ocean of saṃsāra — as all rivers and streams come together in the sea,

**4** — tathā akulavīreṣu sarvadharmā layaṅgatāḥ |
sarvādhāramaśeṣasya jagataḥ sarvadā prabhuḥ ||
> so in the Akulavīra all dharmas go to dissolution. The support of all, of the entire world, always the Lord.

**5** — sahajānandaṃ na vindanti sarvadharmasamāsṛtāḥ |
ajānantamalairgrastā mahāmāyāndhacchāditāḥ ||
> Those who run after all the dharmas do not find the bliss of the innate (sahajānanda); the unknowing are seized by the impurities, covered by the blindfold of the Great Māyā.

**6** — śāstrajālena santuṣṭā mohitāstyajayantitāḥ (?) |
na vindanti padaṃ śāntaṃ kaulānāṃ niṣkalaṃ gurum ||
> Satisfied with the net of scriptures, deluded — they do not find the tranquil state, the partless guru of the Kaulas.

**7** — saṃvādayanti ye kecin nyāyavaiśeṣikāstathā |
baudhāstu arihantā ye somasiddhantavādinaḥ ||
> Whatever [schools] dispute — the Nyāya and Vaiśeṣika [logicians], the Buddhists, the Jainas (arhants), the Somasiddhānta-vādins,

**8** — mīmāṃsa pañcasrotāśca vāmasiddhāntadakṣiṇāḥ |
itihāsapurāṇañca bhūtatatvantu gāruḍam ||
> — Mīmāṃsā, the Five Streams (pañcasrotas), the left and right Siddhāntas, Itihāsa and Purāṇa, the Gāruḍa [tantra] and the doctrine of the elements (bhūtatattva) —

**9** — ebhiḥ śaivāgamaiḥ sarvaiḥ parokṣañca kriyānvitaiḥ |
savikalpasiddhisañcāraṃ tat sarvaṃ pāpabandhavit ||
> — all these, with all the Śaiva āgamas, indirect (parokṣa), joined with ritual action: the attainment and movement with constructs (savikalpa) — all that destroys sin-bonds [yet remains bound].

*Notes: the entire learned cosmos — logic, Buddhist, Jain, Mīmāṃsā, both Siddhāntas, even the Śaiva āgamas — is *indirect* (parokṣa) and *joined with action* (kriyānvita): the siddhi it yields is still vikalpa-borne. The Akulavīra's claim: beyond all of it.*

**10** — vikalpabahulāḥ sarvairmithyāvādā nirarthakāḥ |
na te muñcanti saṃsāre akulavīravivarjitāḥ ||
> Full of constructs, all these are false teachings, worthless — they do not release [one] in saṃsāra, those devoid of the Akulavīra.

**11** — sarvajñaṃ sarvamāsṛtya sarvato hitalakṣaṇam |
sarveṣāṃ siddhistatra stāḥ sarvasiddhiñca tatra vai ||
> Resorting to the all-knowing, the all, whose mark is the good of all — the perfection of all [beings] is there, and all attainments are there indeed.

**12** — yatrāsau akulavīro dṛśyate sarvatomukham |
taṃ viditvā paraṃ rūpaṃ mano niścalatāṃ vrajet ||
> Where that Akulavīra is seen, facing everywhere — knowing that supreme form, the mind goes to stillness.

**13** — śabdarūparasasparśagandhañcaivātra pañcamam |
sarvabhāvāśca tatraiva pralīṇāḥ pralayaṃ gatāḥ ||
> Sound, form, taste, touch, and the fifth, smell — all beings are dissolved there, gone to dissolution.

**14** — bhāvābhāvavinirmukta udayāstamanavarjitaḥ |
svabhāvamatimataṃ śāntaṃ mano yasya manomayam ||
> Freed from being and non-being, free of rising and setting — the tranquil state of one's own nature, thought, whose mind is made of mind.

*[X: "svabhāvamatimataṃ" — doubtful reading; sense: the tranquil own-nature, beyond the mind's constructs.]*

**15** — akulavīramiti khyātaṃ sarvādhāraparāparam |
nādhāralakṣabhedantu na nādagocare paṭhet ||
> It is known as the Akulavīra — the support of all, beyond the beyond. Neither in the distinctions of supports and marks, nor in the domain of nāda, is it to be read [found].

**16** — hṛdi sthāne na vaktre ca ghaṇṭikā tālarandhrake |
na iḍā piṅgalā śāntā na cāstīti gamāgame ||
> Not in the heart, not in the place of the mouth, not in the uvula (ghaṇṭikā) or the palatal aperture; not iḍā, not piṅgalā — the tranquil [state] is not [there], so say āgama and its negation.

**17** — na nābhicakrakaṇṭhe ca na śire naiva mastake |
tathā cakṣurunmīlane ca na nāsāgranirīkṣaṇe ||
> Not in the navel-cakra or the throat, not in the head, not at all in the skull; nor in the opening of the eyes, nor in gazing at the tip of the nose —

**18** — na pūrakakumbhake tatra recake (ca) tathā punaḥ |
na bindubhedake granthau lalāṭe na tu vahnike ||
> — not in inhalation, retention, nor again in exhalation; not in the piercing of the bindu, not in the knots, not in the forehead, not in the fire[-cakra].

*Notes: the systematic negation of every technique (asana/cakra/prāṇāyāma/dṛṣṭi/bandha) — the Akulavīra is *beyond the kula's own machinery*. The same "apophatic terminus" as the Kaulajñānanirṇaya's formless liṅga and the Śivasūtra's unobscured consciousness: the unlocatable, unstainable, technique-free state.*
