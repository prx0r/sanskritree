## Paṭala 4 — the sāmrājya-dīkṣā and the vidyā-classes

**The guru's cross-āmnāya authority** — gurustu dakṣiṇāmnāyī sarvāmnāyaṣu dīkṣitaḥ | ūrddhāmnāyī tathānyeṣu pūrvāmnāyī pareṣu ca || dvayostu paścimāmnāyī uttarastvadhare bhavet | adhare naiva kutrāpi gurusvaṃ pāralaukikīṃ ||
> The guru of the dakṣiṇāmnāya is initiated in all the āmnāyas; the ūrdhvāmnāyī [in] the others; the pūrvāmnāyī in the rest; the paścimāmnāyī in the two; the uttara [is] the highest (adhara? — the least of the lower). In the lower [guru], nowhere [is] the guru's other-worldly [power].

**The fruits by āmnāya** — dakṣiṇopāsakaḥ kāle ūrddhaḥ sāyujyamāpnuyāt | devatāyāstathā pūrvaḥ sārupyaṃ labhate paraḥ || sāmīpyaṃ cottare lokamadhogastyai hi kaṃ phalaṃ | lobhatyāgaiścāpakāro hyadhomārgepyudarkakṛt ||
> The dakṣiṇa-worshipper, in time, [reaches] the highest — the sāyujya (the union); the pūrva one obtains the sārūpya (the likeness) of the deity; the sāmīpya (the proximity) in the uttara [āmnāya]; for the lower-world [guru], what fruit? By greed and giving-up the harm [is] done — even on the lower path, the after-effect.

**The guru's own-siddhi** — mantrāntare ca saṃsiddho gururmantraṃ pravacchitī | yathoktā caraṇāt tasya siddhiḥ śiṣyasya jāyate || kṛpā vaśāt siddhamantraṃ dadāti ca yadā guruḥ | vinājapaṃ vināpūjāṃ siddhiyet tat kare sthitāḥ ||
> A guru perfected in another mantra teaches the mantra; from his conduct as declared, the disciple's siddhi arises. When the guru, by grace, gives the accomplished mantra — without japa, without worship, the siddhi stands in [his] hand.

**The eclipse-dīkṣā** — tatra sūrjagrahaḥ śaste na tu catugraha kvacit | candragrahe tu dāridraṃ saure sarvasamṛddhayaḥ || sūryagraha śiva proktaścandrāṣyaśaktireva ca | sūryagrahe śaktidīkṣā paśudīkṣānākārayet ||
> There the sun-eclipse is praised, but not the moon-eclipse anywhere; at the moon-eclipse [comes] poverty, at the solar [eclipse] all-prosperity. The sun-eclipse is proclaimed Śiva; the moon, the power (śakti); at the sun-eclipse [one should perform] the śakti-dīkṣā; the paśu-dīkṣā one should not do.

**The Kālī-names (the sāmrājya's goddesses)** — niḥkalāguhyaśaṃśaṃtā kālī bhedena kīrtitā | kevalayaṃ bhavenmadhāhvayī sāmrājyarūpiṇī || candrāgni vedapaṃcārṇā tathā nīlā kramena ca | haṃsatārā mahānīlā tadante vīnaśāṃbhavī ||
> The Kālī — the partless, the secret, the tranquil — is proclaimed in her division; the Kevalā becomes the Mādhavī (the honey-named), the sovereign-formed. Candra, Agni, Veda, Pañcārṇa, and Nīlā in order; Haṃsa, Tārā, Mahānīlā, and after them the Vīṇā-Śāmbhavī —

**The sāmrājya-classes** — kevalā tu bhaven madhā siddhikriyā dvidhā | tritayā divyasāmrājya me dīkṣāṃ prakīrtitā || devarūpā divyarūpā mahāparā | sarvarūpā sarvapūrvā mahāsāmrājyameva yā || siddhi ṛddhi krameṇaivaḥ bhedavāhulyamiritaṃ | vidyārājyā vidhādīkṣā cchinnamastā vidhau smṛtā ||
> The Kevalā becomes the honey; the siddhi-kriyā twofold; the triad [is] the divine-sovereignty; the dīkṣā is proclaimed. The god-formed, the divine-formed, the Mahāparā; the all-formed, the all-first — that [is] the great-sovereignty. The siddhi, the ṛddhi, in order — the great multiplicity of the divisions is declared; the vidyā-sovereignty, the dīkṣā's rule, [and] the Chinnamastā in the rite is remembered.

**The sixteen/seventeen** — candravahni raso rakta śyāmā ca ṣoḍaśī tathā | paraṣoḍhā saptadaśī tadajātā parā bhavet ||
> Candra, Agni, Rasa, the Rakta, the Śyāmā, and the Ṣoḍaśī likewise; the Para-ṣoḍhā (?) — the seventeenth, born of it, becomes the supreme (parā).

**The sāmrājya-constituents** — dāpi malini kullā kramādvighvādi rājyada | vagalāyā mahesāni sāmrājya parameṣyate || candra veda sūkṣameru mantrīhīnaṃ śivāntaraṃ || hṛdayaṃ ca śatārṇā ca paṃcāstraṃ kullukā tathā | gāyatrī krame yogena sāmrājya parameṣṭhyadhṛk || etat paṃcakasaṃyuktoḥ sarvasāmrājya buddhidhṛk | śrīdivyasarvasāmrājya dīkṣāsarvottarā bhavet ||
> The Dāpī (?), the Mālinī, the Kullā, in order from the Vighna [onward], giving the rule; for Vāgalā, O great Lady, the sovereignty is the supreme. Candra, Veda, Sūkṣma, Meru — the mantrin... the Hṛdaya, the Śatārṇā, the Pañcāstra, the Kullukā, the Gāyatrī — by the ordered yoga, the sovereignty, holding the supremacy. Joined with this fivefold, [one becomes] the holder of the all-sovereignty's intelligence: the blessed divine all-sovereignty dīkṣā becomes the supreme of all.

**The vidyā-hierarchy** — tatopi dīkṣā deveśi nāsti brahmāṇḍa golake | daśavidyā prabhedāśca mahāvidyā pra *? dajā || siddhividyā prabhedāśca mahāmantrāstathaiva ca | tadaṃgamantrā deveśi śāvaraṃ daśaśāvaraṃ || kālaśāvarakaṃ devi tathaiva siddhaśāvaraṃ | tridhādarśanamantrā ca tathāmnāya kramod bhavet ||
> Beyond this there is no dīkṣā, O Devī, in the globe of the Brahmāṇḍa: the ten-vidyā divisions, the mahāvidyā... the siddhi-vidyā divisions and the mahā-mantras likewise; their limb-mantras, O Devī, the śāvara, the ten-śāvara; the kāla-śāvara, O Devī, and the siddha-śāvara — the threefold darśana-mantras, and the āmnāya-order arises.

**The svapna-stuti (the dream-praise)** — bhagavan devadeveśa śūladbhṛvṛṣavāhana | iṣṭāniṣṭa samācakṣa svapnasuptasya sāpravaṃ || niśānte paśyati svapnaṃ subhaṃ vāyadivāśubhaṃ | oṃ hṛtsakalalokāya viṣṇuve prabhaviṣṇuve || viśvārūpāya svapnādhipataye namaḥ | namo jāya trinetrāye piṃgalāya mahātmane | vāmāya viśvarūpāya svapnādhipataye namaḥ ||
> "O Lord, god of gods, bearing the trident, riding the bull — tell the desired and undesired [dream] of the sleeper, [O lord of] the dream. At night's end one sees a dream, auspicious or inauspicious." Oṃ to Viṣṇu, the lord of all the worlds, the mighty Viṣṇu; homage to the all-formed, the lord of dreams; homage to the Jāya, the three-eyed, the tawny, the great-souled; to the Vāma, the all-formed, the lord of dreams, homage.

**The dream-fruits** — svapne tu prathame yāme saṃvatsara phalaṃ bhavet | ṣadbhirmāsaidviyātthastribhimāsaistriyāmakaḥ || caturthe māsamātreṇa prātaḥkāle tu satvaraṃ ||
> In the dream of the first watch, the fruit of a year arises; by six months [the fruit] of the second [watch]; by three months, of the third watch; in the fourth, by a mere month — at the morning time, quickly.

**The dusvapna-śānti (the bad-dream pacification)** — trirātro doṣito bhūtvā haviṣyāsī purohitaḥ | sakṣīrāṇāṃ ghṛtāṃktānāṃ samidhānāṃ śataṃ dehet || pāśānāṃ tu gāyātryā śūdraḥsvāhaviṣya bhuk | dīpaṃ dadyād vipragṛhe viṣṇo gehe śivālaye || evaṃ dṛṣṭā śubhaṃ svapnaṃ gṛhnīyāmanumanyathā | gṛhī * *? rita ca syādvānyaṃ vedaṃ vicintayet ||
> Having fasted three nights, the purohita, eating the haviṣya, should offer a hundred fuel-sticks anointed with ghee, with milk; of the pāśas, with the Gāyatrī — a śūdra, eating the haviṣya [as] the svāhā [oblation]...; he should give a lamp in the brāhmaṇa's house, in Viṣṇu's abode, in the Śiva-temple. Thus, having seen a good dream, one should accept it, approve it; ...the other Vedas he should consider.

**The kaulikopadeśa (the Kaulika-teaching)** — nyāsajālaṃ vi * * *? śaṃkhapātrādi sthāpanaṃ | kṛtvā devī samabhyarcamakāraṃ paṃcakaiḥ plutaḥ || kāraṇaṃ taṃ mukhe paścā mantravidyāṃ samurcaye | kathitoyaṃ maheśāni upadeśastu kaulikaḥ ||
> The net of nyāsas, the setting-up of the conch and the vessels — having done [this], having worshipped the Devī, filled with the five makāras — then the cause in the mouth, he should recite the mantra-vidyā. This, O great Lady, is declared the Kaulika-teaching.

**The secrecy's curse** — anyāyena cāyo dadyāt gṛhnānyāyataśca yaḥ | dadato gṛhnato vāpi kulaśāpo bhaviṣyati || guruśiṣyāvubhau mohādaparīkṣya parasparaṃ | aśāstrīyopadeśaṃ tu yo gṛhnāti dadāti ca || bhujīte tāvubhau ghorānnarakānekaviṃśatiḥ ||
> He who gives [it] unlawfully, and he who receives unlawfully — whether giving or receiving, the kula-curse will [fall]. Both guru and disciple, in delusion, not testing each other — he who receives and gives the un-śāstric teaching — those two enjoy the twenty-one terrible hells.

## Paṭala 5 — the pūrṇābhiṣeka-dīkṣā

**The full-anointing** — atha pūrṇābhiṣekadīkṣā māhapūrṇābhiṣekadīkṣā tu candrasūryāpraha bhavet || sundarī tāliṇīkālī kramadīkṣātrigāminī | kramapūrṇe maheśāni kramācchustu haviṣyati ||
> Now the full-anointing dīkṣā — the great full-anointing dīkṣā occurs at the sun-and-moon[-eclipses]. Sundarī, Tālinī, Kālī — the ordered dīkṣā going through the three; in the completion of the order, O great Lady, [the fruit] will be [attained] from the order.

**The states** — candrāgni pakṣaṣoḍhāṣya parānirvāṇa tatparā | ṣaṭsāṃbhavaṃ tato devi cakrasaṃbhedanena ca || svacakraṃ deha vijñānaṃ parakāye praveśanaṃ | etat jñānā bhaven maedhā dīkṣā proktā mayā tava ||
> The moon, the fire, the fortnight, the sixteenth — intent on the supreme-nirvāṇa; the six-Śāmbhava, then, O Devī, by the piercing of the cakra: the knowledge of one's own cakra and body, the entry into another's body — from this knowledge the honey (maedhā?) arises; the dīkṣā has been declared to you by me.

**The Kālī-sequence** — hādāvidaṃ nigaditaṃ śṛṇukādau maheśvari | candrāgnirasarājñī ca mahārājñī tataḥ śive || mahākāmākalākālī guhyakāli tataḥ śive | guhyakālī dvidhā proktā sakalāniḥkalā kramāt || daśavaktrā tu sakalā proktā vā sṛṣṭi kā kalā | ugrā saumyā mahākrūrā tridhā sāpi ||
> The ha-series has been declared; hear the ka-series, O great Lady: Candra, Agni, Rasa, the Rājñī, and the Mahārājñī, then, O Śive; the Mahākāma, the Kalā, the Kālī, the Guhyakālī, then, O Śive. The Guhyakālī is declared twofold — the sakalā and the niḥkalā, in order; the ten-faced is the sakalā, declared; the creation-activity; the fierce, the gentle, the great-cruel — she, threefold.

**The eclipse-law** — candra grahe viṣṇu dīkṣā paṃcarātre na kārayet | candrasūrya grahe dīkṣā sarvadīkṣā * * * *? | candragrahe śaktidīkṣā sarvadīkṣottamattamā ||
> At the moon-eclipse one should not perform the Viṣṇu-dīkṣā in the Pāñcarātra; at the sun-and-moon-eclipse the dīkṣā, all-dīkṣā...; at the moon-eclipse the śakti-dīkṣā [is] the supreme of all dīkṣās.

**The time's freedom** — na vāratithinakṣatrādi na māsa niyamastathā | na yogaṃ karaṇaṃ nāpi na vicāraṃ samācaret || devī bodhaṃ samārabhya yāvatmyā navamī tithi | kṛtātasuvudhai dīkṣā sarvābhīṣṭaphalaprade ||
> Neither the day, the tithi, the nakṣatra and the rest, nor the month, nor the rule; neither the yoga, nor the karaṇa, nor the consideration should one practice. Beginning from the Devī's awakening, until the ninth tithi — [this] the wise do: the dīkṣā giving all the desired fruits.

**The guru's will** — bodhane naiva dugāyāḥ kālākālaṃ na śodhayet | aśokākhyāṣṭamī yatra rāmākhyā navamī tathā || guro vā śrutadṛṣṭe ca siddhikṣetre suśobhane | āgatirguruddaivādyadadīdīkṣā tadā bhavet || yadaivecchā tadā dīkṣā gurorājñānurūpataḥ | na tithirna vrataṃ homo na snānaṃ na japa kriyā || dīkṣāyāḥ kāraṇaṃ kiṃtu svecchā vāpte tu sadguroḥ ||
> In the awakening, the milkless one — do not purify [by] time-or-non-time. Where [is] the eighth named Aśokā, the ninth named Rāmā — for the guru, in the heard-and-seen, in the auspicious siddhi-field, when the coming [is] by the guru's fate, then the dīkṣā occurs. Whenever the desire [is], then the dīkṣā, according to the guru's command: no tithi, no vow, no homa, no bathing, no japa-rite — the dīkṣā's cause, rather, [is] the own-will, when a good guru is obtained.
