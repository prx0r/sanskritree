# 1. Unmattabhairavahṛdaya (M00275)

### Time-place-context
- **PERIOD:** early Bhairava-layer yantra-text; the six acts (ṣaṭkarmāṇi) with animal offerings — the ritual register of the Kaula cultus.
- **PLACE/PATH:** M00275 e-text (data under Dyczkowski's supervision, rev. 2012).
- **GENRE/REGISTER:** yantra-ritual manual (the Unmattabhairava's yantra); terse technical prose.
- **FRAME:** the Unmattabhairava — one of the early Bhairava forms (cf. the Akulavīra's "unmatta" family). No anchor found in the searches.

## The opening (vv. 1–12)
**1** — śṛṇu rudra mahābhāga kāruṇi puruṣottamaḥ | unmattabhairavo yantradhāraṇo buddhimatācchṛṇu ||
> Hear, O great-fated Rudra, compassionate, supreme person: the Unmattabhairava, the bearer of the yantra — hear with understanding.

**2** — liṅgākāraṃ lakṣmībījaṃ khaṇḍaṃ bhaṅgaṃ triyugmakam | patākāṃ pādaviṃ māyāṃ caturmārgaprakāśitām ||
> The liṅga-formed [yantra] with the Lakṣmī-bīja; the pieces (khaṇḍa) and the fractures (bhaṅga); the tri-partite; the banner (patākā), the foot-path (pādavī), the māyā — shown on the four paths.

**3** — parikhābhiḥ parimitāṃ koṣṭe bindusamanvitām | ye'tra madhye śeṣarūpaṃ muṇḍapaktivirājitām ||
> Bounded by the moats (parikhā), in the enclosure (koṣṭha) joined with the bindu; within, in the middle, the serpent-form (śeṣarūpa), adorned with the row of skulls.

**4** — mahāvāsukināgena veṣṭitā vādalāñchanā | śastraiḥ pāṣāṇa astrābhyāṃ veṣṭitai cāmarairyutā ||
> Wound about by the great Vāsuki-serpent, marked with the wind (vāda); surrounded by weapons, stones, and missiles, furnished with fly-whisks.

**5** — nānāśastraṃ ca ghaṇṭāṃ ca vṛttadvārasamanvitām | bhairavo nāmayantraiśca lakṣmīṃ strīṃ kāmarājitā ||
> With manifold weapons and the bell, furnished with the round gate: Bhairava, with the bowing-yantras, and Lakṣmī the woman, ruled by Kāma —

**6** — varavarṇābījayuktāṃ sadāraṃ sakalā mahān | yete yantrāṇi bhairavyā prasiddhaṃ śaṃkara śṛṇu ||
> — joined with the fine-syllable bīja, with the wife, the great all-together. These are the yantras of Bhairavī — the well-known, O Śaṅkara, hear:

**7** — dhārayetsādhaka śreṣṭa yaṃ likhedraktacandanaiḥ | māraṇe śatrusaṃhāre mārjāraiḥ śoṇitairlikhet ||
> The best of sādhakas should bear it — writing it with red sandal: for killing and destroying enemies, he writes with the blood of cats.

**8** — vaśīkaraṇakāryeṣu likhetstrīrajadhārayā | uccāṭane ca unmāde mohane śatrupiḍite ||
> For the works of subjugation he writes with menstrual blood (rajas) of a woman; for expulsion, madness, and the crushing of enemies —

**9** — sādhakai śośitai padmyāṃ tallikhedbhūrjapatrakam | eko ācāramātrāṇāṃ validānai pṛthak pṛthak ||
> — the sādhaka writes it with [the blood?] on a birch-bark, in the lotus [?] — [X: corrupt pāda; "padmyāṃ" doubtful]. One [verse], of the measures of conduct, with the offerings separately, each apart.

**10** — māraṇe ghātayenmatsyaṃ smaśāne rajanīmukhe | śatrusaṃhārakāle tu kṛṣṇameṣaṃ samarpayet ||
> For killing, he strikes a fish in the burning ground at nightfall; at the time of destroying enemies he offers a black ram.

**11** — vaśīkaraṇamohanyāṃ mārjāraṃ ghātayāmi te | uccāṭane ca unmāde sūkaraṃ ghātayāmi te ||
> For subjugation and delusion I strike a cat for you; for expulsion and madness I strike a boar for you.

**12** — rātrau pūjāṃ bhairavasya yantrarājo maheśvaraḥ | nānāmāṃsai trikhaṇḍābhi matsyamāṃsādisaṃyutām ||
> At night, the worship of Bhairava — the king of yantras, the great Lord — with manifold meats, with the three parts, joined with fish-meat and the rest.

Notes: the yantra-king's practice; the blood-inks (cat-blood, menstrual blood); the animal-offerings per act. [X: vv. 2, 9 have transmission-wobbles ("pādaviṃ", "padmyāṃ") — collation needed. The "kāmarājitā" (ruled by Kāma) of the Lakṣmī-figure — the kāma-bīja complex of our Kubjikā work.]

# 2. Kulānanda (M00513)

### Time-place-context
- **PERIOD:** Matsyendra-school text (Bagchi bundle, 1934 — same as KJN/Akulavīra); the siddhi-practice register of the same 10th–11th c. Kaula dialect.
- **PLACE/PATH:** Calcutta Sanskrit Series 3, pp. 107–113; e-text M00513.
- **GENRE/REGISTER:** siddhi-manual (purabheda — the "city-fracture," pāśastobha, divyadṛṣṭi). Dialogue-frame.
- **FRAME:** the same Pārvatī-questioning frame as the KJN; the practices (flame-fixing in the heart-lotus, the two-finger point, the uvula) parallel the KJN's cakra-practice and the Akulavīra's negation-list (the same techniques, here taught positively).

## The opening (vv. 1–17)
**1** — kailāsaśikharāsīnaṃ devadevaṃ jagadagurum | paripṛcchatyumādevī ekānte jñānamuttamam ||
> Seated on the peak of Kailāsa, the god of gods, the guru of the world — Umā-devī asks in private for the supreme knowledge.

**2** — bhedanīmuttamabhedaṃ yathā dehavyavasthitam | kathayasva purābhedaṃ kulānandeṣu cottamam ||
> The supreme non-difference, its dividers, as it stands in the body — tell the ancient "fracture" (purabheda), the supreme among the kula-delights (kulānanda).

**3** — sthānāntaraviśeṣeṇa vijñānaṃ kathayasva me | sadyaḥ pratyayakārakaṃ yathā dehe vyavasthitam ||
> Tell me the knowledge according to the differences of the stations, producing immediate conviction (sadyaḥpratyaya), as it stands in the body.

**4** — pāśastobhañca vedha(na)ñca dhūnanaṃ kampanaṃ tathā | khecaraṃ samarasañcaiva balīpalitanāśanam | sarvaṃ tattu sureśvara kathayasva mama prabho ||
> The noose-thrust (pāśastobha), the piercing (vedhana), the fumigation (dhūnana), the trembling (kampana), the sky-going (khecara), the equal-taste (samarasa), and the destruction of gray-hair-and-age (balīpalitanāśana) — all that, O Lord of the gods, tell me, my Lord.

**5** — śṛṇu devi pravakṣyāmi purabhedaṃ samuttamam | etat kaulikaṃ jñānaṃ kulānande cāṣṭottamaḥ ||
> Hear, O Devī — I will declare the supreme purabheda: this is the Kaulika knowledge, in the Kulānanda, the excellent.

**6** — brahmasthāne yat kamalaṃ catuḥṣaṣṭidalānvitam | tatraiva manasā rodhya lakṣayeddīpaśikhān vratī ||
> The lotus in the brahma-station, endowed with sixty-four petals: there, blocking [the senses] with the mind, the vow-holder should fix on the lamp-flames.

**7** — paśyati sarvadehe tu divyadṛṣṭirvarānane | tena lakṣitamātreṇa jāyate siddhiruttamā ||
> He sees within the whole body — the divine sight, O fair-faced one; by the mere fixing of that, the supreme siddhi arises.

**8** — tasyopari nābhimnadavadā (?) vicakṣaṇe | tathā coparitiṣṭhati devo bhairavo jagatprabhuḥ ||
> Above it, in the navel [?] [X: corrupt], the wise one; and above, stands the god Bhairava, the lord of the world.

**9** — tasyāgre manasāropya dṛḍhabhūtavicakṣaṇāḥ | jāyate dhvanibhirjātiṃ tatra marudviśeṣataḥ ||
> Before him, mounting [the awareness] with the mind, the firm-experienced wise ones: from the sounds, the arising of [vision?]; there, particularly from the wind (marut).

**10** — etad vijñānamātreṇa āveśaṃ kurute dhruvam | jāyate paramaṃ sthānaṃ nātra kāryavicāraṇāt ||
> By the mere understanding of this he surely makes the possession (āveśa); the supreme station arises — no deliberation of works here.

**11** — athānyaṃ paramaṃ devi sadyaḥ pratyayakārakaṃ | taṃ pravakṣyāmi he devi śṛṇuṣvāyatalocane ||
> Now the other supreme [practice], O Devī, producing immediate conviction — that I will tell, O Devī, hear, O wide-eyed one:

**12** — aṅgulyadvitīyaṃ devi yat pūrvaṃ kathitaṃ mayā | tatraiva sthāpayedvittaṃ kathitaṃ jāyate dhruvam ||
> The two-finger [point], O Devī, which was told before by me: fixing the mind (vitta?) there — what was told surely arises.

**13** — atītānāgatañcaiva vartamānaṃ tathaiva ca | tatraivotpādayet sṛṣṭiṃ tīvrajyotiḥsamākṛtiḥ ||
> The past, the future, and likewise the present: there he produces creation, of the form of the fierce light.

**14** — hṛdisaṃsthakamalaṃ hitvā mūrdhni yān prapūrayet | pāśastobhaṃ karotyevaṃ yadi viśramate manaḥ ||
> Leaving the heart-lotus and filling the head [with breath] — he makes the noose-thrust thus, if the mind rests.

**15** — athānyaṃ paramaṃ devi divyadṛṣṭi pravartate | jvalajjvalanamadhyasthaṃ dvijagranthi nirīkṣayet ||
> Now the other supreme [practice], O Devī: the divine sight proceeds — he should gaze on the fire-knot (dvijagranthi) standing in the midst of the blazing fire.

**16** — tatraiva manasā rodhya cūlikāgrantu mānayet punaḥ | sṛṣṭiṃ pravartante devi nātra kāryavicāraṇāt ||
> There, blocking [the senses] with the mind, he should honor the uvula-knot (cūlikā-granthi) again — the creations proceed, O Devī, no deliberation of works here.

**17** — tadvedhañca śuddhasārañca hitaṃ mayā na prakāśitam | kathayasva viśeṣeṇa yathā jānāmi tatkṣaṇāt ||
> That piercing and the pure essence, the good, was not revealed to me — tell it specifically, so that I know it instantly.

Notes: the purabheda-practices: the flame-fixing (dīpaśikhā), the two-finger point, the fire-knot gaze, the uvula — the siddhi-mechanics of the Kaula body. [P: pāśastobha (noose-thrust) — the exact KJN 4.2/4.7 term; khecara/samarasa — the Akulavīra's terms (there negated, here taught); balīpalitanāśana (gray-hair destruction) — the rasāyana-claim of the KJN 5.]

# 3. Jñānasaṅkalinītantra (M00191)

### Time-place-context
- **PERIOD:** a "jñāna" (knowledge) tantra of the Kaula-Saiva mix — the date-layer is uncertain (pre-19th-c. print; the vocabulary — Śāmbhavī-vidyā, kulavadhū — is the Kaula philosophical register). [X: dating — pass 2.]
- **PLACE/PATH:** Caṭṭopādhyāya's Calcutta edition, end of 19th c., Bengali script; e-text M00191.
- **GENRE/REGISTER:** philosophical dialogue (Pārvatī-Śiva), jñāna-kathā — the "knowledge" tantra; uses the fourteen-vidyās and the avidyā-critique.
- **FRAME:** opens with the question of jñāna beyond creation/destruction — the same "knowledge beyond the dyads" as the Akulavīra, in question-form.

## The opening (vv. 1–15)
**1** — kailāśaśikharāsīnaṃ devadevaṃ jagadgurum | pṛcchati sma mahādevī brūhi jñānaṃ maheśvara ||
> The great Devī asked the god of gods seated on Kailāsa's peak, the guru of the world: tell the knowledge, O great Lord.

**2** — kutaḥ sṛṣṭirbhaved deva kathaṃ sṛṣṭirvinaśyati | brahmajñānaṃ kathaṃ deva sṛṣṭisaṃhāravarjitam ||
> From what does creation arise, O god? How does creation perish? How, O god, [is there] the brahman-knowledge free of creation and dissolution?

**3** — avyaktācca bhavet sṛṣṭiravyaktācca vinaśyati | avyaktaṃ brahmaṇo jñānaṃ sṛṣṭisaṃhāra varjitam ||
> From the unmanifest (avyakta) creation arises; in the unmanifest it perishes. The unmanifest is the brahman's knowledge, free of creation and dissolution.

**4** — oṅkārādakṣarāt sarvāstvetā vidyāścaturdaśa | mantra-pūjā-tapo-dhyānaṃ karmākarma tathaiva ca ||
> From the syllable oṃ, all these fourteen vidyās: mantra, worship, austerity, meditation, and likewise action and non-action —

**5** — ṣaḍaṅgaṃ vedāścatvāro mīmāṃsā nyāyavistaraḥ | dharmaśāstrapurāṇāni etā vidyāścaturdaśa ||
> — the six limbs [of the Veda], the four Vedas, Mīmāṃsā, the Nyāya-extension, the dharma-śāstras, the Purāṇas: these are the fourteen vidyās.

**6** — tāvad vidyā bhavet sarvā yāvad jñānaṃ na jāyate | brahmajñānaṃ padaṃ jñātvā sarvavidyā sthirā bhavet ||
> All this is vidyā only as long as knowledge is not born; knowing the brahman-knowledge station, all the vidyās become stable.

**7** — vedaśāstrapurāṇāni sāmānyagaṇikā iva | yā punaḥ śāmbhavī vidyā guptā kulavadhūriva ||
> The Vedas, śāstras, Purāṇas are like common courtesans (sāmānyagaṇikā); but the Śāmbhavī-vidyā — hidden like a kula-bride (kulavadhū).

**8** — dehasthāḥ sarvavidyāśca dehasthāḥ sarvadevatāḥ | dehasthāḥ sarvatīrthāni guruvākyena labhyate ||
> All the vidyās are in the body; all the deities are in the body; all the tīrthas are in the body — by the guru's word it is obtained.

**9** — adhyātmavidyā hi nṛṇāṃ saukhyamokṣakarī bhavet | dharma karma tathā japyametat sarvaṃ nivartate ||
> The inner knowledge (adhyātmavidyā) indeed gives men happiness and liberation; dharma, karma, and japa — all that ceases.

**10** — kāṣṭhamadhye yathā vahniḥ puṣpe gandhaḥ payo'mṛtam |
> As fire [latent] in wood, fragrance in the flower, nectar in milk —

*(the simile-series continues; the text's middle section covers the Śāmbhavī-vidyā's nature and the body-location of the knowledge.)*

Notes: the Śāmbhavī-vidyā as the hidden kula-bride (kulavadhū) — [P: the "guptā kulavadhūr iva" image — the hidden-woman motif of the Kaula transmission (cf. our Kulacūḍāmaṇi's "kulasundarī" list); the fourteen-vidyās critique — the vidyā-hierarchy of the tradition's epistemology; the dehastha-doctrine (everything in the body) — the body-ontology of the KJN's kulapiṇḍa.]

# 4. Kulasāra (M00285)

### Time-place-context
- **PERIOD:** late-medieval Kaula doctrine text (the varṇa-question and the soma-myth suggest the consolidating Kula-orthodoxy). [X: dating — pass 2.]
- **PLACE/PATH:** M00285 e-text (Dyczkowski-supervised data entry).
- **GENRE/REGISTER:** doctrinal dialogue (Devī-Īśvara-Brahmā); the kula-doctrine's soteriology and the restoration-myth.
- **FRAME:** the Devī asks "the doctrine of the kaulikas"; the answer includes the myth of the gods' loss of soma-power — the ritual-ecological justification of the kula-mata.

## The opening (vv. 1–22)
**1** — devadeva jagannātha sarvāṅgamavisārada | śrotumicchāmi tatvena kaulikānāṃ mataṃ vibho ||
> God of gods, lord of the world, skilled in all the limbs — I wish to hear truly the doctrine of the kaulikas, O Lord.

**2** — kecid vadanti munayaḥ strīvargaphaladaṃ mataṃ | caturvargapradaṃ kecid vadanti muni sattamaḥ ||
> Some sages say the doctrine gives the fruit of the class of women (strīvarga); others, O best of sages, say it gives the four aims.

**3** — tasyāśāmavidhiṃ deva śrotumicchāmichāmi tatvata | etanme saṃsayaṃ chindhi kenācin tamidaṃ sataṃ ||
> Its ritual of the women (aśāmavidhi), O god, I wish to hear truly — cut this doubt of mine with something, this true [teaching].

**4** — ahaṃ te kathayiṣyāmi kulasāraṃ samāsata | etat prasanna mayā devi brahmāgre kathitaṃ purā ||
> I will tell you the Kulasāra in brief: this, pleased, was told by me, O Devī, to Brahmā in the beginning.

**5** — tato brahmā samāgreṇa nijagnāda yathā śṛṇu | devayāni yadā devi kvacinādenu jālaye ||
> Then Brahmā with his host declared it — hear how: when, O Devī, the divine beings, somewhere, in the net of the nāda...

**6** — modayaḥ māsamantrārthi mṛtasaṃjīvamāya vai | tadātmānāṃdadau tasmai tatra satvaka visvarāṃ ||
> [X: corrupt pādas — "modayaḥ māsamantrārthi" unclear; sense: the gods, deprived of the soma, sought the reviving [power]]. Then he gave himself to that [asking]... the sound (?), the universal...

**7** — madhyaṃ yātubhiḥ sarvatra hlālāmati vināsanī | tato devāṃ surāṃ tyaktvā somapārāsaṃkhyā? bhava ||
> [X: corrupt] ...the middle... abandoning the divine beings and the surā [liquor]...

**8** — tvadva prabhuti devānāṃ balahānirajāyata | tadā devāraṇe jaghna dānavāmvargakāṃkṣiṇe ||
> Then the loss of the gods' power arose; then in the forest of the gods they struck the dānavas desiring the host...

**9** — tadā mayā vidhi pṛṣṭo devānāṃ hitakāmyayā | kenopāyena deveśa devānāṃ vijayi bhavet ||
> Then the ritual was asked of me, desiring the good of the gods: by what means, O Lord of gods, would the gods become victorious?

**10** — surāvihīnāstri daśā balahinā bhavaṃ ita | kathaṃ śuddhi bhavet tasyā vāsā medhyā bhavennavā ||
> The three [worlds?] without the surā, weakened in power... how would there be purity? Would the woman's use (vāsā) become pure or not?

**11** — kaulikaṃ matamādhyāyaṃ kimuvarga catusrayaṃ | prāpnuvanti yathāmartyo tanmetyaṃ vaktumarhasi ||
> The Kaulika doctrine, its study, the four classes [?] — as mortals obtain them, that you should tell.

**12** — mayā śrutvā purā deva varṇāsra caturaḥ kilaṃ | tatra sarvasya varṇasya brāhmaṇaṃ gururucyate ||
> Heard by me before, O god, the four varṇas indeed — of every varṇa, the brāhmaṇa is declared the guru.

Notes: the soma-loss myth (the gods weakened without the surā — the kula's ritual restores the power through the female principle); the varṇa-question (the brāhmaṇa as guru of all). [X: vv. 5–11 are transmission-wobbled — collation needed; the "strīvarga/aśāmavidhi" (the woman-class practice) — the kula's tantric core.]

# 5. Azeṣakulavallarī (M00311)

### Time-place-context
- **PERIOD:** author = **Gaṇananāthabhaṭṭa** (a named Kashmiri ritual author — the kula-paddhati literature); date [X] — the pīṭha/nyāsa liturgy is the mature Kula-ritual form.
- **PLACE/PATH:** NGMCP manuscript 5-4656 (Reel A 175/11) — Nepalese transmission of a Kashmir-author text.
- **GENRE/REGISTER:** ritual paddhati (the "all-kula vine" — the complete ritual procedure); prose liturgy with mantra-nyāsas.
- **FRAME:** the Mārtaṇḍabhairava liturgy — the sun-Bhairava complex (cf. the KJN's sūrya-meditations and the Kubjikā paṭala 5's sun-disc).

## The opening (the liturgy's first section)
**Invocation** — oṃ namaḥ śrīśrītripurāmbike ||
> Oṃ, homage to the blessed Tripurāmbā.

**The Mārtaṇḍa-nyāsa** — oṃ hrīṃ śrīṃ hrāṃ hrīṃ saḥ mārtaṇḍabhairavāya prakāśaśaktisaṃhitāya sa kaṇṭhāc chiraparyantam | nyaset | hrāṃ hṛdayāya namaḥ | hrīṃ śirase svāhā | agnimaṇḍalāya daśakalātmane namaḥ | iti sampūjya tatra tāmrapātraṃ pratiṣṭhāpya sūryamaṇḍalāya dvādaśakalātmane namaḥ | iti saṃpūjya pavitrajalenābhyukṣya somamaṇḍalāya ṣoḍaśakalātmane namaḥ | ityabhyarcya ||
> Oṃ hrīṃ śrīṃ hrāṃ hrīṃ saḥ — to Mārtaṇḍa-Bhairava, united with the power of light (prakāśaśakti) — from the throat to the top of the head, let him place [the nyāsa]: hrāṃ to the heart, homage; hrīṃ to the head, svāhā; to the fire-disc of ten kalās, homage. Having worshipped thus, placing the copper vessel there — to the sun-disc of twelve kalās, homage; having worshipped, sprinkling with the purifying water — to the moon-disc of sixteen kalās, homage; having adored —

**The kalā-offerings** — tadyathā | laṃ prabhutāyai namaḥ | vaṃ vimalāyai namaḥ | raṃ rasāyai namaḥ | haṃ paramamukhāyai namaḥ | ityāgneyādi madhyāntam | skandātmane dharmāya namaḥ | lomātmane jñānāya namaḥ | paṃkeruhātmane vairāgyāya namaḥ | kaṇikātmane aiśvaryāya namaḥ | ityuparyupari | rāṃ dīptāyai namaḥ | rīṃ śūṣmāyai namaḥ | rūṃ jayāyai namaḥ | raṃ bhadrāyai namaḥ | raiṃ vibhūtyai namaḥ | rāṃ vimalāyai namaḥ | roṃ amoghāyai namaḥ | raṃ vidyutāyai namaḥ | raḥ savato mukhyai namaḥ | iti śaktinavakaṃ pūrvādi dalamadhye cābhyarcya ||
> Namely: laṃ to Prabhutā, homage; vaṃ to Vimalā, homage; raṃ to Rasā, homage; haṃ to Paramamukhā, homage — thus from the fire[-corner], through the middle, to the end. To the Skanda-self, to Dharma, homage; to the Loma-self, to Jñāna, homage; to the Paṅkeruha-self, to Vairāgya, homage; to the Kaṇika-self, to Aiśvarya, homage — thus, one above another. rāṃ to Dīptā, homage; rīṃ to Śūṣmā; rūṃ to Jayā; raṃ to Bhadrā; raiṃ to Vibhūti; rāṃ to Vimalā; roṃ to Amoghā; raṃ to Vidyutā; raḥ to Savitṛ-mukhyā — thus the nine powers (śaktinavaka), adored in the middle of the eastern petal and the rest.

> To the Brahmā-Viṣṇu-Śiva-natured heroic yoga-seat (śauryayogapīṭha), homage. Having adored the whole set of mantras — aiṃ hrāṃ hrīṃ saḥ, to the Mārtaṇḍa-Bhairava form, homage. In the directions: soṃ to Soma, homage; vuṃ to Budha; vṛṃ to Bṛhaspati; śuṃ to Śukra; aṃ to Aṅgāraka; śaṃ to Śanaiścara; rāṃ to Rāhu; keṃ to Ketu; aṃ to Aruṇa. aiṃ hrīṃ śrīṃ gaṃ to Gaṇapati, homage; ikṣāṃ to the Kṣetrapāla; oṃ to the Door-Śrī; oṃ to the Threshold — having adored, the right foot... entering, and the naked [rite] in the corners... oṃ gaṃ to Gaṇapati; oṃ duṃ to Durgā; oṃ vaṃ to Vaṭuka; oṃ kṣāṃ to the Kṣetrapāla — having worshipped thus.
