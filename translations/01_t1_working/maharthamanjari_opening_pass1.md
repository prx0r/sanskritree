## The maṅgala

**1** — namo nālayate śuṇḍāṃ viṣāṇena mṛṇāline | pratyakkamalakandāya karṇābhyāṃ parṇaśāline ||
> Homage to the one who with his trunk fondles the lotus-stalk, who wears the leaf by his ears — to the inner lotus-root: [homage] to Gaṇeśa.

**2** — jayatyamūlamamlānamauttaraṃ tattvamadvayam | spandāspandaparispandamakarandamahotpalam ||
> Triumphs the rootless, unwilting, supreme, non-dual principle — the great lotus whose nectar is the trembling and the untrembling and the all-trembling (spanda-aspanda-parispanda).

**3** — kāruṇyāmṛtasindhoruditamivāvartamīkṣaṇāpāṅgāt | mauktikamayaṃ dadhānā tāṭaṅkaṃ jayati gauravī mūrtiḥ ||
> May the weighty form (gauravī mūrtiḥ) triumph — bearing, as it were, the pearl-made earring arisen from the whirlpool of the compassion-nectar-ocean, from the corner of [her] glance.

**4** — sphūrtaye viśvaśilpasya śrīśivānandamūrtaye | nityonmeṣanimeṣāyai nistuṣāyai namastviṣe ||
> Homage to the radiance (tviṣe) that is the blessed Śivānanda-form, for the flashing-forth of the world's craft — the ever-opening-and-closing, the chaffless (nistuṣā).

**5** — yasmādanuttaramahāhradamajjanaṃ me saubhāgyaśāmbhavasukhānubhavaśca yasmāt | tatsvātmacitkramavimarśamayaṃ gurūṇā-mavalliyugmamuditoditavīryamīḍe ||
> From which [comes] my immersion in the unsurpassed great pool, and from which the auspicious Śāmbhava-joy's experience — that pair of the gurus' feet, its power risen and arisen, made of the self-conscious order-of-reflection (svātma-cit-krama-vimarśa) — I praise.

**6** — namo nikhilamālinyavilāpanapaṭīyase | mahāprakāśapādābjaparāgaparamāṇave ||
> Homage to the one skilled in dissolving all stain — to the supreme atom of the pollen of the great-prakāśa's lotus-feet.

**7** — gorakṣo lokadhiyā deśikadṛṣṭyā maheśvarānandaḥ | unmīlayāmi parimalamantargrāhyaṃ mahārthamañjaryām ||
> I, Maheśvarānanda — a Gorakṣa in the world's view, in the deśika's vision — open the Parimala, the inner-apprehendable [commentary] on the Mahārthamañjarī.

**8** — svakriyāyā api vyākhyāṃ svayameva prayuñjmahe | uparyapyātmasaṃrambhasambhogāmreḍanotsukāḥ ||
> Even for my own work I myself provide the commentary — above [the verse], eager for the repetition of the enjoyment of my own fervor.

**9** — yadvā vineyajanacittacamatkriyārtha-matrodyamo'yamudito'stu tadetadāstām | saṃkṣepavistaravibhāgaviviktaśobhaḥ puṣpāñjalirbhavatu vāṅmaya eṣa śambhoḥ ||
> Or rather — for the wonder of the minds of the teachable people, may this endeavor arise; let that be. May this verbal handful-of-flowers of Śambhu, adorned with the division into brief-and-extended, be [accepted].

**10** — avagataśivadṛṣṭipratyabhijñārthatattva-kramasaraṇirahasyollāsasarvasvavedī | gurucaraṇasaparyācāturīcidghano'haṃ gahanamapi hṛdantarvyoma tad vyākaromi ||
> I who know the whole secret-springing-essence of the Krama-path's truth — [having] understood the meaning of the Śivadṛṣṭi and the Pratyabhijñā — I, dense in the skill of serving the guru's feet, unfold even the dense inner-sky of the heart.

**11** — iha mahati rahasyonmīlane maṅgalāya prabhavati mama saṃvidyoginīnāṃ prasādaḥ | api tu kulasaparyābimbasaṃbandhyavandhyāḥ sakṛdapi matimanto nainamudghāṭayantu ||
> Here, at the great opening of the secret, for the auspicious [rite] the grace of my awareness-yoginīs prevails; but the intelligent ones — barren through their connection with the reflection of the kula-worship — let them not open this, even once.

**12** — svapnasamayopalabdhā sā sumukhī siddhayoginī devī | gāthābhiḥ saptatyā svāpitabhāṣābhirastu samprītā ||
> That fair-faced goddess, the siddha-yoginī, obtained at the time of a dream — may she be well-pleased with the seventy gāthās in her own-awakened speech.

**13** — vardhatāṃ deśikaḥ śrīmān saṃvinmārgaśca vardhatām | māheśvarāśca vardhantāṃ vardhatāṃ ca maheśvaraḥ ||
> May the blessed deśika flourish; may the path of awareness flourish; may the Māheśvaras flourish; and may Maheśvara flourish.
