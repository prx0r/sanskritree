## Prakāśa 2 — the praise of the Kaulika

**2-1** — kulapūjā samaṃ nāsti puṇyamanyajjagattraye | tasmād yaḥ pūjayed bhaktyā bhuktimuktyoḥ sabhājanam ||
> There is no merit equal to the kula-worship in the three worlds; therefore he who worships with devotion is the vessel of bhukti and mukti.

**2-2** — anadhītopyaśāstrajño gurubhakto dṛḍhavrataḥ | kulapūjāratoyastu same priyatamo bhavet ||
> Even one unread, unversed in the śāstras, devoted to the guru, firm in vows — he who delights in the kula-worship becomes the dearest to me.

**2-3** — kīrtyamāne kule yeṣāṃ romāṃcogadgadasvaraḥ | ānandaḥ śrūyate devi kathitāḥ kaulikeśvarāḥ ||
> Those whose hair stands on end, whose voice chokes, when the kula is praised — whose bliss is heard, O Devī, those are declared the lords of the Kaulikas.

**2-4** — sarvadharmādhiko loke kuladharmaḥ śivoditaḥ | iti ye niścatadhīyaḥ proktāste kaulikottamāḥ ||
> "The kula-dharma, spoken by Śiva, exceeds all dharmas in the world" — those who hold this conviction are proclaimed the best of the Kaulikas.

**2-5** — yo bhavet kulatattvajñaḥ kuladharmaviśāradaḥ | kulārcanarataḥ sasyāt kaulikodāmbhikāḥ priye ||
> He who knows the kula-principle, is skilled in the kula-dharma, delights in the kula-worship — he becomes the udāmbhika (?) of the Kaulikas, O dear one. [X: "udāmbhikāḥ" corrupt]

**2-6** — kulabhaktān kulācārān kulajñānān kulavratān | prīto bhavati yo dṛṣṭvā kaulikaḥ satu me priyaḥ ||
> He who, seeing them, is pleased — the kula-devotees, the kula-practitioners, the kula-knowers, the kula-vowed — that one is the Kaulika, dear to me.

**2-7** — saṃsmṛtya kīrtito dṛṣṭvā vandito bhāṣitopi vā | punāti kuladharmīṣṭaścāṃḍālopi yadachayā ||
> Whether remembered, praised, seen, bowed to, or spoken [with] — the devoted-to-kula-dharma purifies, even a caṇḍāla, by his shadow.

**2-8** — sarvajño vāpi mūrkho vāpyuttamo vādhamo'pi vā | devi yatra kulajñānī tatrāhaṃ ca tvayā saha ||
> Whether all-knowing or a fool, the highest or the lowest — O Devī, wherever the kula-knower [is], there [am] I, with you.

**2-9** — nāhaṃ vasāmi kailāśe na merau na ca mandire | kulajñā yatra tiṣṭhanti tatra tiṣṭhāmi bhāminī ||
> I do not dwell on Kailāsa, nor on Meru, nor in the temple: where the kula-knowers stand, there I stand, O fair lady.

**2-10** — tatvatraya śrīcaraṇamūlamantrārthatatvavit | devatā gurubhaktaśca kaulikaḥ syānnadīkṣayā ||
> The knower of the three-principles' truth, the meaning of the root-mantra at the blessed feet, devoted to the deity and the guru — he becomes a Kaulika [even] without the dīkṣā.

**2-11** — kulayogī cared yatra sasanmārgaḥ kuleśvari | sadūramapigantavyaṃ yatra māheśvarojanaḥ ||
> Wherever the kula-yogī, with the true path, moves, O lady of the kula — even from afar the Māheśvara man is to be sought.

**2-12** — iṣṭavyaśca prayatnena tatra sannihitastvayam | atidūrasthito vāpi dṛṣṭavyaḥ kuladeśikaḥ ||
> And he is to be sought with effort; he is present there — even standing very far, the kula-teacher is to be seen.

**2-13** — samīpe vartamāno'pi na dṛṣṭavya paśuḥ priye | kulajñānī bhaved yatra sadeśaḥ puṇyabhāgjanaḥ ||
> Even the paśu standing nearby is not to be seen, O dear one; where the kula-knower is, [there] the man of the same place shares the merit.

**2-14** — darśanādarcanāt tasya trisaptakulamuddharet | kulajñāni namālokya svasantānagṛhasthitam ||
> From seeing and worshipping him, one raises up the thrice-seven lineage (kula); looking at the kula-knower dwelling in his own lineage-house...

**2-15** — nṛtyanti pitaraḥ sarve yāsyāma paramāṃ gatim | samyak saṃśanti pitaraḥ suvṛṣṭirmivavarṣikāḥ ||
> All the fathers dance: "we shall go to the highest way" — the fathers rejoice rightly, like [the earth at] a good rain after the drought.

**2-16** — kuleṣu putro vā pautro vā kauliko bhavet | sadhanyaḥ khalulokesmin puruṣaḥ kṣīṇakalmaṣaḥ ||
> In the lineages, whether a son or a grandson becomes a Kaulika — truly blessed in this world is that man, his stain worn away.

**2-17** — yat samīpaṃ samāyānti kulācāryā mudā priye | kaulikendri samāyāte kaulikā va sathaṃ prati ||
> Where the kula-teachers come with joy, O dear one — when the lord of the Kaulikas comes, the Kaulikas [come] toward the place.

**2-18** — samāyānti mudādevi yoginyo yogibhiḥ saha | praviśya kulayogīśaṃ bhuṃjete pitṛ devatāḥ ||
> The yoginīs come with joy, O Devī, with the yogis; entering the lord of the kula-yogis, the fathers and the deities enjoy.

**2-19** — tasmāt saṃpūjayed bhaktyā kulajñāne va nā parān | abhyarcyayitvā tvāṃ devi tvadbhaktyānnārcayeti ye ||
> Therefore one should worship with devotion the kula-knowers, not others: "having adored you, O Devī, [we do] not worship [others] by your devotion" — those who [say so]...

**2-20** — pāpāṣṭāstvat pradattasya bhājanaṃ na bhavanti te | yat kṛtaṃ kulaniṣṭānāṃ taddevānāṃ kṛtaṃ bhavet ||
> Those [who] are not the vessel of the eight sins given [by you]; what is done for the kula-devotees becomes done for the gods.

**2-21** — devāḥ kulapriyāḥ sarve tasmāt kaulikamarcayet | natuṣyāmyahamanyatra tathā bhaktyā supūjitaḥ ||
> All the gods love the kula; therefore one should worship the Kaulika. I am not pleased elsewhere, thus worshipped with devotion.

**2-22** — kaulikaircitaḥ samyag yathā tuṣyāmi pārvati | yat phalaṃ kaulikendrāṇāṃ pūjanāllabhate priye ||
> As I am pleased [when] properly worshipped by the Kaulikas, O Pārvatī — whatever fruit one obtains from worshipping the lords of the Kaulikas, O dear one —

**2-23** — tatphalaṃ nāpnuyāttīrthatapodānayamabrataiḥ | dattamiṣṭaṃ hutaṃ japtaṃ pūjitaṃ taptamambike ||
> — that fruit one does not obtain by the sacred-fords, austerity, giving, the yama-vows; [nor by] the given, the offered, the sacrificed, the recited, the worshipped, the heated, O Ambikā.

**2-24** — kaulikasya bhavadvyarthaṃ kaulikaṃ yo va mānayet | śmaśānaṃ tadgṛhaṃ devi bhasmanī bahutaṃ yathā ||
> He who would honor the Kaulika's... — his house is a burning-ground, O Devī, like the ash [of a fire]... [X: "bhavadvyarthaṃ" corrupt]

**2-25** — yathā śaktyā tu yat kiṃcid yodadyāt kulayogine | viśeṣatithiṣu prītyā tat phalaṃ naiva gaṇyate ||
> Whatever one gives to the kula-yogī, to the extent of one's power, on the special tithis, with love — that fruit is never counted [as spent].

**2-26** — yo devi svayamādgāyakulajñānā śubhe dine | abhyarcya devatā budhyā gandhapuṣpākṣatādibhiḥ ||
> He who, O Devī, on an auspicious day, having himself adored the kula-knowers and the deity, with understanding, with fragrance, flowers, unbroken-rice and the rest —

**2-27** — mādibhiḥ pañcamudrābhiḥ sadbhaktyā paritoṣayet | teṣu tuṣṭeṣvahaṃ tuṣṭastuṣṭāḥ syuḥ sarvadevatāḥ ||
> — pleases [them] with the five makāra-mudrās and with true devotion: when those are pleased, I am pleased; all the deities become pleased.

**2-28** — bhaganīṃ vā sutāṃ bhāryāṃ yo dadyāt kulayogine | madhumattāya deveśi tasya puṇyaṃ na gaṇyate ||
> He who gives a sister, a daughter, or a wife to the kula-yogī, drunk with the wine — O Devī, his merit is not counted [as spent].

**2-29** — anikhātavinikṣiptam aprayatnena varddhitam | paralokasya pātheyaṃ vīravaktrerpitaṃ madhu ||
> [The wine] placed without being buried, grown without effort — the provision for the other world: the honey (madhu) offered into the vīra's mouth.

**2-30** — pāpācārasamāyuktaṃ sarvalokabahiṣkṛta | trāyate hi kuladravyaṃ kulagīśvarārpitam ||
> [Even one] joined with evil conduct, cast out of all the world — the kula-substance (kula-dravya), offered to the lord of the kula, saves [him].

**2-31** — yasmin deśe vasedvīraḥ kulapujārataḥ priye | sopi deśo bhavet pūtaḥ kiṃ punastat purasthitaḥ ||
> In whatever land the vīra dwells, delighting in the kula-worship, O dear one — even that land becomes pure; what [of] one standing in that city?

**2-32** — kaulikendre sakṛd bhakte puṇyaṃ koṭiguṇaṃ bhavet | kiṃ punarbahubhirmuktaistat puṇyaṃ naiva gaṇyate ||
> In a lord of the Kaulikas, once devoted, the merit becomes a crore-fold; what [of] the many released [by him]? That merit is never counted.

**2-33** — tasmāt sarvaprayatnena sarvāvasthāsu sarvadā | kuladharmaratobhūyāt kulajñāninamarcayet ||
> Therefore, with every effort, in every state, always, one should delight in the kula-dharma and worship the kula-knower.

**2-34** — yo nindāstutiṃ śītoṣṇasukhaduḥkhādibaṃdhuṣu | nāśaktaḥ sa tu yogīśo harṣāharṣavivarjitaḥ ||
> He who is not able [to be moved] by praise-and-blame, cold-and-heat, pleasure-and-pain, in the relatives and the rest — he is the lord of the yogis, free of elation and dejection.

**2-35** — niḥsaṃkalpavikalpaśca nilonopādhivāsataḥ | nijasvarūpanirmāyāḥ sayogī paratattvavit ||
> Free of the saṃkalpa-and-vikalpa, from dwelling free of the high-and-low conditions — the yogī, the knower of the supreme principle, [is] free of the māyā of his own form.
