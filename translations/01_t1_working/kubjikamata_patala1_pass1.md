## The maṅgala — the akula/kula cosmology

**1/1** — saṃvartāmaṇḍalānte kramapadanihitānandaśaktiḥ subhīmā saṃsṛjyādyaṃ catuṣkam akulakulagataṃ pañcakaṃ cānyaṣaṭkam | catvāraḥ pañcako 'nyaḥ punar api caturas tattvato maṇḍaledaṃ saṃsṛṣṭaṃ yena tasmai namata guruvaraṃ bhairavaṃ śrīkujeśam ||
> At the end of the saṃvarta-maṇḍala — the bliss-power placed in the step-of-krama, the very terrible — having emitted the first fourfold, the five gone into akula-and-kula, and the other six; four, five, another, again four, in truth: homage to the guru-most, Bhairava, the blessed Kujeśa, by whom this maṇḍala was emitted.
> *[X: the maṅgala's arithmetic — the 4 + 5 + 6 + 4(+5+4) schema; the report's note: the akula/kula juxtaposition is the compressed cosmology. The "krama-pada" (the step of krama) is the report's krama-dossier: ritual/cognition-sequence — here the bliss-power's place in the maṇḍala's order.]*

## The revelation-site — Santāna-city

**1/2** — śrīmaddhimavataḥ pṛṣṭhe trikūṭaśikharāntagam | santānapuramadhyagam anekākārarūpiṇam ||
> On the back of the blessed Himavat, reaching the summit of the Trikūṭa: the many-formed one dwelling in the midst of Santāna-city —

**1/3** — tryasraṃ vai triprakāraṃ tu triśaktitriguṇojjvalam | candrasūryakṛtālokaṃ vahnidedīpyavarcasam ||
> — triangular, threefold in kind, blazing with the three powers and the three guṇas; lit by moon and sun, radiant with the fire's brilliance —

**1/4** — trisandhyāveṣṭitaṃ divyaṃ prākāratripathānvitam | dvārapālatrayopetaṃ trikapāṭārgalānvitam ||
> — the divine [city] girdled with the three junctures, furnished with the wall and the three ways; with the three door-keepers, with the triple bolt-and-bar —

**1/5** — anekaratnasandīptam udyānavanamaṇḍitam | vasantaguṇasampannaṃ satatānandapūritam ||
> — blazing with manifold jewels, adorned with parks and groves; endowed with the spring's qualities, ever filled with bliss —

**1/6** — santānabhuvanaṃ divyaṃ divyādivyair niṣevitam | tatra taṃ bhuvaneśānaṃ vyaktāvyaktaṃ sanātanam ||
> — the divine abode of Santāna, served by the divine-and-non-divine: there [stands] that lord of the abode, the manifest-unmanifest, the eternal —

**1/7** — kāryakāraṇabhāvena kiñcit kālam apekṣayā | tiṣṭhate bhairavīśāno maunam ādāya niścalam ||
> — the lord of the Bhairavīs stands, by the relation of effect-and-cause, for some time, in expectation, taking up the motionless silence.

**1/8** — tatra devagaṇāḥ sarve sakinnaramahoragāḥ | kurvanti kalakalārāvaṃ samāgatya samīpataḥ ||
> There all the god-hosts, with the kinnaras and the great serpents, make a clamorous din, having come near.

## The Himavat-dialogue

**1/9** — śrutvā kalakalārāvaṃ ko bhavān kim ihāgataḥ | himavān tu prasannātmā gacchāmo 'nveṣaṇaṃ prati ||
> Hearing the clamorous din: "Who are you, and why have you come here?" — Himavat, his mind pleased, [thought] "let us go in search."

**1/10** — yāvat sa paśyate tatsthaṃ śivajñānāvalokanāt | tāvat paśyati śrīnātham āgataṃ tu mamāśrame ||
> As soon as he saw him standing there, by the sight of the Śiva-knowledge — then he sees the blessed Lord come to my hermitage.

**1/11** — gatas tūrṇaṃ prayatnena yatrāste bhagavān prabhuḥ | sakuṭumbaḥ stutiṃ divyāṃ himavān vākyam abravīt ||
> Having gone swiftly, with effort, to where the blessed Lord sat — with his family, Himavat spoke the divine praise:

**1/12** — adya me saphalaṃ janma adya me saphalaṃ tapaḥ | adya me saphalaṃ sthānaṃ jīvitaṃ saphalaṃ mama ||
> "Today my birth is fruitful; today my austerity is fruitful; today my station is fruitful; my life is fruitful —

**1/13** — adya dhanyaḥ kṛtārtho 'ham adya me saphalā gatiḥ | adya me saphalaṃ sarvaṃ trailokyaṃ sacarācaram ||
> — today I am blessed, my aim accomplished; today my way is fruitful; today all the three worlds, moving and unmoving, are fruitful for me —

**1/14** — yan nātha bhavadaṅghribhyām aṅkitaṃ mastakaṃ mama | tena vikhyātakīrtis tu bhaviṣyāmi jagattraye ||
> — since, O Lord, my head is marked by your two feet, by that I shall become famous in the three worlds —

**1/15** — tvatprasādena deveśa sarvajñatvaṃ labhāmy aham | kim anena na paryāptaṃ yad āyāta-m-iha prabhuḥ ||
> — by your grace, O Lord of gods, I obtain all-knowingness; what is not accomplished by this, that you have come here, O Lord?

**1/16** — kiṃ kurmaḥ kā gatir mahyam ādeśo dīyatāṃ prabho | himavantavilāpo 'yaṃ śrutvaivaṃ sakuṭumbinaḥ ||
> What shall we do? What is my way? Let the command be given, O Lord." — hearing this lament of Himavat with his family,

## The Kālī-boons

**1/17** — uvāca bhagavān nāthaḥ prahasyemāṃ giraṃ śubhām | himavanta gire sādhu tuṣṭo 'haṃ tava klinnayā ||
> the blessed Lord, smiling, spoke this auspicious speech: "O Himavat, O mountain, well done — I am pleased by your devotion."

**1/18** — prārthayasva varaṃ kiñcid dāsyāmo manasepsitam | himavanta mahāprājña tuṣṭo 'haṃ paramārthataḥ ||
> "Ask some boon — we will give what your mind desires; O Himavat, great sage, I am pleased in truth."

**1/19** — kiṃ kṛtaṃ me maheśāna svakīyadayayā prabho | rucitaṃ kuru deveśa himavān abravīd idam ||
> "What has been done by me, O great Lord, by your own grace, O Lord? Do what is pleasing" — Himavat said this.

**1/20** — parvatollapitaṃ śrutvā uvācedaṃ sureśvaraḥ | prasannagirayā divyaṃ varaṃ dātuṃ samudyataḥ ||
> Hearing the mountain's speech, the Lord of the gods said this, ready to give the divine boon with a pleased heart:

**1/21** — prathamaṃ tāvat tubhyaṃ hi pañca ślokān paṭhet tu yaḥ | sannidhānaḥ prayatnena bhaviṣyāmo hy avaśyataḥ ||
> "First, indeed, for you: whoever recites five ślokas [of this], I will certainly be present [to him] with effort —

**1/22** — dvitīyaṃ sannidhāno 'haṃ bhaviṣyāmi tavādhvare | tṛtīyaṃ sarvaśailānāṃ rājatvaṃ cakradhāriṇaḥ ||
> — secondly, I will be present at your sacrifice; thirdly, [the boon of] lordship over all the mountains, O disc-bearer —

**1/23** — caturthaṃ mama tulyatvaṃ pañcamaṃ mokṣadaṃ nṛṇām | evaṃ pañca varās tubhyaṃ himavanta punar vada ||
> — fourthly, equality with me; fifthly, the giver of liberation to men. Thus five boons for you, O Himavat — speak again."

**1/24** — kim anyena mahādeva ātmatulyas tvayā kila | kṛto 'haṃ tat kim anyena kiṃ tu devābhayaṃ dada ||
> "What [need] of another, O great God — I am made equal to yourself, indeed. What [need] of another? But give, O God, the fearlessness."

**1/25** — evaṃ brūtha punaḥ kiñcid yat te manasi rocate | tad arpayāmy ahaṃ sarvaṃ pūrvam evoditaṃ mayā ||
> "Speak thus again something that pleases your mind — I give all that, as was said before by me."

**1/26** — āśrame sati sarvatra prāticāraṃ vinā na hi | tatra ḍikkarikā mahyaṃ kariṣyaty upalepanam ||
> "In the hermitage, everywhere, there is no protection without a counter-rite; there the Ḍikkārikā will make the plastering for me —"

**1/27** — sā ca dharmapravṛttā ca yena tat kriyatāṃ prabho | iṣṭā sā mama deveśa kālikā ca kumārikā ||
> "— and she is intent on the dharma; therefore let that be done, O Lord. That [one] is dear to me, O Lord of gods, the Kālikā, the maiden."

**1/28** — evaṃ babhūva tasmād vai tatrasthā guṇaśālinī | prasādayati deveśaṃ vinayādyair anekadhā ||
> Thus it came to be; therefore the virtuous one standing there pleases the Lord of gods with her humility and the rest, in manifold ways.

**1/29** — vinayenopasaṅgamya stutistotrair anekadhā | kālena bahunā kālīm uvācedaṃ kuleśvaraḥ ||
> Having approached with humility, with praises and hymns in manifold ways — after much time, the Lord of the kula spoke this to Kālī:

**1/30** — tuṣṭo 'haṃ kālike tubhyaṃ brūhi kiñcin manepsitam | yat tvayā dhāritaṃ citte tat prārthaya hy aśaṅkitā ||
> "I am pleased, O Kālikā; tell me something your mind desires. Whatever you have held in your mind — ask it without fear."

**1/31** — labdhvā praṇayasadbhāvaṃ tyaktalajjā manotsukā | vadate nātha nāthas tvaṃ bhavāsmākaṃ surārcitaḥ ||
> Having obtained the true affection, abandoning shame, eager in mind, she says: "O Lord, you are our Lord, worshipped by the gods."

**1/32** — evaṃ śrutvā maheśāno vākyam ānandasambhavam | tataḥ sampāditaṃ śīghram ājñānandaguṇojjvalam ||
> Hearing this speech born of bliss, the great Lord then made [it] quickly, blazing with the qualities of the command and bliss —

**1/33** — ājñāsanasamārūḍhaṃ preritānantaśambhunā | darśitaṃ nikhilaṃ sarvaṃ pūrvasantānagocaram ||
> — mounted on the seat of the command, impelled by the All-Śambhu: the whole [past], within the earlier Santāna[-lineage], was shown.

**1/34** — tataḥ prabuddhabhāvātmā vadaty evaṃ kuleśvarī | darśitaṃ nikhilaṃ mahyaṃ kim āścaryaṃ kujeśvara ||
> Then the lady of the kula, her nature awakened, says thus: "The whole has been shown to me — what wonder is this, O Kujeśvara?"

**1/35** — viditaṃ nātha me sarvaṃ kriyākāraṇagocaram | yasmāt sampadyate hy evaṃ tad ācakṣva kujeśvara ||
> "All is known to me, O Lord, within the reach of the action-and-cause; [but] that from which this comes to be thus — tell that, O Kujeśvara."

**1/36** — ājñāto guṇam aiśvaryaṃ sañjātaṃ parameśvara | asya tantrārthasadbhāvaṃ brūhi me paramārthataḥ ||
> "The quality and the lordship, [all that is] commanded and arisen, O supreme Lord: tell me the true being of this tantra's meaning, in truth."

**1/37** — dṛṣṭaṃ samastaparyantaṃ bhavadājñāṣaḍadhvaram | brūhi nirdeśataḥ sarvaṃ yadi tuṣṭaḥ kujeśvara ||
> "The whole, to its end, the six-sacrificed command of you — [is] seen. Tell all in detail, if you are pleased, O Kujeśvara."

**1/38** — ājñāto guṇasadbhāvaṃ brūhi deva guṇodayam | yathā drakṣyāpitaṃ sarvam ājñādvāreṇa me 'khilam ||
> "[What is] commanded, the being of the qualities — tell, O God, the arising of the qualities; as all was made to be seen by me through the door of the command."

**1/39** — pūrvavṛttāntasadbhāvaṃ pūrvapāṭhaśrutaṃ ca yat | pūrvakalpārthanirdeśam ājñāto jñāpitaṃ tvayā ||
> "The being of the earlier events, and what was heard in the earlier reading, the teaching of the earlier kalpa's meaning — commanded, was made known by you."

**1/40** — pūrvasandarśitaṃ deva ājñāguṇamahodayam | tadbhraṃśād bhraṃśam utpannam ato 'rthaṃ kathaya sphuṭam ||
> "The great-arising of the command's qualities was shown before, O God; from its falling, the fall has arisen — therefore tell the meaning clearly."

**1/41** — kalpe kalpe tvayā deva saṃhitārtham anekadhā | mantratantrakriyāyogāḥ kathitā nāvadhāritāḥ ||
> "In kalpa after kalpa, by you, O God, the saṃhitā's meaning — the mantras, the tantras, the rites, the yogas — has been told in manifold ways, [yet] not grasped."

**1/42** — idānīṃ saṃsphuṭaṃ sarvam ājñāguṇamahodayam | yasmāt sañjāyate sarvaṃ tatprabhāvaṃ vada prabho ||
> "Now [let it be] fully clear — the great-arising of the command's qualities; [tell] that power from which all arises, O Lord."

**1/43** — ānandaś cāvaliś caiva prabhur yogī tathaiva ca | atītaś caiva pādaś ca ṣaṭ prakārāḥ kathaṃ sthitāḥ ||
> "The bliss (ānanda) and the āvali, the lord (prabhu) and the yogī likewise, and the atīta and the pāda — how do the six kinds stand?"

## The Bhairava's answer (the paṭala's doctrinal core)

**1/44** — sādhu sādhu mahābhāge mahānandavidhāyini | pṛcchitaṃ yat tvayā vākyam atyadbhutam anāmayam ||
> "Well done, well done, O great-fated one, maker of the great bliss: the speech you have asked is most wonderful, free of disease —

**1/45** — gopitaṃ sarvarudrāṇāṃ vīrāṇāṃ bhairaveṣu ca | siddhakramaṃ nirācāraṃ tathāpi kathayāmi te ||
> — the siddha-krama (the accomplished order), the rite-less — hidden from all the Rudras, the vīras, and the Bhairavas; yet I will tell it to you —

**1/46** — siddhamārgakramāyātaṃ siddhapaṅktivyavasthitaṃ | gopitaṃ sarvamārgeṣu tavādya prakaṭīkṛtam ||
> — the [teaching] come by the siddha-path's order, standing in the siddha-line: hidden on all the paths, today made manifest for you.

**1/47** — pūrvasañcodito devi tvayāhaṃ tvaṃ mayā punaḥ | atra kalpe mayā tubhyaṃ tvaṃ punar mama dāsyasi ||
> "O Devī, I was first impelled by you, and you [again] by me: in this kalpa [the teaching] [is given] by me to you; you will give it back to me."

## The Mālinī-emergence (1/70–1/81) — the phonemic core

*The edition jumps 1/47 → 1/70 (a gap in the manuscript-witnesses; the GRETIL note: "After 70ef ABEFG continue"). The surviving sequence: the praise of Bhairava (1/70), then the self-born Rudraśakti's emergence from the phonemes (1/71–81) — the paṭala's doctrinal heart, and the report's flagged mālinī/mātṛkā dossier in action.*

**1/70** — ārādhayantaṃ deveśaṃ na jānāti tapotkaṭā | tataḥ stavena divyena devenānandabhṛdgirā | divyastotraṃ samārabdham aśeṣārthaprabodhakam ||
> The fierce-in-austerity [Kālī], worshipping the Lord of gods, does not know [him]; then, with the divine praise, by the Lord with the bliss-bearing speech, the divine hymn was begun, awakening all meanings.

**1/71** — evaṃ samyagvidhānena rudraśaktiḥ svayambhunā | nirmitā svāṅgajair varṇair nādiphāntasvarūpiṇī ||
> Thus, by the right rite, the Rudraśakti was fashioned by the Self-born — from his own limbs' phonemes, of the nature of the nāda-and-phānta (the a–kṣa series):

**1/72** — sarvākṣaramayī devī sarvalakṣaṇalakṣitā | utpannā sumahātejā bhairavābhimukhe sthitā ||
> — the goddess made of all the syllables, marked with all the marks, was born with great brilliance, standing before Bhairava.

**1/73** — vadate mālinī kas tvaṃ devo 'haṃ kim upāgataḥ | māṃ tvaṃ kathaṃ na jānāsi devi tvaṃ kena nirmitā ||
> Mālinī speaks: "Who are you, O god, and why have you come? How do you not know me, O Devī? By whom were you fashioned?"

**1/74** — sṛṣṭikrīḍāvatārārthe mayā utpāditā priye | tvam evotpāditaḥ kena brūhi vākyaṃ tu bhairava ||
> "For the sake of the creation-sport's descent you were produced by me, O dear one. And you — by whom were you produced? Speak the word, O Bhairava."

**1/75** — varṇarāśir ahaṃ bhadre svayambhūr jagataḥ patiḥ | mamāṅgasambhavair bījais tvam evotpāditā mayā ||
> "I am the mass of phonemes, O blessed one, the Self-born, the lord of the world; by the seed[-letters] born of my limbs you yourself were produced by me."

**1/76** — vīrāvalīti tena tvaṃ rudraśaktiḥ praśasyase | vadate mālinī kruddhā tvatsvakīyaiḥ śarīrajaiḥ ||
> "Therefore you are praised as the Vīrāvalī, the Rudraśakti." Mālinī, angered, speaks: "By your own body-born phonemes —"

**1/77** — varṇair utpāditāhaṃ te gṛhṇa varṇān svakān iha | prasārya varṇamālāṃ tu tattvākāraṃ svarūpiṇam ||
> "— I was produced by your phonemes. Take back your own phonemes here!" — spreading out the garland of phonemes, the form of the tattva, the self-natured —

**1/78** — pūrvabījatanur bhūtvā prasuptāmṛtakuṇḍalī | kutaḥ sarve gatā varṇā bhrāntacittaḥ sureśvaraḥ ||
> — she who had become the body of the prior seed, the sleeping-nectar Kuṇḍalinī: "Where have all the phonemes gone?" — the Lord of the gods, his mind whirling,

**1/79** — paraṃ vismayam āpannaḥ kṣaṇam ekaṃ vitarkitaḥ | lolībhūtās tu te sarve jīvatattve layaṃ gatāḥ ||
> — attained the highest wonder, pondering a single moment: "All those phonemes, become fluid, have gone into dissolution in the life-principle."

**1/80** — aho devyāḥ prabhāvas tu iti cintā jagatpateḥ | stunoti vividhaiḥ stotrair devo bhuvanamālinīm ||
> "Ah, the power of the Devī!" — such the Lord of the world's thought: the god praises the Bhuvanamālinī with manifold hymns.

**1/81** — kāvarṇā kāmarūpe pur eva purigatā jālapīṭhe jikā yā ṣaḍbhinnā madhyapīṭhe tripathapadagatā tvaṃ ca śṛṅgātakārā | siddhair yā veṣṭitāṅgī parivṛtacaturaiḥ ṣaṣṭibhir yogivṛndair yuktā hṛtpaṅkajena ḍaralakasahajā pātu māṃ rudraśaktiḥ ||
> May the Rudraśakti protect me — she of the kā-phoneme in the kāma-form, gone into the pur in the pur[-city]; the jikā in the jāla-pīṭha, the six-fold-divided in the madhya-pīṭha, you who go by the three-way path, and the śṛṅgātaka-formed; she whose limbs are wrapped by the siddhas, surrounded by the clever sixty yogi-hosts, joined with the heart-lotus, born together with the ḍaralaka — may she protect me.

*iti kulālikāmnāye śrīkubjikāmate candradvīpāvatāro nāma prathamaḥ paṭalaḥ* — "Here ends, in the Kulālikāmnāya, in the blessed Kubjikāmata, the first paṭala called the Descent at Candra-dvīpa."
