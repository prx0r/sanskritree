# Paṭala 6 (completion) — the kāla-vañcana (the death-deceiving)

**6/8–6/14** — nābhisthaṃ hṛdi kaṇṭhe ca vaktranāsāpuṭantathā | karṇasthaṃ śrūyate devi pāramparyakramāgatam || kaulikantu idaṃ devi karṇāt karṇasamāgatam | anena jñānamātreṇa śrutena ca varānane || mucyate sarvapāpebhyo bhavadbhiḥ samupārjitam | ṣaṇmāsādabhyasedyastu kāṣāyaṃ bandhavarjitam || asyāpi śokasantāpajarāvyādhirvinaśyati | sarvavyādhivinirmukto yogibhiḥ sahamelakam || aṇimādiguṇaiśvaryaṃ siddhiśca mānasī bhavet | etatte jīvamabhyāsaṃ kathitaṃ te ca bhairavi || kulāgamasubhaktāya gurudevyā prapūjake | sarvabhāvopapannasya dātavyaṃ kṛtaniścaye || māyāvine śaṭhe krūre prapañcairliṅgine tathā | na deyaṃ kaulikaṃ sāraṃ yoginīnāntu sambhavam ||
> In the navel, the heart, the throat, the mouth, the nose-apertures; in the ear it is heard, O Devī — coming by the succession of the lineage (pāramparyakrama). This Kaulika [teaching], O Devī, comes from ear to ear (karṇāt karṇam). By the mere knowledge of this, and its hearing, O fair-faced one, one is freed from all the sins acquired in the [previous] existences. Whoever practices for six months, free of the ochre-robe and the bonds — for him too the grief, the burning, old age, and disease perish; freed of all diseases, the meeting with the yogins (yogibhiḥ saha melakam); the lordship of the aṇimā-and-the-rest guṇas, and the mental siddhi arise. This jīva-practice has been told to you, O Bhairavī. To the one devoted to the kula-āgama, worshipping the guru-and-goddess, endowed with all the states, settled in resolve, it is to be given; to the illusionist, the cheat, the cruel, the one marked by the prapañcas — the Kaulika essence, born of the yoginīs, is not to be given.

**6/17–6/28** (the kāla-vañcana practices): the tongue-to-palate nectar-drinking; the death-conquest in a month; the six-month disease-release; the year's practice destroys old age and death; the distant hearing; the poison-immunity (viṣaṃ na kramate dehe daṃśito'pi mahoragaiḥ); the amṛta in the dvādaśānta; the gray-hair-destruction; the tongue-curling practice in all states (tiṣṭhañjāgran svapaṅgacchan bhuñjan maithune rataḥ — standing, waking, sleeping, walking, eating, in union) —

**6/27–6/28** — na mṛtyurbhavate tasya abdāt svacchandago bhavet | hṛdisthañca manaḥ kṛtvā yāvadunmanatāṃ gataḥ || āgataṃ nāśayenmṛtyuṃ ghaṭikārddhe varānane | kālasya vañcanaṃ devi sugopyaṃ prakaṭīkṛtam ||
> No death arises for him; within a year he moves at will. Having fixed the mind in the heart, gone to the beyond-mind (unmanatā) — he destroys the death that has come within half an hour, O fair-faced one. The deception of time (kāla-vañcana), O Devī, the well-hidden, has been made manifest.

# Paṭala 7 — the gray-hair-stopping (palitastambha) and the eleven stages

**7/1–7/4** — palitastambhavijñānaṃ jarāmaraṇanāśanam | tritattvaṃ katha me nātha tvameva śaraṇaṃ gatā || palitastambhavijñānaṃ śṛṇu tvaṃ vīramātare | devyāścakragataṃ cintya(?) pratipadāyāṃ śaśiryathā || śītalaṃ candrasaṃkāśaṃ vartistu ghṛtabodhitā | tatra caiva śikhā līnā asthicakre varānane || bhāvayet keśaskandhasthaṃ lagnaṃ vai añjanantathā | sādravaṃ kṛṣṇarūpaṃ ca balīpalitanāśanam ||
> The knowledge of the gray-hair-stopping, the destruction of old age and death — the triple principle: tell me, O Lord — you alone are my refuge. The gray-hair-stopping knowledge — hear, O mother of heroes: contemplating [the moon] in the goddess's cakra, like the moon on the first day; the cool, moon-like flame of the ghee-kindled lamp; there the crest (śikhā) is dissolved in the bone-wheel (asthicakra). He should contemplate [it] standing at the hair-roots, affixed like the collyrium (añjana): the moist, black-formed — the destruction of age and gray-hair.

**7/5–7/31** (the full practice-series): the ādhāra-śaktisthāna with the black-collyrium amṛta; the sun-as-enemy/moon-as-friend meditation; the solitary-place rules (the underground house, free of mosquitoes; the black-garment); the six-month siddhi "equal to the yoginī-station"; the kāmadeva-comparison; the brahma-granthi with the blue-añjana; the piṅga-granthi's sixteen-spoked cakra with the sixteen vowels; the seven stages of the keśa-skandha; the yoginī-melaka; the eleven siddhi-stages (7/20–7/24: the first — the body-hair thrill; the second — the disease-destruction; the third — the poetry with ornaments; the fourth — the speech-mastery; the fifth — the distant hearing; the sixth — the earth-leaving [?]; the seventh — the yoginī-melaka; the eighth — the old-age-removal; the ninth — the fierce-speed; the tenth — the manifold forms; the eleventh — the tranquil, threefold-free, worshipped like Śiva) —

**7/25–7/31** — icchayārohaṇaṃ kuryādvarṇanaṃ nṛtyameva ca | yatastatraiva gantavyaṃ yatra vā rocate manaḥ || manorodhaṃ na kartavyaṃ yadā jñātaṃ hi kaulikam | prapañcarahitaṃ śāstraṃ prapañcarahito guruḥ || prapañcarahito mantraḥ prapañcarahitaḥ śivaḥ | etat kaulāgamaṃ nātha aprapañcaiḥ paśuḥ priye || dambhitamātmanastaistu punardambhayate param | kecidajñānato naṣṭāḥ kecinnaṣṭāḥ pramādataḥ || keci(j) jñānāvalayena kecinnaṣṭairvināśitāḥ | kampastobhe tathā roṣā mudrāmutpatanantathā || bhavate nātra sandehaḥ saptarātreṇa kaulave | yāṃ yāṃ spṛśati hastena yāṃ yāṃ paśyati cakṣuṣā || śuddhaṃ bhavati tat sarvaṃ parabindukiraṇāhatam ||
> By the will he should make the ascent, the speech, the dance — for one must go only where the mind delights. No restraint of the mind is to be made, when the Kaulika is known: the śāstra free of the prapañcas, the guru free of the prapañcas, the mantra free of the prapañcas, Śiva free of the prapañcas — this is the Kaula-āgama, O Lady; by the non-prapañcas [only] the paśu, O beloved. Some are destroyed by ignorance, some by carelessness, some by the ring of knowledge [?], some destroyed by the destroyed [teachings]. The tremor-thrust, the anger, the mudrā, the leaping — these arise, no doubt, within the seven nights in the Kaula[-practice]. Whatever he touches with his hand, whatever he sees with his eye — all that becomes pure, struck by the ray of the supreme bindu (parabindu).

# Paṭala 8 — the śakti-classes, the kṣetras, the pīṭhas, and the cakra-series

**8/1–8/10** — atyantagahanaṃ nātha sugopyaṃ guhyamuttamam | akule tu kulaṃ devaṃ kathaṃ jātaṃ hi bhairava || kṣetrajā pīṭhajā vāpi yogajā mantrajā tathā | sahajā kulajā vāpi aṣṭāṣṭakamatantathā || pūjākramavidhānantu kulasiddhaṃ vada prabho | gurupūjāvidhānantu sarvaṃ saṃkṣepato vada || śṛṇuṣvaikāgracittantu yoginīvīramātare | ekānte vijane sthāne puṣpadāmopaśobhite || sudhūpadhūpitaṃ kṛtvā matsyamāṃsarā ... vam | bhakṣyabhojyasamāyukto madirānandasaṃyutam || śaktiyukto mahātmānaḥ sahajā kulajāpi vā | antyajā vā mahādevi pṛthagbhedaṃ vadāmyaham || vivāhaṃ tu kṛtaṃ yasya sahajā sa tu ucyate | kulajā veśyamityāhurantyajāvarṇa antyajā ||
> The extremely deep, O Lord — the well-hidden, the supreme secret: how, O god, was the kula born in the akula, O Bhairava? The kṣetra-born, the pīṭha-born, the yoga-born, the mantra-born; the innate (sahajā), the lineage-born (kulajā) — the eight-by-eight doctrine. Tell the rule of the worship's procedure, the kula-siddhi, O Lord; the guru-worship's rule — tell all in brief. — Hear with one-pointed mind, O mother of the yoginī-heroes: in the solitary, deserted place adorned with flower-garlands; having made [it] fragrant with fine incense, with the fish-meat and the rest, joined with the eatables-and-foods, with the joy of the wine (madirānanda); joined with the power (śakti), the great-souled: the innate or the lineage-born, or the outcaste (antyajā), O great Devī — I will tell the distinctions separately. He whose marriage is made [with the śakti] is called the innate; the lineage-born they call the courtesan (veśyā); the outcaste [is] the antyajā of the varṇas.

**8/11–8/17** (the antyajā-śakti and the kṣetra-jā): the crystal-pure, pearl-garlanded, sky-garlanded (vyomamālinī) great power; the betel-filled mouth, the saffron and musk; the sixty-four and the fifty-eight worship; the red-garmented with the armlets; the kṣetra-jā born at the fields: Karavīra, Mahākāla, Devīkoṭi, Vārāṇasī, Prayāga, Caritraikāmraka, Aṭṭahāsa, Jayantī —

**8/18–8/22** (the bīja-lines and the pīṭha-list): the kṣetra-jā bīja-lines (hrīṃ śrīṃ koṅkaṇā-i pāda... through the yugas); the pīṭha-jā at the seats: **Kāmākhyā** (the first pīṭha, with the seven upapīṭha-devinīs), Pūrṇagiri, Oḍḍiyāna, Arvuda-marddha —

**8/23–8/31** — pīṭhopapīṭhasandohe ye jātā varayoginī | etaistu pūjitā bhadre sarve sidhyanti mātarāḥ || yogābhyāsena ye siddhā mantrāṇāmārādhanena tu | yogena yogajā mātā mantreṇa mantrajā priye || sahajā mātarā devyā rūrūyuddhairmahābalāḥ | bhakṣitaṃ tu caruṃ divyaṃ saptajanmāntikaṃ paśum || teṣāṃ garbhe prasūtānāṃ niryāsaprāśitena ca | garbhe jātena deveśi garbhaṃ jānanti ātmanaḥ || brāhmī māheśvarī caiva kaumārī vaiṣṇavī tathā | vārāhī vajrahastā ca tathā yogeśvarīti ca || aghoreśī ca vikhyātā mātaryā vyāpakāḥ smṛtā(ḥ) |
> In the aggregate of the pīṭhas and upapīṭhas, whatever excellent yoginīs are born — by them worshipped, O blessed one, all the Mothers attain siddhi. Those perfected by the practice of yoga — by the adoration of the mantras — the yoga-born Mother by yoga, the mantra-born by the mantra, O beloved. The innate Mothers of the Devī, mighty in the Rūrū-battles: having eaten the divine caru, the paśu [is destroyed] for seven births. Of those born in their wombs, by the eating of the sap: born in the womb, O Lady of the gods, they know their own womb. Brāhmī, Māheśvarī, Kaumārī, Vaiṣṇavī, Vārāhī, Vajrahastā, Yogeśvarī, Aghoreśī — the famous eight Mothers, the pervaders.

**8/31–8/44** (the aṣṭāṣṭaka — the eight cakras): the eight-by-eight = the sixty-four, by the rule; the first — the yoginī-melaka-cakra giving the aṇimā-guṇas; the second — the great cakra of all-attraction (sarvākṛṣṭi); the third — the entry into another's body (parakāyapraveśa); the fourth — the śāntika-cakra giving liberation (muktir muktipradaṃ); the fifth — the silencing of the wind and the rest; the sixth — giving dharma-artha-kāma-mokṣa; the seventh — the army-stopping (sainyastambha); the eighth — the icchā-siddhi-cakra with the six acts (māraṇa, uccāṭana, stambha, moha) —

**8/44** — vadanotiṣṭhamahācakraṃ kulabhaktyāmadhiṣṭhitam | aṣṭāṣṭakavidhānantu jñātvā sidhyati nānyathā ||
> The great cakra standing in the mouth [?] [X], governed by the kula-devotion: knowing the eight-by-eight procedure, one attains siddhi, not otherwise.

# Paṭala 9 — the guru-lines (gurupaṅkti)

**9/1–9/15** — gurupaṅktiṃ pravakṣyāmi siddhapaṅktiṃ sulocane | yoginīpañktivinyāsaṃ kathayāmi tava priye || sarvasiddhiyoginīnāṃ khecarīṃ sarvamātarīm | sarvabhūcarīsarvagocarayoginīnāṃ sarvakṣetrakam || sarvamantrajāḥ sarvayogajāḥ sarvapīṭhajāḥ | sarvasahajāḥ sarvakulajā sarvadvārapālikāḥ || sarvagarbhajāḥ kṛte ca dvāpare trete kaliyuge mahātape | caturyugavibhāgena yoginīsiddhipūjitāḥ || guhyānāṃ paramaṃ guhyantava bhaktyā prakāśitam ||
> I will tell the guru-line (gurupaṅkti), the siddha-line, O fair-eyed one; the placing of the yoginī-line I tell you, O beloved: of all the siddha-yoginīs — Khecarī, the all-Mother; of all the bhūcarīs and gocarī-yoginīs, the all-field (sarvakṣetra); the all-mantra-born, the all-yoga-born, the all-pīṭha-born, the all-innate, the all-lineage-born, the all-door-guardians; the all-womb-born, in the Kṛta, Dvāpara, Tretā, and the Kali age, O great ascetic — by the division of the four yugas, worshipped with the yoginī-siddhi. Of the secrets the supreme secret — to you, by [your] devotion, it is made manifest.

**9/6–9/10** (the guru-names): the line: Viśva-pāda, Vicitra-pāda, Śveta-pāda, Bhaṭṭa-pāda, **Macchendra-pāda**, Bṛhīṣa-pāda, Vindhya-pāda, Śabara-pāda, Mahendra-pāda, Candra-pāda, Hiḍinī-pāda, Samudra-pāda, Lavaṇa-pāda, Dumbara-pāda, Deṇe-pāda, Dhīvara-pāda, Siṃhala-pāda; with the guru-paramaguru-parameṣṭhi-pūjya-mahāpūjya and the Lākinī-Ḍākinī-Śākinī-Kākinī-Yākinī; the hrīṃ-and-śrī-placement —

**9/7–9/15** — etat siddhāśca yoginyā asmin siddhāḥ kulāgame | anena sadṛśaṃ jñānaṃ na bhūyo na bhaviṣyati || kaliyuge mahāghore raurave'tyantabhīṣaṇe | sañjātāḥ ṣoḍaśa siddhā asmin kaule sulocane | kṛte ca dvāpare trete siddhāyai vīravanditāḥ | teṣāṃ nāmavidhiṃ vakṣye śṛṇu tvaṃ varalocane || mṛṣṇipādāḥ | avatārapādāḥ | sūryapādāḥ | dyutipādāḥ | omapādāḥ | vyāghrapādāḥ | hariṇipādāḥ | pañcaśikhipādāḥ | komalapādāḥ | lambodarapādāḥ || ete pūrvamahāsiddhāḥ kulakaulāvatārakāḥ | caturyugeti deveśa svatantra-kulacodakā(ḥ) || asya jñānaprabhāveṇa bahavaḥ sidhyanti mānavāḥ | daśakoṭipramāṇantu i(da)m kaulaṃ parodbhavam ||
> By this the siddhas and the yoginīs attain siddhi in this kula-āgama; no knowledge like this — no more, and none will be. In the Kali age, the great terrible, in the utterly fearsome Raurava [hell], the sixteen siddhas arose in this Kaula, O fair-eyed one; in the Kṛta, Dvāpara, Tretā, honored by the heroes for the siddhās. Their name-procedure I will tell — hear, O lovely-eyed one: Mṛṣṇi-pāda, Avatāra-pāda, Sūrya-pāda, Dyuti-pāda, Oṃ-pāda, Vyāghra-pāda, Hariṇī-pāda, Pañcaśikhi-pāda, Komalā-pāda, Lambodara-pāda. These are the earlier great siddhas, the descendings of the kula-and-Kaula; in the four yugas, O Lady of the gods, the independent kula-impellers. By the power of this knowledge many men attain siddhi: this Kaula, of the ten-crore measure, is the supreme arising.

**9/11–9/15** — sārātsārataraṃ bhadre mahākaulasya śobhane | icchākhyā yoginī siddhairmāyāntaṃ para-icchayā || khecarīṇāṃ punaḥ paścādicchayā prakaṭīkṛtam | mātarāṇāṃ punardevi khecaraiḥ kathitaṃ priye || mātarāṇi gamiṣyanti bhūcarīṇāṃ kuleśvari | bhūcarīṇāṃ subhakṣāṇāṃ bhūcarī kathitaṃ priye || yoginīnāṃ kule jātau labhate kaulikī sphūṭam | caturaśīti sahasreṣu yoniyantreṣu pīḍitāḥ || puṇyātmānaṃ kulāścaryaṃ paścāj jñānamimāṃ labhet | bhuktimuktimahāsiddhiḥ yoginīnāṃ priyo bhavet ||
> **The more-essential-than-the-essential** (sārātsārataraṃ), O blessed one, of the great Kaula, O lovely: the yoginī named Icchā [was made manifest] by the siddhas, up to māyā, by the supreme will; then, by the will, made manifest to the Khecarīs; then, O Devī, told to the Mothers by the Khecarīs; the Mothers will go to the Bhūcarīs, O Lady of the kula; of the Bhūcarīs, the well-eaten [teaching], the Bhūcarī [transmission] is told, O beloved. Born in the yoginīs' kula, one clearly obtains the Kaulikī; pressed in the eighty-four thousand yoni-yantras; the meritorious, the wonder of the kula — afterward this knowledge he obtains: enjoyment, liberation, the great siddhi; he becomes dear to the yoginīs.

Notes: the transmission-chain (Icchā-yoginī → Khecarīs → Mothers → Bhūcarīs) — the fourfold speech-current of the lineage; the 84,000 yonis (the yoni-arithmetic of the kula); sārātsārataraṃ — the Kubjikā/Akulavīra term again.

# Paṭala 10 — the eight cakras' meditations

The Devī asks the cakra-dhyānas; Bhairava gives them one by one:
- The first (the aṣṭaka of the letters lā...hlā with the body-map: kṣa in the brahmarandhra, laṃ on the brows, ha in the brow-mid, sa in the mouth, ṣa at the throat, śa in the heart, va at the navel, hla at the...): the crystal-pure, upward-radiant, eight-spoked divine lotus — free of the pairs, the anger-less, the void-free; the village-dharma to be abandoned; the past-future-present, the distant hearing, the noose-thrust, the animal-seizure, the possession, the death-destruction, the immortality, the transformation —
- The second (the aṣṭapatra of the tejas): the death-destruction by the āgama, the city-shaking, the manifold forms —
- The third (the great cakra of the nine tattvas): the six-month desire, the gray-hair-destruction, the distant sight, the piercing at a hundred yojanas —
- The fourth (the śāntika-cakra): the ease-and-love, the immortality, the death-by-word, the disease-conquest, the sixteen-year form —
- The fifth (the dhūmra aṣṭapatra): the three-worlds-shaking, the speech-removal, the army-stopping —
- The sixth (the golden cakra of the rājan): the icchā-siddhi, the past-future, the aṇimā-guṇas —
- The seventh (the full-moon cakra): enjoyment-liberation, old-age-death-destruction, the entry into another's body —
- The eighth (the aṣṭapatra): dharma-kāma-artha-mokṣa; the color-doctrine (red = subjugation, yellow = stambha, white = nourishment/mokṣa, black = māraṇa, smoke = uccāṭana; the milk-white = the death-conqueror) —

**10/31–10/32** — agnijvālā sudīptābhaṃ dhyānaṃ caivāṣṭamaṃ sadā | ekaikasya sthitandevi etaddhyānantu aṣṭadhā || cāpañca kañcukī devi praṇipatya muhurmuhuḥ | adya me saphalaṃ jātammahākauleṣu bhairava ||
> The blazing-fire-bright — the eighth meditation always: of each one standing, O Devī, this meditation is eightfold. The five-kañcukī [?] [X], O Devī, bowing again and again: "today my [birth] has become fruitful in the great Kaulas, O Bhairava."

# Paṭala 11 — the carukādvaita (the non-dual caru)

**11/1–11/43** (the pañcāmṛta doctrine): the question of the caru and the dvaita; the non-dual (advaita) alone gives siddhi; the dvaita-doer is seized by the yogins; the samaya-fallen is the paśu; the five amṛtas (the pañcāmṛta): **viṣṭhā, dhārā, mṛta [amṛta], śukra, rakta-majjā** (11/11: "viṣṭhaṃ dhārāmṛtaṃ śukraṃ raktamajjāvimiśritam | etat pañca pavitrāṇi nityameva kulāgame" — "the feces, the stream, the nectar, the semen, mixed with blood-and-marrow: these five are the purifiers, ever, in the kula-āgama"); the nitya/naimittika distinction (the cow-meat, ghee, blood, milk, curd for the naimittika); the animal-list (dog, cat, camel, jackal, horse, tortoise, boar, cat, frog, crab, the śalākī, the cock, the various kula-animals, the deer, buffalo, rhinoceros; the fish "as obtained"); the paśu-destruction for seven births; the threefold flesh (pakkṣa-āmla-madhura); the surā-rule (the tree-born, root-born, flower-born, fruit-born; the peṣṭī, mādhvī, gauṇḍī); the advaita-fallen go to the Avīci and Raurava hells; the advaita-abandoner is the paśu among the yoginī-hosts; the secrecy (hidden like the son-of-the-mother's-lover — the "jananījāragarbha" — the most shameful secret!); the lotus-leaf-untouched-by-water image (padmapatre yathā toye — the yogī untouched by merit and sin); the siddha-yoginī caru (the piṇḍa's rakta-śukra drinking); the Śākinī-flesh, the devī-flesh; the vukapuṣpa, śivāmbu (the Śiva-water), rakta-śukra, surā; the kuṇḍa-gola; the tilaka-forbidden; the eighty vātas destroyed, the kuṣṭha destroyed; the snake-skin image; the three-worlds-subjugation; the icchā-rule (icchāṃ vinā na kuryāttu balātkāreṇa tañcarum — "without the will one should not make that caru by force").

**11/43** — carukādvaitamācāraṃ kathitaṃ jñānanirṇayam —
> The caru-non-dual observance — the knowledge-determination — has been told.

# Paṭala 12 — the vessels (pātra) and the conduct (caryā)

**12/3–12/16** — prathamaṃ bālarūpeṇa dvitīyam unmattakākṛtim | tṛtīyaṃ rājarūpeṇa caturthaṃ kaśmalaṃ priye || svātantraṃ pañcamaṃ devi ṣaṣṭhañca vīranāyakam | gandharvasaptamannāma nagnaṃ caivāṣṭakaṃ priye || tridaṇḍañcaiva deveśastathānyaṃ vedavikrayam | yāṃ yāntu kurute icchāṃ tāṃ tāñcaiva vrataṃ priye || akulañca kulaṃ jñātvā kulaṃ devyā subhāmini || bhairavaṃ pūjitavyañca kulaiḥ siddhisamanvitam | svaguruṃ pujayennityaṃ triṣkālaṃ bhāvitātmanaḥ || manasā karmaṇā vācā guruñcaiva svakaṃ na tu | nivedayet prayatnena bhuktimuktijigīṣayā || gurukaulāgame bhakti advaitācārabhāvitaḥ | īpsitaṃ tu vrataṃ kuryādadvaitena samanvitam ||

Bhairava is to be worshipped by the kulas, joined with siddhi; one's own guru is to be worshipped daily at the three times, with the self cultivated [by the mind]; by mind, action, and speech, one should not [fail to] offer one's own guru, with effort, desiring enjoyment and liberation. The Viśvāmitra-skull (kapāla) is the supreme of all vessels, O beloved.

# Paṭala 13 — the mokṣa-nyāsa

**13/1–13/8** — mokṣapratyayasaṃvitti śṛṇu tvaṃ vīranāyike | adhorddhañca labhejjīvo jñātvā taṃ muktibhojanam || haṃsa haṃsa vadennityaṃ dehasthāvarajaṅgame | śrutvā tasya patirdivyaṃ yāti mokṣavaraṃ śubham || yena vyāptaṃ ca trailokyaṃ jagad yasmin vyavasthitam | taṃ jñātvā gūḍhasadbhāvaṃ sarvajñatvamprajāyate || na tena vinā mucyante vyavahārānna hi tadvinā | śubhāśubhaṃ ca saṃbhoktaṃ na tena rahitaṃ kvacit || na manañcittamālabdhaṃ na peyaṃ dhāraṇaṃ priye | unmanantu mano yasya tasya mokṣo bhavantīha || dvādaśānte yadā paśyet sphūrantaṃ maṇimālikā || tasya mokṣo bhavedyastu pāpapuṇyairna lipyate | devayāne mahāyāne yasya cittaṃ sadā priye | tasya mokṣo na sandehaḥ padmapatrāmbubinduvat ||
> The conviction of liberation (mokṣapratyaya), hear, O heroine: the jīva obtains the high-and-low, knowing that liberation-food. "Haṃsa, haṃsa" he should utter always, in the stationary-and-moving of the body: hearing his, the Lord goes to the divine, the auspicious supreme liberation. By whom the three worlds are pervaded, in whom the world stands — knowing that hidden reality, omniscience arises. Without it they are not released, nor without it from the dealings; the good-evil to be enjoyed is never without it. No mind, no citta is taken up; no drink, no retention, O beloved: he whose mind is the beyond-mind (unmanā) — for him liberation arises here. When at the dvādaśānta he sees the flashing pearl-garland (maṇimālikā) — for him liberation arises; he is not touched by sin and merit. He whose mind is ever in the path of the gods, the great path — for him liberation, no doubt, like the water-drop on the lotus-leaf.

**The nyāsa-body-map** (13/7–13/8): iṃ-gude (the anus), ikṣu-meḍhre (the penis), iyau-nābhau (the navel), imau-vaktre (the mouth), ivau-dakṣiṇanāsāyām (the right nostril), ilau-vāmapuṭe (the left nostril), irau-dakṣiṇataḥ, iḍo-dakṣiṇakarṇaḥ (the right ear), iśau-vāmakarṇaḥ, ihrau-bhrūmadhye (the brow-mid), saṃ taṃ-lalāṭe, saṃ aṃ-vāmakarṇe, saḥ yaṃ-dakṣiṇakarṇe, saḥ paṃ-vāmacakṣuḥ, saḥ ṛ-dakṣiṇacakṣuḥ, vaḥ taṃ-vāmanāsikā, saḥ puṃ-dakṣiṇanāsikā, saḥ rūṃ-vaktram, saḥ yaṃ-nābhi, saḥ ṣu-meḍhraḥ, saḥ laṃ-gude —

> This nyāsa-order, O Devī, in whose body it proceeds — for him liberation, no doubt, preceded by the supreme awareness. When at the body's fall, O Devī, the skull breaks — then [the mokṣa]. [X: the verse trails]

The Śrīnātha with the yoginī, the Vṛnda-form, O radiant one — the conch-handed he should always meditate: what is thought by the mind, he obtains.

# Paṭala 20 — the mokṣa-practice and the śakti-vīra marks

**20/1–20/22** (the kapāla-bheda and the śaktis): the upward-pressure at the heart-throat; the prāṇāyāma-utpāta; **the skull-breaking** (kapālaṃ bhedamayāti yānti jīvo nirañjanam — "the skull goes to breaking; the jīva goes to the Stainless [nirañjana]"); "sa śivo vyāpako bhūtvā kartā hartā varānane" — becoming the pervasive Śiva, the doer, the destroyer; the śaktyācāra-practice (the restrained-food-and-sex; the anāmā-pressure upward; the śyāma-thread-mark; the six-month and the year's practice; the another-body-entry); the **śakti-names** (20/10–20/12): "anādinidhanā śaktiricchā nāma śivodbhavā | vyomamālinī sā bhadre khecarīti nigadyate || vāmākhyā kuṇḍalīnāma jyeṣṭhā caiva manonmanī | rudraśaktistu vikhyātā kāmākhyā tu gīyate" — the beginningless-endless power named Icchā, born of Śiva; she is called the sky-garlanded, the Khecarī; the named-Vāmā, the Kuṇḍalinī; Jyeṣṭhā, Manonmanī; the famous Rudra-śakti, sung as Kāmākhyā; "mātṛkā śabdarā(śiśca) sarvagrantheṣu kīrtyate" — the mātṛkā, the sound-mass, is celebrated in all the books; the **vīra-śakti marks** (20/13–20/22): the white-eyed, the curly-haired, the moon-toothed, the lovely-speaking; the kula-devotion, the guru-goddess-worship; the beautiful, the well-browed, the well-foreheaded; the kula-āgama-devotion, the fear-free; the good-natured, the fortunate, the world-delighting; "durlabhā lokamadhye tu" — hard to find in the world; the **vīra-marks**: the handsome, the good-natured, the truth-speaker, the resolute; the guru-kula-devoted, the true-jointed, the anger-free; the brave, the fortunate, the self-body-convinced; "īdṛśī bhavate vīrā rudraśaktyā hyadhiṣṭhitā" — such a woman becomes the vīrā, governed by the Rudra-śakti; "yattejaḥ sa mahāliṅgamutpattisthitikārakaḥ" — the brilliance is the great liṅga, the maker of origin-and-stay.

# Paṭala 21 — the saṃgraha (the summary of the kula-texts)

**21/1–21/10** — kathayāmi na sandeho saṃgrahācāralakṣaṇam || pañcapañcāśiko devi mataṃ vai yogalakṣaṇam | tasya bhedopabhedena kaulaśāstre vinirṇayam || kulapañcāśikāmūlaṃ tathā ca kulasāgaram | kulogho hṛdayañcaiva bhairavodyānakaṃ tathā || candrakaulañca veṣṭiñca(?) tathā vai jñānanirṇayam | asya madhye viniṣkrāntaṃ sambaranāma viśrutam || sṛṣṭikaulaṃ mahākaulaṃ timiraṃ ca tathāparam | siddhāmṛtaṃ tu kaulaṃ mātakaulaṃ tathāparam || śaktibhedaṃ tathā kaulaṃ ūrmikaulamanuttamam | caturyugeṣu deveśi jñānakaulasya saṃgatiḥ || siddheśvaraṃ tathā cānyaṃ kaulaṃ vai vajrasambhavam | meghajāñca dūrataṃ tasmin kaule vinirgatam || aṅgasparśaniścaraṃ devi vajradādyāmṛtaṃ tathā | pūrṇimāyāṃ amāvasyāṃ aṣṭamī ca caturdaśī || sajīvaṃ matsyamadyañca māṃsañcaiva baliṃ da(det) | vyākhyā caiva tu kartavyā ācāryaḥ śaṅkavarjitam || na tithirna ca nakṣatraṃ nopavāsaṃ vidhīyate | yatra tatra sthito yogī jñānamevaṃ samāśrayet ||
> I will tell, no doubt, the mark of the summary-conduct: the Pañcapañcāśika, O Devī, the yoga-mark-doctrine — by its divisions and sub-divisions, the determination in the Kaula-śāstra. The Kulapañcāśikā-mūla, and the Kulasāgara; the Kula-ogha, the Hṛdaya, the Bhairavodyāna; the Candra-kaula and the Veṣṭi, and the Jñānanirṇaya — from within these issued the famous [text] named the Sambara. The Sṛṣṭi-kaula, the Mahā-kaula, the Timira, the other supreme; the Siddhāmṛta-kaula, the Māta-kaula, the other supreme; the Śaktibheda-kaula, the unsurpassed Ūrmi-kaula: in the four yugas, O Lady of the gods, is the meeting of the Jñāna-kaula. The Siddheśvara, and the other — the Vajra-born kaula; the Megha-born, the Dūrata — issued in that Kaula; the Aṅgasparśa-niścaya, O Devī, the Vajradā, the Āmṛta. On the full-moon, the new-moon, the eighth, the fourteenth: the living fish and the wine, and the flesh as the bali is to be given; the explanation is to be made; the ācārya free of doubt. No tithi, no nakṣatra, no fasting is prescribed: wherever he stands, the yogī should resort to this knowledge.

# Paṭala 22 — the completion of the teaching

**22/1–22/12** — praṇamya prāñjali ...... va adyāhaṃ tava vallabha | adyāhaṃ kṛtakṛtyā tu hara siddhivināyakaḥ || ṣaḍmukhaśca mahākāla(ḥ) kālikā yoginī tathā | nandīśo bhaṭṭakā caiva droṇakā ca tatha .... || vijayā tu mahābhāgā ṣaḍyoginyastu mātarāḥ | kaulāmṛte na saṃtṛpto ahaṃ sarve'pi bhairava || yat purā gopitaṃ bhadre tat sarvaṃ prakaṭīkṛtaḥ | bhūloke devatānāñca kathitavyaṃ kulāgame || siddhānāñca viśeṣeṇa khyāpanīyaṃ kulānva(yam) | samayācārasamopetaṃ vijñānaṃ dvaitavāsinām || guptaliṅgī sadā kālaṃ gurupūjanatatparaḥ | vatsalaḥ sarvalokānāṃ sarvajīvadayānvitaḥ || bhairavavacanaṃ śrutvāsarvaromāñcakañcukāḥ | santuṣṭamanasaḥ sarve daṇḍavat pati(tāḥ) bhuvi || kulabhāvaparāḥ sarve kṛtakṛtyā surādhipāḥ || adya me gahanaṃ naṣṭaṃ ajñānapaṭalāhatam | saṃsārataruvistīrṇamadya chinnaṃ tvayā prabho || pāśajālatamoghañca chinnādya duḥkhasaṃśayaḥ | daśakoṭistathā cārddhaṃ nāmnā jñānasya nirṇayaḥ || tasya madhye imaṃ nātha sārabhūtaṃ samuddhṛtam | kāmarūpe imaṃ śāstraṃ yoginīnāṃ gṛhe gṛhe || nigrahānugrahañcaiva siddhimelāpakaṃ tathā | kurvanti satataṃ devi asya jñānaprasādataḥ || candradvīpaṃ mahāśāstraṃ avatīrṇaṃ sulocane | kāmākhye gīyate nāthe mahāmatsyodarasthitiḥ ||
> Bowing with joined palms...: "today I am yours, O beloved; today I am fulfilled — Hara, the lord of the siddhi-host." Ṣaṇmukha, Mahākāla, Kālikā the yoginī, Nandīśa, Bhaṭṭakā, Droṇakā; Vijayā, the great-fated — the six yoginī-Mothers: "I am not satiated with the kula-nectar — we all, O Bhairava." — "What was hidden before, O blessed one, has all been made manifest: in the world of the earth, to the deities, it is to be told in the kula-āgama; and to the siddhas especially the kula-lineage is to be proclaimed; the samaya-observance-joined knowledge for the dwellers-in-duality. The hidden-liṅga one, always devoted to the guru's worship, affectionate to all the worlds, endowed with compassion for all beings." Hearing Bhairava's speech, all with the thrilling hairs, the delighted minds, fell flat on the ground: all in the kula-state, fulfilled, O lord of the gods. "Today the deep [darkness] is destroyed, struck by the veil of ignorance; the wide tree of saṃsāra is today cut by you, O Lord; the net of bonds, the darkness — today cut, the doubt of suffering. The ten-crore-and-a-half, named the Jñānanirṇaya: from within it, O Lord, this essence has been drawn out. At Kāmarūpa this śāstra, in the yoginīs' house after house; the restraint-and-grace, the siddhi-melaka — they do always, O Devī, by the grace of this knowledge. The Moon-island, the great śāstra, descended, O fair-eyed one; at Kāmākhyā it is sung, O Lord: the abode in the great fish's belly."

# Paṭala 23 — the yoginīs' forms on earth

**23/1–23/16** — kaulave yoginī deva sañcaranti kathaṃ bhuvi | tanmamācakṣva deveśa bhaktyā jānanti bhūtale || martye'smin devatānāntu sañcāraṃ śṛṇu bhāmini | kapotikā tathā gṛdhrī haṃsī caiva nakhī tathā || khañjaḥ ...... bhāṣī tu kokābhāṣī tu sundari | ulukī pecakī vā tu sararī vā gūlī tathā || śṛgālī ajā mahiṣī (uṣṭrī) mārjāranakulīi tathā | vyāghrī hastī mayūrī ca kukuṭī na ............... || anyāni yāni rūpāṇi saṃsthitāni mahītale | tāni rūpāṇi saṃgṛhya yoginyaḥ krīḍante bhuvi || nipatanti yadā bhadre abhakṣyeṣu kulādhipe | tadrūpaṃ kathyante ................. yuktāvadhārayet || hayaśca nakharaḥ sarpa citrikotmānasastathā | vṛścikodhyantaraścāno mūṣako darduraḥ priye || grahabhūtasvarūpeṇa jvālāgniśāstrasaṅkaṭaiḥ | veda ......................vyādhirājānañcaiva taskarāḥ || vidyuttuṅgo tathā gaṇḍa vyāghrasiṃhagajastathā | anekākārarūpeṇa bhayaṃ nānāvidhaṃ viduḥ || catuḥṣaṣṭiśca yoginyo...............pyanti sādhake | evaṃ rūpaṃ samāsṛtya kṣipraṃ gṛhṇanti taṃ paśum || kopantu naiva kartavyaṃ bhāṣamāṇaṃ surādhipe | kumārikā striyo vāpi bhāṣamāṇe kadācana || yathā śaktyā sadā kālaṃ strī caiva vratamāsthitam | pūjanīyā prayatnena kumāryaśca kulāśritaiḥ || punnāgaṃ nāgacaṃpādyairmucukundaiśca pāṭalaiḥ | pārijātodbhaveddivyairdāḍimaiḥ kusumojjvalaiḥ || bandhukaḥ kusumodbhūtaiścampakaḥ kusumojjvalaiḥ | jātyutpalakadambaiśca karṇikāraiḥ siṃhakeśaraiḥ || ambhorūhaistathā puṣpaiḥ śubhaiḥ śālāvibhūṣitaiḥ | raktāmbaradharāṃ dhyāyedraktagandhānulepitām || ātmānaṃ yoginīsiddhe ratnamālā vibhūṣitām | dhyāyettāṃ satataṃ vīrāṃ bahiḥ pūjāṃ vivarjayet | pūjanaṃ kathitaṃ bhadre bhuktimuktiprasādhakam ||
> In the Kaula, O god, how do the yoginīs move on earth? Tell me, O Lord of gods — they know by devotion on the earth. — In this mortal [world] the deities' movement — hear, O radiant one: the dove (kapotikā), the vulture (gṛdhrī), the goose (haṃsī), the nakhī; the lame [X], the kokā-speaking; the owl (ulukī), the peacock-owl [?] (pecakī), the sararī, the gūlī; the jackal (śṛgālī), the she-goat (ajā), the she-buffalo (mahiṣī), the she-camel, the cat-and-mongoose; the tigress, the elephant, the peahen, the hen... — whatever other forms stand on the earth — taking those forms, the yoginīs play on the earth. When they fall, O blessed one, upon the uneatable [offerings], O lord of the kula — that form is told... one should determine with the [right] method. The horse, the tiger, the serpent, the citrikā, the otmānasa; the scorpion, the otantara, the dog, the mouse, the frog, O beloved; in the form of the planets and the beings, with the flames, the fires, the weapons' straits; the Vedas..., the disease-kings, the thieves; the lightning-tuṅga, the gaṇḍa, the tiger, the lion, the elephant — with the manifold forms they cause the manifold fears. And the sixty-four yoginīs... the sādhaka: resorting to such a form, they quickly seize that paśu. No anger is to be made, speaking, O lord of the gods; to the maidens and the women speaking at any time. As the power, always, the woman with the vow: the virgins are to be worshipped with effort by the kula-dwellers. With the punnāga, the nāgacampā, the mucukunda, the pāṭala; with the divine pārijāta-born, the pomegranate, flower-bright; with the bandhuka-flower-born, the campaka flower-bright; with the jātī, the utpala, the kadamba, the karṇikāra, the siṃhakeśara; with the lotus-flowers and the auspicious, hall-adorned [flowers] — he should meditate on the red-garmented, red-sandal-anointed [yoginī]; the self, in the yoginī-siddhi, adorned with the jewel-garlands — he should meditate on that vīrā always; the outer worship he should avoid. The worship has been told, O blessed one, effecting enjoyment and liberation.

# Paṭala 24 — the closing (the worship's completion)

**24/1–24/12** — saphalamadya me nātha kāruṇyaṃ tava bhairava | asurāṇāṃ surāṇāñca prabhutvamadya me prabho || mahākaulaṃ mahājñānaṃ kulakaulasya nirṇayam | bhrāntisaṃsāragahanamajñānapaṭala(nta)maḥ || saṃsārapāśajālañca adya? nirṇāśitaṃ tvayā | adyamāpāditā muktiḥ svatantrā bhuvi durlabhā | punaḥ kathaya kauleśa dehasthaṃ siddhapūjanam || .........ṣandigdhaṃ kathayiṣyāmi kaule'smin pūjanaṃ śṛṇu | kulasiddhāśca yoginyo rudrāścaiva kulādhipe || devyāścakragatāḥ sarve hṛdi madhye śire'pi vā | bahisthaṃ pūjaye..............pūjāvidhi priye || sugandhakundaketakyā mālikājātikotpalaiḥ | campakaiḥ kiṅkarātaiśca nīlotpalasugandhibhiḥ || puṣpairnānāvidhaiścaiva śatapatraiśca .............. | tarujātantaśva rādeyā (?) dravyā(pi) madhumiśritām || māṃsampalambalirdeyā tāmbūlaṃ candrasaṃyutam | dhūpacandanasaurabhyaṃ aguruṃ mṛganābhikam || raktapuṣpairviśeṣeṇa sugandha (dhūpadīpitam) | ugrāṇi yāni puṣpāṇi gandhahīnaṃ na dāpayet || bahisthaṃ pūjanaṃ proktaṃ adhyātmaṃ śṛṇu sāmpratam | raktāmbaradharāḥ sarve raktagandhānulepanāḥ || ṣoḍaśākṛtayaḥ sa ....................... vibhūṣitāḥ | prasannavadanāścaiva pibantyo madirāsavam || icchārūpadharāḥ sarve jarāmaraṇavarjitāḥ | sṛṣṭipravartakāḥ sarve varadānaikatatparāḥ || i...........ḍādhyāyedacirāntatsamo bhavet ||
> Today fruitful, O Lord — your compassion, O Bhairava; the lordship over the asuras and the suras — today mine, O Lord. The great-Kaula, the great-knowledge, the determination of the kula-and-Kaula; the deep thicket of the delusion-saṃsāra, the darkness of ignorance — today destroyed by you. Today liberation is obtained, the independent, hard to obtain on earth. Tell again, O Lord of the Kaula, the siddha-worship in the body. — ...undoubted, I will tell the worship in this Kaula — hear: the kula-siddhās, the yoginīs, the Rudras, O lord of the kula — all standing in the Devī's cakra, in the heart, the middle, or the head. The outer worship... the worship-rule, O beloved: with the fragrant kunda, the ketakī, the mālikā, the jātī, the utpala; with the campaka, the kiṅkarāta, the fragrant blue-lotus; with the manifold flowers and the hundred-petaled; the tree-born... the substance mixed with honey; the flesh-piece-bali is to be given, the betel joined with camphor; the incense, the sandal-fragrance, the aloe, the musk. With the red flowers especially, the fragrant, the incense-lit: the fierce flowers — the fragrance-less he should not give. The outer worship has been told; the inner, hear now: all red-garmented, red-sandal-anointed, of the sixteen forms, adorned; with serene faces, drinking the wine-and-āsava; all bearing the will-forms, free of old age and death; all effecting creation, intent on the giving of boons — meditating on her, within no time he becomes equal to her.

**The close (25/1–25/3)** — guhyapadadharaṃ proktaṃ daśakoṭistu nirṇayaḥ | siddhānāṃ yoginīnāṃ ca hṛdayaṃ para(ma)durlabham || guhyād guhyataraṃ nāthe sārāt sārataraṃ param | samyaganmayā bhadre aprakāśyaṃ prakāśitam || mahākaula mahādevi śatairdaśabhiḥ sammitam | jñānasya nirṇaye sāraṃ nāmnā sāhasrikaṃ matam ||
> The bearer of the secret station is proclaimed — the ten-crore determination: the heart of the siddhas and the yoginīs, the supremely hard to obtain. More secret than the secret, O Lord; **the more-essential-than-the-essential, the supreme** (sārātsārataraṃ param) — rightly by me, O blessed one, the unrevealed has been revealed. The great-Kaula, O great Devī, measured by ten hundreds [=1000]: in the Jñānanirṇaya the essence, named the Thousand-fold (Sāhasrika), is held.
