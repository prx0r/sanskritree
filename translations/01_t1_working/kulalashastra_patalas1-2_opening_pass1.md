## The opening rites (the saṃprokṣaṇa)

**The maṅgala-dhyāna** — śuklāmbaradharaṃ viṣṇuṃ śaśivarṇaṃ caturbhujam | prasannavadanaṃ dhyāyet sarvavighnopaśāntaye || gajānanaṃ bhūtagaṇādhi sevitaṃ | kapittha jambūphalasāra bhakṣitam | umāsutaṃ śokavināśakāraṇaṃ | namāmi vighneśvara pādapaṃkajam ||
> One should meditate on Viṣṇu, white-robed, moon-colored, four-armed, serene-faced, for the stilling of all obstacles. I bow to the lotus-feet of Vighneśvara — the elephant-faced, served by the lord of the bhūta-hosts, eating the essence of wood-apple and rose-apple fruits, Umā's son, the maker of sorrow's destruction.

**The saṃprokṣaṇa-frame** — uttarāyaṇe śubhamāse uttirā... aśvani dvitīyā tṛtīyā pañcami saptami ekādaśi amṛtayogaṃ siddhayogaṃ śubhayogaṃ śuddhayogaṃ rājapāpaṃ purohitapāpaṃ yajamāna naṣṭaṃ dhanadhānyanāśaṃ artyanāśaṃ varṣaśūnyaṃ dīkṣā ||
> [The auspicious times:] in the uttarāyaṇa, in an auspicious month, on the Uttarā (Uttarāṣāḍhā, Uttaraphālgunī), Mṛgaśīrṣa, Maghā, Hasta, Citrā, Svāti, Anurādhā, Revatī, Aśvinī; on the 2nd, 3rd, 5th, 7th, 11th [lunar days]; the amṛta/siddha/śubha/śuddha-yogas; the king's sin, the purohita's sin, the patron's loss, the loss of wealth-and-grain, the loss of livelihood, the year of drought, the dīkṣā — [all to be known for the rite].

**The 18 saṃskāras** — nirīkṣaṇa prokṣaṇa tāḍana abhyukṣaṇaṃ catussaṃskāraṃ || khananāvakiraṇa pūraṇa samīkaraṇa secana nikuṭṭana sammārjana lepanāni aṣṭādaśa saṃskāram || kalāmayakalpana trisūtra veṣṭana rekhā caturvinyāsaḥ vajrīkaraṇa catuṣpādakalpana paramīkaraṇa ityaṣṭādaśa saṃskāraṃ kalpya ||
> nirīkṣaṇa (inspection), prokṣaṇa (sprinkling), tāḍana (striking), abhyukṣaṇa (besprinkling) — the four saṃskāras; khanana (digging), avakiraṇa (scattering), pūraṇa (filling), samīkaraṇa (leveling), secana (watering), nikuṭṭana (pounding), sammārjana (wiping), lepana (plastering) — the eighteen; the kalā-construction, the triple-thread wrapping, the rekhā (line), the four-placing, the vajra-making, the four-foot construction, the paramī-karaṇa — having performed the eighteen saṃskāras thus.

**The sage-placing** — pūrvaṃ pulastya devānāṃ agnisomarasākramam | yāmye tu tārat sthāpyā naihṛtyāṃ bhṛgumeva ca || mārkaṇḍeyo vāruṇe cāpi vasiṣṭho vāyutaḥ kramāt | some tu kauśikaṃ kṛtvā agastyaṃ īśameva ca ||
> In the east, Pulastya; for the gods, in the Agni-Soma-Rasa order; in the south, Tāra is placed; in the south-west, Bhṛgu himself; Mārkaṇḍeya in the west, Vasiṣṭha in the wind-corner, in order; in the soma-corner, making Kauśika, and Agastya in the īśāna-corner.

## Paṭala 1 — the tantrāvatāra (the descent of the tantra)

**The frame-dhyāna** — kailāsa śikhare ramye... tatra madhye sukhāsīnaṃ devadevaṃ maheśvaram | vividhairgaṇa saṃghaiśca rudra devagaṇaissaha || ṛṣibhiśca mahāpūtaiḥ gandharvādyā svaturogaṇaiḥ | praṇamya śirasā bhūmau brahmā vacanamabravīt ||
> On Kailāsa's lovely peak... there, in the middle, sitting at ease, the god of gods Maheśvara, with the gaṇa-hosts and the Rudra-god hosts, with the great-purified ṛṣis and the gandharvas and turogaṇas — bowing to the ground with his head, Brahmā spoke the word.

**Brahmā's praise** — namastasmai sureśāya śivāya paramātmane | rudrāya devadevāya śaṃkarāya svayaṃbhuve || bhasma nirmalagātrāya caturveda hitāyine... nīlakaṇṭhāya sarvāya sadyojātāya vai namaḥ | viṣṇu rūpāya rūpāya vāmadevāya vai namaḥ || mahādevāya haṃsāya aghorāya namo namaḥ | bhavotbhavāya śāntāya tatpuruṣāya namo namaḥ || umānāthāya devāya īśānāya namo namaḥ | sadāśivāya śāntāya sadasadviśvakāriṇe || vyomāya vyomarūpāya vyomavyāpī namo namaḥ | anantāya namastubhyaṃ ānandāya namo namaḥ ||
> Homage to him, the lord of the gods, the auspicious, the supreme self; to Rudra, the god of gods, Śaṅkara, the self-born; to the one pure-bodied with ashes, the worker of the four Vedas' good... homage to Nīlakaṇṭha, to the All, to Sadyojāta; homage to the Viṣṇu-form, to Vāmadeva; homage to Mahādeva, Haṃsa, Aghora; to Bhavodbhava, the Śānta, Tatpuruṣa; homage to Umā's lord, Īśāna; to Sadāśiva, the Śānta, the maker of the whole world existent-and-nonexistent; homage to the sky, the sky-formed, the sky-pervader; homage to you, the Endless, the Bliss.

**The question** — brūhi me devadeveśa śāstramāhātmyamuttamam ||
> "Tell me, O god of gods, the supreme majesty of the śāstra."

**The answer** — sādhu sādhu samahyānāṃ sarvaloka hitāvaham | sarva kāmyārtthasidhyartthaṃ sarvavyādhi vināśanam || sarva siddhikaraṃ śreṣṭhaṃ sarvapāpaharaṃ param | devā asurabhinnāṃ ca madhubhojanakāraṇam || mohinī rūpadhṛk viṣṇu komalā sarvasundarī | asurān vaṃcayitvā tu surebhyo bhojanandadau || dṛṣṭvā tu mohinī mohāt śivaṃ paramakāraṇam | śāstāraṃ sarvakartāraṃ suṣuve tābhi deśatām ||
> "Well done, well done — [this śāstra is] the good of all the worlds, the success of all desires, the destruction of all diseases, the supreme performer of all siddhis, the remover of all sins. Of the gods and asuras divided, it is the cause of the nectar-food. Viṣṇu, wearing the Mohinī-form, soft, all-beautiful — having deceived the asuras, gave the food to the gods. Seeing Mohinī, from his delusion, Śiva, the supreme cause, begat the Śāstṛ (Skanda), the doer of all — thus it is taught."

**The essence-metaphors** — puruṣo haro haraśśakti stayoryoge bhavattadam | puṣpe ca gandhavañceti dehe ca prāṇavattathā | tileṣu ca yathā tailaṃ... || mādhavaḥ prakṛtiḥ proktaḥ śivaśca puruṣastathā | śivamādhava saṃyogātproktaṃ brahma mahātbhutā ||
> Hara is the person (puruṣa); the śakti is Hara's power — their union becomes that [world]; as fragrance in the flower, life-breath in the body, oil in the sesame... Mādhava is declared the prakṛti, Śiva the puruṣa; from the Śiva-Mādhava union the great Brahmā is proclaimed.

**The śāstṛ's worship** — śāstāraṃ bhukti muktāraṃ sarveṣāṃ carācaram | śāstā śāstravatāraṃ ca tasmāt saṃsevitaṃ tvayā || vakṣyehaṃ sarva rakṣārthaṃ śṛṇuṣva kamalāsana | caturvidhānāṃ tantrāṇāṃ śrotrāṇāṃ ca caturvidhā ||
> The Śāstṛ, giver of bhukti and mukti, [lord] of the moving and unmoving; the Śāstṛ, the descent of the śāstra — therefore to be served by you. I will declare for the protection of all — hear, O lotus-seated: of the four kinds of tantras, the hearers (śrotṛs) are fourfold.

**The four hearers** — umā caiva hari skando dhātā śroktāra eva ca | śrotā ca pārvatītantraṃ nāmaśāntika mucyate || śrotā nārāyaṇo raktā nāmināṃ vijayantathā | skandenā dhivra tantrāṇāṃ raudramityavidhīyate || tantraṃ ca śrīkarantāya srotā yasya pitāmahaḥ ||
> Umā and Hari, Skanda, Dhātā — the hearers indeed. The hearer of the pārvatī-tantra is called the śāntika; the hearer Nārāyaṇa [of] the vijaya...; by Skanda the raudra tantras are declared; of the śrīkara-tantra the hearer is the Pitāmaha (Brahmā).

**The counts** — adhyāyameva tantrāṇāṃ ekāśītiṃ pṛthak pṛthak || tantrāṇāṃ sva sva saṃkhyānāṃ ṣaṭsahasra mudāhṛtam | caturviṃśatidaṃ sahasraṃ tantrāṇāṃ ca samuccayaḥ ||
> Of the tantras, each separately has eighty-one chapters; of the tantras, by their several numbers, six thousand are declared; twenty-four thousand are the total of the tantras.

**The transmission** — śivena devya dattantu devyādattantu nandine | nandine brahmaṇā dattaṃ brahmaṇāṃ ṛṣidattakaiḥ || ṛṣīṇāṃ mānuṣe dattaṃ ityetai rāgamotbhavam | āgamotbhavatā proktaṃ sarvamevantu kārayet ||
> Given by Śiva to the Devī, by the Devī to Nandin, by Nandin to Brahmā, by Brahmā to the ṛṣis, by the ṛṣis to humankind — by these it arose from the āgama; declared as āgama-born, one should perform all [rites] accordingly.

**The mantras** — caturvidhena mantreṇa śāstrāgama mihocyate | śāstrāgamantu mantreṇa saṃhitai sāṃgatat kramāt || āraṇaṃ sūtra vāṭaṃ ca tathācāryaṃ tathaiva ca | vyāhṛti mantra sāvitrī havya vāhana mantravit || śrīrudraṃ puruṣa sūktaṃ ca camakaṃ paṭhya vācakam || etat mantra vidhānena etat śāstra parāyaṇāḥ | sarvamantraṃ tathā caiva sarvaśāstra parāyaṇāḥ ||
> By the fourfold mantra the śāstra-āgama is declared: the mantra, the saṃhitā, the section, in order — the āraṇa, the sūtra-vaṭa, the ācārya; the vyāhṛtis, the sāvitrī, the havya-vāhana-mantra, the mantrin versed in the vājana-mantra; the śrīrudra, the puruṣa-sūkta, the camaka recited by the reciter. By this mantra-procedure, intent on this śāstra, [one is] intent on all mantras and śāstras.

**The kulāla's genealogy** — brahmaputrāṃbhasaścaiva aṃbhasotputradāntakaḥ | dāntaputrā jaṭādhārī tatputrasya kulālakaḥ || yāgasvā muni kulālaṃ cāmṛtkartā ca kulālakāḥ | indra yāgantathā kṛtvā yāgasvā munināmakaḥ | kulena śreṣṭha ityasmān kulāla iti cocyate ||
> The Brahmā-son's waters, and the waters' son, Dāntaka; Dānta's son the jaṭā-dhārī; his son the kulālaka. The yāgasvā-muni [is] the kulāla, and the clay-maker (mṛtkartā) is the kulālaka. Having performed the Indra-yāga, the muni named yāgasvā — from "the best by the kula (line)" the Kulāla is so called.

**The potter's rank** — brahmāṇastu kriyāpātraṃ mṛtkartā nāma eva ca | sarvadeva ghaṭatyāga kulālasya vidhikramam || caturvarṇāmi munināṃ caiva brāhma ṛṣistu kulālakaḥ | kulālastu muniśreṣṭhā vedādhyāya parāyaṇaḥ ||
> The brahmins are the ritual-vessel; the clay-maker is named so indeed; the offering of all the gods' vessels [is] the kulāla's rite-procedure. Of the four varṇas of the sages, the kulālaka is the brāhma-ṛṣi; the kulāla is the best of sages, intent on the Veda's study.

**The kulāla's duties** — tantra yajño mantra saṃpannaṃ śāstu dīkṣā samācaret | dhārayettu śikhā sūtraṃ sadākālaṃ prajāpate || tarpaṇaṃ japahomaṃ ca sandhyānuṣṭhāna tatparam | pūrvāparaṃ kulānānāṃ śāstra muṣṭyārcanāgatā || trisandhyaṃ japamādhyāya śikhā yajñopavītavān | devārtthaṃ brāhmaṇārthaṃ ca sarvapātrāṇi kārayet || somayāji sahasrebhyo deva bhaktirviśiṣyate | deva bhakti sahasrebhyaḥ kulālaśca viśiṣyate || kulālasya sahasrebhyo śāsturarcana paṇḍitaḥ || strī karārghya midantantra dharma kāmārtha muktidam ||
> The tantra-sacrifice, mantra-endowed, [one should] perform the śāstṛ's dīkṣā; he should wear the śikhā-thread at all times, O Prajāpati; intent on tarpaṇa, japa, homa, and the sandhyā-rite; reciting the japa three times [daily], wearing the śikhā and the sacred-thread — for the gods' sake and the brāhmaṇas' sake he should make all the vessels. Devotion to the gods exceeds a thousand somayājins; the kulāla exceeds a thousand god-devotees; the sage of the śāstṛ's worship exceeds a thousand kulālas. This tantra, the making of the woman's offering (strī-karārghya), gives dharma, kāma, artha, and mukti.

**The śāstra's scope** — karṣaṇādi pratiṣṭhāntaṃ pratiṣṭhādyutsavāntakam | utsavādīniśāntyantaṃ śāstā śāstramidaṃ smṛtam || tasyārcanāvahaṃ caityūpatsarvaṃ sāvadhārakam | tasya vṛttintathācāraṃ catvariṃśati saṃskṛtam || devārtthaṃ snapanādīnāṃ dvijānāṃ yajña karmaṇām | nṛpāṇāmabhiṣekārtthaṃ sidhyartthaṃ brahmaṇā ca vai || lakṣaṇaṃ sarvapātrāṇāṃ yāga pātrasya lakṣaṇam | kulālaśāstramityuktaṃ sarvaprāṇi hitāvaham ||
> From the plowing to the pratiṣṭhā, from the pratiṣṭhā to the festival's end, from the festival to the night's end — this is remembered the śāstṛ's śāstra. For his worship the caitya-rites, all with care; his livelihood and conduct, the forty-fold saṃskṛti. For the gods' snapanas, the twice-born's sacrifice-works, the kings' abhiṣeka, the brahmins' siddhi — the marks of all the vessels, the mark of the yāga-vessel: it is called the Kulālaśāstra, the good of all creatures.

**The three divisions** — evaṃ strīkara tantraṃ syāt tridhā bhedantathaiva ca | saṃhitāyā tato dhyāyā kalpasya trividhantathā || karṣaṇādi pratiṣṭhāntaṃ saṃhitā nāmamucyate | pūjāmantraṃ tatodhyāyaṃ bhuktimukti varapradam || iṣṭakarmāṇi kṛtavān viprakalpoktaṃ vidhimācaret | saṃhitodāna mārgeṇa kṛtavān tat kulālakām || adhyāyoktavidhānena ātmārtthaṃ pūjayet kramāt | tattat kāmyārttha sidhyarthaṃ coraśatru vināśanam || śāstra karmoktamārgeṇa kartavyammantra vādinām | etattantravidhiṃ karma kulālasya yogamucyate || anyeṣāṃ saṃhitā mārgaṃ yogyamapyabhidhīyate | etat karmāṇi sarvāṇi vakṣyehaṃ pitāmaha ||
> Thus the tantra is the strī-kara (the woman-making), threefold in division — the saṃhitā, the dhyāya, the kalpa, threefold. From the plowing to the pratiṣṭhā is called the saṃhitā; then the pūjā-mantras, the dhyāya, giving bhukti and mukti. Having done the prescribed works, one should follow the brāhma-kalpa's rite; having done it by the saṃhitā's path, that kulālaka; by the dhyāya's procedure one worships for one's own sake, in order; for each desire's success, the destruction of thieves and foes. By the śāstra's declared path, the mantra-speakers should act — this tantra-rite is declared the kulāla's yoga. For others the saṃhitā-path is declared fitting; all these works I will declare, O Pitāmaha.

*iti īśvaraprokte śāstrāgame kulālaśāstre śrīkaratantrannāma prathamaḥ paṭalaḥ* — "Here ends, in the śāstra-āgama proclaimed by Īśvara, the Kulālaśāstra, the first paṭala named the Śrīkara-tantra."

## Paṭala 2 — the tantu-lakṣaṇa (the sacred thread's marks)

**The thread's nature** — athātassaṃpravakṣyāmi tantūnāṃ lakṣaṇaṃ kramāt | garbhādhāna vivāhāntaṃ ṣoḍaśāntaṃ tathaiva ca || agni kārya vidhiḥ proktaṃ tantūnāṃ lakṣaṇaṃ śṛṇu ||
> Now I will declare the marks of the threads in order: from the garbhādhāna to the marriage, the sixteen [rites]; the fire-work's rite is declared — hear the threads' marks.

**The spinner** — brāhmaṇī rājaputrī vā vaiśyakanyāthavā punaḥ | athavā śūdrakanyātha tantūnāṃ parikalpayet || kārpāsantu tathā gṛhya tantūnāṃ parikalpayet | prātaḥ kāle tu pūrve tu sāvitrīmācamanantathā ||
> A brāhmaṇī, a king's daughter, a vaiśya maiden, or a śūdra maiden, one should appoint for the threads; taking cotton, one should prepare the threads; in the morning, first, [performing] the sāvitrī and the sipping (ācamana) —

**The measure** — caturaṃgulamātreṇa ṣaṇṇavatyanta sūtrakam | tena tat triguṇa skandandīrgha sūtramudāhṛtam || hastandakṣiṇamūrdhvaṃ syāt pūrvāgraṃ cāparā gṛhi | brahmacārī trisūtraṃ syāt gṛhastho dviguṇaṃ bhavet || vānaprasthaṃ tṛtīyantu sanyāsi syāccaturtthakam | ūrdhvanābhiranāyuṣyaṃ adhonābhirdhanakṣayam || nābhimātraṃ prakurvīta upavītasya lakṣaṇam ||
> By the four-aṅgula measure, the thread up to ninety-six [aṅgulas]; by that, triple, the long thread is declared on the shoulder. The right arm up, the end to the east... the brahmacārī wears the triple thread; the householder double; the vānaprastha third; the saṃnyāsin fourth. Above the navel [hanging], life; below the navel, loss of wealth; to the navel's measure one should make the upavīta's mark.

**The thread-deities** — oṃkāraḥ prathamastantu dvitīyo'gni daivatam | tṛtīyaṃ nāga daivatyaṃ caturtthaṃ somadaivatam | paṃcamaṃ vāyudaivatyaṃ ṣaṣṭhaṃ ca pitṛdaivatam || saptamaṃ sūrya daivatyaṃ aṣṭamaṃ viṣṇudaivatam | navamaṃ rudradaivatyaṃ ityete tantu devatāḥ || triguṇena trimūrtissyāt bandhuśaktiḥ śivaḥ paraḥ | brāhmaṇastantudhāreṇa śivedyo vidhipūrvakam ||
> The first thread is the Oṃ-kāra; the second has Agni for its deity; the third the nāga-deity; the fourth the soma-deity; the fifth the wind-deity; the sixth the pitṛ-deity; the seventh the sun-deity; the eighth the Viṣṇu-deity; the ninth the Rudra-deity — these are the thread's deities. By the triple[-twist] the trimūrti arises; the kin-power (bandhuśakti), Śiva the supreme: the brāhmaṇa, by the thread's bearing, [becomes] Śiva-identified, according to the rite.

**The varṇa-threads** — jaṭādhāri vidhiṃ vakṣye kṣatri vaiśya vidhikramam | vaiśya yajñopavītaṃ ca vara śaiva trisūtrakam || sanyāsasya daśānāntu ityete tantu lakṣaṇam || devārtthaṃ brāhmaṇārtthaṃ ca triguṇaṃ sūtradhāraṇam | śūdrasya tantu dhāreṇa rājarāṣṭraṃ vinaśyati || grāmaṃ grāmapatiṃ caiva bhūmyādirehi bhasmayet ||
> I will declare the jaṭā-dhārī's rite, the kṣatriya-vaiśya order; the vaiśya's upavīta, the excellent Śaiva triple-thread. The saṃnyāsin's ten-fold [knots?] — these are the threads' marks. For the gods' sake and the brāhmaṇas' sake, the triple-thread bearing [is for them]; by a śūdra's thread-bearing, the king and the kingdom perish; [the śūdra's thread] would burn the village, the village-lord, and the lands with fire.

*iti brahmaprokte kulālaśāstre upavīta lakṣaṇaṃ samāptam* — "Here ends, in the Brahmaprokta Kulālaśāstra, the upavīta-lakṣaṇa."

**The deity-worship order** — sarvadevārcanaṃ kṛtvā sarvaloka vidhikramam | brahmārcanavidhiṃ caiva brāhmaṇānāntu kalpayet || viṣṇu pūjāvidhiṃ kṛtvā vaighānasya pāṃcarātrataḥ | śivapūjāvidhiṃ kṛtvā śivadvijā vidhīyate || ārya śāstārcanaṃ kṛtvā kulāla brahmarṣistathā | bauddha mārupahaṃcaiva vidhisaṃpūjayet kramāt || bhadrakāli vidhe pūjā bhāraśaivaṃ vidhikramam | tattaddaivantu pūjānāṃ tattaddeva priyantathā || tattaddevānya pūje tu rāja rāṣṭraṃ vinaśyati ||
> Having done the worship of all the gods, by the world's order, one should arrange the Brahmā-worship for the brāhmaṇas; having done the Viṣṇu-pūjā by the vaikhānasa and pāñcarātra; having done the Śiva-pūjā, the Śiva-twice-born is made; having done the Ārya-Śāstṛ's worship, the kulāla, the brahmarṣi likewise; the Bauddha and the Mara-pūjā (?) one should worship in order; the Bhadrakālī-pūjā, the Bhāraśaiva rite — each deity's worship pleasing each deity; in a pūjā to another's deity, the king and the kingdom perish.
