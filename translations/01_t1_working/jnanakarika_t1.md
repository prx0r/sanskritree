## The working translation (paṭala 1 — the mokṣa-section)

**The homage** — oṃ namaḥ śivāya | siddhebhyo puruṣebhyo namaḥ | śrīśaṅkarapādebhyo namaḥ | apratihatatattvebhyo namaḥ
> Oṃ, homage to Śiva; homage to the perfected men (siddha-puruṣa); homage to the blessed feet of Śaṅkara; homage to the unobstructed principles.

**1/1** — athātaḥ saṃpravakṣyāmi jñānasarvaṃ (su)bhāṣitam | kārikā śrī(jñānā)khyātaṃ visarja jñānakārikā(m)
> Now, therefore, I will declare the all-knowledge well-spoken: the kārikā named the glorious Knowledge — the emission (visarga), the Jñānakārikā. [X: the pāda is corrupt — "visarja" (the emission) unexplained; collation]

**1/2** — kārikānāma vijñānaṃ śrūyante ca.....vala | sāmprataṃ kathayiṣyāmi yogināṃ yuktakāraṇam
> The knowledge named the kārikā is heard... now I will tell the yogins' fitting cause. [X: lacuna]

**1/3** — muktimārgī ca (sarva)to sarvendriyavivarjitāḥ | pañcapañcavinirmukto............varjitam
> The liberation-path... free of all the senses; freed from the five-and-five... [X: lacunae]

**1/4** — ete surāstaṃ jñānaṃ mukti........vān yathāsthitaḥ | sarvavyā(dhi)vinirmuktaṃ yacca indriyagocara(m)
> These gods — that knowledge, liberation... as it stands; free of all diseases, and what is within the senses' range. [X: lacunae]

**1/5** — dvādaśāntaṃ tathā..............śāntaṃ tathaiva ca | dvādaśāntaṃ kalāśataṃ varṇākṣaravivarjitam
> The dvādaśānta likewise... the tranquil likewise; the dvādaśānta with its hundred kalās, free of the letters and syllables.

**1/6** — sarvajñañca sadā śāntaṃ (sarvā)śravavivarjitam | brahmā viṣṇustathā rudra īśvaraḥ śiva eva ca
> The all-knowing, ever tranquil, free of all listening; Brahmā, Viṣṇu, Rudra, Īśvara, and Śiva himself —

**1/7** — mūrtirūpaṃ sthitaṃ heturanuttaraṃ yathāsthitaḥ | rūpakāyaḥ sadātītaṃ mantratantrāvivarjitam
> — the embodied form standing as the unsurpassed cause, as it stands; the form-body, ever beyond, free of mantra and tantra.

**1/8** — ātmāparavinirmuktaṃ pakṣātītaṃ śrayam | ....lakṣaṇā
> Free of self and other, beyond the alternatives, the refuge... [X: lacuna]

**1/9** — sarvadvandvavinirmuktaṃ niścitantu nirākulam | sākulatvaṃ yadā cittaṃ viṣayāsaktacetasām
> Free of all the pairs, certain, unagitated. When the mind is "with-the-kula" (sākula), of those whose minds cling to objects —

**1/10** — tadā bhramanti saṃsāraṃ māyāmalavimohitāḥ | etaddvandvasamākhyātaṃ saṃsārasya tu nirṇayam
> — then they wander in saṃsāra, deluded by māyā's impurity. This is declared the pair (dvandva) — the determination of saṃsāra.

**1/11** — sāmprataṃ kathayiṣyāmi muktistadastu durlabham | yena vijñānamātreṇa muktirbhavati yoginām
> Now I will tell the liberation — hard indeed to obtain: **by the mere knowledge of which, liberation arises for the yogins.**

**1/12** — sarvasaṃsārarahita-muktiścaiva nirākulam | niścitaṃ niścalaṃ sāmyaṃ nirdvandvaṃ ca nirākulam
> The liberation free of all saṃsāra, unagitated; the certain, unmoving, equal — free of the pairs, unagitated.

**1/13** — bhaven mokṣo narāṇāñca dvandvabhāvavivarjitam | niścittaṃ cittarahitaṃ kārikājñānamuttamam
> Liberation arises for men, free of the state of the pairs: the thoughtless, mind-free, supreme knowledge of the kārikā.

**1/14** — etadbhedaṃ mayākhyātaṃ mokṣasyaiva tu nirṇayam | sarvāśrayavinirmuktaṃ cittāścai(va) nirāśrayam
> This division has been told by me — the determination of liberation itself: free of all supports, and the mind-stuff supportless.

**1/15** — cittāśrayaṃ bhaveddharmaṃ nirāśrayaṃ mokṣadaṃ smṛtam | devo devī tathānyañca dharmādharmavivarjitam
> The support-of-mind is dharma; the supportless is remembered as giving liberation. The god and the goddess and the other — free of dharma and adharma.

**1/16** — dharmādharmāśrayaṃ proktaṃ saṃsārasya tu bandhanam | āśrayaṃ bandhamityuktaṃ muktiścaiva nirāśrayam
> The support of dharma-and-adharma is proclaimed the bondage of saṃsāra. Support is called bondage; liberation is the supportless.

**1/17** — nirāśrayaṃ bhavettattvamacintyamuktilakṣaṇam | viṣayāvasthaṃ yadā cittaṃ dvandvamuktivivarjita(m)
> The supportless is the principle, the mark of the unthinkable liberation. When the mind stands in objects, free of the pairs and liberation —

**1/18** — tadā bhramati saṃsāraṃ yattaṃ niścayatālayaḥ | mano'vasthāvinirmuktaṃ yāvattaṃ cañcalībhavet | kathantaṃ niścalaṃ devi jñātavyaṃ jñānakārike
> — then it wanders in saṃsāra; that is the absorption of certainty. Until the mind, free of the states of mind, ceases its restlessness — how is it known as the unmoving, O Devī, in the Jñānakārikā?
