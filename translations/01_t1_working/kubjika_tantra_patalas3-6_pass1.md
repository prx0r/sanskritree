### Time-place-context assessment
- **PERIOD:** the mantra-paṭalas (3–4) and the practice-paṭalas (5–6) — the consolidated vidyā-science of the late Kālīkula layer; the deity-yoga of paṭala 6 (Dākṣiṇā-Kālikā in the six cakras) is the classic late-medieval form.
- **PLACE/PATH:** Bengali transmission (the e-text's "p. 5/p. 6/p. 7/p. 8" page-breaks preserved; dialectal spellings: "sampaṃpradā" for saṃpradā, "kevavaṃ" for kevalaṃ, "smṛntrā" for smṛtā).
- **GENRE/REGISTER:** mantraśāstra + deity-yoga (dhyāna-sādhana). Paṭala 3 = the bīja-analysis of the Mahāvidyā-mantras; paṭala 4 = the individual vidyā-mantras (Pracaṇḍacaṇḍikā, Dhūmāvatī, Vagalā, Mātaṅgī, Kamalā); paṭala 5 = the caitanya-meditations; paṭala 6 = the Dākṣiṇā-Kālikā cakra-visualization ending in the yonimudrā.
- **FRAME:** the paṭalas answer the opening's program: paṭala 2 promised sādhana/yantra/mantra/mantrārtha; paṭala 3 delivers the mantrārtha of the Bhuvaneśī-vidyā; paṭala 6 delivers the yonimudrā promised at 1.12. The bīja-theology continues paṭala 2's krīṃ-analysis with the full Mahāvidyā set (śrībīja, māyābīja, kāmabīja, kūrcabīja, vāgbhava, pretabīja).

# Paṭala 3 — the meanings of the mantras (mantrārtha) of the great vidyās

## The Bhuvaneśī-vidyā (3.1–3.22)

**3/1** — atha vakṣye maheśāni mantrārthamahamuttamam | bhuvaneśī mahāvidyā sarvāmnāyairnamaskṛtā ||
> Now I will declare, O great Īśānī, the supreme meaning of the mantra: Bhuvaneśī, the great vidyā, adored by all the transmissions (āmnāya).

**3/2** — hakāro brahmarūpaśca sarvaviśvaprakāśakaḥ | rephaścandramasojyoti- rīkāraḥ paramaḥ punaḥ ||
> The ha is of Brahmā-form, illumining the whole world; the repha is the moon's light; the ī again is the supreme.

**3/3** — nirguṇonādabinduśca madhyamāhlādakārakaḥ | brahmaṇopāsitā vidyā śāpagrastā varānane ||
> The attributeless nāda-bindu is the cause of delight in the middle [speech]. The vidyā worshipped by Brahmā, struck with a curse (śāpagrastā), O fair-faced one —

**3/4** — aikārāt pṛthivī devī viśvarūpā sanātanī | yonirūpā mahādevi mahāpātakanāśinī ||
> — from the ai is the goddess Earth (pṛthivī), eternal, of universal form; of yoni-form, O great Devī, destroying the great sins.

**3/5-3/6** — hakāraḥ paramo bindu rephoyaṃ paripālakaḥ | īkāreṇa mahādevi sakalārthapradāyinī || śivenopāsitā vidyā paramārthapradāyinī | ākārāt sūryarūpā ca māyāviśvaprakāśinī ||
> The ha is the supreme bindu; this repha is the protector; with the ī, O great Devī, she gives all ends. Worshipped by Śiva, the vidyā gives the supreme end; from the ā is she of sun-form, the māyā illumining the world.

**3/7-3/8** — krau/kārāt janikā śaktiḥ kāmākhyā mokṣadā smṛtā | śakāraḥ kṛṣṇarūposau sarvajantuhiterataḥ || īkāre sthitirūpā ca rephe nityaḥ sanātanaḥ | cidrūpā nādaśaktiśca paramāhlādadāyinī ||
> From the krauṃ is the mother-power, Kāmākhyā, remembered as giving liberation; the śa is the black-formed one, intent on the good of all beings. In the ī is she of persistence-form; in the repha the eternal one; the consciousness-formed nāda-power, giving the supreme delight.

**3/9-3/11** — lakṣmīrūpā mayā devi caturvargapradāyinī | hakāro vāsudevaśca rephaḥ sṛṣṭipradāyakaḥ || īkāre tripurā devī nādabinduśivātmikā | nakāre pālikāśaktirmakāre nāśakāriṇī || namaścakāmadaḥ proktaḥ sarvārthasādhanottamaḥ | bhakāre bhagavatī devī vakārai varuṇātmikā ||
> Of Lakṣmī-form, O Devī, giving the four aims; the ha is Vāsudeva, the repha gives creation; in the ī is the goddess Tripurā, of nāda-bindu-Śiva nature; in the na is the protecting power, in the ma the destroying one. The namaḥ is proclaimed the giver of desire, the supreme means to all ends; in the bha is the goddess Bhagavatī; in the va she of Varuṇa-nature.

**3/12-3/15** — takāre tāriṇī devī viśvasya prāṇavallabhā | īkāre mātṛkā devī jagatāṃ jananī smṛtā || makāre śivarūpā ca śivaṃ viśvasya dāyinī | akāre rūdrarūpaśca viśvasya nāśarūpiṇī || hakāre yoginī devī yogināṃ yogarūpiṇī | ekāre jananī devī viśvotpannā tu mātṛkā || śakāre viśvaśaktiśca vakāre paripālinī | repho ca jñānadā devī sarvajñānaprakāśinī ||
> In the ta is the goddess Tāriṇī, the beloved of the world's life; in the ī is the goddess Mātṛkā, remembered as the mother of the worlds; in the ma she of Śiva-form, giving Śiva to the world; in the a he of Rudra-form, of the world's destruction-nature; in the ha the goddess Yoginī, of yoga-form for the yogins; in the e the mother-goddess, the Mātṛkā from whom the world arose; in the śa the world-power, in the va the protectress; the repha is the goddess giving knowledge, illumining all knowledge.

**3/16-3/18** — īkāre ca mahādevi caitanyānandavarddhinī | atha śṛṇu mahādevi rephe ca prāṇavallabhaḥ || nakāre vandinī devī sarveṣāṃ nandavarddhinī | svāhā caiva mahāvidyā agnijihvā prakīrtitā || praṇavādyā mahāvidyā jñānināṃ jñānadā matā | māyābījaṃ yadā devi caturvargapradāyinī ||
> In the ī, O great Devī, she increases consciousness and bliss; now hear, O great Devī, of the repha — the beloved of life. In the na is the goddess Vandinī, increasing the joy of all; svāhā is the great vidyā, proclaimed the tongue of fire. The vidyā beginning with the praṇava is held to give knowledge to the knowers; when the māyā-bīja [is joined], O Devī, she gives the four aims.

**3/19-3/22** — śrīvījādyā yadā devi tadā sampattidāyinī | praṇave puṭitāṃ vidyāṃ sahasraṃ prajapet sudhīḥ || sarvadoṣopaśāntyarthaṃ phaladaḥ parikīrtitaḥ | bhuvaneśī yathā devi cānnapurṇā tathā parā || sarasvatīṃ tathā padyāṃ vidyāñcaiva triśaktikām | ādyāñcaiva mahādevīṃ jānīyāt surasundarīm || tripuṭāñca tathā durgāṃ tvaritāṃ bhuvaneśvarīm | jānīyāttu mahādevīṃ satyaṃ suniścitam ||
> When it begins with the śrī-bīja, O Devī, then she gives prosperity. The wise one should chant the vidyā wrapped in the praṇava a thousand times — for the appeasement of all faults, it is proclaimed fruit-giving. As is Bhuvaneśī, O Devī, so is the supreme Annapūrṇā. Sarasvatī, and the pādyā-vidyā, the Triśaktikā — and the primal great goddess, one should know, O lovely lady. Tripuṭā, Durgā, Tvaritā, Bhuvaneśvarī — as the great goddess one should know [them], truly, with certainty.

Notes: [P] the vidyā-name constellation (Bhuvaneśī, Annapūrṇā, Sarasvatī, Triśaktikā, Tripuṭā, Durgā, Tvaritā, Bhuvaneśvarī) — the same goddess-family as our ŚS 1.3's four śaktis and the Kālīkula lists. The "śāpagrastā" (3/3) — the curse-on-the-vidyā motif (the vidyā was cursed to be hidden — cf. the paśu-exclusion).

## The individual vidyās' bījas (3.23–3.28)

**3/23-3/24** — tripuṭāyāḥ kāmavījaṃ kevalaṃ kāmanārthadam | śrībījañca mahādevi śrīrdevī ca prakīrtitā || tvaritāyā mahādevi māyābījañca bījakam | triśaktiḥ praṇavo devi muktidaṃ pretabījakam ||
> Of Tripuṭā, the kāma-bīja alone gives the end of desire; and the śrī-bīja, O great Devī — the goddess Śrī is proclaimed. Of Tvaritā, O great Devī, the māyā-bīja is the seed; the praṇava is the Triśakti, O Devī; the preta-bīja gives liberation.

**3/25-3/28** — khecarañca mahādevi khecaraṃ parikīrtitam | vadhūbījaṃ maheśāni astrarūpaṃ prakīrtitam || durgāyāśca mahāmantro mahādurgasya tārakaḥ | praṇavobrahmarūpaśca māyābījañca muktidam || hrī/bījañca maheśāni sarvadurgatitārakam | durgā durgasya hantrī ca dru/kāre viśvarūpakaḥ || namo mokṣasya dātā ca mahāmantraḥ prakīrtitaḥ | mahiṣamarddinī devī durgāvat parikīrtitā ||
> The khecara [bīja], O great Devī, is proclaimed the sky-goer; the vadhū-bīja, O great Īśānī, is proclaimed of weapon-form. The great mantra of Durgā carries across the great strait (mahādurga); the praṇava is of Brahmā-form; the māyā-bīja gives liberation. The hrīṃ-bīja, O great Īśānī, carries across every bad state (durgati); Durgā is the slayer of the strait; in the druṃ is the universal-formed one. The namaḥ is proclaimed the giver of liberation, the great mantra; the goddess Mahiṣamardinī is proclaimed equal to Durgā.

## The Śrī-vidyā section (3.29–3.64)

**3/29-3/31** — atha vakṣye mahāmantraṃ nirguṇaṃ paramaṃ punaḥ | śrīdevyāśca maheśāni mantrārthaṃ mantramuttamam || hakāraḥ kevalaṃ devi nirguṇaḥ paramākṣaraḥ | śrūyatāṃ sāvadhānena nirvāṇapadadāyakaḥ || sakāre nirguṇā devī īkāre nirguṇā sadā | lakāre nirguṇā devī śrūyatāṃ girinandini ||
> Now I will declare the great mantra, the attributeless, the supreme again — the meaning of the mantra of the goddess Śrī, the supreme mantra, O great Īśānī. The ha is alone, O Devī, attributeless, the supreme syllable; hear with attention: it gives the nirvāṇa-station. In the sa is the attributeless goddess; in the ī always the attributeless; in the la the attributeless goddess — hear, O daughter of the mountain:

**3/32-3/35** — māyābījantu saguṇam śivaśaktiyutaṃ priye | hakāraḥ paramobrahma nirguṇaśca sanātanaḥ | śakāraḥ śambhurityukto nirguṇaśca nirañjanaḥ || lakāre nirguṇā devī nirlepā niṣkalā parā | māyābījantu deveśi nirguṇaṃ paramātmakam || sakāraḥ śāntidodevaḥ sarvaśāntipradāyakaḥ | kevalaṃ brahmarūpaśca satyaṃ devi na saṃśayaḥ ||
> The māyā-bīja is with-attributes, joined with Śiva-śakti, O beloved. The ha is the supreme Brahmā, attributeless, eternal; the śa is declared Śambhu, attributeless and the Stainless (nirañjana). In the la the attributeless goddess, the unstained (nirlepā), the partless, the supreme. The māyā-bīja, O Lady of the gods, is attributeless, of the supreme self. The sa is the peace-giving god, granting all peace; alone of Brahmā-form — truly, O Devī, no doubt.

**3/36-3/38** — kakāraḥ kāmadevosau sarveṣāṃ kāmadaḥ smṛtaḥ | lakāre kāminīdevī sarveṣāṃ kāmarūpiṇī || kāmākhyā mokṣadātrī ca kamasya pālinī sadā | bhuvaneśyā mahāvījaṃ caitanyānandavarddhakam || pañcadaśākṣarī vidyā kāmenopāsitā dhruvam | īkārañca mahāvidyā hakārādyā maheśvari ||
> The ka is the desire-god, remembered as granting desire to all; in the la is the goddess Kāminī, of desire-form for all. Kāmākhyā gives liberation and ever guards desire. The great bīja of Bhuvaneśī increases consciousness and bliss; the fifteen-syllable vidyā, surely worshipped by Kāma. And the ī is the great vidyā beginning with ha, O great Īśvarī —

**3/39-3/42** — agastyopāsitā vidyā bhuvanatrayadurllabhā | dvikūṭādyo hakāroyaṃ sakāraśca trikūṭake || hakārojyotirūpoyaṃ śrūyatāṃ varavarṇini | kakāre ca mahādevi kevalaṃ kāmanārthakaḥ || hakāraśca maheśāni śambhurdevo maheśvari | ekāre navadurgā ca īkāre śambhumātṛkā || kevalaṃ mokṣadā devi lakāre viśvarūpiṇī | māyābījaṃ maheśāni triśaktiḥ parikīrtitā ||
> — the vidyā worshipped by Agastya, hard to obtain in the three worlds. With two knots (dvikūṭa) is this ha; with the sa in the three-knotted (trikūṭa). This ha is of light-form — hear, O fair-colored one. In the ka, O great Devī, is alone the end-of-desire. The ha, O great Īśānī, is the god Śambhu, O Īśvarī; in the e are the nine Durgās; in the ī the mother of Śambhu; alone giving liberation, O Devī; in the la the universal-formed one. The māyā-bīja, O great Īśānī, is proclaimed the Triśakti.

**3/43-3/49** — hakāre ca kakāre ca ekāre ca maheśvari | brahmāṇī vaiṣṇavī devī rudrāṇī parikīrtitā || īkāre ca lakāre ca māyābījena sundari | brahmā viṣṇurmaheśaśca satyaṃ devi na saṃśayaḥ || sakāre ca kakāre ca ekāre ca sureśvari | brahmaśaktirviṣṇuśaktirmaheśī ca prakīrtitā || manupratiṣṭhitā vidyā caturvargaphalapradā | niṣkalā ca maheśāni sakalā parikīrtitā || sakārādyā mahāvidyā māyādyā ca sureśvarī | sarvasampaṃpradā devī candraloke pratiṣṭhitā || hakārādyā maheśāni brahmaviṣṇuśivātmikā | māyāntā ca maheśāni triśaktiḥ parikīrtitā || brahmarūpā mahāvidyā kuveropāsitā dhruvam | dhanadātrī mahādevi trivargaphaladāyinī ||
> In the ha, the ka and the e, O great Īśvarī: Brahmāṇī, Vaiṣṇavī, and the goddess Rudrāṇī are proclaimed. In the ī, the la, with the māyā-bīja, O lovely one: Brahmā, Viṣṇu, Maheśa — truly, O Devī, no doubt. In the sa, the ka, the e, O lady of the gods: the Brahmā-power, the Viṣṇu-power, the Maheśī are proclaimed. The vidyā established by Manu gives the four aims' fruit; the partless, O great Īśānī, and the with-parts are proclaimed. The vidyā beginning with sa, or with māyā, O Sūreśvarī, the goddess giving all prosperity, established in the moon-world. Beginning with ha, O great Īśānī, of Brahmā-Viṣṇu-Śiva nature; ending with māyā, O great Īśānī, it is proclaimed the Triśakti. The great vidyā of Brahmā-form, surely worshipped by Kubera: the giver of wealth, O great Devī, granting the three aims' fruit.

**3/50-3/64** (the trikūṭa-arithmetic and the secrecy rules): the vidyās beginning with ka/ha/sa, ending with māyā — the full and the great-formed; the Satī-worshipped, the U-pāmudrā; the trikūṭā (three-knotted) of sṛṣṭi-sthiti-laya nature; worshipped by Maṅgala; the bīja-triads (Brahmāṇī/Vaiṣṇavī/Śacī; Kālikā/Vākyarūpiṇī/Bhairavī; Vaiṣṇavī/Sarasvatī/Kamalā/Hākinī) —

**3/63-3/64** — yatirvā bhupatirvāpi nānyaḥ śaktaḥ sureśvari | śūdraścāpi maheśāni na kuryānmantracintanam || rājyaṃ deyaṃ maheśāni na dadyāt ṣoḍaśākṣaram | śirodeyaṃ maheśāni na prākāśyaṃ sureśvari ||
> Whether a renunciant (yati) or a lord of the earth (bhūpati) — no other is capable, O Sūreśvarī; and a śūdra, O great Īśānī, must not perform the mantra-contemplation. The kingdom may be given, O great Īśānī — but one must not give the sixteen-syllable [vidyā]; the head may be given, O great Īśānī — but it must not be revealed, O Sūreśvarī.

Notes: the secrecy climax — the head-or-kingdom formula (the vidyā is worth more than either). The trikūṭa (three-knotted) structure of the vidyā (ha-sa-la + māyā-bīja = the haṃsī-vidyā family: hrīṃ!). [P: the "śūdra" exclusion — the paśu-exclusion of 1.12/1.48 in social form.]

## The Bhairavī-vidyās (3.65–3.93)

**3/65-3/70** — tava snehānmahādevi kathitaṃ varavarṇini | atha vakṣe jagaddhātri bhairavyā mantramuttamam || yasya vijñānamātreṇa rājābhogaphalaṃ labhet | muktidaṃ kāmadaṃ devi paramānandakārakam || hakāraśca sakāraśca vakāraśca maheśvari | śivaḥ paraśivaścaiva parāparaśivaḥ punaḥ || ekāraḥ śaktirūpaśca nādabinduḥ sanātanī | hakāraśca sakāraśca kakāraśca maheśvari || parā ca paramā caiva īśvarī parikīrtitā | vakāraśca īkāraśca bhairavī bhayanāśinī || sampaṃpradā yadā devī tadā sampaṃ pradāyinī | caitanyabhairavī mantraṃ kevavaṃ mokṣasāthanam ||
> Out of love for you, O great Devī, it has been told, O fair-colored one. Now I will declare, O holder of the world, the supreme mantra of Bhairavī: by the mere understanding of which one gains the fruit of kingly enjoyment; giving liberation, giving desire, O Devī, causing supreme bliss. The ha, the sa, the va, O great Īśvarī: Śiva, Paraśiva, and Parāpara-Śiva. The e is of power-form; the nāda-bindu is the eternal. The ha, the sa, the ka, O great Īśvarī: Parā, Paramā, and Īśvarī are proclaimed. The va and the ī: Bhairavī, the destroyer of fear. When she gives prosperity, O Devī, then she is the giver of prosperity — the Caitanya-Bhairavī, the mantra that alone is the means to liberation.

**3/71-3/78** — kāmeśvarīmahāmantraṃ nādabindusvarūpakam | catūrvargapradaṃ devi śṛṇuṣva haravallabhe || ṣaṭkuṭā bhairavī devī ṣaṭkarmaphaladāyinī | brahmāṇī vaiṣṇavī caiva rūdrāṇī parikīrtitā || bhogadā mokṣadā devī prathame bhogadāyinī | madhyame yogadā proktā ante ca jñānadā smṛtā || nādabindusamyayogāccaturvargaphalapradā | rudrāṇī vaiṣṇavīmantraścaturvargaphalapradaḥ || hakāre ca sakāre ca thakāre ca maheśvari | phakāre ca rakāre ca nādabindusamanvitā || hākinī śākinī caiva ḍākinī ca maheśvari | pretabījasamāyogāt pañcapretaḥ prakīrtitaḥ || bhuvaneśībhairavīṇāṃ mantraśca bhupradāyakaḥ | hamaṃ kāmasvarūpaśca kalarīrājyabhogadā || pretabījaṃ mahādevaḥ sarvadurgatināśakaḥ | nādaśaktakalā caiva bindurūpaḥ sadāśivaḥ ||
> The Kāmeśvarī great mantra, of nāda-bindu form, giving the four aims, O Devī — hear, O beloved of Hara. The six-knotted (ṣaṭkuṭā) Bhairavī, O Devī, gives the fruit of the six acts; Brahmāṇī, Vaiṣṇavī, Rudrāṇī are proclaimed. Giving enjoyment and liberation, O Devī: in the first [knot] she gives enjoyment; in the middle, yoga is proclaimed; at the end she is remembered as giving knowledge. From the union with the nāda-bindu she gives the four aims. The Rudrāṇī-Vaiṣṇavī mantra gives the four aims' fruit. The ha, the sa, the tha, O great Īśvarī, the pha and the ra, joined with the nāda-bindu: Hākinī, Śākinī, and Ḍākinī, O great Īśvarī; from the union with the preta-bīja, the five Pretas are proclaimed. The mantra of Bhuvaneśī and Bhairavī gives the earth. The ha-maṃ is of Kāma-form; [the vidyā] gives the enjoyment of the kingdom of the arts (kalā). The preta-bīja is the great god, destroying every bad state; the nāda is the kalā of the power; the bindu-formed one is Sadāśiva.

**3/79-3/93** (the Vālikā-mantra and the closing rules): the aīṃ (from ai = the world-mother), klīṃ (the bindu-mother), auṃ with sa (the rasanā — tongue); the vāgbhava and kāma-bījas with sauḥ; the rudra-forms; the ten-syllable vidyā with kravauṃ (ha sa rauṃ) — the giver of siddhi to all; the sixteen-syllable with the praṇava; the nine-knotted vidyā giving the nine siddhis; the aiṃ wrapped in māyā chanted 108 times —

**3/88-3/93** — ai/kāraṃ māyayā devī puṭitaṃ śatadhā japet | tejasvitāmavāpnoti sarvakāmaphalaṃ labhet || kinneti mokṣadāmantro mahāmantraḥ prakīrtitaḥ | annapūrṇeśvarī devī bhairavī parikīrtitā || annadā mokṣadā caiva bhairavī ca dhruvaṃ priye | brahmāṇī vaiṣṇavī devī rudrāṇī ca sarasvatī | namo bhagavatī devī sarvakāmaphalapradā || māheśvarī mahādevi pañcarudraḥ prakīrtitaḥ | śrīvījapuṭitenaiva śatamaṣṭottaraṃ japet || yadā caiva maheśāni mantroyaṃ phaladaḥ smṛtaḥ | kiñciccaiva maheśāni kathitaṃ prāṇavallabhe || ma prakāśyaṃ mahādevi paśoragre kadācana ||
> The aiṃ wrapped in māyā, O Devī, one should chant a hundred times: he attains brilliance and gains the fruit of all desires. The "kiṃ" is the liberation-giving mantra, proclaimed the great mantra. The goddess Annapūrṇeśvarī is proclaimed Bhairavī: giving food and giving liberation — the Bhairavī, surely, O beloved. Brahmāṇī, Vaiṣṇavī, Rudrāṇī, and Sarasvatī — namaḥ to the goddess Bhagavatī giving the fruit of all desires. Māheśvarī, O great Devī, is proclaimed the five Rudras. Wrapped in the śrī-bīja alone, one should chant 108 times — then, O great Īśānī, this mantra is remembered as fruit-giving. Something, O great Īśānī, has been told, O beloved of life. It must not be revealed, O great Devī, before the bound one (paśu) at any time.

# Paṭala 4 — the vidyā-mantras of the individual goddesses

## Pracaṇḍacaṇḍikā (4.1–4.9)

**4/1-4/2** — pracaṇḍacaṇḍikāmantraṃ śṛṇu pārvati yatnataḥ | na prakāśyaṃ mahādevi na prakāśyaṃ kadācana || rahasyaṃ paramaṃ devi triṣu lokeṣu gopitam | devyāścakṣuḥ kakāreṇa lakāreṇa ca tārikā ||
> The Pracaṇḍacaṇḍikā mantra — hear, O Pārvatī, attentively. It is not to be revealed, O great Devī, never at any time to be revealed. The supreme secret, O Devī, hidden in the three worlds: the goddess's eye is in the ka; with the la she is the ferry-woman (tārikā).

**4/3-4/6** — īkāre ca bhrurormadhyaṃ nādabinduḥ śikhā dhruvam | kūrccabījaṃ kapālañca vāgbhavaṃ vākyameva ca || bhuvaneśī nāsikā ca vajraṃ kaṇṭhaṃ prakīrtitam | trayonādaḥ śaktirūpojihvāñca rocanīyakam || yacca vai dantapaṃktiñca kūrccabījañca mānasam | kūrcaprāṇamayaṃ devi phaṭkāraṃ stanayugmakam || aghoraṃ svāhayā caiva jānīyāt surapūjite | pañcākṣarī mahāvidyā ḍākinī parikīrtitā ||
> In the ī is the space between the brows; the nāda-bindu is the crest (śikhā), surely; the kūrca-bīja is the skull; the vāgbhava is speech itself. Bhuvaneśī is the nose; the vajra is proclaimed the throat; the three-nāda of power-form is the tongue, and the rocanī. Whatever is the row of teeth — the kūrca-bīja is the mind; the kūrca is made of life-breath, O Devī; the phaṭ-kāra is the pair of breasts. The aghora with svāhā — one should know, O worshipped-of-the-gods: the five-syllable great vidyā is proclaimed Ḍākinī.

**4/7-4/9** — ekādaśākṣarīvidyā varṇinī parikīrtitā | dhanadā mokṣadā devī sarvakāmaphalapradā || asyaiva pūjākāle ca bhuvaneṣu ca sundari | patirvā bhūpatirvāpi nānyosti pūjakaḥ punaḥ || ārādhane mahādevyāḥ śastraghātaṃ labhet dhruvam | śastraghātād bhavenmuktiḥ satyaṃ satyaṃ sureśvari ||
> The eleven-syllable vidyā is proclaimed the Varṇinī: giving wealth, giving liberation, O Devī, granting the fruit of all desires. At the time of her worship, in the worlds, O lovely one: whether a lord (pati) or a lord of the earth (bhūpati) — there is no other worshipper. In the adoration of the great goddess, one surely obtains the stroke of the sword (śastraghāta); from the stroke of the sword comes liberation — truly, truly, O Sūreśvarī.

Notes: the body-mapping of the vidyā (the mantra's letters = the goddess's limbs); the śastraghāta-liberation (the sword-stroke as the dīkṣā of the fierce goddess — cf. the Vīramāṃsaka doctrine of the MBT line; [P: Dyczkowski's MBT work covers the sword-liberation doctrine — the "head-offering" tradition]).

## Dhūmāvatī (4.10–4.14)

**4/10-4/14** — śṛṇu devi sadā bhadre bhaktānāṃ trāṇakāriṇi | dhūmāvatīṃ mahāvidyāṃ mahāyogaprabhāviṇīm || tasyāmantraṃ pravakṣyāmi śṛṇuṣva haravallabhe | dhakāre sarvadhamanī ukāre pālinī smṛtā || caitavyarūpiṇī devī mahākālanitamvinī | dhṛkāre nāśinī devī mahāpralayakāriṇī || makāre mārarūpā ca ākare ratisundari | vakāre kāmarūpā ca tikāre tīkṣnadhāriṇī || svāhā caiva maheśāni bhairavī parikīrtitā ||
> Hear, O Devī, ever blessed, protecting the devoted: the great vidyā Dhūmāvatī, pervading with great yoga. I will tell her mantra — hear, O beloved of Hara. In the dha is the blower of all (sarvadhamanī); in the u the protectress is remembered. The goddess of consciousness-form, the destroyer of the great death (mahākāla); in the dhṛ is the destroying goddess, making the great dissolution. In the ma she of death-form; in the ā the lovely Rati; in the va she of desire-form; in the ti the bearer of sharpness. And svāhā, O great Īśānī, is proclaimed Bhairavī.

Notes: the Dhūmāvatī-bīja "dhūṃ" (dha-u-dhṛ-ma + the rest) — the smoke-goddess of the Mahāvidyā list (our paṭala 1's 1/41); the "mahākālanitamvinī" — destroying the great death (cf. 1.20's mahākālavināśinī).

## Vagalā (4.15–4.21)

**4/15-4/21** — vagalā ca mahāvidyā tasyāmantraṃ śṛṇu priye | tasya vijñānamātreṇa kṛtakṛtyo bhavennaraḥ || praṇavaṃ nirguṇaṃ devi nāsā caiva triśaktikā | vagalā viṣṇurudrāṇī lakāraśca harapriye || mukhīti devatāḥ sarvāḥ sarvadaityavināśinī | sarvaḥ sarveśvaraḥ prokto duṣṭānāṃ duṣṭanāśinī || vāṇīvākyasvarūpā ca mukhamohanakāriṇī || stantayeti vaśīrūpā jagatāṃ vaśakāriṇī | jihvā caiva mahāmāyā jihvārūpā prakīrtitā || kīlayaḥ kīlayaśceti jagatkṣobhaṇakāriṇī | vuddhiñca dhuddhirūpā sā nāśayo nāśakāriṇī || māyā ca praṇave caiva sarveṣāṃ jñānakāriṇī | svāhā caiva mahāvidyā yajñarūpā havirbhujaḥ ||
> And Vagalā, the great vidyā — hear her mantra, O beloved: by the mere understanding of it a man becomes fulfilled (kṛtakṛtya). The attributeless praṇava, O Devī, and the nose is the Triśaktikā; Vagalā, Viṣṇu-Rudrāṇī, and the la, O beloved of Hara. The "mukhī" — all the deities, the destroyer of all demons; "sarvaḥ" — the lord of all is proclaimed, the destroyer of the wicked for the wicked. Of the form of Vāṇī's speech, she bewitches the mouth. "Stantaya" — of subjugating form, subjugating the worlds. And the tongue is the great illusion (mahāmāyā), proclaimed of tongue-form. "Kīlaya, kīlaya" — she agitates the world. The "buddhi" and the "dhuddhi"-formed one, the destroyer of the destroyers. The māyā in the praṇava gives knowledge to all; and svāhā is the great vidyā, of sacrifice-form, eating the oblation.

Notes: the Vagalā-mantra (the famed "vagalāmukhī" bīja: hlīṃ vagalāmukhī sarvaduṣṭānāṃ... the stambhana-mantra) — the "kīlaya kīlaya" (nail, nail!) — the stambhana-act; the "mukhī" (the face-sealing).

## Mātaṅgī (4.22–4.23) and Kamalā (4.24–4.30)

**4/22-4/23** — mātaṅgī ca mahāvidyā sarvatantre pratiṣṭhitā | śṛṇu devi mahābhadre bhaktānāṃ trāṇakāriṇi || śrībījaṃ viśvarūpañca māyābījañca kāmadam | kṣaṃ bījaṃ kṣetradañcaiva mātaṅgī madanāturā || svāhā ca brahmarūpaiva brahmaṇā ca pratiṣṭhitā ||
> And Mātaṅgī, the great vidyā, established in all the tantras — hear, O great blessed one, protecting the devoted: the śrī-bīja of universal form, the māyā-bīja giving desire, the kṣaṃ-bīja giving the field (kṣetra) — Mātaṅgī, tormented by love (madanāturā). And svāhā is of Brahmā-form, established by Brahmā.

**4/24-4/30** — atha vakṣye maheśāni kamalāmantramuttamam | yasya vijñānamātreṇa samṛddhiṃ labhate dhruvam || bhogadā mokṣadā devī lakṣmīrūpa prakīrtita || śrībīje kevalā lakṣmīrnādo mokṣasya dāyakaḥ | ekākṣarī mahāvidyā sarvakāmapradāyinī || vāgabhavādyā mahādevī vāgīśatvapradāyinī | lajjābījaṃ maheśāni brahmaviṣṇuśivātmakam || kāmabījantu deveśi lakṣmīrmāyā sarasvatī | nādaścaitanyarūpo'sau viṣṇuḥ paraśivomataḥ || mantravyākhyā maheśāni saṃkṣepāt kathitā mayā | na prakāśyamidaṃ devi paśoragre kadācana || svarga martye ca pātāle devāśca siddhi dāyakāḥ | praśaṃsanti sadā sarve taṃ dṛṣṭvā sādhakottamam ||
> Now I will declare, O great Īśānī, the supreme mantra of Kamalā: by the mere understanding of it one surely gains prosperity. Giving enjoyment and liberation, O Devī, she is proclaimed of Lakṣmī-form. In the śrī-bīja alone is Lakṣmī; the nāda gives liberation. The one-syllable great vidyā granting all desires. Beginning with the vāgbhava, the great Devī grants lordship of speech (vāgīśatva). The lajjā-bīja, O great Īśānī, is of Brahmā-Viṣṇu-Śiva nature. The kāma-bīja, O Lady of the gods: Lakṣmī, Māyā, Sarasvatī; the nāda, of consciousness-form, is held to be Viṣṇu, the supreme Śiva. The exposition of the mantras, O great Īśānī, has been told by me in brief. This is not to be revealed, O Devī, before the bound one at any time. In heaven, the world, and the nether world, the gods, the givers of siddhi, ever praise him, seeing the best of sādhakas.

# Paṭala 5 — the meditations on consciousness (caitanya-dhyāna)

**5/1** — bhagavan sarvadeveśa lokānāṃ hitakāraka | caturvargapradaṃ devi caitanyaṃ me prakāśaya ||
> O Lord, god of all gods, doer of good for the worlds — reveal to me the consciousness that gives the four aims, O Devī.

**5/2-5/4** — śṛṇu devi pravakṣyāmi caitanyaṃ paramādbhūtam | rahasyaṃ paramaṃ guhyaṃ gopanīyaṃ tvayā punaḥ || cicchaktyādhvanitaṃ devi pariṇāmakrameṇa tu | varṇabhāvaṃ samāśritya nirguṇaṃ saguṇātmakam || ṣaṭcakrañca tathā bhittvā śabdarūpaṃ sanātanam | nādabindusayāyuktaṃ caitanyaṃ parikīrtitam ||
> Hear, O Devī — I will declare the consciousness, the supreme wonder: the supreme secret, to be guarded by you again. Vibrated by the consciousness-power (cicchakti), O Devī, by the order of transformation (pariṇāmakrama), taking refuge in the state of the phonemes — attributeless, of the with-attributes self: having pierced the six cakras, the eternal sound-form, joined with the nāda-bindu — that is proclaimed consciousness.

**5/5-5/10** (the anāhata-meditation): without it japa and worship never succeed: in the middle of the anāhata [cakra] is strung the supreme phoneme; by the suṣumnā-path it issues to the throat-region; the consciousness, of yoga-form for the yogins; in the thousand-petaled, phoneme-formed by the order of transformation; in the middle of the pericarp, joined with the nāda-bindu; the mantra-letters are strung in the consciousness-power —

**5/11-5/13** (the mātṛkā-wrapping): or in another way: first uttering the kāma-bīja, the ramā-bīja, the śakti-bīja, then the mātṛkā; the root-mantra wrapped [by them], chant 108 — he gains it ten-million-fold, no doubt.

**5/14-5/17** (the sun-disc meditation): contemplating the root-mantra in the middle of the sun-disc, chanting 108 — of the root-vidyā's form; contemplating the guru there, of eternal Śiva-form, and the power (śakti) there, of eternal Brahmā-form — for him nothing is unattainable in this world; liberation stands in his hand, O Devī.

Notes: the caitanya-dhyāna paṭala: the consciousness as the phoneme-strung-through-the-cakras; the three practice-forms (anāhata, mātṛkā-wrapping, sun-disc-with-guru-śakti). [P: the mātṛkā-wrapping of paṭala 2's puṭita-rules; the sahasrāra/karṇikā vocabulary of KJN 3/6-9; the guru-śakti pair of the KJN's transmission-frame.]

# Paṭala 6 — the Dākṣiṇā-Kālikā deity-yoga (cakra-visualization) and the yonimudrā

**6/1-6/3** — atha vakṣye maheśāni śāradendunibhānane | atīva paramaṃ guhyaṃ na prakāśyaṃ kadācana || rahasyaṃ paramaṃ guhyaṃ sarvadeveṣu vanditam | caturvedairagamyantu sāṃsārārṇavatārakam || sarvāmnāyairmahādevi vanditaṃ surasundari | gopanīyaṃ gopanīyāṃ gopanīyaṃ sureśvari ||
> Now I will declare, O great Īśānī, fair as the autumn moon: the extremely supreme secret, never to be revealed at any time. The supreme secret, adored by all the gods, unattainable by the four Vedas, carrying across the ocean of saṃsāra. Adored by all the transmissions, O great Devī, O lovely goddess: it must be guarded, guarded, guarded, O Sūreśvarī.

**6/4-6/7** (the night-practice): na prakāśyamidaṃ devi svayoniriva pārvati | niśīthe muktakeśastu nagnaḥ śaktisamanvitaḥ || cintayediṣṭadevīñca yogināṃ yogarūpiṇīm | guhyadeśe vāmapādagulphaṃ saṃyojayet sudhīḥ || śarīrañca sthirikṛtya jihvāyāṃ tālukāṃ nyaset | nāsāgre netrayugmañca karttavyañca maheśvari || kaṇṭhāsamaṃ tathā kṛtvā cintayedvāhinīṃ śivām | bhujaṅgarūpiṇīṃ devīṃ mūlādhāranivāsinīm ||
> This is not to be revealed, O Devī, like one's own yoni, O Pārvatī. At midnight, with loose hair, naked, joined with the power (śakti), he should contemplate the desired goddess, of yoga-form for the yogins. In the secret place, the wise one joins the left foot's ankle; making the body firm, he places the tongue against the palate; the gaze on the tip of the nose — and this must be done, O Īśvarī. Then, making the throat-level [breath], he contemplates the flowing Śiva [power] — the serpent-formed goddess dwelling in the mūlādhāra.

**6/8-6/15** (the mūlādhāra-visualization of Dākṣiṇā-Kālikā): in the morning-lotus, in the circle of the fire-eater [the sun], seated — the four-armed great goddess, nourished by the supreme nectar; dark-colored (śyāma), seated on the great lotus; ever holding in the right hands the fear-not and the boon; in the left holding the sword and the skull; with loose hair, smiling face, dwelling in the burning ground; with protruding teeth, naked, sky-clad (digambarī); a girdle made of a mass of corpses' hands, laughing-faced; wearing a hanging garland of skulls, ever-auspicious; standing with the right foot on the heart of a corpse; extremely lustful for union with Mahākāla; with the three eyes of sun-moon-fire; holding the half-moon; with high, full breasts; surrounded on four sides by the terrible howling Śiva-women (śivā); uncrossable by the four Vedas, adorned by all the gods; giving desire, of desire-form, protecting the devoted — the terrible one giving the four aims: **I worship the Dākṣiṇā-Kālikā** (vandedakṣiṇakālikām).

**6/16-6/26** (svādhiṣṭhāna): contemplating the moon-form in the svādhiṣṭhāna; the stainless goddess of universal form; with the fear-not, the boon, the bow and the arrow, the noose and the goad; joined with the arrow-liṅga; blue-colored, of fire's brilliance; with lolling tongue, of Viṣṇu-form, eternal; with the two vine-earrings, destroying the wicked demons; clad in tiger-skin, dwelling in the brahmarandhra; with a happy, serene face — then in the maṇipūra: the mass of brilliance, eternal; sky-clad, terrible-faced, loose-haired, four-armed; red-colored, wearing the red skin; on the lotus seat, adorned with all the serpents; holding conch, discus, mace, lotus, worshipped by the gods; giving dharma and artha, protecting the devoted; giving all siddhis, bewitching the worlds; the great illusion, ever giving knowledge; the beautiful, the delightful, the Vāmā, giving the four aims; the peaceful, fond of peace, the fierce, destroying the fierce demons; fond of honey and meat, the black one, delighting Yaśodā; attainable through devotion, dwelling with Mahākāla; the eternal mother of all, worshipped by the gods.

**6/27-6/39** (anāhata, viśuddha, ājñā, sahasrāra): in the anāhata, the supreme deity: eight-armed, red, the Triśakti; holding bow, arrow, sword, discus, noose, goad; adored by Brahmā and the gods; holding the half-moon, high-breasted; on the lotus seat, the great illusion, going by the Brahmā-path. In the viśuddha great cakra, the supreme Īśvarī: ten-armed, yellow, eternal; holding skull, shield, sword, mirror, fly-whisk on the right, standing on the kāma-bīja; on the left sword, great lotus, knife, noose, goad; on the great lion-seat; clad in divine garments, crowned with matted hair; adored by Brahmā, Viṣṇu, Maheśa. In the ājñā-cakra, the two-armed, seated on the lion's shoulder, lovely; holding the boon and the fear-not; adorned with manifold jewels; with the matted-hair crown and the half-moon; with the jewel-girdle and jewel-garlands; clad in red garments, served by the lotus-lord [Viṣṇu]; calming the suffering of saṃsāra, ferrying across its ocean — by the suṣumnā-path, contemplating the meditation-formed one. By the yoga of piercing the six cakras, contemplate the supreme-supreme Īśvarī; in the thousand-petaled lotus, the supreme Īśvarī —

**6/40-6/56** (the sahasrāra): with all the tīrthas, nectar on every petal; adorned with all the seasons from spring; white, the supreme thousand-petaled lotus; joined with all the gods, endowed with all the powers; made of all mantras, adorned with the four Vedas; the eleven Rudras, of Brahmā-Viṣṇu-Śiva nature; the golden pericarp — there the goddess: the consciousness-formed supreme Śiva, eternal, attainable in meditation; the more-essential-than-the-essential, beyond even the beyond, beyond thought and speech; the eternal bliss, unceasing, incomparable, uncrossable by the gods, supreme; of Śiva-śakti nature, attributeless, of the with-attributes self; like the flame of a lamp, the meditation-form of the yogins; of consciousness-form, nourished by the supreme nectar. In its middle, the goddess: the eternal three-guṇas; with terrible fangs, terrible mouth, lolling tongue, four-armed; with the freshly-cut head and the sword in the left raised hand-lotus; with the fear-not and the boon in the right raised hands; of the dark-cloud's brilliance, slender, terrible-lipped, sky-clad; with the garland of severed heads hanging from the neck, smeared with dripping blood; with the two corpse-earrings, fearsome; with the three eyes of the sun-disc form; with blood-streams dripping from the two mouth-corners, face glittering; loose-haired, smiling-faced, dwelling in the burning ground; girdled with a mass of corpses' hands, laughing-faced; standing on the heart of the corpse-formed great god; seated for union with Mahākāla, tormented by desire; surrounded on the four sides by the terrible howling Śiva-women; with an utterly serene face, smiling lotus-face — there, in the anāhata, with the brilliance of ten million lightnings.

**6/55-6/60** (the ascent): above it the peak (śikhara), the supreme kalā of consciousness-form; with it, contemplate the self become one; going by the Brahmā-path, by the order of the piercing of the liṅga; with the brilliance of ten million suns, the coolness of ten million moons; the nectar, the visarga, of supreme-bliss nature; going to the divine kula-and-akula, entering the kula again —

**6/58-6/60** — mūlādhārasaroje tu trikoṇaṃ sumanoharam | kāmaṃ bhramantaṃ tanmadhye vālārkakoṭisannibham | tadurddhe kuṇḍalīśaktistaḍitkoṭisamaprabhām || prasupubhujagākārāṃ sārddhatrivalayānvitām | sudhādhāratrikoṇe tāṃ bhāvayet surasundarīm ||
> In the lotus of the mūlādhāra, the lovely triangle; Kāma moving in its middle, like ten million rising suns; above it, the serpent-power (kuṇḍalīśakti) of the brilliance of ten million lightnings, of the form of a sleeping serpent, with the three-and-a-half coils; in the nectar-holding triangle one should contemplate her, O lovely one.

**6/61-6/67** (the yonimudrā): raising the power [upward] step by step, in the stations; in the moon above it, in the vodhinī [channel?], in the nāda, at the end of the nāda, in the śakti — the mantra-letters strung in the consciousness-power are to be contemplated; showing the self's being in the supreme sky, nourished by the supreme nectar, without pūjā or homa — the mantrin-yogī, free of all sin, sees the desired goddess. In six months all siddhi; the eighteen great siddhis at the year's end. **He is Śaṅkara himself, he is the imperishable Viṣṇu, he is the supreme Brahmā, the supreme deity —**

**6/67** — iti te kathitā devi yonimudrā maheśvari | vatsareṇa labhet siddhiṃ pūrṇakāmaphalaṃ labhet ||
> **Thus, O Devī, the yoni-mudrā has been told to you, O great Īśvarī: within a year he attains siddhi, he gains the fruit of fulfilled desire.**

Notes: paṭala 6 = the full circle: the yonimudrā promised at 1.12 (mantrārtha/mantracaitanya/yonimudrā) is delivered here as the cakra-by-cakra deity-yoga ending in the kuṇḍalinī-ascent. The Dākṣiṇā-Kālikā visualization (the corpse, the sword, the severed heads) is the classic Kālīkula form — [P: cf. the Dyczkowski MBT volumes' treatment of the Kaula goddess-cults; the "śivā" (the howling Śiva-women) of 6/14, 6/53; the kula-akula ascent of 6/57 (the kula/akula pair of the Akulavīra tradition — our glossary's Bagchi gloss confirmed in practice); the trikoṇa (triangle = the yoni of the Ciñcinī note) — the yonimudrā IS the triangle-plus-serpent ascent.]
