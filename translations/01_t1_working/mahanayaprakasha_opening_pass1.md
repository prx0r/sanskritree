## The maṅgala — the goddess-energies

**1** — divyā visrambhabhūḥ śāntā daśākhaṇḍāvabhāsasūḥ | sā śivabrahmaśabdodyaccidoghā bhāsatāṃ satām ||
> May the divine [goddess], the ground of trust, the tranquil, she who gives rise to the illumination of the ten divisions (daśākhaṇḍa-avabhāsa) — she whose consciousness-flood surges up at the words "Śiva" and "Brahmā" — shine for the good.
> *[The report's Krama-arithmetic: the "ten divisions" are the Krama's daśa-kālikā (the ten Kālīs of cognition) — the report's Kālī-series. The concordance can verify the daśākhaṇḍa-term.]*

**2** — caturaṇḍyāḥ ṣaḍadhvīṃ yā jāṭharīkṛtya mūrchati | tulyaprapañcanirvāṇā sāvyāt kulakṛśodarī ||
> May the slender-waisted lady of the kula protect [us] — she who, having swallowed the six courses of the four eggs (caturaṇḍā), is absorbed; she whose world-emanations are equal in their dissolution.

**3** — raudrī śaktir jīyād vibaddhabhāvā ghanaṃ virāḍvargam | rucitairvividhopāyair maheśahṛdaye niyojayitum ||
> May the Raudrī-power triumph — she who binds the dense host of the virāṭ-beings in their states, to yoke them in the heart of Maheśa through various pleasing means.

**4** — pīṭhe 'sminn uḍḍiyāne paramaparamalaṃ jñānam asty ugra-caryā prākpratyakpīṭhayugme 'py akulakulamahonāmake śāktamiśram | nānaivaṃ pīṭhatejo vyatiṣajati mitho 'yaṃ tu nityaṃ mahārthaḥ sphurjaddhāmeti pīṭhaprasṛmaramahasā sādhirājyena jīyāt ||
> In this seat, the Uḍḍiyāna — the knowledge, the supreme of the supreme, is there: the fierce-conduct; in the pair of the front-and-back seats, too, the mixed-Śākta named the Akula-Kula-great. Not thus does the seat's fire mingle the two mutually; rather this great-meaning, its brilliance flashing, with the ever-outpouring majesty of the seats' light, crowned with the rule — may it triumph.

**5** — saṃkhyātavarṇapadakamantrādhvānam avasyatā | mayā tadvācyasarvasvaṃ vakṣyate 'tra vicintyatām ||
> The counted phoneme-word-mantra-path, necessarily — by me the whole of what is to be expressed by that will be declared here; let it be considered.

## The granthārambha (the text's own beginning, after the maṅgala)

**1** — yo 'lakṣi divyaughamahāpravāhāc chabdārthavisphūrjathur ādidevyāḥ | mayīyakallolakalānidānaṃ svarūpaviśrāntiphalaḥ sa no 'stu ||
> May he be for us the fruit-of-rest-in-own-nature — he who was perceived from the great current of the divine flood of the first-goddess, the flashing-forth of word and meaning, the source of the māyā-wave's kalās.
> *[The commentary (interleaved): the verse glosses the four-speech (parā/paśyantī/madhyamā/vaikharī) — the divine flood = the parā-vāk; the word-meaning-flash = the madhyamā-vāk; the māyā-wave = the vaikharī. The report's visarga/phonemic dossier in the Krama's form.]*

**2** — parāparaṃ darśayantī śūnyāśūnyaṃ nijaṃ vapuḥ | jīyāc chrīmaṅgalotkīrṇacitrapatralatāyitam ||
> May she triumph who shows the supreme-and-non-supreme, [her] own body the void-and-non-void, [adorned] like the painted leaf-and-vine engraved with the blessed auspicious [marks].
> *[The commentary: śūnyāśūnya = the pure consciousness-form, not the mere absence — the report's śūnya-dossier in the Krama's register.]*
