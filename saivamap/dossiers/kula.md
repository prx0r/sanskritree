| Tradition | Sense | Evidence |

## The key passages (concordance-verified)

**Kaulajñānanirṇaya (Kaula)** — the lineage-body:
- `KJN 3/9` — sarvavyāpī nirañjanaḥ — the pervasive lotus above the cakra-arithmetic (the kula's transcendent terminus; via the glossary)

**Kubjikāmata (Kubjikā)** — the akula/kula cosmology:
- `KMT 1.1` — saṃvartāmaṇḍalānte... akulakulagataṃ pañcakaṃ — "at the end of the saṃvarta-maṇḍala... the five gone into akula-and-kula" (our T1: the compressed cosmology — the report's flagged verse)
- `KMT 17.77–82` — the mantra-deha that is kulātmaka (via the report's glossary)

**Kaularahasya / Kulapradīpa (Kaula)** — the lineage-sense in the dīkṣā-theology:
- `KRH 1.17` (via our T1) — sākṣāt paraśivaṃ karṇāt karṇagataṃ kulam — "the kula, gone from ear to ear, is directly the supreme Śiva"
- `KPD 1-16` (via our T1) — the path-hierarchy ending in "kaulāt parataraṃ na hi"

**Tantrāloka (Trika)** — the akula-pole:
- `TĀ 3.143` (via the report) — the supreme anuttara-abode is akula; visarga is its kaulikī śakti

## The working translation-policy (from the reference map)

- Retain **"Kula"** when the whole technical system is activated.
- "family/lineage" when the concrete Yoginī-classification is dominant (the early sense).
- "aggregate/body/complex" only where the passage itself supports it (Kubjikā's mantra-body).
- **Never** auto-translate as "family" — the reference map's signature warning.
