# akula · śakti · parāmarśa · svātantrya — the powers

**Counts (corpus-representation, not influence):** akula 4,924 · 373 · śakti 39,310 · 477 · parāmarśa 2,116 · 117 · svātantrya 2,530 · 169

## akula — अकुल — the transcendent pole

| Tradition | Sense | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Abhinava/Trika | the supreme/transcendent pole, explicitly identified with anuttara | TĀ 3.143; the report: "best understood relationally with kula" |

**Key passages (concordance-verified):**
- `KMT 1.1` — akulakulagataṃ pañcakaṃ — "the five gone into akula-and-kula" (our KMT T1: the compressed cosmology — the pair in the maṅgala)
- `TĀ 3.143` (via the report) — the supreme anuttara-abode is akula; its visarga is the kaulikī śakti

**Policy:** **akula** transliterated with "the transcendent pole" gloss; always relational with kula — never "not-family."

## śakti — शक्ति — the power

| Tradition | Sense | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Spanda | power/dynamism constitutive of manifestation and cognition | SPK 1, 18–19 |
| Kubjikā | goddess/mantric power, phonemically articulated | KMT 1.71–81; 2.1; 4.110 |
| Abhinava/Trika | freedom/power of consciousness, differentiated and reintegrated | TĀ 1.5; 3.143–44; 33.20–29 |

**Key passages (concordance-verified):**
- `TĀ 1.5` — svātantryaśaktiḥ kramasaṃsisṛkṣā... tribhir vibhūtiḥ — "the power of freedom, the will-to-create in order... the majesty" (our T1: the powers as the Lord's vibhūti)
- `KMT 1.71` — rudraśaktiḥ svayambhunā nirmitā — the self-born Rudraśakti (our KMT T1)
- `ŚS 1.13` — icchāśaktir umā kumārī — "the power of will is Umā the maiden" (the glossary)
- `MNP` — raudrī śaktir jīyād vibaddhabhāvā — "may the Raudrī-power triumph, binding the host of virāṭ-beings" (our Mahānayaprakāśa T1)

**Policy:** **śakti = "power"** — never bare "energy" (the report's signature warning: "a single English 'energy' seriously impoverishes it"). Translate by context: the goddess, the causal power, the freedom — the report's śakti-dossier.

## parāmarśa — परामर्श — the apprehension

| Tradition | Sense | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Pratyabhijñā/Trika | apprehension/self-apprehension, the act by which experience is gathered into unified awareness | TĀ 33.20–29; the report: "related to but not always interchangeable with vimarśa" |

**Key passages (concordance-verified):**
- `TĀ 33.20–29` (via markguidance) — parāmarśa as the gathering-act
- `APS` — the reflexive-knower's apprehension (our APS T3's frame)

**Policy:** "apprehension" — distinct from vimarśa (the gathering vs the self-grasping); keep the pair's difference.

## svātantrya — स्वातन्त्र्य — the inherent freedom

| Tradition | Sense | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Spanda/Pratyabhijñā/Trika | the inherent autonomy/freedom of conscious agency | SPK 7; TĀ 1.5; the report: "'free will' is too narrow — ontological efficacy as well as volition" |

**Key passages (concordance-verified):**
- `TĀ 1.5` — svātantryaśaktiḥ... vibhor vibhūtiḥ — "the power of freedom... the Lord's majesty" (our T1: the first power)
- `TĀ 1` (M00092:54) — śuddhasvātantryaśaktirūpā — "of the form of the pure-freedom-power" (the report's register)
- `TĀ 1` (M00092:59) — svasvātantryamāhātmyāt abhāsamātrasāratayā — "by the greatness of its own freedom, [the world] as the essence of mere appearance" (the freedom's world-production)
- `SPK 7` (via the report) — the svātantrya of the pulse

**Policy:** **svātantrya = "the inherent freedom"** — ontological efficacy, not mere free-will; the report's warning.
