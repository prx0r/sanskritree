# saṃvit — सम्वित् — the cognition / conscious awareness

**Counts:** 2,656 occurrences · 206 texts (corpus-representation, not influence)

## The sense-trajectory (from the reference map)

| Tradition | Sense | Evidence |
|---|---|---|

## The key passages (concordance-verified)

**Mahānayaprakāśa (Krama)** — the cognition's outflow:
- `M00033:509` — saṃvit bhāsate, tathā cāgamaḥ — "the cognition shines; and so the āgama" (the cognition's self-manifestation)
- `M00033:527–528` — sūkṣme 'pi saṃvitprasare... bhūmikātrayasaṃbhavaḥ, sthāne ca saṃvitprasaraḥ — "in the subtle outflow of cognition... the arising of the three levels; and in [its] place, the cognition's outflow" (the saṃvit-prasara — the Krama's sequence-engine)
- `M00033:598` — nanu kimiyam ekaiva saṃvit, uta anubhūtiviśeṣāt pṛthak pṛthak — "is this cognition one only, or separate by the difference of the experiences?" (the Krama's own one-vs-many question — the report's A/B/C problem in the Krama's voice)

**Mahārthamañjarī (Krama)** — the saṃvid-yoginīs:
- `m.11` — mama saṃvidyoginīnāṃ prasādaḥ — "the grace of my awareness-yoginīs" (the cognition's yoginī-powers)
- `colophon` — mahārthasaṃvidaṃ... deshiko vijayate — "the teacher triumphs in the great-meaning cognition" (the text's own title-term)

## The working translation-policy

- **saṃvit = "cognition" / "awareness"** — the conscious awareness as such.
- In the **Krama**, prefer "cognition" with the sequence-force: the saṃvit-krama is the cognition-as-sequence, the engine of the Kālīs-as-moments.
- In **Pratyabhijñā/Trika**, "awareness" as the self-luminous ground (the report's A-thesis).
- Never "consciousness" when the Krama's dynamic sequence is active (that flattens the saṃvit-krama into a static witness — the report's process-vs-witness warning).
