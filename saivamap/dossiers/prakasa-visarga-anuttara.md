# prakāśa · visarga · anuttara — the Trika's cosmological triad

**Counts (corpus-representation, not influence):** prakāśa 13,660 · 409 texts · visarga 2,014 · 231 · anuttara 1,310 · 143

## prakāśa — प्रकाश — the manifest luminosity

| Tradition | Sense | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Trika/Pratyabhijñā | manifest luminosity; awareness as that in virtue of which things appear | TĀ 3.133 etc.; the report: "'light' is metaphorically suggestive but can mislead if treated as physical luminosity" |

**Key passages (concordance-verified):**
- `TĀ 1.1` (via the M00092 commentary) — hṛdayam anuttarāmṛtakulaṃ — the heart as the unsurpassed-nectar-kula (the prakāśa's seat; our T1)
- `Mahārthamañjarī g.2` — vardhatāṃ mahāprakāśo vimarshavicchuritaniścalodyotaḥ — "may the great-prakāśa flourish, the unmoving upsurge suffused with reflexion" (the engine-verse; our T3: the prakāśa/vimarśa pair)
- `MNP` (the Mahānayaprakāśa's title-word) — the illumination of the Great Way

**Policy:** "manifest luminosity" / transliterate **prakāśa** in the technical pair; never physical "light."

## visarga — विसर्ग — the emission

| Tradition | Sense | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Trika/Kaula | emission, release, manifestation — the power by which the supreme projects itself | TĀ 3.141–46; the report: "carries phonemic, cosmological and in some contexts erotic/ritual resonances" |

**Key passages (concordance-verified):**
- `TĀ 1.1` — tadubhayayāmalasphuritabhāvavisargamayaṃ hṛdayam — "the heart made of the emission of the being flashing in the yāmala of the two" (our T1's maṅgala — the report's flagged kula/visarga cosmology)
- `TĀ 1` (M00092:76) — visargaprasarasvabhāvatvena jagadvaicitryabījabhūtaṃ — "by the nature of the visarga's outflow, the seed of the world's variegation" (the emission as the world's seed)
- `TĀ 1` (M00092:81) — tadāsya paramaṃ vaktraṃ visargaprasarāspadam — "then its supreme mouth is the ground of the visarga's outflow"

**Policy:** "emission" as the literal anchor; tag the register separately (phonemic / cosmogenic / Kaula-power / erotic) — the report's visarga-dossier's six-way tag.

## anuttara — अनुत्तर — the Unsurpassed

| Tradition | Sense | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Mature Trika | the unsurpassed/ultimate; the concentrated Parā/Ekavīrā and the supreme state | TĀ 1.1, 1.5; 3.143; the report: "not merely the adjective 'highest' — a highly loaded technical designation" |

**Key passages (concordance-verified):**
- `TĀ 1.1` — hṛdayam anuttarāmṛtakulaṃ — "the heart, the unsurpassed-nectar-kula" (our T1: the maṅgala's ground)
- `TĀ 1.5` — anuttaraṃ me prathayatsvarūpam — "let the Unsurpassed manifest my own nature" (our T1: the ground of the three powers)
- `TĀ 1` (M00092:42) — "anuttarāmṛtakulam" iti... — the commentary's own gloss on the term

**Policy:** **Anuttara / the Unsurpassed** (capitalized, the technical designation) — never merely "highest"; the report's loaded-term warning.
