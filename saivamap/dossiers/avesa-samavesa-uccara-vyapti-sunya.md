# āveśa · samāveśa · uccāra · vyāpti · śūnya — the possession, articulation, pervasion, void

**Counts (corpus-representation, not influence):** āveśa 5,434 · 366 · samāveśa 760 · 113 · uccāra 1,900 · 275 · vyāpti 2,421 · 226 · śūnya 6,274 · 384

## āveśa / samāveśa — आवेश / समावेश — the entry / possession

| Tradition | Sense | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Trika | the entry into / possession by the divine — the samāveśa-state | TĀ (the uccāra/āveśa complex); the report's āveśa-dossier |

**Key passages (concordance-verified):**
- `TĀ 1` (M00092:32) — samāveśadaśollāsena — "by the surge of the state of entry" (the possession as the practice's goal)
- `TĀ 1` (M00092:172) — śivaśaktisamāveśamayatvābhidhānasyeṣṭeḥ — "the designation of the entry-into-Śiva-Śakti being intended" (the divine entry)
- `TĀ 1` (M00092:322) — parameśvarasamāveśaśālitvāt — "from possessing the entry into the supreme Lord" (the state's ground)

**Policy:** "entry" (āveśa) / "the [supreme] entry" (samāveśa) — the state of possession-by-the-divine; the report's āveśa-dossier.

## uccāra — उच्चार — the articulation

| Tradition | Sense | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Trika | the mantra's articulation/emission — the inner sound-rise | TĀ 15's uccāra-system |

**Key passages (concordance-verified):**
- `TĀ` (M00092:2473) — uccārādiḥ arthaḥ tatsādhanam — "the uccāra-and-the-rest, the object, its means" (the articulation-complex)
- `TĀ` (M00092:2611) — uccārādyāḥ... bāhyāḥ kuṇḍamaṇḍalādayaḥ — "the uccāra-and-the-rest are internal; the external are the kuṇḍa, the maṇḍala and the rest" (the internal articulation vs the external rites)

**Policy:** **uccāra = "the articulation"** (the mantra's inner rise); the report's phonemic dossier.

## vyāpti — व्याप्ति — the pervasion

| Tradition | Sense | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Trika | pervasion — the all-pervading nature | the TĀ's vyāpti-language |
| Nyāya (Hop 10) | the pervasion-relation (the anumāna's vyāpti) | the Tarkasaṃgraha (our Hop-10 entry) |

**Key passages (concordance-verified):**
- `TĀ` — the vyāpti of the Lord (the pervasion as the ontological all-ness)
- `Tarkasaṃgraha` — vyāpti as the inference's pervasion-relation (our T1: "vyāpti/pakṣadharmatā")

**Policy:** "pervasion" — ontological in the Trika (the Lord's all-ness), logical in the Nyāya (the inference's relation); the cross-tradition fork noted.

## śūnya — शून्य — the void

| Tradition | Sense | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Trika/Krama | the void as the apophatic terminus — NOT mere absence | the report's śūnya-dossier; the Mahānayaprakāśa's śūnyāśūnya |

**Key passages (concordance-verified):**
- `MNP` — śūnyāśūnyaṃ nijaṃ vapuḥ — "her own body the void-and-non-void" (our T1: the commentary: "the pure consciousness-form, not the mere absence")
- `JKK 3/13` — śūnyaṃ niranñjanaṃ jñātvā — "knowing the void, the Stainless" (our T3: the apophatic terminus — the glossary's nirañjana-link)

**Policy:** **śūnya = "the void"** with the apophatic force — never mere "emptiness-as-absence" where the pure-consciousness-form is meant (the report's warning; the MNP's own gloss).
