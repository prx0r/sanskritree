| Tradition | Sense | Evidence |
|---|---|---|

## The key passages (concordance-verified)

**Tantrāloka 1.5 (Trika)** — the report's flagged cluster:
- `AbhT_1.5` — svātantryaśaktiḥ kramasaṃsisṛkṣā kramātmatā ceti vibhor vibhūtiḥ — "the power of freedom, the will-to-create in order (krama-saṃsisṛkṣā), and the order-nature (kramātmatā) — this is the Lord's majesty" (our T1: krama beside svātantrya — the report's warning: do not infer the Krama-school from the noun alone)

**Kubjikāmata 1.1 (Kubjikā)** — the krama-pada:
- `KMT 1.1` — kramapadanihitānandaśaktiḥ — "the bliss-power placed in the step-of-krama" (our T1: the krama as the maṇḍala's order; the report: "KMT freely uses krama procedurally AND technically; capitalize 'Krama' only when the lineage is demonstrable")

**Mahānayaprakāśa (Krama)** — the school's architecture:
- `M00033` (the daśākhaṇḍa) — the ten Kālīs as the sequence of cognition (our T1: the Krama's defining move)
- `M00033:598` — kimiyam ekaiva saṃvit... — the one-vs-many cognition question (the sequence's self-examination)

**Jñānakārikā / Kulapradīpa (Kaula)** — the generic sense:
- `JKK 2.22` — siddhakramavivarjitam — "devoid of the siddha-order" (our T3: the graded path, NOT the Krama-school — the report's rule applied)
- `KPD 1-22` — the graded order (the kula-manual's procedural sense)

## The working translation-policy (from the reference map)

- **sequence / succession / ordered process** — the default.
- **"Krama" capitalized ONLY when the sectarian identity is demonstrable** — the report's signature rule.
- In the **Krama-school texts**: sequence is simultaneously ritual and phenomenological (the saṃvit-krama).
- In **Abhinava**: often generic; carry the inherited resonance only where the text supports it.
