## saṃhāra / sṛṣṭi — संहार / सृष्टि — retraction / creation

| Tradition | Sense | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Spanda/Trika/Kubjikā | retraction-withdrawal vs creation-emission; the cosmic pair | SPK 6; TĀ 32.46, 59; the report: "context ranges from cosmic process to ritual/internalized operation" |

**Key passages (concordance-verified):**
- `SPK 1.1` (via the anchor) — yasyonmeṣanimeṣābhyāṃ jagataḥ pralayodayau — "by whose opening and closing the universe is destroyed and created" (the sṛṣṭi/saṃhāra as the Lord's blink)
- `SPK 1.6` — sahāntareṇa cakreṇa pravṛttisthitisaṃhṛtīḥ — "by the inner wheel: creation, maintenance, dissolution" (our R1's restored verse)
- `TĀ 1` (M00092:107) — sṛṣṭisthityupasaṃhārarūpā — "of the form of creation, maintenance, withdrawal" (the triad)

**Policy:** "creation / retraction" (sṛṣṭi/saṃhāra) — the cosmic pair; "withdrawal" or "dissolution" for saṃhāra by context (process vs rite).

## cakra — चक्र — the wheel

| Tradition | Sense | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Trika/Spanda | the wheel of powers — the aggregate of the energies | SPK 1; the ŚS 2.7's mātṛkā-cakra |
| Kaula | the ritual circle (the cakra-rite) | the Kaularahasya's Bhairavī-cakra |

**Key passages (concordance-verified):**
- `SPK 1.1` — śakticakravibhavaprabhavaṃ — "the source of the Wheel of Energies" (our R1: the anchor's "Energies," capitalized)
- `ŚS 2.7` — mātṛkācakrasaṃbodhaḥ — "the awakening of the Mātṛkā-wheel" (the phonemic aggregate)
- `KRH` (our T1) — the Bhairavī-cakra: pravṛtte bhairavīcakre sarve varṇā dvijātayaḥ — "when the Bhairavī-cakra is begun, all the varṇas are twice-born" (the ritual circle's equality)

**Policy:** "wheel" for the power-aggregate (Spanda/Trika), "cakra [the rite]" for the ritual circle (Kaula) — the register-fork noted.
