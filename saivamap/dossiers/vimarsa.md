## The key passages (concordance-verified)

**The A-thesis's engine (the report's flagged core):**
- `ĪPK 1.5.11–14` (via markguidance) — vimarśa as the self-nature of manifestation; without it, manifestation is "crystal-like, hence inert" (the report's A-thesis statement)
- `Tantrāloka 33` (via markguidance) — parāmarśa as the gathering of experience into unified awareness (the report's parāmarśa-dossier: "related to but not always interchangeable with vimarśa")

- `m.5` — tatsvātmacitkramavimarśamayaṃ gurūṇām avalliyugmam — "that pair of the gurus' feet, made of the self's consciousness-order-reflexion" (our T3's Krama-perspective: the guru-as-krama, the reflexion that IS the sequence)
- `m.10` — vimarshadashāmukhānīta... (the R2's g.2 engine-verse) — "the unmoving upsurge suffused with reflexion (vimarsha-vicchurita)" — the report's prakāśa/vimarśa pair in the Krama's own voice

## The working translation-policy

- **vimarśa = "reflexive apprehension"** — NEVER merely "reflection" (the report's explicit warning: that falsely implies discursive thought).
- **parāmarśa = "apprehension"** — the gathering-act, related to but distinct from vimarśa.
- In the **Krama**: the reflexion-that-is-the-sequence (vimarśa as the saṃvit-krama's self-grasping).
- In **Pratyabhijñā**: the A-thesis's reflexion-pole — manifestation without vimarśa is inert (the crystal-image).
