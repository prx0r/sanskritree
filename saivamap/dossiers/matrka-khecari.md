# mātṛkā · khecarī — the phonemic and the sky-moving

**Counts (corpus-representation, not influence):** mātṛkā 5,756 · 295 texts · khecarī 1,121 · 195

## mātṛkā — मातृका — the Matrix (the alphabet as the goddess)

| Tradition | Sense | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Mantric Śaiva-Śākta | the Mother/phonemic matrix — the power embodied in the letters | KMT 4.110: Śakti identified with Mātṛkā, Mātṛkā as Śiva-natured; the report: "an obvious candidate for a cross-text phonemic dossier" |

**Key passages (concordance-verified):**
- `ŚS 1.4` — jñānādhiṣṭhānaṃ mātṛkā — "the ground of knowledge is Mātṛkā" (the glossary's attestation)
- `ŚS 2.7` — mātṛkācakrasaṃbodhaḥ — "the awakening of the Mātṛkā-wheel" (the matrix as the kalā-aggregate; the glossary)
- `KMT` (the mālinī-sequence) — the varṇas as the goddess's body (our KMT T1)

**Policy:** **Mātṛkā** (capitalized, transliterated) — the power of action as the alphabet-matrix; never "alphabet" — the goddess-load is essential.

## khecarī — खेचरी — the sky-moving

| Tradition | Sense | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Kubjikā | the goddess/power of the "sky/space," the mantra-body, Mālinī's structured divine body | KMT 17.77–82; the report: "not reducible to the later Haṭhayogic tongue gesture" |
| Trika (Abhinava's synthesis) | mudrā, internal condition/power, movement through space, creation/retraction, possession by the goddess | TĀ 32.31–65; the report: "an excellent test case for polysemy within one chapter" |

**Key passages (concordance-verified):**
- `KMT` — the sixteen-part goddess, Mālinī, mantra-deha, kulātmaka body (the report's note; our KMT frame)
- `zrīvidyā` corpus (via the concordance, 44 hits) — khecarī in the mātṛkā-sequences: "khecarī śaktiratulā vyomāmbā vyomarūpiṇī" (the sky-formed power) — the goddess-lists
- `TĀ 32` (the report's polysemy-test) — mudrā + inner state + cosmic power

**Policy:** **khecarī** transliterated (the goddess/power-name); gloss "sky-moving" at first occurrence; never reduce to the haṭha-yoga tongue-gesture — the report's warning.
