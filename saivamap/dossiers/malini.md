# mālinī — मालिनी — the phonemic goddess

## The key passages (concordance-verified)

**Kubjikāmata paṭala 1 (Kubjikā)** — the self-born Rudraśakti:
- `KMT 1.71` — rudraśaktiḥ svayambhunā nirmitā svāṅgajair varṇair nādiphāntasvarūpiṇī — "the Rudraśakti, fashioned by the Self-born from his own limbs' phonemes, of the nature of the nāda-and-phānta" (our T1: the phonemic emergence)
- `KMT 1.73` — vadate mālinī kas tvaṃ... — "Mālinī speaks: who are you..." (the goddess as interlocutor — the report's "at once deity, alphabet, causal power")
- `KMT 1.75` — varṇarāśir ahaṃ bhadre — "I am the mass of phonemes" (the self-identification)
- `KMT 1.76` — vīrāvalīti tena tvaṃ rudraśaktiḥ praśasyase — "therefore you are praised as the Vīrāvalī, the Rudraśakti"

**The report's note (KMT 1.71–81):** "Mālinī can simultaneously be Rudra's Śakti, the mass of phonemes, the origin of mantras, a goddess who speaks to Bhairava, a form of knowledge-power, and a power associated with desire and action. Those are not adequately described by choosing one English equivalent for śakti."

## The working translation-policy

- **Mālinī = the phonemic goddess** — kept as the proper name (the deity-alphabet-mantra-ontology overlap).
- Never flatten to "the alphabet" — the goddess-load is essential.
- "Vīrāvalī" and "Rudraśakti" as her epithets (the report's goddess-as-power register).
