# Kubjikātantra Paṭala 1, Verses 1.9–1.32 — Pass 1 Working Translation

*Hop 6-branch (Kālīkula), batch pass 1 — same context as the 1.1–1.8 v2 treatment (`kubjika_tantra_v2_three_readings_with_trails.md`).*
*Text: Chatterjee ed. (late 19th c., Bengali characters) via Muktabodha M00030 · Anonymous, late medieval (≈15th–17th c.?) Kālīkula compilation in Kubjikā lineage dress · No English anchor exists — this is first translation; provenance and dating arguments in the v2 file.*

## The transmission frame (1.9–1.14)

**1.9** — na prakāśyaṃ na prakāśyaṃ na prakāśyaṃ kadācana |
idantu paramaṃ guhyaṃ triṣu lokeṣu durlabham ||
> Not to be revealed, not to be revealed, never at any time to be revealed — this is the supreme secret, hard to obtain in the three worlds.

**1.10** — yajjñātvā ca mahādevi yogīśo'si sadāśiva |
janmāntarasahasreṇa kathituṃ naiva śakyate ||
> Knowing which, O great goddess, you become the Lord of Yogins, Sadāśiva — [yet] it cannot be told even through a thousand other lives.

*Notes: the triple negation (triple = absolute, cf. 1.8's triple oath) + the conditional frame "if you know this you become Sadāśiva" — the vidyā as the identity-granting secret. [X: "yogīśo'si" — "you become the Lord of Yogins" — address to Pārvatī as the archetypal sādhaka.]*

**1.11** — tava snehātmayā devi kathayiṣyāmi tat śṛṇu |
gopitavyaṃ gopitavyaṃ gopitavyaṃ tvayā punaḥ ||
> Out of my love, whose essence is you, O Devī, I will tell it — hear: it must be guarded, guarded, guarded by you again.

**1.12** — na prakāśyaṃ mahādevi paśoragre kadācana |
mantrārthaṃ mantracaitanyaṃ yonimudrāṃ na vetti yaḥ ||
> Never to be revealed, O great goddess, before the bound soul (paśu) at any time. Whoever does not know the mantra's meaning (mantrārtha), the mantra's consciousness (mantracaitanya), the yoni-mudrā —

**1.13** — na siddhyati varārohe kallakoṭiśatairapi |
kathayāmi mahāmantraṃ mantrārthatvaṃ sūreśvari ||
> — does not attain perfection even by hundreds of crores of kalpas. I will tell the great mantra, the being-of-the-mantra's-meaning (mantrārthatva), O Sūreśvarī.

*Notes: the three unknowables (mantrārtha, mantracaitanya, yonimudrā) — meaning, consciousness, and gesture of the mantra — are the siddhi-conditions; the paśu (bound one) cannot see it. "mantrārthatva" — the abstract: what it is *for a mantra to have meaning* — i.e., the vidyā-ontology itself. [X: "sūreśvarī" — "Lady of the Heroes/Sun" — epithet of the goddess; cf. the Vīreśa terminology in the Śivasūtra's Vārttika — same Kaula vocabulary.]*

**1.14** — yena vijñānamātreṇa sarvasiddhīśvarobhavet |
kevalaṃ bhāvaśuddhyā ca mantrārthaṃ prāṇavallabhe ||
> By which, through mere understanding (vijñānamātra), one becomes the lord of all powers (sarvasiddhīśvara) — only by the purity of one's being (bhāvaśuddhi) is the mantra's meaning [attained], O beloved of my life.

*Notes: vijñānamātra + bhāvaśuddhi — knowing + purity of being, the two conditions; "kevalam" (only) stresses bhāvaśuddhi as the operative one. This is the jñāna-mātra doctrine of 1.15 and 1.20 (jīvanmukti by hearing) — the vidyā-śravaṇa complex.*

## The vidyā revealed (1.15–1.20)

**1.15** — kevalaṃ jñānamātreṇa jīvanmukto dhruvaṃ naraḥ |
kālī tārā mahādevī bhuvaneśī ca ṣoḍaśī ||
> By mere knowledge alone a man is surely liberated-while-living (jīvanmukta): Kālī, Tārā, Mahādevī, Bhuvaneśī, and Ṣoḍaśī —

**1.16** — bhairavī cchinnamastā ca vidyā dhūmāvatī priye |
vagalā caiva mātaṅgī kamalā ca prakīrtitā ||
> — Bhairavī, Chinnamastā, and the vidyā Dhūmāvatī, O beloved, Vāgalā, Mātaṅgī, and Kamalā are proclaimed.

*Notes: the Daśamahāvidyā list in canonical order (see v2 file: this list is the late-medieval consolidation; "vagalā" for bagalā = Bengali transmission [X]). The ten vidyās *are* the answer to Pārvatī's question — the "essence of the Vedas" is a goddess-list.*

**1.17** — brahma-viṣṇu-maheśādyaiś caturvedair namaskṛtā |
bhogadā mokṣā devī koṭibrahmāṇḍagopitā ||
> Worshipped by Brahmā, Viṣṇu, Maheśa and the rest, by the four Vedas — the goddess who gives enjoyment and liberation, hidden in ten million egg-universes.

**1.18** — caturvargapradā devī nirvāṇapadadāyinī |
devadevaprāṇamayī sarvadevasvarūpiṇī ||
> The goddess who grants the four aims (dharma, artha, kāma, mokṣa), who grants the state of nirvāṇa, who is the life-breath of the god of gods, whose form is all the gods.

**1.19** — brahmaviṣṇumaheśānāṃ dhyānagamyā jaganmayī |
sṛṣṭisthitikarī devī viṣṇurūpā maheśvarī ||
> Attainable in meditation by Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Maheśa — she who is all worlds, who performs creation and persistence, Viṣṇu-formed, the Great Īśvarī.

**1.20** — maṇimaṇḍapasaṃsthā ca mahākālavināśinī |
asyāḥ śravaṇamātreṇa jīvanmuktaś ca sādhakaḥ ||
> Established in the jewel-pavilion (maṇimaṇḍapa), the destroyer of the Great Death (mahākāla) — by the mere hearing of her, the sādhaka is liberated-while-living.

*Notes: the maṇimaṇḍapa (jewel-pavilion) = the goddess's throne/mandala (cf. the Kaulajñānanirṇaya's lotus/maṇḍala doctrine we translate in the Kaula file); mahākāla = the Great Time/Death — her destroying even him; śravaṇamātra (mere hearing) = the doctrine of 1.14-15 fulfilled.*

## The Kali-yuga claims (1.21–1.24)

**1.21** — ke gotre na samudbhūtāḥ kālīṃ sādhitmudyatāḥ |
tat śrutvā pitaraḥ sarve nṛtyanti hṛṣṭamānasāḥ ||
> Whatever clans (gotra) have not arisen [in?] — those intent on accomplishing Kālī: hearing this, all the ancestors dance with rejoicing minds.

*[X: "ke gotre na samudbhūtāḥ" is garbled in the printed edition — likely "ye gotre na samudbhūtāḥ" or "kegotrāḥ..."; sense: the ancestors of even unworthy clans are liberated by the hearing — a hyperbolic siddhi-claim. Marked for collation.]*

**1.22** — nṛtyanti kinnarāḥ sarve gāthāṃ gāyanti sādhakāḥ |
adeyāpi mahāvidyā kalau pūrṇaphalapradā ||
> All the kinnaras dance; the sādhakas sing the song — even without formal initiation (adeyā api), the great vidyā gives full fruit in the Kali age.

*Notes: "adeyā" — "not-to-be-given" — i.e., the vidyā bestows fruit even where the formal transmission is impossible (Kali-age concession); cf. 1.30's sampradāya-emphasis — the tension is structural in late Kaula texts.*

**1.23** — śrutismṛtividhānena pūjā kāryā yugatraye |
āgamoktavidhānena kalau devān yajet sudhīḥ ||
> By the rule of śruti and smṛti worship is to be performed in the three yugas; in the Kali age the wise man should worship the gods by the rule taught in the āgamas.

**1.24** — na hi devāḥ prasīdanti kalau cānyavidhānataḥ |
kālikā mokṣadā devi kalau śīghraphalapradā ||
> For the gods are not pleased in Kali by any other rule — Kālikā, O Devī, gives liberation in the Kali age, granting swift fruit.

*Notes: the yuga-theology: āgama replaces śruti/smṛti in Kali; Kālikā as the Kali-appropriate goddess — the standard late-Kālīkula apologetic (cf. 1.22).*

## The consorts' supremacy (1.25–1.31)

**1.25** — gurormukhānmahāvidyāṃ gṛhṇiyāt pāpanāśinīm |
brahmāṇī kurute sṛṣṭiṃ na tu brahmā kadācana ||
> One should receive the great vidyā, the destroyer of sin, from the guru's mouth. Brahmāṇī performs creation — never Brahmā at any time.

**1.26** — ata eva maheśāni brahmā preto na saṃśayaḥ |
pālanaṃ vaiṣṇavī devi na tu viṣṇuḥ kadācana ||
> Therefore, O great Īśānī, Brahmā is a corpse (preta) — no doubt. Vaiṣṇavī performs preservation, never Viṣṇu.

**1.27** — ata eva maheśāni viṣṇuḥ preto na saṃśayaḥ |
rudrāṇī kurute grāsaṃ na tu rudraḥ kadācana ||
> Therefore, O great Īśānī, Viṣṇu is a corpse — no doubt. Rudrāṇī performs the swallowing, never Rudra.

**1.28** — ata eva maheśāni rudraḥ preto na saṃśayaḥ |
brahmaviṣṇumaheśādyā jaḍāś caiva prakīrtitāḥ ||
> Therefore, O great Īśānī, Rudra is a corpse — no doubt. Brahmā, Viṣṇu, Maheśa and the rest are proclaimed inert (jaḍāḥ).

*Notes: the consort-supremacy doctrine: the gods are corpses (preta) — their power lives in their śaktis. The same "inert gods" theme as in our Spandakārikā (the familiar Tantric reversal: "sadāśiva... the gods are but the ornaments of the goddess"). This is the theological core of the paṭala: Śiva himself answers — through his śakti.*

**1.29** — prakṛtiñca vinā devi sarvakāryākṣamo dhruvam |
dharmādharmādikaṃ yasmāt kurute sādhakottamaḥ ||
> Without prakṛti, O Devī, [the gods are] surely incapable of any action — because the best of sādhakas performs dharma, adharma, and the rest [through her].

**1.30** — tat sarvam anugṛhṇāti vāmikā ca na saṃśayaḥ |
saṃpradāyavidhānena kartavyañca maheśvari ||
> And all that the Vāma-side (vāmikā) graciously accepts — no doubt; and it must be done by the rule of the lineage (sampradāya), O great Īśvarī.

*Notes: vāmikā/vāmā = the "Left" power — the goddess as the leftward (vāma) current — cf. the four yonis (Ambā, Jyeṣṭhā, Raudrī, Vāmā) in the Śivasūtra's Vārttika we translated: the same four-śakti family. "kurute sādhakottamaḥ" — the sādhaka does dharma/adharma [and transcends both through the goddess] — the kulācāra doctrine's justification.*

**1.31** — saṃpradāyavihīnānāṃ phalaṃ na syāt sureśvari |
yā tu kṛṣṇā saiva kṛṣṇas tathā brahmā prakīrtitaḥ ||
> For those without the lineage there is no fruit, O Sūreśvarī. Whatever is black (kṛṣṇā), that indeed is Kṛṣṇa; so too Brahmā is proclaimed [identical with his form].

*[X: "yā tu kṛṣṇā saiva kṛṣṇaḥ" — "the black one is Kṛṣṇa" — identity of the goddess Kālī (the black) with Kṛṣṇa (the dark god) — a syncretist equation; the verse's second half is corrupt in the edition — collation needed.]*

## The conclusion: bheda/abheda (1.32)

**1.32** — abhedaṃ cintayedyastu muktistasya kare sthitā |
vibhinnaṃ cintayedyastu mūḍhabuddhiḥ sa pātakī |
bhedako narakaṃ gacchet yāvadindrāś caturdaśa ||
> Whoever thinks non-difference (abheda) — liberation stands in his hand. Whoever thinks difference (vibhinna) — he is a fool, a sinner: the one who makes division goes to hell until the fourteen Indras have passed.

*Notes: the paṭala's closing doctrine — abheda (non-difference) as the soteriological criterion, bheda as the path to hell; "until the fourteen Indras pass" = until the end of the cosmic cycle. This is the classic late-Kaula abheda-thesis — same as the Kaulajñānanirṇaya's liṅga-doctrine we translate next.*
