## Why This Is Next

∂ᵢ (field boundary) IS the partition problem — the single hardest unresolved issue in the entire project. The TĀ says deha-bhedāt (body differences contract consciousness). Ratié says other subjects are reflections of one consciousness. The audit says the reflection model solves ontological compatibility but NOT phenomenal exclusion. This deepdive would synthesize everything known about how boundaries are constituted.

## Key Questions

### 1. Is the self a thing, a process, or an inference?

- Timalsina's Aham paper: three levels — empirical ego, subject of experience, absolute I-consciousness
- Depersonalization: the ego model can weaken without consciousness ending
- Meditative selflessness: ego reduction WITH preserved basal self-affection (ρᵢ↑, ownership↓)
- Depersonalization: ego reduction WITHOUT preserved basal self-affection (ρᵢ↓, ownership↓)
- The difference is not the boundary — it's the affect

### 2. What determines the boundary?

- TĀ 10:12113: dehabhedāt — body differences
- Levin: cognitive light cone — the scale of goals the system can sense, model, and act on
- Active inference: Markov blankets — statistical separation of internal from external
- Wolfram: observer quotienting — equivalence relations imposed by computational boundedness
- **Critical problem:** These are NOT the same boundary. Skin ≠ peripersonal space ≠ cognitive light cone ≠ Markov blanket ≠ phenomenal field.

### 3. Why doesn't the boundary scale up?

- If Levin's bioelectric networks can integrate cells into tissues, why isn't the tissue the subject?
- If ΦID can identify synergistic information at the group level, why isn't the group the subject?
- The Śaiva answer: deha-bheda — anusandhāna does not cross bodies. But what ABOUT cross-body integration? (E.g., pareidolia, mirror-touch synesthesia, group flow)

### 4. The body as a regime of determination

- The body is not a container but a dynamically maintained set of capacities and limitations
- It determines: what can be sensed, what can be acted upon, what must be regulated, what events belong to its history
- Formal: Bᵢ = ⟨Sᵢ, Aᵢ, Vᵢ, Γᵢ, Cᵢ⟩
- The body boundary (∂_skin) ≠ causal boundary (∂_causal) ≠ functional boundary (∂_functional) ≠ phenomenal boundary (∂_phenomenal)

### 5. The reflection model (pratibimba) — what it actually says

- Ratié's corrected reading: the behavior of another subject within MY stream is the reflection (pratibimba); the other stream is INFERRED as the original (bimba)
- This solves: "other subjects can affect my field without entering it" (Dⱼ ⊄ Dᵢ but Rⱼ→ᵢ ⊂ Dᵢ)
- This does NOT solve: "why is Dᵢ exclusive of Dⱼ?"
- The TĀ adds: this exclusivity is mediated by body differences (deha-bheda)

### 6. The central problem

The boundary can be identified at multiple levels (skin, peripersonal space, cognitive light cone, anusandhāna domain). Which one IS the phenomenal boundary? The TĀ says: the one where anusandhāna stops. But anusandhāna stops at the body — and the body is itself a nested hierarchy of boundaries (cells, tissues, organs, organism). Why does IT stop at the organism level?
