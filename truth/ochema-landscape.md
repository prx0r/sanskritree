# Ochema in the Landscape: Where We Fit and Where We Diverge

---

## The Closest Frameworks

Seven existing frameworks share significant overlap with Ochema.
Each contributes something we use; each misses something we add.

---

## 1. Dual-Aspect Monism (Spinoza → Pauli–Jung → Atmanspacher)

**Closest ontological relative.** The shared claim: one reality with two
irreducible modes of access. Atmanspacher's version is the most formally
developed — it includes the "epistemic split" that separates the underlying
unity into mental and physical aspects.

| Shared | Divergence |
|---|---|
| One reality, two aspects | Atmanspacher treats the split as epistemic; Ochema adds that the split has a specific physical implementation (bioelectric exclusion) |
| Mental and physical are equally real | Atmanspacher does not specify which physical systems instantiate the split; Ochema gives material enactment criteria |
| The underlying domain is neutral | Ochema does not commit to the nature of the underlying domain beyond what the convergence methodology recovers |

**Ochema's addition:** The epistemic split is not abstract — it is enacted
by a specific biological operation (E_i). Dual-aspect monism says *there is
a split*. Ochema says *here is how the split works and what implements it*.

## 2. Process Philosophy (Whitehead → Rescher)

**Closest metaphysical relative.** Reality is composed of processes (actual
occasions) rather than substances. Occasions of experience are the basic
units. Each occasion prehends its predecessors and contributes to its
successors. Free will is inherent.

| Shared | Divergence |
|---|---|
| Process ontology — reality is becoming, not being | Whitehead's occasions are atomic and discrete; Ochema treats the process as continuous and materially extended |
| Occasions have both physical and mental poles | Ochema shares dual-aspect but rejects panexperientialism — not every process has a phenomenal aspect |
| Creativity is the ultimate principle | Ochema replaces creativity with exclusion as the fundamental operation |
| God is a special actual entity | Ochema has no God — the framework is metaphysically minimal |

**Ochema's addition:** Material enactment. Whitehead's occasions are
abstract — any occasion of experience is the same kind of thing regardless
of substrate. Ochema says material differences change what can be enacted.

**Ochema's subtraction:** No God, no eternal objects, no panexperientialism.
Ochema is metaphysically lighter.

## 3. Neurophenomenology (Varela → Lutz → Thompson)

**Closest methodological relative.** First-person and third-person evidence
must jointly constrain theories of consciousness. The "hard problem" is
addressed through pragmatic methodological integration rather than
metaphysical speculation.

| Shared | Divergence |
|---|---|
| First-person + third-person joint constraint | Varela focused on neural correlates; Ochema targets bioelectric + multiple physical models |
| Phenomenology is data, not decoration | Ochema uses phenomenal partitions as formal constraints, not just qualitative reports |
| Embodied, enactive, situated | Ochema adds the exclusion operation as the specific mechanism of embodiment |

**Ochema's addition:** The joint constraint is formalized as a fixed-point
convergence (A_i* = Fix(𝕽, 𝕻_i, 𝓤)). Neurophenomenology says "use both kinds
of evidence." Ochema says "here is exactly how to combine them into a
bearer recovery methodology."

## 4. Free Energy Principle / Active Inference (Friston)

**Closest formal relative.** The Markov blanket partition separates internal
and external states. Free energy minimization drives self-organization.
Active inference generates actions that reduce surprise.

| Shared | Divergence |
|---|---|
| A partition separates system from environment | Friston's partition is statistical (Markov blanket); Ochema's is operational (exclusion) |
| The system acts to maintain its own organization | Shared — both models emphasize self-maintenance |
| Perception and action are coupled | Shared |
| Biological systems minimize free energy | Friston is committed to variational inference as the universal mechanism; Ochema treats this as one possible implementation |

**Ochema's addition:** Apoha as the partition operation — exclusion is not
statistical inference but constitutive determination. The subject pole is
co-emergent with the world pole, not a Bayesian posterior.

**Divergence:** Friston claims the free energy principle is a mathematical
truth that applies to all self-organizing systems. Ochema is not committed
to this universal claim — the exclusion operation is a hypothesis for
conscious systems, not a mathematical necessity for all systems.

**Key difference in scope:**
- Friston: all biological systems minimize free能量 → Markov blanket → Bayesian brain
- Ochema: some biological systems implement E_i: Ω → (P_i, W_i) → bioelectric exclusion → phenomenal subject

## 5. Information Philosophy (Doyle)

**Closest predecessor.** Information is causally efficacious. Dual-aspect
monism. Two-stage generate→select model. Adequate determinism. Three-level
ontology. Downward causation. The ERR model. Cosmic information creation.

| Shared | Divergence |
|---|---|
| Information has causal power | Doyle treats information as an immaterial substance; Ochema treats it as physically enacted distinction |
| Dual-aspect monism | Shared |
| Generate→select is universal | Shared — Levin confirms Doyle's intuition |
| Adequate determinism | Shared |
| Downward causation | Shared — Levin confirms what Doyle predicted |
| Three-level ontology | Ochema does not commit to three fixed levels; treats organizational levels as system-relative |

**The critical divergence: Substrate neutrality**

Doyle treats information as substrate-independent — mental software can run
on any hardware. This is the Information Philosophy's functionalism.

Ochema's Material Enactment Principle says: same abstract transition
diagram ≠ same materially available dynamics. The substrate matters
constitutively.

**Doyle's ERR model vs. Ochema's memory:**
- Doyle: neural Hebbian wiring + playback
- Ochema: distributed bioelectric attractors + morphogenetic targeting
  (generalized beyond neurons, confirmed by Levin)

**Doyle's two-stage vs. Ochema's two-stage:**
- Doyle: quantum randomness generates alternatives, adequate determinism selects
- Ochema: generate→select is universal in biology; source of generation can be
  deterministic chaos, active inference, or quantum randomness — not committed
  to one mechanism

## 6. Integrated Information Theory (Tononi)

**Closest competitor.** Φ (phi) measures integrated information. Consciousness
IS integrated information. A system is conscious to the degree that its
mechanisms are irreducible to independent parts.

| Shared | Divergence |
|---|---|
| Consciousness requires integration | Ochema agrees but does not reduce consciousness to integration |
| The subject is a maximum | Ochema rejects that a single scalar can select the subject — multiple models and partitions are needed |
| Causal structure matters | Tononi focuses exclusively on causal structure within a fixed set of elements; Ochema includes materiality, history, and phenomenology as independent constraints |

**Ochema's critique:** IIT attempts a purely physical selector (Φ_max).
That's exactly what Ochema argues cannot work — physical descriptions
underdetermine subject selection. Tononi's "main complex" is just one
of many possible partitions, selected by a specific measure with specific
analytical choices.

**IIT's critique of Ochema:** IIT provides a principled mathematical
definition of consciousness. Ochema provides a methodology for bearer
recovery. IIT is a theory; Ochema is a framework.

## 7. Enactivism / Autopoiesis (Varela, Maturana, Thompson, Di Paolo)

**Closest biological relative.** Living systems are autopoietic — they
self-produce. Cognition is embodied action (enaction), not representation.
Sense-making is the generation of meaning through the organism's
interaction with its world.

| Shared | Divergence |
|---|---|
| Organization is self-maintaining | Enactivists focus on autopoiesis (self-production); Ochema focuses on exclusion (self-individuation) |
| Cognition is embodied | Shared |
| Meaning is enacted, not represented | Ochema formalizes this through the norm structure N_i and valence hypothesis |
| The organism is the unit | Ochema adds that the organism is only one candidate — nested closures exist |

**Ochema's addition:** Explicates what enactive "sense-making" means in
terms of exclusion operations and bioelectric implementation. Enactivism
describes what organisms DO; Ochema describes what organisms ARE.

## Where Ochema Stands Alone

No existing framework combines all of the following:

1. **Joint physical-phenomenal fixed-point** for bearer recovery (not a purely physical selector)
2. **Material Enactment Principle** (substrate specificity, not functionalism)
3. **Exclusion as partition operation** (apoha: E_i: Ω → (P_i, W_i))
4. **Bioelectric implementation** (Levin's experimental programme)
5. **Kañcukas as constraint families** (five analytic dimensions of finite limitation)
6. **Generate→select as universal biology** (confirmed by Levin; predicted by Doyle)
7. **Explicit evidence grading** (B through E — no overclaiming)
8. **Falsification conditions** (nine specific ways the framework could be falsified)

The value proposition is not "we solved consciousness." It is: *we provide a
defensible methodology for finding where consciousness lives in a biological
system, grounded in independently confirmed experimental results, without
metaphysical overreach.*
