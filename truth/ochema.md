# Ochema

## A Joint Physical–Phenomenal Methodology for Bearer Recovery

---

## Preamble

This document states what survives rigorous formal critique. It does not claim
to solve subject individuation, consciousness, or the hard problem. It provides
a defensible methodology for recovering the physical scale at which conscious
processes are implemented in a given organism.

The name *ochema* (ὄχημα) — the Greek term for "vehicle" — is chosen deliberately.
In Neoplatonic philosophy, the ochema is the luminous vehicle that mediates
between the soul and the body. Here it denotes the framework's role: not the
subject itself, but the methodological vehicle through which candidate bearer
models can be investigated.

All claims are graded: B (experimentally confirmed), C (supported by converging
evidence), D (plausible hypothesis), E (speculative). No claim is graded A
because the framework makes no deductive proofs about consciousness.

---

## 1. The Problem

A theory of consciousness must separate four questions. This framework addresses
only the subset it can defend.

| Question | Addressed? | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Q1: Why is anything manifest? | No | Primitive — not addressed |
| Q2: What individuates subjects? | Partially | Method for bearer recovery, not deduction |
| Q3: What structures the field? | Partially | Bridge hypotheses, not derivations |
| Q4: Which physical organization implements it? | Yes | Empirically investigable |

### 1.1 The selector problem (Λₚ)

Given a conscious organism, which physical process within it is the bearer of
that consciousness? Physical descriptions admit multiple partitions at multiple
scales — neural, bioelectric, metabolic, mechanical, sensorimotor. Different
analytical choices produce different candidate bearers.

The framework does not claim to prove which candidate is the bearer. It provides
a methodology for identifying the best candidate model given available evidence,
and specifies how rival models could be discriminated.

**Status: C (supported — the problem is real; no current metric uniquely selects).**

## 2. The Evidence: Bioelectric Organization

### 2.1 Established results

Developmental bioelectricity has established the following:

1. **Pattern persistence:** Planarian target morphology survives complete cell
   turnover. The pattern is not in DNA — perturbing the bioelectric field changes
   it permanently, and the altered pattern persists across multiple rounds of
   amputation and regeneration. [B — multiple labs]

2. **Downward causation:** Organism-level bioelectric goals constrain individual
   cell behavior. Cells follow the field, not their own genome. [B — confirmed]

3. **Cognitive light cone:** Gap junction coupling determines the spatial and
   temporal scale of agency — what a system can sense, value, and act upon.
   [B — Levin lab]

4. **Generate–select:** Every biological system at every scale generates
   alternative states and selects among them. [B — confirmed across phyla]

5. **Material specificity:** The computation performed by a bioelectric network
   depends constitutively on ion channel types, gap junction topology, hydration
   state, and membrane composition. [B — Fields & Levin 2103.17061]

### 2.2 What these results do NOT establish

- That bioelectric organization constitutes phenomenal boundaries
- That bioelectric exclusion is equivalent to phenomenal privacy
- That bioelectric fields are conscious
- That any unique physical system is selected by these criteria

The term "exclusion" in the bioelectric context (gap junctions open/closed,
signals included/excluded) describes biological signal processing.
Whether this maps onto phenomenal exclusivity is a hypothesis, not a result.

**Status for consciousness-specific inference: E (speculative).**

---

## 3. The Methodology: Joint Convergence

### 3.1 The approach

Instead of searching for a single physical selector, the framework proposes
that candidate bearer models be evaluated by their convergence across multiple
independently motivated descriptions.

Define a candidate set 𝒜 = {A₁, ..., Aₙ} of physical processes within an
organism. Each candidate is scored by:

```
𝒬(A) = w₀ O(A) + w₁ X(A) + w₂ H(A) + w₃ C(A) + w₄ P(A)
```

where:
- O(A): organizational closure adequacy
- X(A): cross-description convergence (neural, bioelectric, metabolic, etc.)
- H(A): historical continuity — how well past states predict future dynamics
- C(A): causal-scale robustness — stability across nearby resolutions
- P(A): psychophysical perturbation fit — do interventions produce predicted changes?

### 3.2 What this achieves

The output is:

```
A* = argmax_A 𝒬(A)
```

This is the best-supported bearer model given current evidence.

**It is not a proof of identity.** 𝒬(A) selects the best model, not the
metaphysically guaranteed subject. Abductive convergence supports
*inference to the best explanation*, not deductive certainty.

**Status: C (supported — cross-description convergence is a legitimate
methodological principle in multiscale science).**

### 3.3 Known limitations

1. **Circularity:** Phenomenal evidence P is already indexed to a subject.
   Using it to select A* means the procedure assumes an individuation to
   find the correlate of that individuation. This is methodologically useful
   but does not derive subjecthood.

2. **Nested candidates:** Organizational closure exists at multiple scales
   (cell, organ, organism). The criteria do not uniquely select one level.

3. **Abductive gap:** Even perfect convergence does not logically entail:
   D_i = A*. It supports best explanation, not identity.

## 4. The Material Enactment Principle

### 4.1 Claim

Same abstract transition table ⇏ same materially available dynamics.

Two systems implementing the same finite input-output map may differ in
counterfactual structure, perturbational response, noise properties,
timescales, and memory effects. Therefore psychophysical theories should
model materially realized causal structure, not merely abstract computation.

### 4.2 Support

- Fields & Levin (2103.17061): Energy budgets at cellular scales are
  insufficient for classical computation — quantum coherence may be required.
  Substrate matters.
- Cadinu et al. (2504.00872): Bioelectrical interfaces depend on specific
  ionic, protein, and membrane properties of non-excitable cells.
- Levin corpus: Changing ion channels or gap junction topology changes
  what a bioelectric network can compute.

### 4.3 What it does NOT establish

- That biological material is uniquely necessary for consciousness
- That any specific material variable (water, lipids, ions) is sufficient
- That abstract functional organization is irrelevant

**Status: B (supported — material specificity of biological computation).**
**Stronger claim (necessary biological substrate): E (unsupported).**

## 5. Organizational Closure

### 5.1 Definition

A system exhibits organizational closure when its network of mutually
dependent constraints collectively maintains the conditions under which
those constraints continue to operate. This is compatible with thermodynamic
openness.

### 5.2 Role in the framework

Closure provides:
- A non-arbitrary biological candidate boundary
- An account of self-maintenance
- A distinction between constitutive components and environmental inputs

### 5.3 Known limitation

Closure is nested. Cells, organs, organisms, and social groups can all
satisfy closure. Therefore closure alone does not uniquely select the
conscious subject.

**Status: B/C (closure is real in organisms; uniqueness for subject selection: E).**

---

## 6. Historical Constitution

Past interactions alter presently realized dispositions. A process is
strongly history-sensitive when:

```
P(x_t+Δ | x_t, H_t) ≠ P(x_t+Δ | x_t)
```

where H_t includes prior states that modify current transition probabilities.

This is a well-established property of biological systems. It supports
diachronic modelling but does not by itself decide which persistence
relation is constitutive of personal or phenomenal identity.

**Status: B (confirmed — non-Markovian dynamics are pervasive in biology).**

## 7. Bridge Hypotheses

The following table states hypothesized structural correspondences between
physical and phenomenal dimensions.

| Phenomenal | Physical Correspondence | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Unity (u_i) | Differentiated integration under perturbation | C |
| Boundary (b_i) | Convergence of neural, regulatory, bioelectric, mechanical boundaries | D |
| Temporality (τ_i) | Active retention and anticipation in non-Markovian dynamics | C |
| Quality (q_i) | Relational structure compared via similarity/discriminability data | D |
| Valence (v_i) | Significance relative to endogenous norm structure | D |
| Agency (a_i) | Counterfactual control over future trajectories | C |
| Subject index (ι_i) | Historical, regulatory, perspective-bearing trajectory | D/E |

None of these correspondences is defined as identity. Each is a research
hypothesis to be tested through selective interventions and dissociations.

## 8. The Kañcukas as Analytic Constraint Families

Abhinavagupta's five kañcukas provide an analytic decomposition of finite
constraint dimensions:

| Kañcuka | Constraint Family |
|---|---|
| Kalā | Action repertoire restriction |
| Vidyā | Epistemic access limitation |
| Rāga | Endogenous significance structure |
| Kāla | Temporal horizon |
| Niyati | Spatial-causal position |

These are phenomenologically derived categories that may be useful for
characterizing the constraint structure of any finite subject. They do not
constitute independent physical operators. In actual organisms, all five
are strongly coupled: knowledge changes action, action changes spatial reach,
desire changes attention.

**Status: D (plausible analytic framework; independence not established).**

---

## 9. Valence as a Research Hypothesis

The framework hypothesizes that valence (ν_i) may correspond to the degree
of coherence or frustration among the organism's norm structure:

```
ν_i ≈ ℱ(𝒩_i, 𝒞_i, 𝒜_i)
```

where 𝒩_i is the norm structure, 𝒞_i are current constraints, and 𝒜_i are
available actions. This is inspired by QRI's symmetry theory and
Abhinavagupta's camatkāra doctrine.

However:
- No mathematical specification of "symmetry" or "coherence" is provided
- Conflict does not always produce negative valence (excitement, curiosity,
  aesthetic depth are counterexamples)
- The equation is not yet an equation

**Status: E (speculative — promising research direction, no current content).**

---

## 10. Falsification Conditions

The framework would be seriously weakened if:

1. Cross-description convergence fails to predict dissociation patterns
   better than single-domain models

2. Candidate bearer models change radically under minor analytical changes
   (no robust plateau exists)

3. History-sensitive models provide no predictive improvement over
   instantaneous models

4. Material specificity makes no difference to psychophysically relevant
   counterfactual capacities

5. Organizational closure and causal-scale robustness provide no
   constraint on candidate bearer models

6. The kañcuka decomposition generates no useful empirical predictions

7. Valence shows no systematic relationship to norm-structure coherence

## 12. What the Framework Actually Provides

The strongest defensible result:

> For a known conscious organism, one can search for a materially enacted
> process that maximizes cross-domain correspondence with measured phenomenal
> and behavioural structure. This recovery methodology can generate testable
> predictions about dissociations, transitions, and perturbations. It does
> not prove numerical identity between the recovered process and the
> phenomenal subject.

This is a legitimate scientific programme. It is not a proof, an identity
theory, or a solution to subject individuation.

The framework's contribution is methodological: a principled approach to
bearer recovery that acknowledges multiscale organization, material
specificity, historical constitution, and the joint use of physical and
phenomenal evidence. Whether convergence ultimately supports identity,
correlation, or some other relation is not settled by the framework itself.

---

## 13. Final Statement

> Ochema is a methodological framework for recovering candidate bearer models
> of conscious processes using joint physical–phenomenal convergence. It
> incorporates bioelectric organization, material enactment, organizational
> closure, historical constitution, and multiscale modelling as evidence
> dimensions. It does not claim to solve the hard problem, the selector
> problem, or the mind-body problem. It provides a vehicle for investigating
> them — a vehicle whose adequacy is measured by predictive success, not
> metaphysical completeness.
>
> The exclusion operation E_i: Ω → (P_i, W_i) is a formal notation for a
> research programme, not an established mechanism. The kañcukas are an
> analytic constraint decomposition, not independent physical operators.
> Valence as symmetry is a hypothesis, not a result.
>
> The framework is substrate-sensitive without being substrate-committed.
> It is historically aware without being historically determinative. It is
> phenomenologically constrained without being phenomenologically derived.
>
> What it is: a methodology.
> What it is not: a solved theory of consciousness.
