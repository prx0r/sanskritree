## Completed Translations

### 1. Ajadapramātṛsiddhi (Establishment of the Non-Inert Knower)
**File:** `/root/projects/sanskritree/truth/truthtranslation`
**Verses:** 27 kārikās with Vṛtti
**Source:** Sanskrit Wikisource + R2 upload
**Status:** ✅ Complete (1643 lines)

**Key arguments found:**
- **Stage A (verses 1-8):** Existence and nonexistence are distinguishable only as manifested or unmanifested. Causal efficacy cannot establish existence without already presupposing manifestation.
- **Stage B (verses 9-15):** Reflexivity argument — bare luminosity without self-apprehension would be inert. "This" (object-presentation) rests in "I" (first-personal self-presence). This is Utpaladeva's strongest argument.
- **Stage C (verses 16-17):** Universalization attempt — "This very self is twofold: limited and unlimited" (v16). "Because it performs connective synthesis, it is one alone" (v17). **Critical failure point:** "sarvārthasaṃvidām" is ambiguous between "all cognitions within one stream" and "all cognitions of every subject."
- **Stage D (verses 18-21):** No inert entity appears beyond its manifestation. Breath/body are objects, not the knower.
- **Stage E (verses 22-27):** Self-resting → freedom → agency → Lordship. **Second major gap:** epistemic self-presence does not entail causal sovereignty.

**Conclusion:** Strong phenomenological core (reflexive manifestation, relational synthesis). Universalization from "all objects are within manifestation" to "all manifestations are numerically one subject" is an invalid quantifier shift. Nyāya plural selves provide the cheapest countermodel.

### 2. Sambandhasiddhi (Establishment of Relation)
**File:** `/root/projects/sanskritree/truth/sambandhasiddhi_translation.md`
**Verses:** 21 kārikās with Vṛtti (prose auto-commentary)
**Source:** Sanskrit Wikisource (KSTS vol. 34, Muktabodha M00080, 1921)
**Status:** ✅ Complete (full translation with detailed commentary)

#### Key Arguments Found

**Opening thesis (verses 1-2):** Śiva's cosmic play operates through relation (sambandha) whose essence is both difference and non-difference. The Lord's body is the relational structure of all reality.

**Stage A — What relation is NOT (prose + verses 1-3):**
- NOT spatial contiguity (father/son far apart would have no relation; iron rods close together would be related)
- NOT an entity residing in two things (dviṣṭha padārtha) — that would also apply to twoness, duality, separateness
- NOT expectation/dependence (*apekṣā/pāratantrya*) — these presuppose consciousness; inert entities cannot have them
- NOT a conventional label derived from etymology (*tailapāyikavad*)

**Stage B — The Buddhist opponent's challenge (prose):**
- Entities are intrinsically unmixed (*amishrāḥ svayaṃ bhāvāḥ*)
- Conceptual construction (*kalpanā*) connects them
- Unity and plurality are contradictory — they cannot co-exist in reality
- Relation is a useful fiction (*saṃvṛti*) for practical purposes, like the universal (*sāmānya*)
- Supporting verse quoted: "Ultimate entities are neither connected nor divided by their own nature. Unity and plurality in them is a disturbance of cognition." (*buddher upaplavaḥ*)

**Stage C — Utpaladeva's response (prose + verses 4-7):**
- **First move:** The Buddhist admits cognition has "many as one" form (*anekaikarūpatā*) — they just call it conceptual. But if all determinate cognition is conceptual (as the Buddhist holds), then "conceptual" ≠ "unreal."
- **Second move (critical argument):** The relational appearance is NOT a correctable error. It is "fused with the appearance of the object itself" (*arthapratibhāsasaṃlagnatvāt*). Unlike the snake-rope illusion, which dissolves when you see the rope, the relational appearance persists under analysis. It is bound up with the very content of the objects.
- **Third move:** The error, if it is one, is the appearance itself (*bhrānta ābhāsa eva tat*) — not a misidentification. Calling it "error" is just a label.
- **Fourth move:** Conceptual construction is not separate from the appearance (*na ca vikalpo 'rthapratibhāsād bhinnaḥ*). This is the Pratyabhijñā doctrine that cognition and its object are not two.

**Stage D — Definition of relation (verses 1-3):**
- *Rūpasaṃśleṣa* (conjunction of forms) = *aikātmya* (identity/being-one-self)
- Relation = the identity of many forms in a single cognitive act
- NOT spatial, NOT causal, NOT conventional — but the unity of consciousness itself
- The term *aikātmya* contains *ātman* — suggesting this unity is self-like, not abstract

**Stage E — Ontological basis (verses 8-11):**
- **Verse 8 (critical for universalization):** "Entities — whose essence is pure consciousness (*cinmātravapus*), though differentiated by Māyā's creation — are not impossible to unify."
  - NOTE: This assumes the Pratyabhijñā ontology (entities' essence = consciousness) — it does NOT prove it. The text references ĪPK for this.
- **Verse 9:** The ordinary knower's cognition is always already an act of reflexive grasp (*parāmarśa*)
- **Verse 10 (unity of the knower):** "By this [singular reflexive awareness], the operation is blocked; it would not touch another conceptual construction, because touching another would entail a division of the knower (*mātṛbheda*)."
  - This shows synchronic unity (within one cognitive act) — NOT diachronic or transpersonal unity
- **Verse 11:** Language reflects cognitive unity — a single word expresses a single reflexive grasp holding many objects together

**Stage F — Grammatical analysis (verses 12-18):**
Detailed analysis of genitive (ṣaṣṭhī), apposition (sāmānādhikaraṇya), compounds (samāsa), coordinated phrases (dvandva), negative constructions, and distinction. Key point: every relation is between TWO things — multi-term relations reduce to pairs. The grammatical form reflects the structure of reflexive awareness.

**Stage G — Conclusion (verses 19-21):**
- These cognitions are NOT erroneous — at every moment they are founded on self-awareness, the infinite consciousness (*svasaṃvitsaṃjñakānantacidvimarśapratiṣṭhitāḥ*)
- Relation is established by self-awareness (*svasaṃvitsiddha*), incontrovertible (*nirbādha*), useful (*upayogavān*)
- It is the full complement of the means (*pūrṇaḥ parikaraḥ*) in the Pratyabhijñā

#### What Sambandhasiddhi DOES Establish
1. Relation is not reducible to convention, spatial contiguity, or causal dependence
2. Relation = the intrinsic unity of what appears as multiple within a single cognitive act
3. This unity is a function of consciousness's reflexive nature (*vimarśa*/*parāmarśa*)
4. The knower is unified within each cognitive act — divided attention would split the subject

#### What Sambandhasiddhi Does NOT Establish (for universalization)
1. That entities' essence IS consciousness (*cinmātravapus*) — this is assumed from the Pratyabhijñā framework (ĪPK), not argued here
2. That the unity of each cognitive act is numerically identical across different cognitive acts or different subjects
3. That all relations are modes of ONE universal consciousness (rather than structurally similar local unifications)
4. The text shows HOW relations could be modes of one consciousness IF the Pratyabhijñā ontology is accepted — it does not prove they MUST be

**Verdict:** Sambandhasiddhi does not independently bridge the universalization gap. It is consistent with the universal subject but does not prove it. The missing premise ("reflexive manifestation cannot be numerically partitioned") remains missing.

## Critical Principles (DO NOT VIOLATE)

1. **Cheapest adequate explanation.** Don't bias toward proving Pratyabhijñā. Reduce the live possibility space.
2. **Weakest claim first.** For every proposed conclusion, ask: what is the weakest claim actually established? Then ask: what premise is required for the stronger claim?
3. **Do not cross transitions silently.** Manifestness → reflexivity → diachronic unity → universalization → freedom. Each arrow is a separate research problem. No text is permitted to inherit a later conclusion merely by establishing an earlier one.
4. **Three translations, kept separate.** Literal ≠ philosophical ≠ doctrinal. Mixing them produces false confidence in doctrinal readings.
5. **Evidence support ≠ acceptance status.** Propagated_score, human_status, and lean_status are separate fields. A popular error can have high graph weight.
6. **Bridge upgrades are the most dangerous operation.** No BRIDGES until:
   - Bidirectional positive probes are proved
   - Negative controls pass
   - The upgrade is persisted in structural_correspondences
7. **Nothing in the Sanskrit text establishes universal consciousness by itself.** The universalization from local reflexivity to cosmic identity requires an additional premise that no text currently supplies. If Īśvarasiddhi also fails to supply it, that is a finding — not a failure.
8. **Do NOT train a neural model.** We have 511 adjudications, not 5,000. Learned ranking would learn noise.
9. **Do NOT build a general Sanskrit translation pipeline.** Translate only the passages where English coverage is thin and the unresolved argument lives.
10. **Hermes with --yolo + skills times out at 3 minutes.** Workaround: provide source material in the prompt, Hermes extracts claims instantly.
