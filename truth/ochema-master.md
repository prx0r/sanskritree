## How to Use This Organization

- **Writing for peer review:** Cite only Tier 1 as evidence.
  Tier 2 as methodology. Tier 3 as future directions.
  Do not cite Tiers 4–5.

- **Writing for informed general audience:** Tiers 1–3 are
  admissible. Tier 4 can be mentioned as "speculative convergence"
  if explicitly labeled as such.

- **Internal project development:** All tiers are usable.
  Tier 4 generates hypotheses to test. Tier 5 provides
  philosophical vocabulary for interpreting results.

- **Channeled material analysis:** Tier 4 documents are the
  correct place. They preserve all insights without claiming
  scientific status.

Nothing is lost. Everything has its place.
