## Key Questions

### 1. What IS valence? The state of the science

- Dimensional models (valence-arousal) vs. discrete emotions
- Panksepp's seven primary-process affective systems: seeking, fear, rage, lust, care, panic, play
- Which map onto which kañcukas? (Rāga = seeking? Kāla = panic at time running out?)
- The valence-as-symmetry hypothesis (QRI) vs. the valence-as-adaptation-rate hypothesis (active inference)

### 2. The adaptation-rate model

- Positive valence = prediction errors being reduced faster than expected
- Negative valence = unexpectedly worsening control
- Why complete predictability becomes boring
- Why manageable surprise is pleasurable
- Why sudden comprehension (insight) produces pleasure
- Why play creates reducible uncertainty
- Formal: νᵢ(t) ∝ -d/dt[unexpected regulatory error]

### 3. Semantic information and valence

- Kolchinsky & Wolpert: semantic information = correlations causally necessary for viability
- The three-level hierarchy: Semᵢ (objective significance) → Vᵢ (regulatory weighting) → νᵢ (felt valence)
- The unresolved bridge: (Semᵢ, Vᵢ) ⟷? νᵢ

### 4. QRI's Symmetry Theory of Valence

- Symmetry → valence hypothesis
- The tyrannity of the intentional object: valence is a field property, not an object property
- The logarithmic scale of pleasure/pain
- What survives from STV after the audit: nothing beyond a research hypothesis

### 5. The Tantrāloka's five blisses gradient

- Innate bliss (nijānanda) → stillness (nirānanda) → supreme (parānanda) → great (mahānanda) → consciousness-bliss (cidānanda)
- Does this correspond to a logarithmic scale?
- Rastogi's two formulations of camatkāra: existential (ρᵢ) vs. experiential (νᵢ)

### 6. The central problem

Is valence a) an intrinsic property of conscious states (realism), b) a relational property of organism-environment interactions (relationalism), or c) an illusion generated by cognitive evaluation (constructivism)?

The Śaiva position: valence (camatkāra) IS the intrinsic character of consciousness (ρᵢ). Local hedonic valence (νᵢ) is a modulation of ρᵢ by regulatory and geometric factors. This is neither realism (valence in the stimulus), relationalism (valence in the interaction), nor constructivism (valence as judgment). It is an identity claim: consciousness = self-affection.
