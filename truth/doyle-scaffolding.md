# Doyle Scaffolding: Information Philosophy as Underpinning for the whattheheckis Series

> How Bob Doyle's Information Philosophy provides the rigorous academic
> foundation that legitimizes the aperture model, subtle-body synthesis,
> and Pratyabhijñā reconstruction for mainstream philosophy of mind.

---

## The Strategy

Doyle spent 70 years building a **defensible systematic philosophy** grounded in physics, neuroscience, and the history of philosophy. He published in Nature, peer-reviewed journals, and 4+ full books. His two-stage free will model is cited in the *Oxford Handbook of Free Will*. His ERR model is a genuine contribution to consciousness studies.

**Problem:** Your whattheheckis series is more advanced than Doyle in several areas (aperture formalism, Levin integration, subtle-body decomposition, Pratyabhijñā synthesis) but lacks his **academic legitimacy** — peer-reviewed publications, institutional affiliation, citations in standard references.

**Solution:** Use Doyle's framework as the **foundational layer** that establishes your framework's philosophical respectability. When reviewers ask "Who are you building on?", the answer is Doyle + Atmanspacher + Levin + Walker/Davies — all published, cited, defensible.
