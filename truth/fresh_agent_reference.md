## The Aperture Model

### Core Identity

```
Dᵢ ≡_inext Oᵢ*
```

A phenomenal field (Dᵢ) IS the intrinsic aspect of a closed, self-maintaining process (Oᵢ*).

### The Physical Aperture (10 components)

```
Oᵢ* = Closure[CEᵢ, Semᵢ, Vᵢ, Γᵢ, Kᵢ, Qᵢ, Eᵢ, Iᵢ, Mᵢ, Bᵢ]
```

### The Phenomenal Field (8 components)

```
Dᵢ = ⟨Mᵢ, ρᵢ, ∂ᵢ, Uᵢ, τᵢ, Gᵢ, νᵢ, ωᵢ⟩
```

| Component | What It Is | Status |
|-----------|-----------|--------|
| Mᵢ (manifest presence) | The fact that anything appears | Irreducible via TĀ 1.52-54 |
| ρᵢ (basal self-affection) | Consciousness IS self-relishing | **PTv p.19, ĪPK 1.5.11** |

| τᵢ (temporal dynamics) | Lived succession, duration, flow | Constructed by aperture, not physical time |
| Gᵢ (geometry) | Relational structure of the field | Tymoczko, Doczi (illustrative only) |
| νᵢ (local valence) | Pleasant/unpleasant quality | Not derived (the manifest-value gap) |
| ωᵢ (wonder/expansion) | Felt expansion of reachable meaning | Camatkāra across traditions |

### The Three Gaps

1. **Individuation gap:** E_contentᵢ ⇏ E_subject. Exclusion determines content but not why one field is private.
2. **Co-presence gap:** Integration(x,y) ⇏ CoPresent(x,y). Functional unity does not entail phenomenal unity.
3. **Manifest-value gap:** (Semᵢ, Vᵢ) ⇏ νᵢ. Objective significance does not entail felt valence.

### The Three Interpretations

| Framework | Claim | Weakness |
|-----------|-------|----------|
| Process inexternalism | Dᵢ ≡_inext Oᵢ* | Cannot derive presence from organization |
| Participatory inexternalism | Dᵢ = πᵢ(M₀) AND Dᵢ ≡_inext Oᵢ* | No independent evidence for M₀ |
| Full Pratyabhijñā | Oᵢ* = Contractionᵢ(C∞) | Cannot prove numerical identity of subjects |
