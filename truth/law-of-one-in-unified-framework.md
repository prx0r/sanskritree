## 3. The Law of One = Dual-Aspect Monism

Ra: "All is one."
MEPIT: D_i ≡_int A_i[Σ_i]. One process, two aspects.

The "Law of One" is the claim that all apparent subjects are the same
process expressing differently. This is exactly what dual-aspect monism
asserts: there is one reality, known from within as D_i and from without
as A_i[Σ_i]. The unity is ontological; the plurality is epistemic.

**The Pratyabhijñā parallel:** ĪPK 1.3.7 says there is "one conscious embodied
being" possessing multiple powers. The "one" = the undivided process.

