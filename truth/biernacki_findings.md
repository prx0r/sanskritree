### 2. "A Cognitive Science View of Abhinavagupta's Understanding of Consciousness" (668 lines)
**File:** `biernacki_A_Cognitive_Science_View_of_Abhinavagupt.txt`

**Core claim:** Abhinavagupta's nondual Śaivism offers a framework useful for cognitive science — specifically, a theory for connecting the material with the phenomenal.

**Key find:** The Sanskrit compound we should have been citing as a primary source for the rasa thesis:

"sarvathā punar avicchinnacamatkāranirapekṣasvātantryāhaṃvimarśe"

Translation: "The freedom of the uninterrupted delight of I-consciousness is completely independent of any reference to anything else."

This is Abhinavagupta directly linking avicchinnacamatkāra (uninterrupted wonder), svātantrya (freedom), and ahaṃvimarśa (I-awareness). The compound says: the freedom OF self-awareness IS uninterrupted delight.

**Push deeper:** Find the exact source of this compound in Abhinavagupta's corpus. Biernacki only cites it as ([2], p. 212). We need the specific text (ĪPK? PTv? TĀ?).

### 3. "Abhinavagupta's Svātantryavāda: Mental Causality and the Freedom of Consciousness" (1351 lines)
**File:** `biernacki_Abhinavagupta_s_Svatantryavada_Mental_Ca.txt`

**Core claim:** Abhinavagupta's svātantryavāda converges with Brouwer's intuitionist mathematics and Gisin's quantum physics. Freedom is not an illusion — it is built into the structure of time and choice.

**Key connections:**
- Brouwer: mathematics is grounded in "the perception of a move of time" — not in a Platonic realm
- Gisin: the Bekenstein bound (no finite volume contains infinite info) means real numbers are processes, not completed objects
- Abhinavagupta: svātantrya is the capacity to act "on the level of Māyā" — within finite constraint
- Even worms and insects have a subjectivity that "stirs in the heart"

**Push deeper:**
- Read Gisin's papers directly (his 2019-2020 papers on intuitionist math and free will)
- Compare Brouwer's "move of time" with the Tantrāloka's spanda (pulsation of consciousness)
- Formalize Eᵢ (exclusion operation) as a choice in intuitionist logic — this would give the carving operation a mathematical foundation

### 4. "Abhinavagupta's Theogrammatical Topography of the One and the Many" / Panentheism paper (675 lines)
**File:** `biernacki_Panentheism_and_Hindu_Tantra_Abhinavagup.txt`

**Core claim:** The political and theological problem of the one and the many is inscribed in Abhinavagupta's grammar. The one does not rule the many — the many constitute the one through relationship.

**Key for us:** This provides a political/democratic reading of nonduality. The "universal subject" is not a cosmic monarch — it is the collective constituted by all the many relationships. This directly challenges the authoritarian reading of Pratyabhijñā universalization.

**Push deeper:** Does this "democratic nonduality" provide a model for how multiple apertures relate without collapsing? If the one IS the many (not above them), then the partition problem looks very different.

### 5. "Material Subjects, Immaterial Bodies" (979 lines)
**File:** `biernacki_Material_Subjects_Immaterial_Bodies_Abhi.txt`
