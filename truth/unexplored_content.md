## Gap 1: proclusss (4776 lines) — The Neoplatonic Participation Model

**Status:** Completely unread.

**What it contains:** A full checkpoint on how Proclus, Plotinus, and Iamblichus address the SAME partition problem. The central Proclean correction:

> "Ground unity need not entail subject identity, field identity or equal access among participants."
> "Many real centres participate in one prior causal order without becoming numerically identical with it or each other."

**Why this is urgent:**
This is the SAME participation model (Πᵢ) that we've been developing from the Śaiva side using the pratibimba (reflection) model. The Neoplatonic henads are structurally identical to the Śaiva reflections — they are real centres that participate in the One without dividing it. This convergence across two independent traditions (Neoplatonism and Pratyabhijñā) is exactly the kind of cross-cultural evidence we've been seeking.

**Key question:** Does the Proclean model solve the problem of WHY πᵢ ≠ πⱼ (participant individuation)? Or does it, like the Śaiva model, describe the structure without explaining the mechanism?

## Gap 2: recognition formulation (1051 lines) — Ratié's Direct Engagement

**Status:** Completely unread.

**What it contains:** A direct formulation of the Pratyabhijñā recognition argument that references Ratié's work and may contain dialogue with her. Criticizes our earlier model as "too mechanistic":

> "Your formulation captures the direction of dependence correctly... But this expression is too mechanistic: M∞ → Lᵢ → Mᵢ. It makes Lᵢ look like an independently existing filter applied to an otherwise featureless universal consciousness. That is not quite Utpaladeva's or Abhinavagupta's position."

**The corrected formulation:**
> C∞ → its own differentiating freedom → ⟨Sᵢ, Wᵢ, Rᵢ⟩

Where C∞ is universal consciousness as prakāśa–vimarśa–svātantrya, Sᵢ is a finite knower, Wᵢ is its field of objects, and Rᵢ is the restricted relation connecting them.

**Why this is urgent:**
This formulates exactly the mistake we've been making — treating Lᵢ (the constraint set) as an external filter rather than as consciousness's OWN differentiating freedom. This is the same correction our v0.2 audit made (removing φ as external measure). It also explicitly frames the triad as cognition + memory + exclusion.

## Gap 4: dossiers_234.md — The TĀ 16.242 Finding

**Status:** Referenced in our TANTRALOKA_REFERENCE but the dossiers file itself never deeply read.

**What it contains:** The crucial TĀ 16.242 verse:

> "bhogyajñānaṃ nānyadeheṣv anusandhānam arhati"
> "Knowledge that is enjoyed in one body cannot perform connective synthesis (anusandhāna) with other bodies."

**Why this is urgent:**
This is Abhinavagupta HIMSELF limiting anusandhāna to one body. This directly constrains Utpaladeva's universalization argument in APS 17. The dossier says: "It's evidence AGAINST the transpersonal universalization reading." This is a major piece of internal counter-evidence we should have integrated into our thesis.
