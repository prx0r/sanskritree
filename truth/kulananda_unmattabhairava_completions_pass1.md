# I. Kulānanda, vv. 35–60 — the fumigation, the sky-going, the mahāvyāpti, and the final practices

**35** — dhūnanaṃ kampanaṃ deva yathākhyātaṃ mamāgrataḥ | tataścāpi saṃśayo deva kathayasva mama prabho ||
> The fumigation (dhūnana) and the trembling (kampana), O Devī, were proclaimed to me before — and yet there is doubt, O god; tell, my Lord.

**36** — śṛṇu devi pravakṣyāmi dhūnanaṃ kampanaṃ sthitam | yā sā mahāvahā nāḍī kucelyākārasaṃsthitā ||
> Hear, O Devī — I will tell the standing of the fumigation and the trembling: that great-flowing channel (mahāvahā nāḍī), standing in the form of a coiled cloth (kucelā) —

**37** — tatraivotpādayet dṛṣṭirdaṇḍākārasutejasā | hṛccakra............. tīvraśakti suvigrahā ||
> — there he should generate the vision with the rod-formed fierce brilliance; the heart-cakra... the fierce power, well-bodied [X: corrupt pāda].

**38** — dhūnanaṃ kampanaṃ caiva nātra kāryavicāraṇāt | athānyaṃ paramaṃ devi khecaratvaṃ śṛṇu priye ||
> The fumigation and the trembling — no deliberation of works here. Now the other supreme [knowledge], O Devī — hear the sky-going (khecaratva), O beloved:

**39** — yā sā mahāvahā nā(ḍī) kuṇḍalyākārasaṃsthitā | padadvandvagatā sā tu mūrdhnisthaṃ kamalaṃ punaḥ ||
> — that great-flowing channel, standing in the serpent (kuṇḍalī)-form: it goes to the pair of feet; and the lotus standing in the head —

**40** — yā vīrataye tajjvalanākāraṃ subhāvayedbudhaḥ | sarvaśaktito madhyata khecaratva bhavedveḥ | navāhamabhṛtaṃ dadaitaḥ (?) ||
> — for the heroism, that fire-form the wise one should contemplate well: from all the powers, in the middle, the sky-going arises [X: "bhavedveḥ" corrupt]; ... [X: the last pāda corrupt].

**41** — athānyaṃ sampravakṣyāmi mahāvyāptiṃ varānane | yāvad......ṣthāneṣu khyātā tasya mayāgrataḥ ||
> Now I will tell the great pervasion (mahāvyāpti), O fair-faced one: as far as... in the stations, proclaimed by me before him.

**42** — tathaiva manasāropyaṃ nāsāgre pavanamānena | manasaṃ tattva vedhayitvā niṣkalaṃ yojayedbudhaḥ ||
> Likewise mounting [the mind], at the nose-tip with the measure of the wind; having pierced the mind with the principle, the wise one should join the partless.

**43** — mahāvyāptirbhaveddevi tavāhamanṛtaṃ vadet | athānyaṃ sampravakṣyāmi yena siddhirbhavet dhruvam ||
> The great pervasion arises, O Devī — would I speak falsehood to you? Now I will tell the other, by which siddhi surely arises:

**44** — vargātītasya dvitīyamanuloma na suvrate | jvalanārūḍhaṃ kṛtvā caturthaṃ svarasaṃyutaḥ ||
> Of the beyond-the-groups (vargātīta) [syllable], the second, in forward order, O good-vowed one: having mounted the fire, the fourth, joined with its vowel —

**45** — bindumastakasambhinnamādau yojayedbudhaḥ | vargātītaṃ punardevi ṣaṣṭhasvaravibhūṣitaḥ ||
> — the bindu-head-pierced, at first the wise one should join; the beyond-the-groups again, O Devī, adorned with the sixth vowel —

**46** — sa śikhe kharabindusaṃyuktaṃ yojayeddevi tat punaḥ | yakārasya pañcamaṃ gṛhya taṃ tṛtīyasvarasamanvitam ||
> — that, in the crest (śikhā), joined with the kha-bindu, should be joined, O Devī, again: taking the fifth of the ya, joined with the third vowel —

**47** — svaraṃ dvitīyasaṃyuktaṃ kārayet punaḥ priyaḥ | vargasya prathamaṃ bījaṃ sṛṣṭerādyato yojayet ||
> — making it joined with the second vowel again, O beloved; the first seed of the group, from the beginning of creation, he should join —

**48** — etaddevi samākhyātaṃ kulavidyāsamanvitam | nityaṃ tu yojayeddevi ekacittastu paṇḍitaḥ ||
> — this, O Devī, is proclaimed, joined with the kula-vidyā: daily he should join [it], O Devī, the pandit with one-pointed mind.

**49** — tasya sarvā bhavet siddhiḥ sarvajñāneṣu sundari | na khedayāmi kasya cittaṃ śiṣye'pyuktvā varānane ||
> His every siddhi arises, in all the knowledges, O lovely one. I do not distress anyone's mind — even told to a pupil, O fair-faced one [X: the sense: the teaching is safe?].

**50-52** (the desire-practice) — athānyaṃ paramaṃ devi kāmena vihvalakārakam | bhagasya madhyastha bhagamadho hutāśanam || preritaḥ pavanaḥ śaktyā jvalanākāratejasā | visantu cintayeddevi sṛṣṭikamalantu yāvat || śravaṇaṃ cintayedamṛtaṃ tejomārgeṇa yogini | bhavanti vihvalā nāryaḥ kṣaṇamekena suvrate ||
> Now the other supreme [practice], O Devī, making [others] overwhelmed with desire: in the middle of the yoni (bhaga), below the yoni, the fire — the wind impelled by the power, with the fire-formed brilliance: entering, he should contemplate, O Devī, until the creation-lotus; the hearing he should contemplate as the nectar, by the path of the brilliance, O yoginī: the women become overwhelmed within a single moment, O good-vowed one.

**53-54** — athānyaṃ paramaṃ devi phalapuṣpākathanaṃ ca | śṛṇuyāt paramādevi aghoravikramā || ākarṣayedbudho vāyuṃ piṇḍasthaṃ rūpaṇamucyate | cakṣuṣā yojayeddevi phalapuṣpantu cintayet | tejasākarṣayet tu.....cittaṃ susaryataḥ ||
> Now the other supreme [knowledge], O Devī, the telling of fruit-and-flower: hear, O supreme Devī, of Aghora's might. The wise one draws the wind, standing in the body — this is called the forming (rūpaṇa); with the eye he should join [it], O Devī; the fruit-and-flower he should contemplate; with the brilliance he draws... the mind, with ease [X: corrupt pāda].

**55** — sarvatra tu mayā jñānaṃ bhāṣitaṃ deva tat purā | adyāpi saṃśayo devi kathayasva mama prabho | saṃgamānmokṣahetukaṃ .......... ||
> Everywhere the knowledge was spoken by me, O Devī, before — and still there is doubt, O god: tell, my Lord, [the knowledge] that is the cause of liberation from union (saṃgama) [X: the verse trails off in the print].

**56-58** — śṛṇu devi pravakṣyāmi yat surairapi durlabham | āsanaṃ tu sthitaṃ kṛtvā........viśva anivṛttaḥ || abhāvaṃ bhāvayeddevi sarvabhāvavivarjitam | cittaṃ tatra sthitaṃ kṛtvā khamadhye viniyojayet || bhāvayet samarasaṃ devi prabhubhṛtyaṃ vicakṣaṇaḥ | etadvijñānamātreṇa gīyate tatra madhyataḥ ||
> Hear, O Devī — I will tell what is hard even for the gods to obtain: having made the seat firm... the world unceasing [X: corrupt]. He should contemplate non-being, O Devī, free of all beings; having fixed the mind there, he should place it in the middle of the space (kha). He should contemplate the equal-taste (samarasa), O Devī — the lord-and-servant [pair], the wise one: by the mere understanding of this, it is sung there, in the middle.

**59-60** — dūrāśravaṇavijñānaṃ vedhastobhaṃ madhyataḥ | āveśaṃ darśanaṃ dūrāt kampastobhaṃ tathaiva ca || parakāyapraveśena sampravarteta yoginaḥ | ...  ..ṭadabhyāsena suvrate ||
> The knowledge of distant hearing; the thrust-and-stab (vedhastobha) in the middle; the possession (āveśa), the vision from afar, the tremor-thrust (kampastobha) likewise. By the entering of another's body the yogī proceeds... by that practice, O good-vowed one [X: the final pāda corrupt].

**Colophon** — iti matsyendrapādāvatāritaṃ kulānandaṃ samāptam | śubhamastu sarvajagatāṃ ||
> Thus the Kulānanda, descended through Matsyendrapāda, is complete. May there be good for all the worlds.

# II. Unmattabhairava, completion — the mudrās, the 12-names stotra, the hṛdaya

## The mudrā-section
**M1** — sumukhaṃ saṃpuṭaṃ caiva vitataṃ vistaraṃ tathā | mahāṃkuśī mahāmāsāṃ varadābhaya saṃyutām ||
> Sumukha, Saṃpuṭa, Vitata, Vistara; the great Aṅkuśī, the great Māsā, joined with the boon and the fear-not —

**M2** — matsyakūrmo varāhāṇāṃ dhenumudrā pradarśayet | mudrā dvādaśa yatkarma kīyate kavalaṃ kṛtā ||
> — of the fish, the tortoise, the boar [mudrās], the cow-mudrā one should show. The twelve mudrās — whatever work is done, with the mouthful (kavala) made [X: "kīyate" corrupt].

## The stotra-section (the 12 names of Unmattabhairava)
**S1** — haṃ guro jagatāṃ nātha trailokyabhārgavaḥ kaviḥ | brahmarṣimuniśārdūla tatrarājo ṛṣeśvaraḥ ||
> Oṃ, the guru, the lord of the worlds, the Bhārgava of the three worlds, the poet; the tiger among the brahmin-sages, the king of the tantras, the lord of the ṛṣis —

**S2** — unmattabhairavo dhīmān nāmnāmāścaryarūpiṇaḥ | nirlajjo paramānanda ghorarūpo bhayaṃkaraḥ ||
> — the wise Unmattabhairava, of wondrous form among the names: the Shameless (nirlajja), the supreme bliss (paramānanda), the terrible-formed, the fearsome.

**S3-S6** — tasyotpatti tataḥ kāryaṃ kāraṇe kiṃ prakāśikā | śrotumicchāmi he nātha tatpacāṃgaṃ phalaṃ samāḥ || kathayāmi prasanno'smi kathaṃ conmattaḥ bhairavāḥ | yatra mantrādi tantrāṇāṃ kiṃ kiṃ kāle praviśyatām || niḥśeṣo vadatāṃ nātha śiṣyaṃ dṛṣṭvā prasannatāḥ | iti vaditvā lokeśaḥ sthitvāgre gurusannidhau || añjaliṃ baddhayāmāsa praṇamya ca muhurmuhuḥ | jagaccailokyaguruṇā prasannaṃ bhavati kṣaṇāt ||
> His origin — then the work — in the cause, what is the manifestation [X]? I wish to hear, O Lord, the five-limbs and their fruit. — I am pleased, I will tell: how [he became] the mad Bhairava; of the mantras and tantras, what enters at what time. — "Tell all, O Lord" — seeing the pupil, pleased; having said this, the lord of the worlds, standing before the guru — he joined his palms, bowing again and again: by the guru of the world, of the three worlds, he is pleased in a moment.

**S7-S8** — śṛṇu śiṣya mahābuddhe devānāṃ devapūjitā | bhaktisaṃtoṣikāvākyaṃ dhanyo māṃ bhavaśevakāḥ || paramānanda connatyā dhīmān cittaṃ bhavatkṛtā | nāmāni dvādaśākhyāni ādau śṛṇu maheśvaraḥ ||
> Hear, O great-minded pupil, worshipped by the gods of the gods: the speech that satisfies devotion — blessed are the servants of Bhava to me. By the rise of the supreme bliss, the wise one — the mind made yours: the twelve-named names — first hear, O great Lord:

**The nyāsa-frame** — oṃ asya śrīparamānandamūrti conmattabhairava dvādaśanāmāni mūlamantrasya nemunyādi ṛṣiḥ ṛgyajuḥ sāmāthavāṇi gāyatrī chandaḥ śrī unmattabhairavo devatā oṃ paramānanda bījaṃ conmattaḥ śaktiḥ bhīmaḍākinyādi saṃhārakīlakaṃ madhumāṃsādi vyatirikta caturvidhapuruṣārtha siddhaye jape viniyogaḥ ||
> Oṃ — of this [mantra of] the twelve names of the Unmattabhairava, the supreme-bliss-formed: the ṛṣi is the sage Nemi and the rest; the meter is gāyatrī of the Ṛg, Yajur, Sāma, Atharva [Vedas]; the deity is the blessed Unmattabhairava; oṃ paramānanda is the seed; unmatta is the power; the nailing (kīlaka) of the destruction of Bhīma-ḍākinī and the rest; the application is the japa for the attainment of the four aims, excluding honey-and-meat and the rest.

**The aṅga-nyāsa** — oṃ hrīṃ aṅguṣṭhābhyāṃ namaḥ | oṃ paramānanda hrīṃ tarjanibhyāṃ svāhā | oṃ unmattabhairavo devatā madhyamābhyāṃ vaṣaṭ | oṃ ḍākinyādi doṣanivāraṇāya hūm anāmikābhyāṃ hūṃ | oṃ nirlajjo bhīṣaṇamūrtaye kaniṣṭikābhyāṃ vauṣaṭ | oṃ hrīṃ hrīṃ paramāndamūrtaye praṃ praṃ praṃ mama lavarayīṃ hrīṃ unmattabhairavāya hūṃ karatalakarapṛṣṭābhyāṃ phaṭ | evaṃ hṛdayādi nyāsān kṛtvā || daṇḍavatpraṇamet ||
> Oṃ hrīṃ, to the thumbs, homage; oṃ paramānanda hrīṃ, to the index-fingers, svāhā; oṃ, the deity Unmattabhairava, to the middle fingers, vaṣaṭ; oṃ, for the warding of the faults of ḍākinī and the rest, hūṃ, to the ring-fingers, hūṃ; oṃ nirlajja, to the terrible form, to the little fingers, vauṣaṭ; oṃ hrīṃ hrīṃ, to the supreme-bliss form, praṃ praṃ praṃ, my... hrīṃ, to Unmattabhairava, hūṃ, to the palms and backs, phaṭ. Having made the heart-and-the-rest nyāsas thus, he should bow with the full prostration.

**The 12 names (S9-S11)** — om unmattaṃ prathamaṃ nāma dvitīyaṃ bhairavam tathā | tṛtīyaṃ bhīmarūpā ca caturthaṃ yoginīpatiḥ || pañcamaṃ mātṛkānāthaṃ nirlajjo caiva ṣaṭamam | saptamaṃ paramānandamaṣṭamamaṣṭabhairavāḥ || navamaṃ gaṇanāthaṃ ca daśamaṃ ḍākinī hataḥ | ghora ekādaśaṃ nāma dvādaśā sātvikī samā ||
> Oṃ — "Unmatta" is the first name; the second, "Bhairava"; the third, "the terrible-formed"; the fourth, "the lord of the yoginīs"; the fifth, "the lord of the Mothers" (mātṛkānātha); the sixth, "the Shameless"; the seventh, "the supreme bliss"; the eighth, "the eight Bhairavas"; the ninth, "the lord of the hosts" (gaṇanātha); the tenth, "the slayer of the ḍākinī"; the eleventh name, "the Ghorā"; the twelfth, "the Sātvikī."

**S12** — eta dvādaśanāmāni unmatta bhairavo haraḥ | hṛdayaṃ tatpravakṣyāmi śṛṇu śiṣya maheśvaraḥ ||
> These are the twelve names — Unmattabhairava is Hara. I will tell that heart[-mantra]; hear, O pupil, O great Lord:

**The hṛdaya-section (H1–H2)** — unmattabhairavo dhīmān tasya vai hṛdayaṃ śṛṇu | vaktuṃ rahasyaṃ deveśa kathayāmi hara tvayā || manovāñchā bhavetsiddhi hṛdayaṃ bharavasya ca | mantreṇa sahito rudra bhairavotpatti sarvadā || purākalpe samudbhūte hari bauddhasvarūpa dhṛk ||
> The wise Unmattabhairava — hear his heart[-mantra]; the secret to be told, O Lord of gods — I tell it, O Hara, to you: the mind's desire becomes siddhi — the heart[-mantra] of Bhava; joined with the mantra, O Rudra, the arising of Bhairava always. In the ancient eon arisen, Hari bearing the Buddha-form —

**The close (H49–H54)** — iti vāgguruṇā vākyaṃ śrutvā śaṃkara śāmbavaḥ | hṛdayaṃ bhairavo nātha vārāṇasyonmattabhairavaḥ || sāṅgaṃ hṛdayamunmattaṃ yo naraḥ paṭhyate sadā | vāñchitā labhate siddhi nānāpīḍāṃ samākulām || grahabhūtādijantūnāṃ ḍākinī doṣanāśakam | kāmākṣāṃ cintidā nityāṃ hṛdaye dhārayetsadā || vidyārthī cāpnuyādvidyāṃ dhanārthī labhate dhanam | mokṣārthī labhate mokṣaṃ siddhyarthī siddhimāpnuyāt || trikāle paṭhate nityaṃ śmaśāne śivamandire | japitvā rajanīmantrai hṛdayaṃ bhairavasya ca || kīlakaṃ tatpravakṣyāmi yantroddhāraṃ samākula | bhavānkṛtyekacitte bho vistarādvatatāmaham ||
> Having heard the word of the speech-guru, the Śāmbhava Śaṅkara: "the heart[-mantra], O Lord — the Unmattabhairava of Vārāṇasī." Whoever always recites the Unmattā heart[-mantra] with its limbs — he gains the desired siddhi, free of the manifold afflictions. The destroyer of the ḍākinī-faults of the planets, the beings and the rest: the Kāmākṣā he should ever contemplate, holding [her] in the heart. The seeker of knowledge gains knowledge; the seeker of wealth gains wealth; the seeker of liberation gains liberation; the seeker of siddhi attains siddhi. Reciting daily at the three times, in the burning ground, in the Śiva-temple; having chanted with the night-mantras the heart[-mantra] of Bhairava — the nailing (kīlaka) I will tell, and the yantra-uddhāra, complete: "O Lord, with one-pointed mind, I will tell at length."

**Colophon** — iti pārameśvaratantre vārāṇasī paṭale unmattabhairavopākhyāne gururudrasaṃvāde śrī-unmatta bhairavahṛdayaṃ samāptam | śubham || saṃ 2972 śā 2836 vaiśākha 2 gate 2 vāsare śrī unmattabhairavadāsa la0 pra0 a0 śarmaṇā lekhī ||
> Thus in the Parameśvara-tantra, in the Vārāṇasī-paṭala, in the episode of Unmattabhairava, in the dialogue of Guru and Rudra, the blessed Unmattabhairava-heart[-mantra] is complete. Auspiciousness.
