# Rasa Leads — New Research Directions from the Rasa Thesis

## Purpose

This document evaluates 12 new research leads that emerged from testing the RASA_THESIS against recent scholarship, cross-cultural philosophy, affective neuroscience, and formal theories of consciousness. Each lead is assessed for: (a) what it adds to the rasa thesis, (b) its evidential strength, and (c) the next specific step required.

---

## Lead 1: Abhinavagupta's Apoha as the Missing Partition Mechanism

**Source:** Catherine Prueitt, "Divine and Human Agency as Art: Abhinavagupta on Aesthetics and World Carving" (2026, Cambridge University Press).

**Claim:** Abhinavagupta repurposes the Buddhist doctrine of apoha (exclusion) as an agentive world-carving operation. A concept does not just define an object by excluding alternatives — it simultaneously carves out the subject's domain of agency.

**Why this matters for the rasa thesis:**
The rasa thesis left the partition problem (Λₚ) open. Apoha as world-carving supplies the missing operation:

\[
E_i: \Omega \rightarrow (P_i, W_i)
\]

Where Eᵢ is an exclusion operator that simultaneously carves out a subject (Pᵢ) and a world (Wᵢ) from a larger possibility domain Ω. The kañcukas become specific exclusion operations:
- Kalā: excludes actions not available
- Vidyā: excludes distinctions not accessible
- Rāga: excludes states not presently fulfilled
- Kāla: excludes times not presently available
- Niyati: excludes locations and causal paths not reachable

**What it adds to the aperture model:**
The phenomenal boundary is not an additional wall — it IS the intrinsic side of the system's constitutive exclusions. This connects directly to aperture exclusion:

\[
\operatorname{AEx}(O_i, D_i)
\]

**Status: High-priority philosophical lead.** The Prueitt paper is new (2026) and needs direct reading. If confirmed, it transforms the kañcukas from passive constraints into active exclusion operators.

**Decisive question:** Does Abhinavagupta use exclusion merely to determine objects (Eᵢ: Ω → Wᵢ), or also to individuate the agent (Eᵢ: Ω → (Pᵢ, Wᵢ))? The difference determines whether the partition problem is solved or merely relocated.

**Next step:** Read the Prueitt paper directly. Verify the claim that exclusion is agentive (not merely conceptual). Formalize the exclusion operator in the aperture framework.

## Lead 2: Michel Henry's Auto-Affection as Independent Convergence

**Source:** Michel Henry (1922-2002), *The Essence of Manifestation*, *Material Phenomenology*.

**Claim:** The most basic manifestation is not perception of an external object but life's immediate self-appearing through auto-affection. Self-consciousness is not something the self possesses but what subjectivity IS.

**Why this matters:**
Henry independently arrives at almost exactly the same pressure as Abhinavagupta: appearing cannot be affectively neutral because its most primitive mode is being affected by its own occurrence. The structural isomorphism:

| Abhinavagupta | Michel Henry | Inexternalism |
|---------------|-------------|---------------|
| Prakāśa + camatkāra | Phenomenality + auto-affection | Mᵢ + ρᵢ |
| Self-manifesting relish | Life experiencing itself | Self-affective manifestation |

**Cross-cultural argument:** This is not a single-text claim. Two independent traditions (7th-century Kashmir Śaivism and 20th-century French phenomenology) converged on the same structural distinction between bare manifestation and self-affective manifestation. This convergence is evidence that the distinction is not culturally arbitrary.

**Status: Strong philosophical lead.** Adds cross-cultural weight to the ρᵢ claim. Henry's sharp distinction between "invisible self-revelation of life" and "external worldly appearing" creates a challenge for inexternalism — the framework must resist splitting the two.

**Decisive question:** Is Henry's auto-affection compatible with the claim that physical organization has an intrinsic aspect (inexternalism), or does Henry require a separate substance?

**Next step:** Henry's *Material Phenomenology*. Compare with Abhinavagupta's concept of vimarśa as intrinsically active (not just receptive) self-awareness.

## Lead 3: Biernacki's Matter of Wonder — Vimarśa as Sentient Agency

**Source:** Loriliai Biernacki, *The Matter of Wonder: Abhinavagupta's Panentheism and the New Materialism* (2023, Oxford University Press).

**Claim:** Abhinavagupta locates subjectivity at the core of matter rather than opposing consciousness to materiality. Vimarśa (reflective awareness) is not merely reflexive representation — it is reflexive representation with endogenous causal and affective force.

**Why this matters:**
Biernacki argues that vimarśa is closer to sentient first-person awareness than generic terms like cit or saṃvid, because vimarśa implies ACTIVITY and an ability to act in material reality. Her chapter on AI argues that this makes vimarśa directly relevant to artificial consciousness.

**What it adds:**
If vimarśa = intrinsic self-articulation WITH causal consequences, then:

\[
\text{vimarśa} \rightarrow \text{intrinsic self-articulation}
\]
\[
\text{rāga} \rightarrow \text{self-relevant valuation}
\]
\[
\text{kalā} \rightarrow \text{effective agency}
\]

A CIMC system would need second-order perception that CHANGES WHAT MATTERS to the same persistent system — not just self-representation but self-representation with endogenous force.

**Status: Strong bridge to AI consciousness.** Directly relevant to the synthetic/artificial consciousness question.

**Decisive question:** Is vimarśa merely reflexive representation, or reflexive representation with endogenous causal and affective force?

**Next step:** Read Biernacki's book, especially the AI chapter. Integrate vimarśa-as-activity into the aperture model.

## Lead 4: Panksepp's Primary-Process Consciousness — Affect Before Representation

**Source:** Jaak Panksepp, *Affective Neuroscience* (1998); "The brain's emotional foundations of human personality" (PubMed 21527289).

**Claim:** Phenomenal consciousness may begin as affective mattering before becoming detailed object cognition. Primary-process affective systems (seeking, fear, rage, lust, care, panic, play) encode basic biological valences before higher conceptual thought.

**Why this matters:**
This reverses the standard order:

Standard: representation → evaluation → feeling
Panksepp: basal affect → salience → object differentiation

This is the empirical version of ρᵢ → νᵢ. If Panksepp is right, the five pure tattvas may describe the differentiation of an originally affective-agency field: self-affective presence → desire (rāga) → attention (vidyā) → object (kalā).

**What it adds:**
A direct biological parallel to the rasa-first hypothesis. Primary-process affect is NOT learned, NOT cognitive, and NOT representational — it IS the most basic form of consciousness.

**Status: Strong empirical parallel.** Needs integration with the kañcuka system. Rāga (attachment) becomes much more central than previously assumed — it may be the proto-affective ground from which the other kañcukas differentiate.

**Decisive question:** Are Panksepp's primary-process affects the biological instantiation of what the Tantrāloka calls the kañcukas? Specifically, is rāga the proto-affective ground?

**Next step:** Map Panksepp's seven primary-process affective systems onto the five kañcukas. Test whether the predicted mapping predicts known affective neuroscience results.

## Lead 5: Valence as Felt Rate of Adaptation

**Source:** Active inference models of valence (Frontiers in Psychology, 2020).

**Claim:** Valence tracks how well an agent is improving its control over its environment. Positive valence = prediction errors being reduced faster than expected. Negative valence = unexpectedly worsening control.

\[
\nu_i(t) \propto -\frac{d}{dt}[\text{unexpected regulatory error}]
\]

**Why this matters:**
This explains several otherwise puzzling facts that static models (including naive STV) cannot:
- Complete predictability becomes boring (no error reduction → zero valence)
- Manageable surprise can be pleasurable (error reduction in progress)
- Progress feels better than static success (derivative, not level)
- Sudden comprehension produces pleasure (rapid error reduction)
- Play deliberately creates reducible uncertainty

**Connection to camatkāra:**
Wonder is not static symmetry. It may arise from an expansion of intelligibility occurring faster than expected:

\[
\text{vismaya} = \text{high novelty}
\]
\[
\text{vikāsa} = \text{expansion of model or action space}
\]
\[
\text{ānanda} = \text{successful integration or increased control}
\]

**Novel prediction:** Camatkāra-like experience peaks when three factors coincide:

\[
\text{novelty} + \text{rapid integration} + \text{expanded perceived possibility}
\]

Too little novelty = boredom. Too much unresolved novelty = confusion/anxiety. The "rasa zone" is an optimal trajectory through uncertainty.

**Status: Strong mechanistic bridge.** Better than STV because it explains temporal dynamics and context-dependence. Directly testable with music, insight problems, and contemplative states.

**Decisive question:** Can the three-factor model (novelty + integration + expansion) predict chill-eliciting stimuli better than a symmetry-only model?

**Next step:** Re-analyze ChillDB stimuli using the three-factor model. Generate stimuli that independently manipulate each factor and measure reported wonder.

## Lead 6: Depersonalization vs. Meditative Selflessness

**Source:** Active inference work on depersonalization (Frontiers in Psychology, 2020).

**Claim:** Both depersonalization and meditative selflessness involve reduced ordinary self-models, yet their affective consequences are opposite — alienation vs. peace. The difference lies in perceived control, interoceptive precision, and whether basal self-affection persists.

**Why this matters:**
This is one of the sharpest empirical tests for the ρᵢ/νᵢ distinction. If autobiographical ego were the source of consciousness or value, weakening it should always be harmful. It is not.

**Inexternalist interpretation:**

Positive case: ρᵢ↑, ownership↓
Negative case: ρᵢ↓, ownership↓

**Abhinavagupta connection:**
Recognition may not eliminate the local field. It reduces ownership contraction while increasing self-relish:

ahaṃkāra contraction↓ while camatkāra↑

**Status: Directly testable discriminator.** Depersonalized patients and experienced meditators both report reduced self-boundary. The difference in ρᵢ (basal self-affection) should be measurable through: interoceptive accuracy, affective report, peripersonal space tasks, and resting-state fMRI.

**Decisive question:** Does meditative selflessness increase or decrease basal self-affection (ρᵢ) as measured by implicit affective processing?

**Next step:** Design a study comparing depersonalized patients, experienced meditators, and controls on measures of implicit affect, self-boundary, and interoception.

## Lead 7: Aesthetic Chills as a Measurable Camatkāra Analogue

**Source:** ChillDB — validated audiovisual stimuli for eliciting aesthetic chills (Nature Scientific Data, 2023).

**Claim:** Aesthetic chills (frissons, goosebumps from music/art) involve wonder, surprise, absorption, bodily transformation, awe, and mixed emotions — making them a closer laboratory analogue to camatkāra than simple pleasantness ratings.

**Why this matters:**
Camatkāra is not "I enjoy this." It is a complex state involving wonder (vismaya), expansion (vikāsa), and bliss (ānanda). Chills share all three components. ChillDB provides validated stimuli for eliciting them.

**Experimental design:**
Measure separately: novelty (N), integration/insight (I), expansion of perceived possibility (E), valence (V), wonder (W), chill occurrence (C). Test:

\[
C, W = f(N, I, E, V)
\]

Rather than assuming C = symmetry.

**Status: Directly usable empirical protocol.** Validated stimuli exist. The three-factor model can be tested immediately.

**Decisive question:** Does the three-factor model (novelty + rapid integration + expansion) predict chill occurrence better than stimulus symmetry alone?

**Next step:** Re-analyze ChillDB stimuli using acoustic/visual novelty metrics, integration rates, and possibility-space expansion measures.

## Lead 8: Formal Quality Spaces Beyond QRI

**Source:** Johannes Kleiner and Tim Ludwig, "What is a Mathematical Structure of Conscious Experience?" (arXiv 2301.11812).

**Claim:** The correct order is: (1) define a phenomenal domain, (2) identify its relations, (3) construct a mathematical structure, (4) test whether the structure preserves those relations, (5) search for physical isomorphisms. NOT: assume symmetry → measure symmetry → call it consciousness.

**Why this matters:**
QRI's STV skips steps 1-3 and jumps to a specific mathematical candidate (symmetry). Kleiner and Ludwig provide a general framework for doing it properly.

**Revised formal programme:**
Let 𝒫ᵢ = (Xᵢ, Rᵢ, Tᵢ, Vᵢ) be a phenomenal structure (qualities, similarity/co-presence relations, transitions, valence ordering). Let 𝒩ᵢ be a neural/computational structure. Test for a structure-preserving map Fᵢ: 𝒩ᵢ → 𝒫ᵢ. The goal is not correlation of individual variables but preservation of relational structure:

\[
R_𝒩(x, y) ≈ R_𝒫(F(x), F(y))
\]

**Status: Methodological correction.** Does not produce new empirical results but prevents premature commitments.

**Decisive question:** Can the Tymoczko chord spaces be reformulated as quality spaces in Kleiner and Ludwig's sense?

**Next step:** Formalize the auditory quality space using Kleiner-Ludwig framework. Test whether Tymoczko's geometry preserves phenomenal similarity relations.

## Lead 9: Structure-Quality Fidelity

**Source:** Ryan Williams, "Structure & Quality: Conceptual and Formal Foundations for the Mind-Body Problem" (arXiv 2505.05481).

**Claim:** Introduces measures of mutual determination between structural and qualitative domains. If valence has causal relevance, organisms whose phenomenal valence systematically misrepresented viability would be selected against.

**Why this matters:**
Under strong inexternalism: physical and phenomenal structures should display high token-level mutual constraint — not merely loose population-level correlation. If radically different phenomenal geometries could accompany identical aperture organization, the identity claim weakens.

**Status: Formal tool for testing the identity claim.** Not yet applied to any specific case.

**Decisive question:** What is the fidelity (F_SQ) between Tymoczko's chord spaces and reported phenomenal similarity spaces?

**Next step:** Compute the structure-preserving map between Tymoczko's chord space geometry and human similarity judgments. Report the fidelity measure.

## Lead 10: Qualia as Measurements

**Source:** Pedro Resende, "Qualia as physical measurements: a mathematical model of qualia and pure concepts" (arXiv 2203.10602).

**Claim:** Qualia spaces and physical measurement spaces share a topological and algebraic structure. A measurement creates a stable classical distinction; a phenomenal quality also appears as a determinate distinction.

**Why this matters:**
This gives a possible formal correspondence:

\[
\text{vimarśa} \sim \text{the physical production of determinate distinctions}
\]

Could one subject be the closure of all measurement-like distinctions that are mutually available within one process? This would connect: determination, co-presence, observer construction, classical information, and aperture boundaries.

**Status: Speculative but formally interesting.** Not yet connected to any empirical phenomenon.

**Decisive question:** Can the closure of a measurement algebra be shown to correspond to the unity (Uᵢ) of a phenomenal field?

**Next step:** Formalize the measurement-algebra model in terms of the aperture tuple. Derive a prediction about when two measurements belong to the same subject.

## Lead 11: Neuroaesthetics as a Warning

**Source:** Neuroaesthetic research reviews (PubMed 26698020).

**Claim:** Aesthetic experience depends on distributed interactions among sensory processing, reward, motor systems, familiarity, expertise, embodiment, and context. No single variable (symmetry, proportion, complexity) predicts aesthetic response reliably.

**Why this matters:**
This weakens any simple claim like "aesthetic pleasure = one symmetry measure." But it STRENGTHENS a processual rasa interpretation: aesthetic relish is not a property of the stimulus but a relation:

\[
\text{Rasa}_i = f(\text{stimulus structure}, Q_i, \Gamma_i, V_i, A_i, C_i)
\]

That is deeply inexternal. The felt quality does not exist solely in the music, the brain, or an abstract geometric form — it exists as the intrinsic aspect of an organism-world process.

**Better term:** "Enacted phenomenal geometry" rather than "stimulus geometry."

**Status: Proper constraint on theorizing.** Prevents overreach.

**Decisive question:** Does the inexternalist framework predict neuroaesthetic findings that single-variable models cannot?

**Next step:** Formalize the "enacted phenomenal geometry" concept. Test whether the quadratic model predicts neuroimaging results better than the linear model.

## Lead 12: Wonder as Expansion of the Aperture

**Source:** Biernacki's analysis of camatkāra as embodied, connected to breath, inward subjectivity, and transformation (OUP Academic, chapter 3).

**Claim:** Wonder is not one valence state within an unchanged subject. It is a temporary expansion of the subject's aperture.

\[
\text{Apt}(O_i) \rightarrow \text{Apt}'(O_i)
\]

Where: 𝒪ᵢ' ⊃ 𝒪ᵢ (expanded action space), 𝒜ᵢ' ⊃ 𝒜ᵢ (expanded affordance space), or Qᵢ' uses a less restrictive partition.

**Phenomenologically:**
- More connections become salient
- The world feels larger
- The ordinary self-boundary weakens
- Novelty and meaning intensify
- Time may expand or suspend
- Valence rises

**Formal hypothesis:**

\[
\text{Wonder}_i = \Delta[\text{ReachableMeaningSpace}_i] > 0
\]

This is a much more specific account of vikāsa: wonder = felt expansion of possible articulation.

**Status: Strong formal hypothesis.** Testable in music, mathematical insight, meditation, nature-induced awe, scientific discovery, and religious ritual.

**Decisive question:** Can reachable-meaning-space be operationalized and measured before/during/after wonder-eliciting stimuli?

**Next step:** Develop a measure of reachable meaning space (e.g., free association fluency, semantic network distance). Apply before/during/after chill-eliciting music.

## Lead 13: Prasiddhi — Certainty as the Intrinsic Felt Quality of Successful Carving

**Source:** Tantrāloka Vol 11, lines 13340-13368; COMPENDIUM §3.4 Missed Lead 4.

**Claim:** A priori certainty (prasiddhi) grounds all knowledge. Babies know that food nourishes without inference. Certainty is not derived from evidence — it IS the ground from which evidence gets its force.

**Why this matters:** If apoha (Lead 1) is the exclusion operation that carves the field, and adaptation rate (Lead 5) tracks how well carving is going, then prasiddhi is the INTRINSIC FELT QUALITY that carving is succeeding. The baby does not infer that milk is good — it experiences goodness directly. This is ρᵢ in its most primitive form: the certainty that THIS is what I need IS the positive valence of getting it.

**Connects Leads 1, 5, and 12 into a complete loop:** apoha carves → geometry structures → adaptation tracks → certainty feels → wonder opens → new carving begins.

**Push deeper:** Operationally, does prasiddhi correspond to low prediction-error variance? Can we measure "certainty" as inverse of surprise and test whether it tracks valence?

## Lead 14: Biernacki's Fermentation Model — Multiplicity Cooperating into Unity

**Source:** Biernacki, "Tantric Bodies, AI Immortality, and a Yogurt Model of Self" (Routledge 2023). Files in clean/resources/by-scholar/biernacki/ and blog sources.

**Claim:** The self is not a binary of body vs. soul or body vs. information. It is a fermentation — a multiplicity of agencies (microbes, sub-personalities, endocrine signals, subtle bodies) that symbiotically cooperate into a coherent unity. Yogurt, kombucha, sourdough.

**Why this matters for the aperture model:** The carving operation (Eᵢ) needs something to carve. Fermentation says what gets carved is not a blank field but an ALREADY MULTIPLICITY of competing/cooperating sub-agencies. The subject IS the successful fermentation — not a controller that manages the multiplicity but the multiplicity's temporary coherence.

**Key Abhinavagupta quotes from Biernacki:**
- "Consciousness alone is the highest ultimate body (vapuḥ) of all entities" — erases body/soul binary
- "What characterizes the soul, consciousness (cid), is itself a body" — radical embodiment

**Links to:** kañcuka system (conditions for successful fermentation), depersonalization (failed fermentation), the Uᵢ binding variable (unity as accomplishment, not given).

**Push deeper:**
- Does Biernacki explicitly connect fermentation to the kañcukas?
- Can fermentation be formalized as an operation over multiple sub-agents?
- Does the subtle body (sūkṣma śarīra) correspond to the aperture's "body within a body"?

## Lead 15: Svātantrya + Intuitionist Mathematics — Freedom as Built into Time

**Source:** Biernacki, "Abhinavagupta's Svātantryavāda" (Bloomsbury 2023). Full text extracted as biernacki_Abhinavagupta_s_Svatantryavada_Mental_Ca.txt.

**Claim:** Abhinavagupta's svātantryavāda converges with Brouwer's intuitionist mathematics and Gisin's quantum physics. Real numbers are processes in time, not pre-existing Platonic objects. The future is genuinely open. Consciousness's "carving" (apoha) is how indeterminacy becomes locally determinate.

**The primary Sanskrit source we should have been citing:**

"sarvathā punar avicchinnacamatkāranirapekṣasvātantryāhaṃvimarśe"

Translation: "The freedom of the uninterrupted delight of I-consciousness is completely independent of any reference to anything else."

This directly links: avicchinnacamatkāra (uninterrupted wonder) + svātantrya (freedom) + ahaṃvimarśa (I-awareness). It is the rasa thesis in Abhinavagupta's own words.

**The math connection:**
- Brouwer: mathematics is "an essentially languageless activity of the mind having its origin in the perception of a move of time"
- Gisin: the Bekenstein bound means no finite volume contains infinite information → real numbers are processes, not completed objects
- Abhinavagupta: svātantrya is the capacity to act "on the level of Māyā" — within constraint

**Push deeper:**
- Read Gisin directly (his papers on intuitionist math and free will are accessible)
- Does Brouwer's "move of time" match spanda (the pulse of consciousness)?
- Can Eᵢ (exclusion) be formalized as a choice operation in intuitionist logic?

## The Updated Frontier

The rasa thesis evolves from:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Consciousness is intrinsically self-affective manifestation}
}
\]

To the more specific claim:

\[
\boxed{
\text{A phenomenal subject is an intrinsically self-affective,}
}
\]
\[
\boxed{
\text{world-carving process whose constitutive exclusions}
}
\]
\[
\boxed{
\text{generate one bounded field, whose geometry and}
}
\]
\[
\boxed{
\text{regulatory trajectory determine local valence, and}
}
\]
\[
\boxed{
\text{whose temporary expansion is experienced as wonder.}
}
\]

Four precise unknowns remain:

\[
\boxed{\text{manifestness} \stackrel{?}{\Rightarrow} \text{self-affection}}
\]
\[
\boxed{\text{exclusion} \stackrel{?}{\Rightarrow} \text{phenomenal boundary}}
\]
\[
\boxed{\text{regulatory trajectory} \stackrel{?}{\Rightarrow} \text{felt valence}}
\]
\[
\boxed{\text{expansion of possibility} \stackrel{?}{\Rightarrow} \text{wonder}}
\]

The most important surprise across these leads: **apoha and camatkāra may be complementary**:

\[
\boxed{\text{apoha carves the finite field;}}
\]
\[
\boxed{\text{camatkāra is how the field intrinsically lives}}
\]
\[
\boxed{\text{its own articulation.}}
\]

That is potentially the next major step beyond the current formulation. If exclusion and self-affection are two sides of the same process — the extrinsic operation and its intrinsic quality — then the partition problem and the hard problem are not two problems but one.
