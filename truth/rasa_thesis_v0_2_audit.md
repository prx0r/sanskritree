# Rasa Thesis v0.2 — Complete Audit

## Overall verdict

The thesis contains a **strong philosophical core**, but its proposed proof currently overstates all four supporting frameworks.

The defensible core is:

Consciousness may be intrinsically self-affective manifestation rather than affectively neutral presence.

The defensible modern extension is:

phenomenal experience has relational structure that may admit mathematical representation.

The unsupported extension is:

geometry = symmetry = beauty = felt quality.

The thesis should therefore be reformulated as **Rasa Inexternalism**, not as a symmetry or golden-ratio theory of consciousness.

---

## 1. The Abhinavagupta material

### What survives

The quotation describing supreme consciousness as "by its very nature" the aesthetic relish of consciousness is genuine Abhinavaguptan material. However, it comes from the Parātrīśikāvivaraṇa and is cited by Dyczkowski while commenting on Tantrāloka chapter 1; it is not itself a root Tantrāloka verse. (Dokumen pub)

This supports: Nature(C∞) = SelfRelish(C∞).

It does not establish: ∀x, [Conscious(x) → Pleasant(x)].

The language concerns supreme consciousness, fullness, freedom, reflexivity, and self-relish. It does not mean that panic, pain, boredom, or confusion are secretly ordinary pleasures.

The strongest direct Tantrāloka evidence found is 4.115: "viśvam etat svasaṃvitti-rasa-nirbharitaṃ rasāt." This strongly links the universe with saturation by the rasa of self-consciousness. "The universe is condensed rasa" is a striking paraphrase, but "filled" or "saturated with the rasa of its own consciousness" is closer to the Sanskrit compound. (Wisdom Library)

### What still requires recovery

The following claims remain unverified because the supplied references are OCR locations rather than scholarly citations:
- ahaṅcamatkāra as "the aesthetic rapture of I am"
- camatkāra as my-ness
- the senses as ciccamatkāra
- the exact vīṇā passage
- the supposed direct relation between colored goddesses and proportion

Each needs: work + Sanskrit + chapter/verse or page + edition + context + Jayaratha's gloss.

## 2. The crucial affective distinction

The thesis should permanently distinguish:

ρᵢ = basal self-affection (manifestation being non-indifferent to its own occurrence)

from:

νᵢ(t) = local pleasant or unpleasant valence (the particular painful, pleasant, tense quality of the current state)

This solves a major ambiguity.

Basal self-affection includes: intrinsic first-person presence, self-savouring, affective immediacy, the fact that manifestation matters to itself. It does not mean positive mood.

The stronger Śaiva position: ρᵢ > 0 while νᵢ(t) < 0. That would mean even painful experience exists within a more fundamental power of self-manifestation, without converting pain into pleasure.

---

## 3. Abhinavagupta's aesthetics gives more than "pleasure"

The strongest relevant scholarship portrays rasa as a transformed mode of emotion rather than simple hedonic enjoyment. Abhinavagupta's aesthetic spectator undergoes generalized emotion, freed from ordinary personal and practical constraints. Negative emotions can be relished aesthetically without becoming ordinary real-world suffering.

Rasa is not: positive stimulus → pleasure response.
It is: emotion + depersonalization from practical ownership + heightened reflexive presence → relish.

This gives a possible new structure:

Rasaᵢ = F(emotion, universalization, reduced egoic appropriation, self-presence).

The crucial variable may not be symmetry. It may be freedom from restrictive ownership while preserving affective intensity. This fits: σ_owner↓ while ρᵢ↑.

---

## 4. Tymoczko's actual contribution

Tymoczko securely establishes that chords can be represented as points in orbifold spaces and that voice leadings can be represented as paths between them. (PubMed)

His five features of tonality are: conjunct melodic motion, acoustic consonance, harmonic consistency, limited macroharmony, centricity.

The valid inference: multiple musical constraints can converge on a restricted family of efficient musical structures.

The invalid inference: constraint convergence = felt beauty.

The useful empirical hypothesis: d_𝒮(x,y) ∼ d_𝒫(F(x),F(y)) where d_𝒮 is musical stimulus-space distance, d_𝒫 is reported phenomenal similarity, and F is the psychophysical mapping.

## 5. "Overdetermination" should be retained—but carefully

Conv(x) = degree to which distinct constraints jointly favor x.

The hypothesis: Conv(x) → experienced rightness, inevitability, or significance.

Three alternatives remain: constitutive (Conv = aesthetic significance), causal (Conv → processing → significance), or correlational (Conv ↔ significance because both depend on another variable).

---

## 6. Doczi and φ: remove from the proof

The golden-ratio component is the weakest part. Reviews report longstanding disagreement over golden-section preference, and recent work warns that many celebrated aesthetic claims are unsupported. (PMC)

Recurrence of φ ≠ universal phenomenal structure. The recurrence can result from packing efficiency, recursive growth, measurement selection, cultural borrowing, retrospective fitting, or publication bias.

Prox(𝒢ᵢ, φ) should be removed from the central formula.

---

## 7. QRI's correct status

QRI's Qualia Formalism is a foundational commitment, not an established result. STV is a hypothesis requiring specification of the phenomenal object, symmetry group, scale, physical measurement, and psychophysical mapping.

The more neutral formal foundation comes from Kleiner and Ludwig, who ask what it means for conscious experience itself to instantiate mathematical structure. (arXiv)

---

## 8. Four spaces must remain separate

𝒮 (stimulus), 𝒩 (neural), 𝒫 (phenomenal), ℛ (report). The complete chain requires f:𝒮→𝒩, g:𝒩→𝒫, h:𝒫→ℛ. Even perfect correlations do not automatically establish 𝒩=𝒫.

---

## 9. Vijñānabhairava: protocol library, not evidence

The Vijñānabhairava provides a diverse repertoire of contemplative instructions. But it does not experimentally demonstrate Mᵢ survives removal of every content variable. The practices lack control groups, standardized outcome measures, selective manipulation, blinded analysis, physiological monitoring, and reproducibility tests.

Correct description: a library of candidate first-person perturbations.

---

## 10. Corrected formal model

Old: νᵢ = f(Overdet(𝒢ᵢ), Prox(𝒢ᵢ, φ), Sym(𝒢ᵢ))

Corrected: νᵢ(t) = Fᵢ(𝒢ᵢ(t), Rᵢ(t), ΔCᵢ(t), Eᵢ(t), Hᵢ, Ξᵢ(t))

Where: 𝒢ᵢ = phenomenal relational structure, Rᵢ = regulatory condition, ΔCᵢ = changes in expected control, Eᵢ = learned expectation, Hᵢ = developmental/cultural history, Ξᵢ = current context.

Within 𝒢ᵢ we can test candidate features: symmetry, constraint convergence, roughness, harmonicity, compression, novelty, metastability. None should be built into the theory before model comparison.

## 11. Aesthetic rasa formulation

Rasaᵢ = F(affective activation, constraint convergence, reduced practical ownership, heightened self-presence, expanded intelligibility)

Rasa is a transformation in how one phenomenal field partitions, owns, and acts upon its own affective content.

Apt(Oᵢ) ⟷ one self-affective phenomenal field.

## 13. Highest-priority commentary questions

1. Is camatkāra present in every cognition or only heightened realization?
2. Is it affective relish, reflexive cognition, agency, or an inseparable unity?
3. How does Abhinavagupta distinguish ānanda, rasa, camatkāra, āsvāda, carvaṇā, vismaya, vikāsa, viśrānti?
4. Why can tragic or painful rasa be relished?
5. Does aesthetic universalization alter phenomenal ownership, field boundary, or only intentional interpretation?
6. Is aesthetic rasa merely analogous to liberation, or a partial manifestation of the same self-relish?
7. Does tanmayatā mean literal identity, absorption, conformity, or dominant attention?
8. How does foundational bliss relate to ordinary suffering?

## 14. Revised empirical programme

Experiment A — Constraint convergence: Manipulate voice-leading efficiency, harmonicity, roughness, expectation, macroharmonic consistency, centricity, familiarity independently. Measure beauty, pleasantness, tension, inevitability, wonder, absorption, emotional intensity separately.

Experiment B — Structural correspondence: Estimate d_𝒮, d_𝒩, d_ℛ. Determine whether stimulus geometry, neural geometry, and reported similarity preserve common relational structure.

Experiment C — Self-affection vs ownership: Compare ordinary wakefulness, aesthetic absorption, contemplative selflessness, depersonalization, emotional numbing, minimal-content states. Measure Mᵢ, ρᵢ, σ_owner, νᵢ, ∂ᵢ separately.

Experiment D — Vijñānabhairava perturbations: Convert selected practices into standardized multivariate interventions. Aim for partial dissociations among phenomenal variables.

---

## Final judgment

Retain: consciousness as intrinsically self-affective manifestation.
Retain: phenomenal structure may be mathematically representable.
Retain as testable: aesthetic significance may track convergence among multiple constraints.

Remove from central argument: φ, naive symmetry identity, goddess-color proportions, the five-bliss/logarithmic-valence mapping, the claim that contemplative texts already supply experimental evidence.

Strongest final formulation: Rasa Inexternalism is the hypothesis that a phenomenal subject is the intrinsic, self-affective manifestation of one objectively individuated process. Its field possesses relational structure that may admit mathematical representation. Local valence is not identical with bare manifestation or basal self-affection; it is modulated by field organization, regulation, expectation, embodiment, history, and context. Physical and phenomenal structures are two modes of access to one token process, not two interacting substances.
